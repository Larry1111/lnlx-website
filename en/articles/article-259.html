English name: University of Oklahoma

School website: http://www.ou.edu

Main Campus: Oklahoma

Admission requirements: IBT score of 79 or IELTS 6.5 or above, high school graduate, GPA above 3.0;

Master's degree requires GRE or GMAT scores, IBT 79 or IELTS 6.5, bachelor's degree, GPA of 3.0 or above.

Dual undergraduate admission requirements: IBT 61, IELTS 5.0, or a C score in the university language center overseas test.

Tuition: Undergraduate $18,000/year; Master's $13,000–26,000/year.

The University of Oklahoma (OU) is a public university founded in 1890. In 2011, it ranked 111th in the overall U.S. university rankings, with about 30,000 students enrolled, and international students making up 5% of the total student body. There are three campuses: the Norman campus, the largest health sciences center campus in Oklahoma City, and the Tulsa campus. The University of Oklahoma has nine undergraduate schools and nine research schools. The Energy Center costs millions of dollars, advancing petroleum engineering, petroleum land management, and geology. Many Middle Eastern countries specifically send students to study all petroleum-related sciences. Besides engineering, the University of Oklahoma is also renowned for its law, architecture, business, meteorology, education, and medicine programs. Its undergraduate programs rank in the top 50, with international trade in the top 15. Education ranks 46th, and Master's in Education ranks in the top 10% nationwide. Environmental Sanitation Engineering ranks 47th. The doctoral program in Interpersonal Communication ranks in the top ten nationwide. The most studied disciplines by freshmen are: Business Management (24%), Mass Communication (13%), Engineering (11%), Social Sciences (10%), and Education (8%).

There are more than 2,000 full-time faculty and staff. The university has more than 20 colleges, mainly: College of Architecture, College of Arts and Sciences, College of Business, College of Education, College of Engineering, College of Fine Arts, College of Atmospheric and Geographic Sciences, College of Earth and Energy, Gaylord College of Journalism and Mass Communication, College of Humanities, College of Allied Health, College of Nursing, College of Dentistry, College of Pharmacy, College of Public Health, and College of Law. It offers 154 undergraduate programs, 160 master's programs, and 80 doctoral programs. The University of Oklahoma Health Sciences Center, located in Oklahoma City, has seven colleges and is one of only four comprehensive academic health centers in the United States.

Oklahoma is a state in the south-central United States. It covers 181089 square kilometers, ranking 20th among the 50 states. The climate is generally mild. The north is usually cooler than other regions; The eastern and southern regions are humid in climate, while the western regions are drier. The average temperatures in January and July are 4°C and 28°C, respectively. General Motors、American Telephone and Telegraph Company and other major companies all have branches in Russia. There are more than 50 companies in Russia Bank and more than 20 credit institutions. Oklahoma has a well-developed education system, with over 50% of its population having received college education, with the University of Oklahoma being the most famous. Agriculture plays an important role in the state's economy, with oil wells distributed throughout the state. Oklahoma has 50 state parks, 28 state recreation areas, and 48 state wildlife management areas. Many of the state's rivers and lakes offer various recreational activities, such as boating, water skiing, swimming, and fishing.

俄克拉荷马大学,The University of Oklahoma (简称OU)是一所公立大学，成立于1890年。2011年美国大学综合排名111名，大学在校学生约三万人，国际学生占学生总数的5%。有三处校园：Norman校园，Oklahoma城最大的健康科学中心校园及Tulsa校园。俄克拉荷马大学设有9个本科学院和9个研究学院，能源中心(Energy Center)耗资数百万元，使得石油工程、石油土地专业管理及地质学向前迈进一步。不少中东国家，都特地遣送学生来学习一切与石油有关的科学。除了工程之外，俄克拉荷马大学的法律、建筑、商科、气象学、教育、医科也很有名，商学院的本科教育居前50，其中国际贸易专业居前15。教育学排名第46，教育学硕士居全美前10%。环境卫生工程专业排名第47。人际传播学博士课程排全美前十。新生攻读最多的学科是：商科管理(24%)、大众传播学(13%)、工程 (11%)、社会科学(10%)、教育(8%)。

全职的教职员工2千多名。大学下设20多个学院，主要是：建筑学院、文理学院、商学院、教育学院、工程学院、美术学院、大气与地理科学学院、地球与能源学院，新闻与大众传媒学院，人文学院、联合健康学院、护理学院、牙科学院、药学院、公共健康学院、法学院。可提供154个本科专业，160个硕士专业和80个博士专业。俄克拉荷马大学的健康科学中心位于俄克拉荷马城，下设七个学院，是全美仅有的四所综合性学术健康中心之一。

俄克拉荷马州，美国中南部一州。面积181089平方公里，在50州内列第20位。气候大体上算是温和的。北方通常比其他的地方来得凉爽；东部与南部气候潮湿，西部则较为乾燥。该州全州一月与七月的平均温度分别为摄氏四度与二 十八度。通用汽车、全美电报电话公司等大公司均在俄城设有分部。俄城共有50多家银行和20多个信贷机构。 俄城教育事业较为发达，50％以上的人受过大学教育, 以俄克拉荷马大学最为著名。农业在该州经济中占有重要地位，油井遍步全州。俄克拉何马州有50个州立公园，二十八处州立休闲区，以及四十八处州立野生生物管理区。在该州的许多河流和湖泊都可以从事各种休闲活动，像是：划船、滑水、游泳、以及钓鱼。
