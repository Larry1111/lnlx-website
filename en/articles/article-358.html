Founded in 1927, it is New Zealand The largest institution of education and research, and also the only truly national university in New Zealand, overseeing five colleges: Business School、Academy of Sciences、College of Art、Faculty of Education And Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences。 In the latest 2008 university rankings in New Zealand, it ranked third. The campus is distributed in Wellington、Oakland And Beipa The city has nearly 40,000 students, including 900 international students from more than 60 countries. Because the school's teaching emphasizes practical application and maintains close ties with businesses, enterprises, and all sectors of society, the curriculum is flexible and practical, and the research atmosphere is lively.

In the more than 70 years since its founding, Massey University has earned widespread acclaim both at home and abroad for its outstanding international education and research, placing it among the world's renowned universities. The school's successful experience is summarized as comprehensively developing students through learning, making friends, physical exercise, campus culture, and social activities, helping them grow into talented individuals. The university is well-known for its programs in technology, science, applied sciences, and business.

Massey University - Specialized

(1) Massey University has the most research achievements in New Zealand

(2) Massey University is also the university in New Zealand that transforms research and academic achievements into productivity

(3) Massey University has the largest business school in New Zealand

(4) Massey University is the world's first university-based rugby training school (National rugby team The sports science research of the all-black team is all at Massey)

(5) Massey University produced the world's first biotechnology scientist (1965)

(6) Massey University is the second established and owned university in the world Department of Food Engineering University.

(7) Massey University is the only one in New Zealand offering it Genetics Universities with undergraduate degrees

(8) Massey University is the only one in the world built on a campus Mouri Hall University (MAORI MEETING HOUSE)

(9) Massey University is the only institution in New Zealand to offer it Maori degree in Visual Arts University

(10) Massey University is the only institution in New Zealand offering a full three-year program Māori Teaching universities

(11) Massey University is the world's first university to offer truly distance teaching, using mail and e-mail methods

(12) Massey University is the only university in New Zealand with an aviation college and its own training airport

(13) Massey University is the only university in New Zealand offering a veterinary undergraduate degree

(14) Massey University is the first university in New Zealand to have a female president

(15) Massey University is the only university in New Zealand to develop satellites

(16) Massey University has the most world-class laboratories in New Zealand

Massey University - School Facilities

The school offers a full range of learning and entertainment facilities, including computer labs, a library, an entertainment center, a cafeteria, and offers various consultations in medicine, health, and job searching

Massey University - School Overview

The year is divided into three semesters: the first semester runs from February to June, the second semester from July to mid-October, and the third semester from November to February of the following year. Classes start in February and July each year.

Accommodation options

Students can choose to live in school dormitories, host families, or freely rent accommodations near the school in combination with other students. The school dormitories offer two types of accommodations: meals and self-cooked meals. Single rooms come with bathrooms, while large single rooms have shared bathrooms; There is a kitchen, laundry room, and a public activity area, with a telephone, microwave, and refrigerator—all fully equipped.

International Student Center

Experts in the International Students Department are responsible for providing various services to overseas students before and during their studies, including airport pickup, accommodation arrangements, medical insurance, and more, ensuring their study and life are safe, enjoyable, and successful academically.

梅西大学-学校设施

学校提供全套的学习和娱乐设施，包括计算机实验室，图书馆，娱乐中心，餐厅，并提供医药、健康、求职等各种咨

梅西大学-学校概况

一年分三个学期，第一学期2 - 6月，第二学期7 - 10月中旬，第三学期11月 - 次年2月。每年2、7月开学。

住宿选择

学生可以选择住学校宿舍、寄宿家庭和与其他学生自由组合在学校附近租房。学校宿舍分供应餐饮和自己做饭两种，单人间带浴室，大单人间带合用浴室；有厨房，洗衣房和公共活动区，电话、微波炉、冰箱一应俱全。

国际学生中心

留学生部的专家负责向海外学生提供入学前和在校期间的各种服务，包括接机、住宿安排、医疗保险等，保证他们的学习和生活安全、愉快，学业有成。
