Study abroad programs at Canadian higher vocational colleges

Canadian higher vocational education is traditionally what we call associate degree education, mainly consisting of two years, though some have three-year programs, primarily aimed at cultivating talent for business, industry, and public service sectors. Higher vocational colleges generally offer vocational-oriented courses and general academic courses. Colleges offer more vocational-related courses than universities, implement smaller class sizes, offer off-campus courses, have a larger ratio of laboratory space to classroom space, use interactive teaching methods, and have relaxed admission standards. They design various courses and practical training for students preparing to join the skilled and professional Canadian workforce in the future. Many higher vocational colleges offer paid internship programs.

In Canada, higher vocational education is widely welcomed by students. Attending university and attending a vocational college are two different choices, rather than just going to college if you can't attend university. Statistics show that 60% of local secondary school graduates go on to college. Canadian college programs generally offer more job courses targeted at the job market than universities, making it easier to find a job after graduation. Canadian colleges incorporate employer-centered courses into comprehensive institutions to respond to national economic policies. Many colleges have obtained accreditation from international standards organizations, and all colleges strive to meet the ever-changing learning needs of society.

Advantages of Canadian Higher Vocational Education (Public Associate Degree Programs):

[if !supportLists]1. [endif]Good employment prospects and strong targeting: Due to Canada's large area and small population, there is a high demand for labor, and there is a severe shortage of senior blue-collar workers, making it very easy to get a college diploma.

[if !supportLists]2. [endif]Lower entry requirements: a formal high school diploma is sufficient, and the English requirement is about 5.5 IELTS (those with insufficient scores can study a language after arriving in Canada).

[if !supportLists]3. [endif]The fees are much cheaper than undergraduates: most colleges charge about 10,000 CAD per year, with the lowest tuition at around 8,000 CAD per year (2 years totaling about 90,000 RMB).

[if !supportLists]4. [endif]Early Employment and Early Immigration: After graduation, you will receive a 3-year work visa, which allows for early employment and immigration compared to the four-year bachelor's program, and enjoys immigration benefits equivalent to bachelor's and master's degrees. After working for one year, you can apply for a green card and then apply for immigration.

[if !supportLists]5. [endif]Work-study and paid internships: You can legally work during your studies, and your wage can supplement daily living expenses (minimum wage of 9 CAD per hour). Most majors offer paid internship courses, which provide opportunities for future employment.

[if !supportLists]6. [endif]After immigrating after graduation, the credits earned can be transferred to university for further study: Higher Vocational College credits can be carried over to undergraduate courses and converted into two years of undergraduate credits. Tuition for further studies after obtaining a green card is one-third that of international students.

[if !supportLists]7. [endif]Unique visa advantage: The Canadian government has specially established the SPP program for the Association of Public Colleges and Universities, which reduces the guarantee fund amount, removes the time requirement for guarantee funds, and increases the approval rate.

[if !supportLists]8. [endif]High immigration scores: The Canadian immigration bureau gives associate degree 22 points, two points more than a four-year undergraduate degree, because college graduates have greater demand in the job market.

加拿大高等职业教育就是我们传统上说的大专学历教育，基本以2年制为主，也有3年制的，主要是针对商业、工业及公共服务业的需求培养人才。高等职业学院科目一般为以职业为目的的课程和普通学术性课程，学院的特点是比大学提供更多与职业有关的课程，实行较小班教学，开设校外课程,实验室面积与教室面积之比率较大，采用交互式教学方法以及宽松的入学标准，他们为那些准备在未来加入有技能、有专业的加拿大劳工队伍的学生设计种种课程及实务训练，许多高等职业学院提供带薪实习课程。

在加拿大，高等职业教育受到广大学生的欢迎,上大学和上大专是两种不同的选择，而不是上不了大学的才上大专。数据统计，当地中学毕业生60%上大专，加拿大的大专课程一般比大学会多一些针对就业市场的，毕业后找工作相对容易。加拿大学院将雇主为中心的课程纳入综合性学府内，以响应国家的经济政策，众多学院已经获得国际标准化组织的认证，所有学院都在为满足社会不断变化的学习需要力求达到优异质量。

加拿大高等职业教育(公立大专课程)的优势：

[if !supportLists]1. [endif]就业前景好,针对性强：由于加拿大面积大人口少，对劳动力需求较高，加拿大高级蓝领岗位人员短缺严重，大专就业非常容易。

[if !supportLists]2. [endif]入学要求较低：有正规高中毕业证即可，英语要求雅思5.5分左右（分数不够者还可以到加拿大后学习一段语言）。

[if !supportLists]3. [endif]费用比本科便宜很多：大多数学院的一年学费在1万加元左右，最低的学费标准约为每年8千加元（2年学费一共约合9万人民币）。

[if !supportLists]4. [endif]早就业早移民：毕业后直接获得3年工作签证，相比四年本科可早就业早移民，并且享受与本科硕士同等的移民待遇，工作一年以后可以申请绿卡，并进而申请移民。

[if !supportLists]5. [endif]勤工俭学和带薪实习：就读期间可以合法打工，工资可以补充日常生活费用（每小时最低工资9加元），大多数专业都有带薪实习的课程，这个实习为今后的就业提供了机会。

[if !supportLists]6. [endif]毕业移民后可再将所修学分转入大学继续修读：高等职业学院的学分可以带到本科课程中，并转换成2年本科学分。获得绿卡以后继续深造的学费是国际学生的1/3。

[if !supportLists]7. [endif]签证独特优势：加拿大政府特意为公立大专学院协会设立了SPP计划，该计划降低了担保资金数额，取消了担保资金的时间要求，提高了获签比例。

[if !supportLists]8. [endif]移民分较高：加拿大移民局打分表给大专22分,比4年大学本科还多两分,就是因为大专生的就业市场需求更大。
