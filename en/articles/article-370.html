University of Padua

Founded in 1222, the University of Padua ranks third among the oldest universities in Europe after the University of Bologna and Paris, located near the beautiful water city of Venice. Its long history has given it an important position in Padua and even throughout Italy, achieving outstanding accomplishments in medicine, astronomy, philosophy, and other fields. From 1592 to 1610, Galileo conducted scientific research here, bringing a strong academic atmosphere to the school. It is also the birthplace of the world's first female university graduate. The university is committed to promoting scientific research and international cooperation. By the 1990s, its scale continued to expand, with teaching and research repeatedly achieving fruitful results, ranking among Italy's top universities. In 2002, the university had 2,200 teachers, 1,800 technical and administrative staff, and 63,000 enrolled students in the 2002-2003 academic year. The university has 9 museums and 54 libraries, with a total collection of 1.9 million volumes, 12,500 journals, 4,000 electronic publications, and 90 databases. The University of Padua has branches in many cities, such as Vicenza, Breisanone, Rovigo, Treviso, Feltre, Castel Franco, Conegliano, Choga, and Asciago.

The university has 13 colleges: College of Agriculture, College of Economics, School of Pharmacy, School of Law, College of Arts and Philosophy, College of Surgery and Internal Medicine, College of Veterinary Medicine, School of Psychology, College of Education, School of Mathematical Physics and Natural Sciences, School of Politics, School of Statistics, and School of Engineering. It has 65 departments, 3 research institutes, and 25 research centers.

The University of Padua has strong research capabilities and abundant achievements, publishing 2,222 academic papers in 2000. It has published 432 papers and 274 academic monographs within the university. In 2002, research funding was approximately 24 million euros. Research in biomedicine, veterinary medicine, humanities, law, and sociology holds an important position in Italy.

Studying at the University of Padua is also a way to enjoy life in Padua, offering an exciting environment that blends modern and traditional arts and culture. Padua continues to grow and has now become the commercial and service center of northeastern Italy. Padua still retains its medieval style: sixteenth-century walls, castles, churches, and tranquil porch streets between magnificent apartments, bustling markets, gardens and riverside paths; A small river flowing into Venice converges with the Bargimione River, which flows to Riviera del Brenta and the former Venetian noble apartments. Padua stretches all the way to the Euganean mountains, but its important geographical location and smooth road and rail transport have driven the development of local tourism and businesses. It takes only twenty minutes to travel from Vicenza, thirty minutes by train from Venice City, and less than half an hour from Adriatic Beach.

The University of Padua is also committed to promoting international student exchanges, including recruiting international students and funding domestic students to pursue further studies abroad. To provide students with more excellent courses, the University of Padua has reached agreements with universities in European countries and overseas countries to exchange international students, cooperate in running schools, exchange faculty and experience, and hold regular seminars in some developing countries. In the 2006/2007 academic year, the University of Padua offered 351 places to Chinese students and actively participated in the Marco Polo Program for Chinese students.

帕多瓦大学成立于1222年，在欧洲仅次于博罗尼亚和巴黎大学，是第三座最古老的大学，也是意大利最大的大学之一，位于意大利美丽的水城威尼斯附近的帕多瓦市。悠久的历史使该校在帕多瓦甚至整个意大利都占据了重要的地位，在医学、天文学、哲学等领域取得了突出的成就。1592年到1610年，伽利略在此从事科学研究，给学校带来了浓厚的学术气氛。该校还是世界上第一位女大学毕业生的诞生地。学校致力于促进科学研究和国际合作，到上世纪九十年代，学校的规模不断扩大，教学和科研屡结硕果，跻身于意大利一流大学之列。2002年学校有教师2200人，技术人员和管理人员1800人，2002-2003学年注册学生63000人。学校共有9个博物馆，54个图书馆，藏书总量达190万册，收集有12500种期刊，4000种电子刊物，并建有90个数据库。帕多瓦大学在许多城市设有分校，如维琴察、布来萨诺内、罗维戈、特雷维佐、费尔特雷、卡斯特尔佛朗科、科内利安诺、乔加、阿西亚戈等。

学校设有农学院、经济学院、药物学院、法学院、艺术与哲学学院、外科与内科学院、兽医学院、心理学院、教育学院、数理物理与自然科学学院、政治学院、统计学院、工程学院13个学院，65个系、3个研究所、25个研究中心。

帕多瓦大学科研实力雄厚，科研成果丰富，2000年发表2222篇学术论文。在同内发表论文432，学术著作274本。2002研究经费约2400万欧元。生物医学、兽医学、人文科学、法学和社会学方面的研究在意大利占有重要地位。

在帕多瓦大学学习同时也是在帕多瓦享受生活，这里有激人奋进的环境，融汇了现代的和传统的艺术与文化。帕多瓦在不断地发展壮大，现已成为意大利东北部商业和服务业中心。帕多瓦依然存留着中世纪的风格：十六世纪的围墙、城堡、教堂和富丽堂皇的公寓间的一条条幽静门廊街道，繁华的市场、隐约可见的花园和河岸小径；一条流向威尼斯的小河与流向Riviera del Brenta和原威尼斯共和国贵族公寓的巴基缪内河交汇。帕多瓦一直延伸到Euganean山脉，但其重要的地理位置和畅通的公路铁路交通带动了本地旅游业和企业的发展。从维琴察到这里只有二十分钟的路程，从威尼斯城火车只要三十分钟，从亚得里亚海滩到此只有不到半小时的路程。

帕多瓦大学还致力于促进国际学生交换，包括吸收国外留学生和资助本国学生出国深造。为给学生提供更多优秀课程，帕多瓦大学与欧洲各国和海外其他国家的大学达成协议，互换国际学生，合作办学，交换师资和经验，在一些发展中国家召开定期研讨会。2006/2007学年帕多瓦大学提供中国学生351个名额，积极参加面向中国留学生实施的马可波罗计划。
