Address: Stephenville, Newfoundland and Labrador

Basic Introduction:

North Atlantic College is the largest comprehensive higher education and skills training center in Canada's Atlantic provinces, offering over 100 full-time diploma and certificate programs in engineering technology, natural resources, and industrial sectors; academic research, applied arts, and tourism; business and information technology; and health sciences, among others. The college's high-quality programs are nationally and internationally accredited, and it has over 50 years of experience working with government and industry partners to identify needs, develop courses tailored to industry needs, and deliver training programs.

North Atlantic College has 17 campuses within the province and an international campus in Qatar, a Middle Eastern country.

Majors and departments

1. Business Administration

The Business/Business Management program offers one-, two-year, and three-year completion options.

Graduates of Business/Business Management have the opportunity to transfer their credits to many of the following institutions and vocational institutions. Graduates can also continue their studies to obtain professional professional qualification certification.

2. Electrical /Major in Electronic Engineering Technology

Graduates of the Electrical/Electronic Engineering Technology program have the opportunity to transfer credits to many institutions, institutions, and vocational associations. Graduates can also continue their studies to obtain relevant vocational qualification certifications. The development of technical and technical programs follows national standards.

3. Machinery /Industrial Engineering major

Graduates of the Mechanical/Industrial Engineering Technology program may have the opportunity to transfer credits to many institutions and professional associations. Upon completing their professional studies, graduates automatically receive membership in the Association of Engineers, Technicians, and Technicians of Newfoundland and Labrador (AETTNL), as well as membership in any similar association in Canada.

地址：纽芬兰-拉布拉多省斯蒂芬维尔市

基本介绍：

北大西洋学院是加拿大大西洋省份中规模最大的综合高等教育和技能培训中心，提供超过100个全日制文凭及证书专业，涉及工程技术、自然资源、工业行业;学术研究，应用艺术和旅游;商业与信息技术;与健康科学等。学院高品质的专业得到国内和国际认证，并超过50年的与政府和行业合作伙伴合作确定需求、顺应行业需求开发课程和提供培训项目的经验。

北大西洋学院在省内有17个校区，并在中东国家卡塔尔设有一个国际校区。

专业和院系

1.工商管理

工商/商务管理专业提供一年、两年和三年的结业选择。

工商/商务管理毕业生有机会将学分转到以下许多院校/职业机构。毕业生也可以继续学习获得专业职业资格认证。

2. 电气/电子工程技术专业

电气/电子工程技术专业的毕业生可以有机会转学分到许多院校机构和职业协会。毕业生也可以继续学习获得相关职业资格认证。技工和技术专业的制定参照国家标准。

3. 机械/工业工程专业

机械/工业工程技术专业毕业生可能有机会转学分到许多院校和职业协会。毕业生在完成专业学习后自动获得纽芬兰-拉布拉多省工程技术人员和技师协会（AETTNL）的会员资格，以及加拿大的任何类似协会的会员资格。
