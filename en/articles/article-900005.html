Comprehensive international education & international study tour services

There are many ways to grow. For example, "Let Go of the Young Eagles" — sending children on an independent journey to explore the world alone, gaining growth through study tours. In the winter of 2020, studying abroad in Liaoning invites you to join us on a coming-of-age study 👣 tour. First stop: Los Angeles

Visit "The Dream-Making Machine of Humanity, the Axis of World Cinema" — Universal Studios Hollywood, visit UCLA — California's largest public university with world-class academic standards. Hollywood Star Avenue, Wen Chinese Theatre — countless stars have left their handprints and footprints, feeling the dazzling starlight here. Disneyland carries the childhoods and dreams of countless children.

Second stop: San Francisco

Visit the University of California, Berkeley — abbreviated as UC Berkeley, the number one public university in the U.S. with a diverse student body and known as the American Public Ivy League. Golden Gate Bridge — a symbol of San Francisco, a massive suspension bridge spanning the bay. Fisherman's Wharf — stroll through here and enjoy street performances, groups of sea lions, and streets full of ethnic charm. Then visit the City Hall and the Palace of the Arts.

Third stop: Silicon Valley

Visit Stanford University, the No.1 university in the western United States, then head to Silicon Valley to visit the world-famous Intel Museum and experience the charm of Intel Corporation, Apple, and others.

Fourth stop: American School

Experience American family life and fully experience the authentic American atmosphere. After entering the school, fully experience and feel American school education, life, and school. Experience a wide variety of learning and extracurricular activities both inside and outside the school.

Liaoning Overseas Study Service Co., Ltd. reserves the right to adjust the itinerary order and schedule appropriately based on actual circumstances, unexpected events, or force majeure, provided the number of attractions is not reduced. Regarding the destination school in the United States, the visa invitation letter school shall prevail; the final group departure notice shall prevail.

Let your dreams soar, and create the future

Registration Instructions Duration: 14 days Suitable age: 12-18 years Departure date: Late January or early February 2020 Tour cost: 33,800 RMB

Costs include: round-trip airfare from Shenyang, domestic transportation in the US, university visits and tourist attractions, three meals (except meals), accommodation and pick-up to and from school, school management fees, and personal accident insurance.

Once the total fees for the above projects are paid, they are non-refundable (if visa is refused, the full project fee will be refunded, and the 1400 yuan visa fee will be collected at your own expense).

Additional gifts: one local U.S. health insurance policy and one exquisite commemorative photo album.

Fees do not include: passport, visa fee, airport meals, and domestic luggage check-in fees within the United States (if any), local outpatient medical insurance in the United States, voluntary medical check-ups, and other personal expenses.
