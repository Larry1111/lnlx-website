Japan has long been one of the main countries Chinese students choose as their study destinations. Especially since 2009, with the smooth implementation of Japan's 300,000 International Students Plan and the 2009 New Study Abroad Policy, the Japanese government has continuously introduced favorable policies for international students, leading more and more Chinese students to choose to study in Japan.

What preparations should be made before studying in Japan?

In recent years, the most difficult issue for studying in Japan was the visa issue. In previous years, many excellent students were rejected by the Japanese Immigration Bureau for baseless reasons, so the success rate of obtaining a visa to Japan was never very high. However, starting in 2009, following the successful announcement of the new study abroad policy, the Japanese government began to place increasing emphasis on students' academic abilities. At that time, most of the reasons for visa rejection focused on the student's academic ability, which mainly reflected in Japanese language skills, i.e., Japanese test scores. For students preparing to study in Japan, to ensure they can successfully obtain a Certificate of Eligibility (return visa) and study in Japan, it is important to start preparing thoroughly in Japanese language learning. While submitting proof of over 150 hours of Japanese language study, it is best to take the Japanese J-TEST or Japanese NAT exam. As long as you obtain a certificate equivalent to Japanese Language Proficiency Level 4, your visa to Japan will be relatively easier. Obtaining such a certificate is not difficult; students with no prior experience achieve an almost 100% pass rate after two to three months of diligent study.

What is the greatest appeal of studying in Japan?

This is mainly reflected in the following four aspects:

1. International students in Japan can work part-time during their studies to supplement living and tuition expenses, which is achievable even for ordinary families.

2. Japan's undergraduate admission rate is 100%, and both countries recognize academic qualifications.

3. During their studies at Japanese universities, international students can enjoy 30%–100% tuition reductions and public medical benefits for Japanese nationals, with about 50% of international students receiving scholarships.

4. Economic exchanges between China and Japan are frequent, and students studying in Japan have good employment prospects whether staying in Japan or returning home after completing their studies.

日本一直是中国留学生选择留学目的国的主要国家之一。特别是2009年以来，随着日本30万留学生计划和2009留学新政的顺利实施，日本政府针对留学生的利好政策不断出台，越来越多的中国学生选择了日本留学。

到日本留学之前要做好哪些准备

前几年，赴日本留学最难的是签证问题，往年经常有些成绩很优秀的学生，但被一些莫须有的理由，而被日本入国管理局拒之门外，所以赴日签证的成功率一直不是很高。但是从2009年开始，由于留学新政的顺利发布，日本政府转而对学生的学习能力越来越重视，所以当年拒签理由基本上都集中在学生的学习能力上面，而学习能力主要就体现在日语的学习方面即日语考试成绩，对于准备赴日本留学的同学们来说，为了保障可以顺利地取得日本在留资格认定书(返签证)、去日本留学，现在开始就要在日语学习方面做好充分的准备，在递交150小时以上日语学习证明的同时，最好参加日语的J-TEST或日语NAT考试，只要获得相当于日语能力四级的证书，赴日签证就相对比较容易了。而获得这样的证书，并不是一件难事，零基础的学生在经过2到3个月的认真学习，考试通过率几乎是100%。

赴日本留学的最大魅力在哪里

主要体现在以下四个方面：

1.留学生在日本留学期间可以勤工俭学，补助生活费和学费的不足，普通家庭也可以做得到。

2.日本的大学本科升学率是100%，而且中日相互承认学历。

3.在日本大学就读期间，留学生可以享受减免30%—100%的学费和日本国民公费医疗待遇，并且留学生获得奖学金的比例约是50%左右。

4.中日经济往来频繁，留日学生不管是留在日本还是学成归国的就职前景都良好.
