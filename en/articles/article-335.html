Universities in Canada that offer dual undergraduate admission

There are two methods for university admission in Canada: one is direct admission, which requires English proficiency and Gaokao scores; The other is dual admission, mainly for students who have not taken TOEFL or IELTS and want to study abroad. The school provides two admission letters: one for the Language Center and one for university major courses. As long as you meet the language requirements, you can directly enroll in the university's major. Therefore, dual admission is also called conditional admission, meaning the prerequisite is meeting the language requirements. In fact, dual admission is more suitable for Chinese students. Therefore, if you apply to a dual admission school, students can seriously take the college entrance exam without any burden! Let's take a look at which Canadian universities offer dual admission

Although Canada has a large land area, its population is only about 33 million, so it does not have as many schools as the United States. Overall, Canada has only about 60 universities, with 43 ranked, divided into three categories. The first category is medical doctoral universities, qualified to offer medicine and law, with numerous postdoctoral research stations, complete colleges and disciplines, and relatively high entry barriers. It can be considered a first-category university in China, but this is not particularly accurate (as it is not comparable); The second category is comprehensive universities, mainly offering undergraduate and master's education, without medical or law schools; The third category is basic universities, mainly offering undergraduate education.

Among these three types of universities, the following universities offer dual undergraduate admissions:

Medical Doctoral University

1. Western University (Ontario, London, Ranked 7th among medical doctoral universities, Dual admission is with King's College)

2. University of British Columbia (Vancouver, British Columbia, ranked 2nd among medical doctoral universities, only offers foundation education)

3. University of Alberta (Edmonton, Alberta, ranked 5th, first attended six months of high school at the Gold Mountain Board of Education)

4. University of Saskatoon (Saskatoon, Saskatoon, ranked 10th among medical doctoral universities, offering dual undergraduate admission)

5. University of Manitoba (Winniburg, Manitoba, ranked 15th among medical doctor universities, offering dual undergraduate admission)

A comprehensive university

1. University of Waterloo (ranked 1st in Waterloo, Ontario; dual admission to the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, IELTS requirement of 4.5)

2. University of Victoria (Victoria City, British Columbia, ranked 2nd among comprehensive universities, can offer conditional admission)

3. University of Gulf (Gulf City, Ontario; ranked 3rd among comprehensive universities, offering undergraduate and master's foundation programs)

4. Simon Flying Sand University (Burnaby, British Columbia, ranked 4th among comprehensive universities, IELTS 5.5 for conditional admission)

5. Memorial University of Newfoundland (St. John, Newfoundland, ranked 5th among comprehensive universities, can offer conditional admission)

6. Carleton University (Ontario, Ottawa, ranked 6th among comprehensive universities, IELTS score 5 for conditional admission)

7. Concordia University (Quebec, Montreal, ranked 8th among comprehensive universities, can provide conditional admission)

8. University of York (Ontario, Toronto, ranked 10th among comprehensive universities, offers conditional admission except for business)

9. University of Windsor (Windsor, British Columbia, ranked 11th among comprehensive universities, conditional admission available)

Basic universities

Almost all offer dual admissions.

加拿大大学录取方式有两种：一是直接录取，需要英语成绩，以及高考成绩；另一是双录取，主要是针对没有考托福或者雅思成绩想出国留学的孩子们，学校会提供两个录取通知书，一个是语言中心的，一个是大学专业课的，只要完成了语言的要求，就可以直接读大学的专业课，所以双录取还有另外一个名字叫做有条件录取，即前提条件是完成语言的要求。事实上，双录取方式更适合中国学生。因此，如果申请双录取的学校，学生就可以认真参加高考，不用有任何负担！下面就看看加拿大有哪些大学提供双录取

加拿大虽然国土面积很大，但因为人口只有3300万左右，所以学校就没有美国那么众多了，综合一下，加拿大一共只有60多所大学，有排名的是43所，分为三类，第一类是医学博士类大学，有资格开设医学和法学，有众多的博士后流动站，学院和学科齐全，门槛较高，可以把它理解为国内的一类大学，但不特别贴切(因为不具备可比性）；第二类是综合类大学，以本科教育和硕士教育为主，没有医学院和法学院；第三类是基础类大学，主要是以本科教育为主。

这三类大学中，能够提供本科双录取的大学如下：

医学博士类大学

2. 英属哥伦比亚大学（卑诗省，温哥华市，医学博士类大学排名第2，只能提供预科教育）

3. 阿尔伯塔大学（阿尔伯塔省，埃德蒙顿市，排名第5，先在金山教育局读半年高中）

4. 萨省大学（萨省，萨斯卡通市，医学博士类大学排名第10，提供本科双录取）

5. 曼尼托巴大学（曼尼托巴省，温尼伯格市，医学博士类大学排名第15，提供本科双录取）

综合类大学

1. 滑铁卢大学（安大略省滑铁卢市第1，双录的是数学和计算机学院，有雅思4.5分的要求）

2. 维多利亚大学（卑诗省，维多利亚市，综合类大学排名第2，可以提供有条件录取）

3. 贵尔夫大学（安大略省贵尔夫市，综合类大学排名第3，提供本科和硕士预科）

4. 西蒙飞沙大学（卑诗省，本纳比市，综合类大学排名第4，雅思5.5分可以提供有条件录取）

5. 纽芬兰纪念大学（纽芬兰省，圣约翰市，综合类大学排名第5，可以提供有条件录取）

6. 卡尔顿大学（安大略省省，渥太华市，综合类大学排名第6，雅思5分可以提供有条件录取）

7. 肯靠迪亚大学（魁北克省，蒙特利尔市，综合类大学排名第8，可以提供有条件录取）

8. 约克大学（安大略省，多伦多市市，综合类大学排名第10，可以提供有条件录取，除了商科）

9. 温莎大学（卑诗省，温莎市，综合类大学排名第11，可以提供有条件录取）

基础类大学

基本都可以提供双录取。
