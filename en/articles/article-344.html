Introduction to TAFE Courses

Published on 2011-08-24·Source: Liaoning Overseas Study Service Co., Ltd.·Viewed 9,094 times

TAFE stands for Technical and Further Education and has a history of over 100 years. TAFE is a unique vocational education and training system in Australia, under the education departments of various Australian states. It is the largest higher education system in the Southern Hemisphere and one of the most successful and distinctive education systems in the world. Its diplomas are recognized by all Australian universities, the United States, the International Universities Alliance (IUA), and Commonwealth countries. The biggest feature of TAFE is its emphasis on practical application; it serves as the best bridge between students and employers. It clearly aims to link academic education with job training, using a practical-focused educational approach, specializing in the academic education of technical personnel. TAFE courses are equivalent to a combination of China’s vocational schools, technical schools, secondary specialized schools, and higher vocational colleges.

TAFE Course advantages

n Strong Practicality — TAFE courses are primarily practical, guided by market demand, closely connected to industry, and have high student employment rates.

n Highly flexible—suitable for different ages and social groups in different industries, with diverse teaching methods: classrooms, live networks, and practical activities.

n Strong practical experience—TAFE Academy works closely with industry associations to help students become familiar with future careers during their studies, and some departments even offer paid internships for students.

n Wide range of courses — TAFE programs cover a wide range of courses and industry fields, including business and trade, beauty and hairdressing, mechanical engineering, tourism, catering and hotel services, nursing care, early childhood education, and more. The categories are complete and there is a wide range of course choices.

n Shorter duration—TAFE courses mainly consist of 1-2 year certificate and diploma programs, with short-term training courses ranging from a few weeks, months, to half a year.

n Highly accredited — All TAFE courses are recognized by the Australian government and industry associations, and the certificates are recognized both in Australia and internationally.

n Low tuition — TAFE tuition is relatively cheaper than higher education, with annual tuition around 10,000 AUD (approximately 60,000 to 70,000 RMB).

n Wide employment prospects—after completing a 2-year program and obtaining a TAFE diploma, those wishing to pursue further education can apply for undergraduate courses in related fields and receive waivers in 4 to 12 subjects.

Admission qualifications

Applicants applying to study vocational education and training courses in Australia must hold a Chinese high school, secondary vocational school, or vocational school diploma.

English proficiency

An IELTS score of 5.5 is required (a score of 5 is sufficient to apply for a student visa).

TAFE是Technical and Further Education（技术与继续教育）的简称，已有100多年的历史。TAFE是澳大利亚一种独特的职业教育培训体系，隶属于澳洲政府的各州教育部，是南半球最大的高等教育系统，也是全球最成功的特色鲜明的教育体系之一，其文凭得到澳大利亚所有大学、美国、国际大学联盟（IUA）和英联邦国家的认可。TAFE最大的特点是学以致用，它是学子和用人单位之间最好的桥梁，明确提出把学历教育与岗位培训联系起来，采用重在实用的教育培训方式，专门从事技术人员的学历教育。TAFE课程相当于中国的职业学校、技校、中专和高等专科学校的综合体。

TAFE课程优势

n 实用性强­——TAFE课程以实用为主，以市场需求为向导，与行业关系紧密，学生就业率高。

n 灵活性强——适合不同年龄，不同行业社会群体，教学手段多样：课堂、现场网络及实践。

n 实践性强——TAFE学院通过与行业协会密切合作，使得学生在学期间即开始熟悉未来的职业，部分学校的学科还为学生安排带薪实习。

n 课程广泛——TAFE课程覆盖相当广泛的课程和行业领域，包括商务贸易、美容美发、机械工程、旅游、餐饮和宾馆服务、护士护理、幼儿教育等各行各业，门类齐全，课程选择余地大。

n 学制较短——TAFE课程以1-2年的证书和文凭课程为主，也有几周、几个月到半年不等的短期培训课程。

n 认可度高——所有的TAFE课程均为澳洲政府和行业协会承认，证书受澳大利亚和国际认可。

n 学费低廉——TAFE学费相对高等教育便宜，每年学费在10000澳元左右（约合人民币六至七万元）。

n 就业范围广——完成2年课程并获得TAFE文凭后，如希望接受继续教育，可申请相关专业的大学本科阶段课程，并获得4至12门学科的减免。

入学学历资格

申请赴澳就读职业教育与培训课程的申请人须具有中国高中、中专或职校毕业证书。

英语水平

雅思成绩需要达到5.5分（雅思成绩达到5分即可申请学生签证）。
