Recently, news from Japan's Ministry of Justice's Immigration Bureau has extended the validity periods for both "study" and "study" visas by three months. This adjustment of just three months has brought clear benefits to international students in Japan. Previously, visas issued by immigration bureaus across Japan were only enough to complete a semester's coursework, and some students had their graduation certificates expire before they could obtain them, so they had to apply for "short-term stay" visas separately. After the adjustment, international students no longer have to worry about "short-term stay" and can travel to Japan a period earlier.

Specific content of policy adjustments

It is understood that currently, Chinese students mainly travel to Japan through two types of visas: the "Student" visa and the "Student Visa." Students attending Japanese universities, junior colleges, vocational schools, and special courses at Japanese universities hold "Student" visas, usually valid for 2 or 1 year. Students attending Japanese language schools generally hold "Student" visas, usually valid for 1 year or half a year. The new regulations extend the "Student" and "Student" visas by 3 months respectively. From now on, the "Student" visa period will be divided into four types: 2 years 3 months, 2 years, 1 year 3 months, and 1 year; The "Student" visa term will also be divided into three types: 1 year 3 months, 1 year, and 6 months.

Now is the time when visas for those born in October will be gradually issued, and various study abroad agencies in Dalian have reported that they have already received the new version of the visa. A representative from one institution told reporters: Recently, two students who applied for a 2-year student visa both received visas valid for 2 years and 3 months.

Visa extensions address practical issues for international students

A student who had just returned from Japan to visit family told reporters: "Most of us want to leave early when going abroad, so we can get into university and move into the dormitory sooner, and adapt to the environment there. But this didn't work before. Some students arrived in Japan in early March, but their visas expired in early March a year or two later, and some schools' graduation ceremonies happened to be at the end of the month. At times like these, you have to apply for a short-term visa, which is quite troublesome. ”

Some students also plan to work in Japan after graduation. They may have already found a job, but simply because the relevant procedures are not yet complete, they cannot convert to a work visa. At such times, students have to go through the trouble of applying for a short-term visa and then switching to a work visa soon after, which is both laborious and time-consuming. Visa extensions basically solve the problems that international students may encounter.

Industry insiders believe that this minor adjustment to student visas is highly practical and reflects the implementation of the "300,000 International Students Plan." Most importantly, it signals that more favorable policies may be introduced, making the journey for Chinese students to Japan smoother.

近期从日本法务省入国管理局传出消息：将“留学”“就学”两类签证的期限分别延长3个月。仅仅3个月的调整，给在日留学生带来明显的利好。以前，日本各地入管局（简称）发出的签证只够学生完成一定学期的课程，有些学生毕业证还没拿到签证就到期了，为此他们还要专门再申请“短期滞在”签证。调整出台后，留学生省去了“短期滞在”烦恼，还可以提前一段时间赴日。

政策调整具体内容

据了解，目前中国学生赴日主要通过两类签证，即“留学”签证和“就学签证”。就读日本大学、短大、专门学校和日本大学别科的学生持有“留学”签证，签证期间通常为2年或1年。就读日本语言学校的学生一般持有“就学”签证，签证期间通常为1年或半年。新规定将“留学”“就学”签证在原来的基础上分别延长3个月。今后“留学”签证的期限将分为四种：2年3个月、2年、1年3个月、1年；“就学”签证的期限也将分为3个种类：1年3个月、1年、6个月。

现在正是今年10月生签证陆续下发的时间点，大连各留学机构反映已经收到了新版本的签证。某机构负责人告诉记者：最近有两个申请2年期留学签证的学生，收到的都是2年零3个月的签证。

签证延长解决留学生现实问题

一位刚刚从日本回国探亲的留学生对记者说：“我们一般人出国都希望早走，好早点办理大学入学、宿舍入住这些事，也好早点适应那里的环境。但这在以前是行不通的。有同学3月上旬到日本，结果到了一年或两年后的3月初签证到期了，有些学校的毕业典礼还偏偏在月末。这种时候，就必须去申请短期签证，比较麻烦。”

还有些学生，毕业后准备在日本工作。他们也许已经找到了工作，但只因为相关的手续还未齐全，不能转为工作签证。这种时候学生又要费力去申请短期签证，不久后再换成工作签，费力又费时。签证的延长，基本解决了留学生可能遇到的上述问题。

业内人士认为，此次关于留学签证的小幅调整实用性强，更是“30万留学生计划”落到实处的体现。最重要的是，这预示着更多利好政策有可能出台，中国学生的赴日路会走得更顺畅。
