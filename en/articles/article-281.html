Founded in 1986, the college is located in Bunkyo Ward, central Tokyo, adjacent to the Tokyo-Japan Friendship Hall, offering convenient transportation and convenient living. The school has advanced review classrooms and a team of staff passionate about Sino-Japanese friendship. Principal Ikutaro Ando is a renowned Sino-Japanese friendship activist. The school specializes in accepting Chinese international students, with enrollment every April and a two-year semester. The school requires students to have high standards, including at least Level 3 Japanese proficiency.

The main features of the school are as follows:

1. Conduct rigorous Japanese language education focused on listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills.

2. Use language classrooms to provide students with accurate and practical Japanese training.

3. Regularly organize various extracurricular activities to interact with Japanese students and help them understand Japan.

Nichichu Academy has been rated as an excellent school by the Immigration Bureau of the Ministry of Justice. Students can obtain a two-year visa at a time, making it one of the best schools where Chinese students can study with peace of mind.

Registration Requirements:

1. Within two years of graduating from high school, vocational, or technical school

2. Within two years of completing a college diploma

3. Bachelor's degree from a university and under 27 years old

该校的主要特点如下：

1． 以听、说、读、写能力为中心对学生进行严格的日语教育。

2． 利用语音教室，对学生进行正确实用的日语训练。

3． 定期举办多种课外活动，与日本学生交流使学生了解日本。

日中学院被法务省入管局评定为优秀学校，学生每次可获两年签证，是中国学生可以安心学习的好学校之一。
