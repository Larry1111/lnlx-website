School Introduction

Founded in 1883, the University of Auckland is now the largest university in New Zealand. Over more than a century, the University of Auckland has become an international center for learning and research. The University of Auckland is located in the heart of Auckland, New Zealand's largest city, and currently has 26,000 enrolled students, including 1,000 international students from over 60 countries worldwide. The participation of international students creates a global learning environment for the University of Auckland.

The University of Auckland's research-based disciplines enjoy a high reputation among universities worldwide, attracting large numbers of international students every year. Graduates from the university are spread across New Zealand and around the world, including fields such as business, law, medicine, and politics.

The University of Auckland currently has three campuses with a total of seven faculties: the Faculty of Architecture, Design and Fine Arts, the Faculty of Arts, the Faculty of Business, the Faculty of Engineering, the Faculty of Law, the Faculty of Medicine, and the Faculty of Science. Additionally, the University of Auckland has more than 30 academic research institutions of various sizes, mainly engaged in research in emerging and interdisciplinary disciplines.

The University of Auckland is currently the only university in New Zealand to join the World Association of 21st Century Universities and the Famous Universities Association around the Pacific. This is enough to prove the university's academic standing in New Zealand and globally.

Course Overview

Undergraduate courses

The University of Auckland offers up to 140 bachelor's degree or certificate programs in various majors. It generally takes three years to complete an undergraduate program. Degree programs are divided into a combination of three different levels. A course refers to a course in a particular major. For example, New Zealand Literature is a course in English majors. Each course you complete will earn you corresponding credits, and the number of credits required depends on the degree you wish to earn. If you want to earn some degrees, you must choose them as your major. Specialized courses are chosen by you and completed in the final stage. For example, if you want to choose an arts degree, you can choose art history, Māori cultural studies, or political issues studies as your major. In business, you can choose accounting, finance, marketing, and information systems as your major; In natural sciences, you can choose fields such as environmental science, sports science, or physics. The University of Auckland also offers a dual bachelor's program, which typically takes four years to complete but is equivalent to six years of study.

学校简介

奥克兰大学成立于1883年，现为新西兰最大的大学。一百多年来的发展，使得奥克兰大学现已发展成为国际性的学习和研究的中心。奥克兰大学位于新西兰最大的城市奥克兰市的市中心，目前在校学生人数为26,000人，其中包括1000名来自于世界60多个国家的国际留学生。国际学生的参与为奥克兰大学营造了一个国际性的学习环境。

奥克兰大学以研究为基础的各类学科在世界各大学中享有极高的声誉，每年吸引着大批的国际学生。学校的毕业生遍布于新西兰和世界各地的，诸如：商业、法律、医学、政治等各个领域。

奥克兰大学现有3个校园，共有七个学院，它们分别是建筑、设计与美术学院、艺术学院、商学院、工学院、法学院、医学院和理学院。另外，奥克兰大学还下设有大小不等的30多个学术研究机构，这些机构主要从事新兴学科和边缘学科的研究。

奥克兰大学是目前新西兰唯一的加入世界二十一世纪大学协会和太平洋周边著名大学协会的大学。这足以证明奥克兰大学在新西兰乃至在世界上学术地位。

课程简介

本科课程

奥克兰大学提供多达140个专业的本科学位或证书类课程。完成本科课程一般需要三年的时间。学位课程分为三个不同级别的课程的组合。一门课程是指某个专业上的一门课程，例如：新西兰文学是英语专业中的一门课程，每修读完一门课程你将获得相应的学分，获得一个学位所需要的学分取决于你希望获得什么样的学位。如果你想获得有些学位，你必须将其选为自己的专业来进行学习。专业课程是由你挑选并在最后一个阶段学习来完成的。例如：如果你想选择艺术类学位，你可以选择艺术史、毛利文化研究或政治问题研究来作为你的专业。在商科方面，你可以选择会计、金融、市场和信息系统作为你的专业；在自然科学方面，你可以选择诸如环境科学、体育科学、物理学等作为自己的专业。奥克兰大学还提供双学士课程，双学士课程通常需要四年完成，但相当于6年的学历。
