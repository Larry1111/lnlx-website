Sookmyung Women's University

1. Overview of the school

Sookmyung Women's University is located in Seoul and is the birthplace of women's education in Korea, dating back to the Japanese occupation1906It was established by Empress Dowager Sunhyun of the Gaochong royal family, and is also Korea's first private female institution with a distinctive ethnic character. 3. Admission Qualifications

1. Both parents are foreign students.

2. Korean citizens and foreigners studying abroad with courses equivalent to Korean elementary, middle, or university education.

3. Master's programs and combined master's-doctoral programs: Applicants must have a bachelor's degree (including fresh graduates) or nationally recognized equivalent or higher academic qualifications.

淑明女子大学

一、学校概况

淑明女子大学坐落于首尔，是韩国女性教育发源地，该校是在日本占领时期的1906年由高崇皇室顺宪皇贵妃亲自设立的大学，也是韩国第一所带有民族色彩的女性私立学府。淑明女子大学已有近100年的历史，以其严肃，贤明，正大的校训为基础，培养了众多具备高素质和高贵品德的女性人才。淑明女子大学现已发展成为拥有8个本科学院，1万余名本科生，12个研究生院，3000余名研究生的一所综合性大学。
