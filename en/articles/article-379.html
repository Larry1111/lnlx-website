What is the biggest fear of studying abroad? Many parents told reporters that their biggest fear is their child not finding a job after graduation, turning 'returnees' into 'overseas waiting'!

Recently, it has been learned that studying in Singapore offers a great opportunity to stay and work locally after graduation. The demand for professional talent in Singapore has always been insufficient, providing a large number of job opportunities for young people. At the same time, the Singapore government encourages outstanding Chinese students to work and settle in Singapore to meet the talent needs of local economic development. Therefore, international students going to Singapore need not worry. As long as you work hard, you can find a job in Singapore after graduation and no longer have to worry about becoming a "sea waiter."

Many institutions have signed employment cooperation agreements with high-quality local companies, allowing international students to directly obtain job opportunities after graduation, which is very beneficial for children to become permanent residents of Singapore. Even if they want to return to China to develop their careers, work experience at Singapore's top companies greatly enhances their job prospects.

Government Attention: Studying in Singapore Is Safe The Singapore government places great importance on education and uses certification to regulate and manage educational institutions, ensuring student safety. "SQC and Case Trust accreditation are the Singapore government's initial review of private institutions, while Edu Trust is a stricter review conducted by the new education department on institutions that have passed the initial review." The Singapore Parliament has passed the "Education Trust Protection Scheme" and established CPE to conduct detailed reviews of non-public educational institutions in six areas: management commitment and responsibility, internal management structure, agent network selection, student protection and services, academic processes and student assessment, and quality supervision and improvement.

Currently, tuition and living expenses for studying in Singapore are generally around 100,000 RMB, and there are various scholarships available for application. To retain talent, the Singapore government has set up many scholarships for overseas students, which is a unique national policy. "I think Singapore has a developed economy and excellent education. Compared to studying in Europe or America, the cost is more than 50% lower, making it very suitable for working families like us." Ms. Chen, who works at a hospital, said in an interview with reporters.

Golden Springboard: Direct Access to Top European and American Universities Every year, many families consider Singapore as a golden springboard to study abroad. The Singapore government has many measures to encourage universities to actively cooperate with European and American universities, including many well-known top institutions in the US, UK, and Germany, most of which adopt a step-by-step joint admissions model. Under this model, after admission, students will study in stages at universities jointly admitted to Singapore and Europe and the US, and upon graduation, they receive diplomas jointly awarded by both universities.

留学最怕什么？不少家长向记者表示，最怕孩子毕业后找不到工作，“海归”变“海待”！

近日了解到，到新加坡留学，毕业后留在当地工作的机会很大，新加坡对专业人才的需求一直处于供不应求的状况，从而为青年人提供了大量的就业机会。同时，新加坡政府也鼓励优秀的华人留学生在新加坡工作和定居，以满足当地经济发展的人才需求。所以，赴新加坡的留学生们不用担心，只要你努力，毕业后就能够在新加坡找工作，从此不必担心变“海待”。

很多院校都和当地优质企业签订了就业合作协议，留学生毕业以后可以直接获得工作机会，这对于孩子获得新加坡永久居民很有利。即便想回国发展，新加坡名企的工作经验也为孩子找工作加分不少。

政府重视，新加坡留学很安全新加坡政府非常重视教育，采取认证的手段对教育机构进行规范和管理，这样能保证学生留学的安全性。“SQC和Case Trust两个认证是新加坡政府对私立院校的初审，而Edu Trust则是新教育部门在通过初审了的院校中进行的更严格的复审。”。新加坡国会通过“教育信托保障计划”并成立CPE，对非公立教育机构在管理层承诺与责任、内部管理架构、代理网络甄选、学生保护与服务、学术流程与生源评估及质量监督与改善等六个方面进行详细审核。

目前，新加坡留学的学费和生活费一般在10万元人民币左右，而且还设有多种奖学金可以申请，新加坡政府为了留住人才，对海外学生有很多奖金设立，这是新加坡国家独有的政策。“我觉得新加坡经济发达，教育也非常优质，比到欧美国家留学费用节约50%以上，非常适合我们这样的工薪家庭。”一位在医院工作的陈女士在接受记者采访时说。

黄金跳板，直达欧美名校每年都会有很多家庭把新加坡作为到其他国外高校留学的黄金跳板。新加坡政府有很多措施鼓励大学积极开展与欧美大学的各种合作，包括美国、英国、德国等众多知名的一流学府，大都采取联合招生分步教学的合作模式。在这种模式下被录取后，会分阶段在新加坡及欧美联合招生的大学进行学习，毕业后可获得两所大学共同颁发的文凭学历。
