Clermont-Ferrand is located in central France, 350 kilometers from Paris, and is directly connected by highways, high-speed trains, and airplanes. Clermont-Ferrand is the capital of the AUVERGNE region and the Puydome department. It is the political, economic, cultural, and educational center of the region, with a population of over 300,000, and is a famous tourist destination in France. The nearby ski resorts are among France's top three ski destinations. The city has two universities (Clermont-Ferrand First and Second University), Clermont-Ferrand Higher Commercial College, the School of Architecture, the School of Arts, and more than ten other higher education institutions.

Clermont-Ferrand has an Aulnat International Airport connecting nearly 30 destinations. Today, the city offers residents the advantages of a metropolis while enjoying the vast natural spaces of Auvanne Volcano, where the Volcano and Earth Science Theme Park and the VULCANIA European Science Adventure Park are located in the city.

Clermont-Ferrand is France's second most natural scenic area and an important industrial city. The world's largest automotive tire manufacturing center—Michelin and Bergougon—are home to major companies. Clermont-Ferrand is also a research center for the French food industry, and the quality of tap water here is close to mineral water. Its metallurgical industry, especially in boiler and machine tool manufacturing, ranks among the best in France. Additionally, its garment and agricultural machinery industries are highly competitive in the market. What makes Clermont-Ferrand people proud is that this city has contributed a great figure to France and the world—Bresse. Pascal, the renowned mathematician, physicist, philosopher, and writer, whose diligent research and pioneering spirit have always inspired every Clermont-Ferrand resident. Clermont-Ferrand has two national comprehensive universities and more than a dozen colleges of applied sciences, with over 50,000 students, making it one of France's university towns. Clermont-Ferrand is only a 3-hour train ride from Paris and only 130 kilometers from Lyon, France's second largest city.

School Introduction

With a history of over 300 years, Clermont-Ferrand II University is named after BLAISE PASCAL, the inventor of the world's first hand-cranked computer. It is a renowned center for literature, social sciences, foreign languages, and natural sciences teaching and research in central France, comprising nine faculties, two vocational schools, one research institute, an astrophysical observation center, and a French language training center for international students.

The nine colleges include: Faculty of Letters, Languages and Humanities; Faculty of Applied Foreign Languages, International Trade and Public Relations; Faculty of Sports and Sciences; Faculty of Psychology, Sociology and Education; Faculty of Precision Sciences and Natural Sciences; Institute of Science and Technology Research; International Trade Vocational College; Industrial Engineering and Systems Vocational College; University Technical College.
Blaise Bascard-Clermont II University has 15,650 enrolled students (including 1,816 international students) and 937 faculty and researchers. The university is a particularly active multidisciplinary comprehensive university center in central France, and together with the University of Auvergne, it brings together all major disciplines of higher education and scientific research.

The university has 39 internationally renowned research laboratories, including 17 collaborative labs with the French National Research Center (CNRS), 2 partner units with the National Institute of Agronomy (INRA), and 16 research groups recognized by the Ministry of National Education's Research Department. The teaching quality in engineering (all fields), precision and natural sciences, sports sciences, psychology and literature, and language and humanities is internationally recognized and enjoys a strong reputation.

The university is particularly renowned for its research areas or key topics in scientific research, including: "Health and Nutrition," "Food Quality," "Efficient and Intelligent Systems and Machines," "Process Chemistry," (regionally) "Sensitive Rural Areas," and "Particulate Physics," "Magma and Volcanoes/Geology/Meteorology," among others.

克莱蒙费朗市位于法国中部，距巴黎350公里，有高速公路、高速火车和飞机直接连接。克莱蒙费朗市是AUVERGNE大区首府和多母PUYDOME省省会，是该地区政治、经济、文化、教育中心，人口30多万，是法国著名的旅游胜地。该市附近的滑雪场是法国三大滑雪胜地之一。该市有两所大学（克莱蒙费朗第一和第二大学）、克莱蒙费朗高商、建筑学院、艺术学院等十多所高等院校。

克莱蒙费朗拥有一个连接近30个目的地的奥尔纳(Aulnat)国际机场，如今，城市既能为其居民提供大都市的种种优势，又可令人享受奥凡涅火山的辽阔自然空间，火山与地球科学主题公园及维尔卡尼亚(VULCANIA)欧洲科学探险公园就设在该市。

克莱蒙费朗是法国第二大自然风景区和一座重要工业城市，世界上最大的汽车轮胎制造中心——米其林和贝尔古尼昂等大公司都建在这里。克莱蒙费朗市同时是法国食品工业的研究中心，这里的自来水质量接近矿泉水。其冶金工业，特别是锅炉制造和机床制造的水平在法国名列前茅。另外它的服装业、农业机械制造业的市场竞争力也非常强。令克莱蒙费朗人引以为豪的是，这座城市为法国乃至全世界贡献了一位伟大的人物——布雷斯.帕斯卡，这位著名的数学家、物理学家、哲学家和作家的刻苦钻研，开拓进取的精神一直激励着每一个克莱蒙费朗人.克莱蒙费朗市有二所国立综合性大学和十几所高等专业学院，有5万多大学生，也是法国的一所大学城。克莱蒙费朗市乘火车到巴黎仅需3个小时，距法国第二大城市里昂仅130公里。
