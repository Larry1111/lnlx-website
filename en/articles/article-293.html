How to apply for a visa for accompanying or visiting family in Japan for studying in Japan

The documents required for domestic relatives visiting Japan are as follows:

First, collect the application form at the consular section or consulate of the local Japanese embassy

Just fill it out as required, and attach a photo of the applicant to the application form. A temporary residence permit refers to an application only required by applicants from areas outside the jurisdiction of the Japanese consulate. Currently, besides the Japanese Embassy in Beijing, there are Japanese consulates in Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenyang, Dalian, Chongqing, and other places. They have jurisdiction over different regions for application procedures. Please confirm which consulate has jurisdiction over your home location. Family relationship notarization refers to the relationship between your parents and yourself; you can handle it at the local notary office.

Second, documents required for the inviter in Japan

This includes a statement of purpose and a stay schedule. The way the invitation letter is written is important. If parents need to come to care for children, care for patients, or attend a wedding ceremony, a doctor's diagnosis and proof of reservation for the venue must be attached. The stay reservation form is even more detailed, including when and which flight to arrive in Japan, daily itinerary arrangements during your stay, residence, contact phone number, when and when to leave Japan and return to China, and so on.

Third, documents required for guarantors living in Japan

If you are a Japanese government-sponsored student, you only need to provide your Certificate of Foreigner Registration and National-Sponsored International Student Certificate; no additional guarantor documents are required. If you are a self-funded student, please have a professor or associate professor at your school act as a guarantor, and only need to submit the professor's employment certificate and personal guarantee letter. Other guarantors must submit other documents besides the employment certificate and personal guarantee letter. If a Japanese guarantor acts as a guarantor, you need a copy of your certificate of residence, a tax payment certificate for the most recent year, or a copy of the final tax return stamped with the tax office's seal. Foreign guarantors in Japan must submit a Certificate of Foreigner Registration and tax payment certificate. However, the foreign guarantor must have a three-year period of residence. If the guarantor is a Japanese national's spouse, permanent resident, or long-term resident, they must be self-sufficient and cannot be a dependent.

4. Materials that can demonstrate the relationship between the applicant and the inviter

This includes photos, letters, and so on. These materials are only required when applying for a 'know someone visit'; parents do not need to submit them. When handling domestic parents' visit to Japan, although there are exceptions where approval is not granted, generally it is relatively easy, and the success rate is quite high. If it is for siblings, it is very difficult to get approval.

办理国内亲属来日探亲访问需要准备的资料如下：

第一 申请书到当地日本大使馆领事部或领事馆领取

按规定填写即可,同时要在申请书上贴好申请者的照片。暂住证 是指非当地日本领事馆管辖区域的申请者提出申请时才需要的。现在,除了北京的日本大使馆外,在上海、广州、沈阳、大连、重庆等地，都有日本的领事馆，管辖不同地区的申请办理手续,请确认你家所在地属于哪个领事馆管辖亲属关系公证是指你父母和你的关系，到当地公证机关办理即可。

第二 在日本邀请人需要提出的资料

包括招聘理由书、滞在预定表。邀请理由书的写法比较重要，如果是需要父母前来照顾生小孩、照看病人或者参加结婚仪式的情况下，要添付医生的诊断书、结婚仪式预定举办场所的预约证明材料等。滞在预定表更需要具体，包括何时乘座哪次航班到达日本，在日本期间每天的行程安排以及住所、联系电话、什么时候乘座哪次航班离开日本返回中国等等。

第三 在日保证人需要提交的资料

如果你是日本国费留学生 ，只需要提供你的外国人登录事项证明书 、国费外国人留学生证明材料即可，无须另外提交保证人资料。如果是自费留学生，请所在学校教授或者副教授作保证人，只需要提交教授的在职证明书和身元保证书即可。除此之外的保人，在职证明书和身元保证书之外还需要提交其他资料。日本人作保人的情况下，需要住民票腾本、最近年度的纳税证明书或者盖有税务署受理印章的确定申告书存本。在日外国人作保人，需要提交外国人登录事项证明书和纳税证明书，但是外国人作保人，必须是持有3年在留期间者方可，如果是日本人配偶者、永远住者配偶者、定住者必须有自立能力，不能是被扶养者。

第四 能体现申请人和邀请人关系的材料

包括照片、书信等。这些材料只是申请“知人访问”的情况下需要提交，父母不需要。办理国内父母来日探亲，虽然也有例外不被批准的，但一般来说比较容易，成功率比较高。如果是办理兄弟姐妹，则很难被批准。
