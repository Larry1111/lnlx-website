Address: Prince George, British Columbia

Basic Introduction:

Founded in 1969, our university transfer program allows students to complete their first and second years of university studies, then transfer to several Canadian universities to study for another two years in Canada to earn their degrees. New Carl College offers the same courses as universities, but with much lower tuition fees and much smaller class sizes. For adult students seeking compact study opportunities, New Carl College offers three postgraduate diploma programs: Information Technology, Tourism Management, and Human Resource Management, as well as various certificates, diplomas, professional and industry courses.

Major or department :

1 .Applied research and innovation

The Applied Research and Innovation Office at New Card College connects industry and community partners with the college's expertise and facilities to address specific innovation and applied research challenges, and enhances student training through applied research projects. Research programs provide students with opportunities to apply what they have learned and help them develop important team-building and project management skills. Since 2010, the college has received over $2.4 million in research funding from government and industry, and has ongoing research projects in forestry, renewable energy, surveying, business, marketing, power engineering, electronics, and prototyping.

2. Diploma in Natural Resources and Environmental Science and Technology

The department was once quite enthusiastic about studying abroad, and China was also one of the destinations for studying abroad (in cooperation with Zhejiang A&F University). Additionally, the new CA College has a research forest and is actively performing in applied research fields, including natural resources.

3. Postgraduate diplomas in Information Technology, Tourism Management, and Human Resource Management

Our postgraduate diploma programs are designed to balance theory and practice, preparing graduates for supervisory and managerial roles in their respective fields. Both Information Technology and Tourism Management offer internships, giving students the opportunity to apply the knowledge and skills they have learned in real-world work environments.

地址：不列颠哥伦比亚省乔治王子城

基本介绍：

新卡学院成立于1969年，我们的大学转学课程让学生完成第一和第二年的大学课程，然后转学到多所加拿大大学在加拿大再就读两年获取他们的学位。新卡学院提供了和大学的相同课程，但相比低得多的学费和小得多的班级规模。对于寻求紧凑的学习机会的成人学生，新卡学院提供了三个研究生文凭课程：信息技术，旅游管理和人力资源管理，另外还提供各种证书、文凭、专业和行业课程。

专业或院系:

1．应用研究和创新

新卡学院的应用研究和创新办公室将行业和社区合作伙伴与学院的专业知识和设施联系起来，以应对具体的创新和应用研究挑战，并通过应用型研究项目来增进对学生的培训。研究项目为学生提供了学以致用的机会，并帮助他们培养重要的团队建设和项目管理技能。自2010年以来，学院已得到来自政府和行业的240多万美元的研究经费，并在林业、可再生能源、测绘学，商务，市场营销，电力工程，电子和原型设计等领域拥有进行中的研究项目。

2.自然资源和环境科技文凭

该部门曾一度较热衷出国学习，中国也曾是出国留学目的地之一（与浙江农林大学合作）。另外，新卡学院拥有一片研究森林，在包括自然资源等专业的应用研究领域表现积极。

3.信息技术、旅游管理和人力资源管理研究生文凭

我们的研究生文凭课程设计旨在达到理论和实践的平衡，为毕业生在各自领域从事监督和管理岗位做好准备。信息技术和旅游管理课程都提供实习期，使学生有机会将所学到的知识和技能应用到实际工作环境中。
