Kyungpook National University

1. Overview of the school

Kyungpook National University was founded in 1946 and is located in Daegu, Korea's third largest city and the capital of education. The university has 14 colleges, 11 research institutes, 28,000 undergraduate students, and 7,000 master's and doctoral students. Kyungpook National University ranks second among national universities and ninth overall, and is one of the top 100 universities in the Asia-Pacific region. The university has the largest IT college in Korea, with its strongest majors being IT and Business.
