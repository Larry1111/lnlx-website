Kansas State University was founded in 1863 and is a public institution, ranked 130th overall in the United States. Its main campus is located in Manhattan, Kansas, and the College of Aeronautics and Technology is based in Salina. Currently, there are 23,581 students from 50 states and more than 90 different countries, including about 1,717 international students and 534 undergraduates from Chinese mainland. Among them, majors such as architecture, engineering, food, aviation, and management are among the top in the U.S. The university offers more than 250 undergraduate programs and more than 100 master's, doctoral, and certificate programs for students to choose from.

Kansas State University was founded in 1863 and is a comprehensive institution of higher education and research. The University of Kansas includes the College of Agriculture, College of Architecture and Design, College of Arts and Sciences, College of Business Management, College of Education, College of Mechanical Engineering, College of Human Ecology, College of Technology and Aeronautics, and graduate school. Of the 20,000 students enrolled, 1,000 are international, accounting for about 5%, from 91 different countries, most pursuing graduate degrees.

Kansas State University is located in the heart of the United States. The campus is divided into two locations: the main campus is 125 kilometers from Kansas City, covering 664 acres, making it convenient for living and shopping, with about 38,000 residents. The new campus is located in Salina, 70 kilometers west of Manhattan, covering about 18,000 acres and housing about 45,000 students.

Since 1986, Kansas State University has excelled in applications for Rhodes Scholarships, Marshall Scholarships, Truman Scholarships, and more, and the university boasts many renowned professors.

堪萨斯州立大学成立于1863年，是一所综合性教学和研究高等学府。堪萨斯大学下设农学院、建筑设计学院、文理学院、商业管理学院、教育学院、机械工程学院、人类生态学院、技术和航空学院以及研究生院等。在校的2万名学生中有1000名是海外学生，约占5%，来自91个不同的国家，多数攻读研究生学位。

堪萨斯州立大学位于美国的心脏地带。校园分为两处，主校园距堪萨斯城125公里，占地664英亩，居住和购物皆很方便，大约有3.8万人。新校园位于曼哈顿西70公里处的萨丽拿城，占地约1.8万亩，约有4.5万人。

从1986年起，堪萨斯州立大学在申请罗德奖学金、马歇尔奖学金、杜鲁门奖学金等方面都首屈一指，该校拥有众多著名的教授。
