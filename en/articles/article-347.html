The Education Section of the Australian Embassy and Consulate in China interprets the adjustment of the student visa policy

On December 16, 2010, Australian Minister for Immigration and Citizenship Chris Bowen and Higher Education Minister Senator Chris Evans announced a strategic review of the student visa program, along with several specific measures to ease pressure on the international education sector. These changes only affect new applicants who submit student visas after the relevant measures take effect. This adjustment to student visa policy is reflected in the following aspects:

· Certificate-level vocational education and training courses

· Prepayment of accommodation fees for formal boarding schools

· New statistics are being published

· Adjustment of Visa Assessment Levels (AL).

· The catalogue of course types and corresponding visa types will be updated

Details are as follows:

From December 16, 2010, flexible assessment has been applied to certificate-level vocational education and training courses. Applicants may enroll in up to four certificate level courses (including Levels 1 to 4), with all certificate courses lasting at least one semester and including one Level 4 certificate course. Previously, only one Level 4 certificate course required at least one year.

From December 16, 2010, prepaid boarding school accommodation fees can be deducted from the living expenses required for visa applications, and the financial guarantee required for the visa application has been reduced. However, the prepaid homestay fees and apartment rent cannot be deducted from the total living expenses required at the time of visa application.

Starting from January 2011, in-depth student visa statistics will be published on the Department of Immigration website, allowing education departments to access more detailed student visa data.

Starting April 2011, the visa assessment level (AL) for Class 573 (higher education) student visas from China will be lowered from level 4 to level 3. This adjustment means the Australian government has lowered the requirements for Chinese students applying for Class 573 student visas to submit supporting documents, mainly covering the following three aspects of financial support:

· Lowering the requirement for the duration of financial sponsorship. At visa assessment level 4, applicants must submit proof of financial sponsorship that covers tuition, living expenses, school-age children's tuition, and travel expenses to Australia for the first 36 months of their and their family (regardless of accompanying or not) residence in Australia. After the visa assessment level is lowered to level 3, applicants only need to submit proof of financial sponsorship to cover tuition, living expenses, school-age children's tuition, and travel expenses to Australia for the first 24 months of their and their family's stay in Australia. This means that from April 2011, the financial guarantee required for subclass 573 student visas has been reduced by one-third.

· Lowered deposit duration requirements. When the visa assessment level is Level 4, applicants must submit proof of deposit at a financial institution for at least 6 months prior to the visa application date. After the visa assessment level is lowered to Level 3, applicants only need to submit proof of deposit at a financial institution for at least 3 months prior to the visa application date. If you have completed 75% of your main course in Australia and wish to renew your visa to complete your studies, the required deposit will no longer be required to be deposited at a financial institution for 3 months.

· Relax requirements regarding the source of the economic security fund. When the visa assessment level is 4, the financial sponsor can be the applicant or their spouse, parents, grandparents, siblings, or uncles (uncles or uncles must reside in Australia and be Australian citizens/permanent residents or eligible New Zealand citizens). After the visa assessment level is lowered to level 3, the sponsor can be the applicant or anyone else.

Additionally, the specific effective date for April 2011 will be announced promptly once confirmed. Class 573 student visas submitted after the specific effective date will be processed according to assessment level 3, while those submitted before the specific effective date will still be processed according to assessment level 4.

From January 1, 2011, students enrolled in the Vocational Education and Training Diploma and Vocational Education and Training Advanced Diploma programs must apply for a Class 572 student visa, with an assessment level of 4. To enroll in the Higher Education Diploma and Higher Education Advanced Diploma programs, a Class 573 student visa is required, with an assessment level of 3. Some universities in Australia also offer vocational education programs. If you are enrolled in the Vocational Education and Training Diploma or Vocational Education and Training Advanced Diploma programs offered by the university, you must apply for a Class 572 student visa, with an assessment level of 4. In other words, the assessment grade corresponds to the course category, not the school offering the course.

In 2010, Senator Chris Evans, Australia's Minister for Higher Education, announced the Australian International Student Strategy developed by the Australian Government Commission, whose core content includes the welfare of international students, consumer protection, the quality of international education, the quality of international student educational experiences, and providing them with the latest and most accurate information. The Australian government hopes to send the message to the world: Australia welcomes international students and will maintain a world-leading position in education.

澳大利亚驻华使领馆教育处解读学生签证政策的调整

2010年12月16日，澳大利亚移民与公民事务部长Chris Bowen和高等教育部长Chris Evans参议员宣布将对学生签证项目进行策略复审，以及多项具体措施以减轻国际教育部门的压力。这些变化只对在相关措施生效后提交学生签证的新申请人有影响。此次学生签证政策调整表现在以下几个方面：

· 证书级别的职业教育与培训课程

· 预付正式寄宿学校的住宿费

· 公布新的统计数据

· 签证评估等级（AL）的调整

· 对课程类型与对应的签证种类目录予以更新

详细信息如下：

从2010年12月16日起，对证书级别的职业教育与培训课程采取灵活的评审。申请人能够配套就读最多四门证书级别的课程（包括一至四级证书课程），所有证书课程的总学期至少为一年，并且课程中包含一门四级证书课程。而此前仅就读四级证书一门课程，就必须至少一年时间。

从2010年12月16日起，学生已预付的寄宿学校的住宿费可以从签证申请所要求的生活费用中扣除，学生在申请签证时所需提供的经济担保金也由此有所降低。但学生预付的寄宿家庭的费用和公寓租金不能从申请签证时所要求的总生活费里扣除。

从2011年1月起，将在移民部的网站上公布有深度的学生签证统计数据，以便教育部门了解更详细的学生签证统计数据。

从2011年4月起，针对来自中国的573类（高等教育）学生签证的签证评估等级(AL)将从4级降低为3级。这项调整意味着澳大利亚政府降低了对申请573类学生签证的中国学生所需提交证明材料的要求，主要涉及经济担保的以下三方面：

· 降低对经济担保年限的要求。签证评估等级为4级时，签证申请人需提交能够支付其和家属（无论是否随行）在澳大利亚居住期间前36个月的学费、生活费、学龄儿童学费以及往返澳大利亚旅费的经济担保证明。签证评估等级降低为3级后，签证申请人只需提交能够支付其和家属在澳大利亚居住期间前24个月的学费、生活费、学龄儿童学费以及往返澳大利亚旅费的经济担保证明。这即意味着，从2011年4月起，申请573类学生签证需提交的经济担保金减少了三分之一。

· 降低对存款存期的要求。签证评估等级为4级时，签证申请人需提交于签证申请日期前在金融机构已存满6个月的存款证明。签证评估等级降低为3级后，签证申请人只需提交于签证申请日期前在金融机构已存满3个月的存款证明。若你在澳大利亚已完成了75%的主课，如果你想续签签证来完成学业，届时所需提供的担保金将不再被要求须在金融机构存满3个月。

· 放宽对经济担保金来源的要求。签证评估等级为4级时，经济担保人可以是申请者本人或其配偶、父母、祖父母、兄弟姐妹、姑姨叔(伯)舅（姑姨叔(伯)舅须常住澳大利亚而且是澳大利亚公民/永久居民或者是符合条件的新西兰公民）。签证评估等级降低为3级后，经济担保人可以是申请者本人或其他任何人。

另，2011年4月的具体生效日期待确定后将及时公布。在具体生效日期后递交的573类学生签证将按评估等级3进行审理，在具体生效日期前递交的573类学生签证将仍按评估等级4进行审理。

从2011年1月1日起，就读职业教育与培训专科文凭（Vocational Education and Training Diploma）和职业教育与培训高级专科文凭（Vocational Education and Training Advanced Diploma）课程，在申请学生签证时要申请572类学生签证，评估等级为4级。就读高等教育专科文凭（Higher Education Diploma）和高等教育高级专科文凭（Higher Education Advanced Diploma）课程，在申请签证时要申请573类学生签证，评估等级为3级。在澳大利亚有些大学也提供职业教育课程。若你就读由大学提供的职业教育与培训专科文凭（Vocational Education and Training Diploma）和职业教育与培训高级专科文凭（Vocational Education and Training Advanced Diploma）课程，在申请签证时要申请572类学生签证，评估等级为4级。也就是说评估等级所对应的是课程类别而非提供课程的学校。

2010年，澳大利亚高等教育部长克里斯·埃文斯参议员公布了澳大利亚政府委员会制定的《澳大利亚国际学生战略》，其核心内容包括国际学生的福利、消费者保护、国际教育的质量、国际学生教育体验的质量以及为他们提供最新的准确信息。澳大利亚政府希望向世界传达这样一个信息：澳大利亚欢迎国际学生，并将在教育领域保持世界领先地位。
