Saint Petersburg State Mining University


[Introduction]: Saint Petersburg State Mining University was founded in 1773 and has a history of over two hundred years. It is the oldest engineering higher education institution in Russian history, having trained countless mining talents since its founding. In 2009, the Mining Institute was selected as one of Russia's first 12 national research universities.

Introduction to the Institution

Saint Petersburg State Mining University (State University of Mineral Resources - Mining - Национальный минерально-сырьевой университ «Горный») is the oldest engineering higher education institution in Russian history. To fulfill the wishes of Russian Emperor Peter I and the famous scientist Lomonosov to develop Russian mining and establish a specialized school for geology and mining, it was founded by Empress Catherine II of Russia in 1773.

Russian President Putin once praised the Mining Academy as "one of the standards of higher education in Russia." Among Russian geological and mining institutions, the Mining Academy holds an unquestionable leadership position and represents the highest standard among all geotechnical institutions in Russia. It has received four national awards. Until 1899, the Saint Petersburg Mining Institute was the only mining technology higher education institution in all of Russia, responsible for cultivating talent in mining, metallurgy, geology, and other fields throughout Russia. To this day, the Mining Institute still plays a pivotal role in scientific research in Russian mining, metallurgy, geology, and petroleum. Especially in polar geological exploration and mapping, fine ore processing for rare earth metals, and ultra-high and low temperature drilling, it possesses world-class technical standards. In 1996, by decree of then Russian President Yeltsin (Presidential Decree No. 1112, July 30, 1996), the Mining Institute, along with Moscow University, Saint Petersburg University, and eight other universities, was listed as part of the Russian Federation's National Historical and Cultural Heritage. In 2009, the Mining Faculty was selected as one of the first 12 national research universities in Russia.

University Motto: Devoted wholeheartedly to the country, willing to dedicate oneself.

Current Principal: Professor Vladimir Litvinenko, Academician of the Russian Academy of Natural Sciences

Professional recommendations

Department of Geology and Exploration

Mineral Geological Survey, Mineral Geology and Exploration, Hydrogeology and Engineering Geology (including: Hydrogeology Specialization and Engineering Geology Specialization), Mineral Survey and Exploration Geophysical Methods, Mineral Exploration Technology and Techniques, Petroleum and Natural Gas Drilling.

Mining Department

Underground mining (including: specialized layered deposit mining and sand mining specialization), open-pit mining (including: specialization in open-pit mining of sand and coal mines and open-pit mining of building materials), physical processes of mining and oil and gas production, oil and gas extraction and operation, environmental protection engineering (geological exploration, mines, metallurgy, oil and gas production) (including: specialized protection around mines and specialized protection around metallurgical industries).

Department of Mechanical Engineering

Mining Machinery and Equipment major (including: Mining Machinery Equipment Design and Manufacturing Specialization and Mining Machinery Equipment Operation Specialization), Metallurgical Machinery and Equipment Major, Industrial Equipment and Process Systems Power Drive and Automation Major (including: Mine Electromechanical Equipment and Automation Specialization and Underground Building Electromechanical Equipment and Automation Specialization), Oil and Gas Field Development and Operation Major, Drilling Major, Oil and Gas Storage and Transportation Major, Economics Major, Accounting Major (Mining and Geological Exploration Enterprises) Economics and Management Major.

Department of Chemical Metallurgy

Major in ore processing, non-ferrous metal smelting, thermophysics and automation of industrial furnaces and ecology, metallurgical process and production process automation.

Enrollment situation

Admissions for the Mining Institute follow the common procedures of Russian higher education institutions. After five years of study, university students can obtain an engineer (economist) certificate. Students who complete their thesis after four years of study can obtain a bachelor's degree. Bachelor's degree holders can enter the master's program, which continues their training according to the direction during the two-year master's program. All university students are guaranteed dormitory accommodation. The Mining College also offers preparatory courses and short-term training classes for candidates, with classes held except on Saturdays and Sundays, mainly focusing on mathematics and physics.

圣彼得堡国立矿业大学


【导读】：圣彼得堡国立矿业大学创立于1773年，至今已有两百多年历史，是俄罗斯历史第上一所工科高等院校，建校至今培养了无数矿业人才，2009年，矿业学院被选为俄罗斯首批12所国家研究型大学之一。

院校介绍

圣彼得堡国立矿业大学(国家矿产资源大学-矿业-Национальный минерально-сырьевой университет «Горный» )是俄罗斯历史第上一所工科高等院校。为了实现俄国皇帝彼得一世与著名科学家罗蒙诺索夫关于发展俄罗斯矿业，建立地矿类专门学校的遗愿，由俄国女皇叶卡捷琳娜二世创立于1773年。

俄罗斯总统普京曾称赞矿业学院为“俄罗斯高等教育的标准之一”。在俄罗斯地矿类院校中，矿业学院拥有无可质疑的领导地位，代表着全俄罗斯地矿类院校的最高水准。曾获得过四个国家级奖项。直到1899年之前，圣彼得堡矿业学院仍是全俄罗斯唯一的矿业技术类高等学校，肩负着全俄罗斯矿业、冶金、地质等领域的人才培育工作。时至今日，俄罗斯采矿、冶金、地质、石油等方面的科学研究中矿业学院仍有着举足轻重的作用。特别是在极地地质勘探测绘，稀土金属精细选矿，超高温超低温钻井等方面拥有世界一流的技术水准。1996年，根据时任俄罗斯总统叶利钦的命令(总统令№1112，1996年7月30日)，矿业学院与莫斯科大学、圣彼得堡大学等8所大学一同被列为俄罗斯联邦民族历史文化遗产。2009年，矿业学院被选为俄罗斯首批12所国家研究型大学之一。

大学校训：竭诚为国，甘于奉献。

现任校长：俄罗斯自然科学院院士弗拉基米尔·利特维年科教授

专业推荐

地质与勘探系

矿产地质普查专业、矿产地质与勘探专业、水文地质与工程地质专业(包括：水文地质专门化和工程地质专门化)、矿产普查与勘探地物理方法专业、矿产勘探工艺与技术专业、石油与天然气钻井专业。

矿业系

地下采矿专业(包括：层状矿床采矿专门化和砂矿采矿专门化)、露天采矿专业(包括：砂矿和煤矿露天采矿专门化和建筑材料露天采矿专门化)、矿山和石油天然气生产的物理过程专业、石油天然气开采与运营专业、环境保护工程(地质勘探、矿山、冶金、石油天然气生产)专业(包括：矿山周围保护专门化和冶金工业周围保护专门化)。

机械系

矿山机械与设备专业(包括：矿山机械设备设计与制造专门化和矿山机械设备运营专门化)、冶金机械与设备专业、工业设备与工艺系统的电力驱动和自动化专业(包括：矿山机电设备与自动化专门化和地下建筑机电设备与自动化专门化)石油天然气系油气田开发和操作专业，钻探专业，石油天然气储存及运输专业经济系会计学专业、(矿山与地质勘探企业)经济与管理专业。

化学冶金系

选矿专业、有色金属冶炼专业、工业熔炉的热物理学和自动化和生态学专业、冶金工艺和生产过程自动化专业。

招生情况

矿业学院的招生工作按照俄罗斯高等学校的共同规程进行。大学生通过五年的学习，可以获得工程师(经济师)证书。大学生通过四年的学习完成毕业论文可以获得学士学位证书，学士学位获得者可以进入硕士阶段学习，在两年硕士期间将按照相应的方向对他们继续培养。所有的大学生都保证有宿舍住宿。矿业学院还为考生设有预科系、短期培训班，其中短期培训班除星期六、日以外都有课，主要补习数学、物理两门课程。
