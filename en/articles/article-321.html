Toulouse II University was founded in 1229 and had already gained great prestige in the sixteenth century. In the 20th century, this university reached its peak of development: the university moved out of Toulouse city center in 1968 and officially settled in the new district of Remihai in 1971. Number of students: 26,296, including 3,372 international students; 877 teachers; 470 non-teaching staff.

The teaching fields at Toulouse II University include: literature, arts, languages, humanities and social sciences, as well as science and technology. Initial academic education programs range from the first stage up to the doctoral degree. Both initial and continuing education offer vocational-oriented training programs. The vocational courses mainly cover professional fields such as audiovisual communication and communication, archives and materials, hospitality and tourism, translation and interpretation, land consolidation, sociology, applied mathematics, culture and heritage, psychology, and more.

Toulouse II University has a Department of Foreign Languages and French Language, dedicated to teaching French to international students from non-speaking countries. The university's distance education services are the most developed among all comprehensive universities in France, allowing most students to complete bachelor's programs through distance teaching, with some even continuing to the French master's program (maitrise). The university has three doctoral faculties: "Literature, Language, Culture," "Time, Space, Society, Culture," and "Behavior, Speech, Education, Socialization, Cognition," responsible for managing doctoral studies. Each year, nearly one hundred doctoral dissertations are defended, including four of them

One Stage of the doctoral dissertation is completed by foreign students who come to study the third stage. The third stage begins with one of the 22 Advanced In-Depth Research Diplomas (DEA) being pursued.

The research work at Toulouse II University relies on 43 research teams. The research areas the university hopes to strengthen include: archaeology and history, linguistics, labor studies, territorial and regional studies, and more. The university's Foreign Languages and French Department organizes summer French courses and also arranges French language instruction throughout the academic year. This French language program prepares students for the DELF (Certificate of French Language Learning) and DALF (Certificate of Advanced French Study) exams. The Information and Communication Technology Coordination Department is responsible for collaborative services among various teaching administrations (such as continuing education, distance education, technology innovation and multimedia working group, central data service, etc.).

图卢兹第二大学建于1229年，在十六世纪时就已赢得了巨大的声誉。在20世纪，这所文科大学更是达到了发展的鼎盛时期：学校于1968年搬离图卢兹市中心，并在1971年在勒米哈伊新区正式落户。学生人数：26296人，其中外国学生3372人； 877名教师； 470名非教学人员。

图卢兹第二大学的教学领域包括：文学、艺术、语言、人文与社会科学以及科学与技术。初始学历教育课程从第一阶段直至博士学位。初始教育和继续教育均设有以职业为目的的培养课程。职业化课程主要涉及的专业领域包括：视听传播交流、档案与资料、旅店与旅游、笔译与口译、国土整治、社会学、应用数学、文化与遗产、心理学，等等。

图卢兹第二大学设有专门为非法语国家留学生教授法语的外国语法语系。学校的远程教育服务处为法国所有综合大学中最为发达，它使大多数专业的学生可通过远程授课修读完学士课程，有的甚至还可延续至法国制硕士(maItrise)课程。学校设有“文学、语言、文化”、“时间、空间、社会、文化”以及“行为、言语、教育、社会化、认知” 三个博士研究生院，负责管理博士阶段学业。每年答辩博士论文近百篇，其中四

分之一博士论文由来校攻读第三阶段的外国学生完成。第三阶段学业以攻读22项高等深入研究文凭(DEA)的其中一项为起点。

图卢兹第二大学的科研工作依靠43个研究团队展开。学校希望加强的科研领域包括：考古学与历史、语言学、关于劳动的研究、国土区域研究，等等。学校的外国语法语系组织暑期法语课，在整个学年期间也安排法语教学。这一法语课程可为参加DELF (法语学习证书)和DALF(法语深入学习证书)考试作准备。信息与交流技术协调部负责各不同教学行政部门的合作服务(如：继续教育、远程教育处、技术创新与多媒体工作组、中央资料服务处，等等)。
