A review of the 2015 Canadian university application deadlines

    Studying in Canada has been a popular choice among Chinese students, attracting countless overseas students with its high-quality teaching, safe and comfortable social environment, excellent employment opportunities, and relaxed Canadian immigration policies.

    Higher education in Canada is mainly public and divided into three levels: universities, colleges, and colleges, with more than 80 universities currently available. Based on university size, degree offerings, faculty strength, and research level, universities can be divided into medical doctoral universities, comprehensive universities, and foundational universities.

    Below, the editor has summarized the application deadlines for the fall 2015 admissions at major Canadian universities. We hope everyone can apply as soon as possible to avoid missing out on their desired schools and majors. Note: Since application deadlines for major universities often change, please refer to the official website for the latest deadlines

    Canadian universities with applications closing at the end of January

    University of Toronto (Online application deadline: January 14 - January 30)

    McGill University (Online application deadline is January 15, 2015; materials must be submitted on March 15)

    University of Guelph (online application deadline before January 15, materials deadline March 1)

    University of British Columbia (Online application deadline is January 31, materials deadline is March 15)

    2. Application deadline for Canadian universities is the end of February.

    Queen's University (Online application deadline is February 1, 2015. A personal statement must be submitted by February 15, and a short or supplementary article must be submitted before March 1)

    University of York (online application deadline is February 4, 2015, including Nursing, Schulich School of Business, Social Work, and the School of Art, Media, Performing Arts, and Design; Other majors are accepted by June 3)

    Victoria University (Online application deadline: February 28)

    Concordia University (Application deadline: February 1)

    University of Saskatchewan (Engineering and Edwards School of Business Online Applications February 15, Materials Deadline March 1) Arts and Sciences Online Applications April 1, Materials Deadline May 1)

    Canadian universities with applications closed at the end of March

    University of Windsor (Online application and application deadline: March 1)

    University of Calgary (Online application deadline: March 1, Material application deadline: March 31)

    Saint Mary's University (Complete applications before March 15 have a chance to receive scholarships; online applications must be completed no later than April 30, and materials completed by July 15)

    Canadian universities with applications closing at the end of April

    University of Ottawa (Online application deadline April 1, materials deadline April 30)

    Carleton University (Online application deadline is April 1, material deadline is May 1)

    McMaster University (Online application deadline is April 1, material application deadline is April 1, including Arts & Science majors, online application deadline is February 1)

    Simon Fraser University (Online application deadline: April 30)

    Brock University (online application completed on April 1)

    Dalhousie University (Application deadline is April 1; some majors such as Pharmacy (February 1), Health Sciences (February 15), Environmental Research (March 1)

    Canadian universities with applications closing at the end of May

    Laurier University (Online application deadline: May 1)

    University of Alberta (Online application deadline: May 1, materials deadline: August 1)

    Canadian universities with applications closing at the end of June

    Western University (Online application deadline: June 1. January 14: Ivey Advanced Entry Opportunity (AEO); February 15: Business Aviation Management and Nursing majors, Virtual Art, Digital Art; March 7: Arts and Humanities Studies)

    Canadian universities with applications closing at the end of July

    University of Manitoba (Application deadline is July 6; it is recommended to submit before March 1 for a chance to receive scholarships)

    Although each university has clearly stated application deadlines, the deadlines vary depending on the major. Moreover, many Canadian universities use a first-come, first-served admission system due to the large number of applicants. Therefore, the earlier you apply to study in Canada, the better. It is recommended that students interested in studying abroad plan their applications early.

    加拿大留学作为中国学生们的热门选择，一直以其高质量的教学水准、安全舒适的社会环境、良好的就业机会和宽松的加拿大留学移民政策等吸引着无数海外留学生留学加拿大。

    加拿大的高等教育以公立为主，分为大学、大学学院和学院三个不同的层次，现有大学 80 多所。依据学校规模、学位设置、师资力量、科研水平等不同可分为医学博士类大学、综合类大学和基础类大学。

    下面小编就为大家总结了加拿大各大知名院校2015年秋季入学的申请截止时间，希望大家抓紧时间尽早申请，以免与心仪的学校和专业失之交臂。注意：由于各大学校申请截止时间常有变动，以学校官网截止时间为准

    1月底截止申请的加拿大大学

    多伦多大学(1月14日-1月30日网申截止)

    麦吉尔大学(网申截止时间是2015年1月15日，3月15日材料截止)

    圭尔夫大学(1月15日前网申截止，3月1日材料截止)

    不列颠哥伦比亚大学(网申截止是1月31日，材料截止是3月15日)

    2月底截止申请的加拿大大学

    女王大学(网申截止时间是2015年2月1日，2月15日需要提交个人陈述，3月1日前需要提交短文，或补充的文章)

    约克大学(网申截止时间是2015年2月4日，包括护理学、舒立克商学院、社会工作专业，艺术、媒体、表演与设计学院各专业;其他专业截止时间是6月3日)

    维多利亚大学(网申截止时间2月28日)

    康考迪亚大学(申请截止时间2月1日)

    萨省大学(工程专业和爱德华兹商学院网申2月15日，材料截止3月1日。文理专业网申4月1日，材料截止5月1日)

    3月底截止申请的加拿大大学

    温莎大学(网申及材料截止日期3月1日)

    卡尔加里大学(网申截止时间3月1日，材料申请截止时间3月31日)

    圣玛丽大学(3月15日前完整申请有机会获奖学金，最晚4月30日前完成网络申请，7月15日材料完成)

    4月底截止申请的加拿大大学

    渥太华大学(网申截止是4月1日，材料截止是4月30日)

    卡尔顿大学(网申截止是4月1日，材料截止是5月1日)

    麦克马斯特大学(网申截止是4月1日，材料截止是4月1日，其中Arts & Science专业，网申截止时间是2月1日)

    西蒙菲沙大学(网申截止时间4月30日)

    布鲁克大学(4月1日完成网申)

    达尔豪斯大学(申请截止时间是4月1日，部分专业如药剂(2月1日)、健康科学(2月15日)、环境研究(3月1日)

    5月底截止申请的加拿大大学

    劳里埃大学(网申截止日期5月1日)

    阿尔伯塔大学(网申截止时间5月1日，8月1日材料截止)

    6月底截止申请的加拿大大学

    西安大略大学(网申截止日期6月1日。1月14日，Ivey Advanced Entry Opportunity (AEO);2月15日，商业航空管理和护理专业，虚拟艺术、数字艺术;3月7日，艺术和人文研究)

    7月底截止申请的加拿大大学

    曼尼托巴大学(申请截止时间7月6日，建议3月1日前提交申请，有机会得到奖学金)

    虽然各大学都已经写明了申请截止时间，但每个学校的不同专业申请截止时间也会有所差别，而且加拿大很多大学因申请人数众多，会采取先到先得的方法进行录取。所以加拿大留学申请还是越早做越好，建议有留学意向的同学在申请前早做申请规划。
