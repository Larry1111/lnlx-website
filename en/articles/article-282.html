Tokyo Riverside Gakuen is located in the bustling area of Taito Ward, Hanakawado, on the banks of the famous Sumida River, a famous river in Tokyo. It is only a five-minute walk from one of Tokyo's famous tourist attractions, Asakusa City, offering a peaceful retreat amid the bustle. Due to its historical significance, the area is thriving with commerce, thriving industries, and low prices, making it a prime place for studying, living, and living.

Tokyo Riverside Gakuen was established in 1989, with a student capacity of 240. It uses a two-part (morning and afternoon) teaching system, with courses for one and a half years and two years, and enrollment periods each October (one and a half years) and April (second year). The school places great emphasis on cultivating students' comprehensive abilities, especially focusing on improving Japanese language skills with a focus on listening and speaking. The school increases students' understanding of Japanese culture and folk customs by organizing them to participate in folk activities such as the Asakusa Sanja Festival and Tokyo Jidai Festival. It helps students integrate into Japanese social life and provides specific guidance for admission to Japanese universities after graduation.

Due to its outstanding management over the years, the school has been recognized by the Immigration Bureau of Japan as one of the few outstanding schools.

东京河畔学园，地处东京市内比较繁华地区的台东区花川户、日本著名河流隅田川河畔，距东京著名旅游景点之一的浅草市仅五分钟步行路程，具有闹中取静的特点。该地区由于历史性的原因，商业繁荣、百业兴旺、物价较低，是学习、生活、居住的好处所。

东京河畔学园成立于1989年，学生定员240名，采用二部制（上、下午）授课方式，其课程设置为一年半和二年，入学时间为每年的十月（一年半课程）和四月（二年课程）。该校重视学生的综合能力的培养，特别是在以听说能力为重点的日本语能力的提高方面具有鲜明的特点。该校通过组织学生参加浅草三社节、东京时代节等民间活动增加对日本文化与民俗的了解。使学生溶入日本社会生活，并对学生毕业后考入日本大学给予具体的指导。

该校由于多年来经营管理相当出色，被日本法务局入管局认定为数目相当有限的优秀学校之一。
