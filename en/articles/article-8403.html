Interpretation of studying and living in Russia

When students first arrive in Russia to study abroad, they first study Russian in the preparatory department. The preparatory department studies 5 days a week, about 6 hours per day. The department divides students into classes based on their major, with 6-8 students per class. In addition to mainly teaching Russian, students also take compulsory courses in Russian economics, geography, history, literature, and computer science. In addition, each class offers foundational knowledge courses tailored to their major. Each academic year has two holidays: a summer vacation of 2 months and a winter vacation of 2 weeks. The school year starts on September 1. As long as students study diligently, it is easy to pass the preparatory graduation exam and successfully enter the department.

Living conditions for students in Russia are generally better than those at domestic universities, typically two to three people per room, with some single rooms. University dormitories have toilets, bathrooms, kitchens, and hot and cold water available all year round. Heating starts in September each year and lasts until May of the following year. In winter, all rooms are equipped with double-glazed windows, with indoor temperatures ranging from about +20°C to +26°C. Heating is available in all public places such as classrooms, corridors, as well as city shops, residences, offices, subways, and public transportation.

Every university has student and faculty cafeterias, usually more than one, and prices are very cheap. International students can dine in any cafeteria or buy groceries at the market to cook for themselves. Russian Study Abroad Network Eating habits differ slightly from Chinese people. They mainly eat pork and beef, with fewer vegetable varieties, and staples include bread and rice; Side dishes are mainly cabbage, onions, radishes, cucumbers, tomatoes, etc. Chicken, eggs, and fish are very cheap in Russia. Students with a better budget can buy various seasonal Chinese vegetables and seasonings at China Houses or Chinese supermarkets.

Russia implements a free foreign exchange system, with exchange points scattered throughout the city, so students can exchange foreign currency for rubles at any time.

According to school regulations, every student must obtain medical insurance. Taking any bus or subway allows students to enjoy discounted fares.

Work-study is possible in Russia. According to Russian law, foreign citizens must obtain a work card, and in principle, students cannot work for money. However, as the number of Chinese people engaged in long-term economic and trade activities, business inspections, and short-term tourism in Russia is increasing year by year, and Russian language talent is extremely scarce, this objectively provides opportunities for work-study. Therefore, currently, international students in Russia generally work as translators or tour guides in their spare time, earning a monthly salary of $800-1000 (professional level), with a daily wage of $30-50. Russia's Study Abroad Network reminds us that since making money abroad is relatively easy, if you are greedy for it, it will inevitably affect your studies. Therefore, appropriate work-study should be encouraged, aiming to gain broad exposure to society, develop life skills, and improve language proficiency.

The former Soviet Union was designated by the World Organization as a welfare country, and many of these benefits have continued to this day. Russian citizens enjoy free medical care and free education. Therefore, babies receive free milk supply from birth and receive regular medical check-ups from doctors; Children enter kindergarten for free at age two, receiving music and dance education; At age six, they enter a ten-year secondary school. According to Russian law, babies born in Russia, regardless of parental nationality, enjoy Russian citizenship.

学生初到俄罗斯留学时，首先在预备系学习俄语。预备系每周学习5天，每天6个小时左右。系里根据留学生将要学习的专业分班，每个班6-8人，除主要讲授俄语课外，学生还必修学习俄罗斯经济、地理、历史、文学及计算机等课程。此外，各个班都分别根据专业开设基础知识课程。每学年有两个假期，暑假2个月，寒假两周。每学年9月1日开学。学生只要认真学习，很容易通过预科毕业考试，顺利入系学习。

学生在俄罗斯的生活住宿条件普遍比国内大学的条件优越，一般两到三人一间，也有单人间，大学宿舍内有卫生间、浴室、厨房，冷热水全年全天供应。每年9月开始供暖到次年5月止。冬天，全部安装双层玻璃，室内温度在+20摄氏度到+26摄氏度左右，在所有公共场所如学校教室、走廊以及城市的商店、住宅、办公室、地铁和公交交通工具里都有供暖设施。

各大学都有学生及教职工食堂，一般不只一个，价格很便宜，留学生可以在任何一个食堂就餐，也可以从市场买菜自己做饭。俄罗斯留学网异性俄罗斯人的饮食习惯同中国人略有差异，他们主要是吃猪肉、牛肉，蔬菜的种类较少，主食有面包、大米;副食菜主要是卷心菜、洋葱、萝卜、黄瓜、西红柿等。在俄罗斯鸡肉、鸡蛋、鱼都很便宜，经济宽裕的同学可以在中国楼或中国超市买到各种中国时令蔬菜及调料。

俄罗斯实行外汇自由兑换制度，换汇点遍布城市各个角落，因此学生可以随时用外汇换取卢布。

根据学校规定，每个学生必须办理医疗保险。乘任何公交车及地铁，享受学生优惠票价。

在俄罗斯可以勤工俭学。根据俄罗斯法律规定，外国公民工作必须办理打工卡，原则上学生是不能打工挣钱的。但是，由于在俄罗斯长期从事经贸活动、商务考察及短期旅游的中国人在逐年增加，而俄语人才又极其短缺，客观上为勤工俭学提供了机会。因此，目前在俄罗斯的留学生，学习之余一般从事翻译或导游工作，月工资在800-1000美金(专业水平)，日工资30-50美元。俄罗斯留学网提醒，由于在外国赚钱较容易，如果贪图去挣钱，势必影响学习。因此，应当鼓励适当的勤工俭学，目的是广泛接触社会、锻炼自己的生活能力和提高语言水平。

前苏联被世界组织评定为福利性国家，有很多福利待遇一直延续到现在。俄罗斯本国公民享受免费医疗、免费教育。因此，婴儿从出生起即享受免费的牛奶供应，定期医生上门检查身体;两岁免费入幼儿园，接受音乐及舞蹈教育;六岁进入十年制中学。根据俄罗斯法律，在俄罗斯出生的婴儿，不论父母国籍，享受俄罗斯国民待遇。
