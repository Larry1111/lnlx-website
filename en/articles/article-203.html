A complete analysis of the situation regarding South Korean student visas

C3 Visa

If you plan to study for a semester at the Language Education Institute, you can go to Korea The embassy (or consulate) applies for a C3 visa with a three-month stay period and can obtain the visa directly. The C3 visa is available at the Korea You cannot postpone your studies; you can only study during your stay.

D-2 visa

It can be approached Korea Apply at the embassy (or consulate) and obtain the visa directly. The D-2 visa can be obtained at the following time Korea Obtain a visa extension. Visa application materials: passport, ID card, visa issuance permit.

D-4 visa

If you plan to study at the Language Education Institute for more than one semester, it is best to apply to Korea You can get an extended D4 visa. In Korea It takes about one month from the Embassy (or Consulate) to the time you apply for a D4 visa and obtain it. Documents required for the visa application include: passport, admission confirmation letter issued by the Language Education Institute, notarized letter of guarantee of identity and passport, ID card, and visa issuance permit.

Extension of the residence period

When extending your visa, you should go to your location (in Korea) Application for extension at the Immigration Administration Office of the Ministry of Justice. Required documents include: passport, foreigner's ID card, certificate of enrollment issued by the college, notarized letter of guarantee, proof of tuition and living expenses paid from overseas (i.e., a bank passbook proving payment from overseas, or a foreign currency purchase certificate issued by the bank when exchanging foreign currency for Korean won), and other documents required by the Immigration Administration Office of the Ministry of Justice.

Certificate of identity

To obtain a visa, one person must be certified Korea Guarantor of identity. The guarantor must be present Korea Adults with occupations residing here. The format of the identity guarantee letter can be obtained at the college. When notarizing the identity guarantee letter, the guarantor must bring their indigenous registration card and seal in person to a law firm or notary office to complete the notarization procedures.

Visa issuance permits

Remains as is Korea Employed adults residing there provide a letter of guarantee, and the guarantor will apply for a visa and grant permit at the Entry-Exit Administration Office of the Ministry of Justice for the Chinese applicant.

Foreigner ID card

Chinese nationals holding D-2 and D-4 visas will be included Korea If the stay is 90 days (three months) or more, you must be in the country Korea Within 90 days, apply for and obtain a foreigner's ID card at the immigration office of the Ministry of Justice in your location. If you do not apply for a foreigner's ID card within 90 days, you will be fined. Documents required to apply for a foreigner's ID card include: passport, certificate of enrollment, or admission certificate.

C3签证

如果准备在语言教育学院学习一个学期，可以向韩国大使馆（或领事馆）申请居留期为三个月的C3签证，并能直接取得签证。C3签证在韩国不能延期，只能在居留期间学习。

D－2签证

可以向韩国大使馆（或领事馆）申请，并能直接取得签证。D－2签证可以在韩国得到签证延期。申请签证材料：护照、身份证、签证颁发许。

D－4签证

如果要在语言教育学院学习一个学期以上，那么最好申请在韩国可以得到延长的D4签证。在韩国大使馆（或领事馆）申请D4签证后到取得签证大约需要一个月。申请签证时所需要材料有：护照、语言教育学院发给的入学认定书、经过公证的身份保证书和护照、身份证、签证颁发许。

居留期延长

签证延长时，应到所在地（在韩国）的法务部出入境事务管理所申请延期。所需材料有：护照、外国人身份证、学院发给的在学证明书、经过公证的身份保证书，还有从海外支付学费和生活费的证明材料（即可以证明已从海外支付的银行存折，或者将外币换为韩币时银行所开出的外币购买证明书），以及其他的法务部出入境管理事务所要求的材料。

身份保证书

为取得签证，必须由一名韩国人进行身份保证。身份保证人必须是在韩国居住的有职业的成年人。身份保证书的样式可在学院领取。身份保证人进行身份保证书的公证时，持其原住民登录证和印章亲自去法律事务所或者公证事务所办理公证手续。

签证颁发许可

由在韩国居住的有职业的成年人提供身份保证书，并由身份担保人在法务部出入境管理事务所为中国申请者申请办理签证颁发许可。

外国人身份证

持有D－2和D－4签证的中国人将在韩国居留90天（三个月）以上时，应在入境韩国90日以内到所在地的法务部出入境管理事务所申请并取得外国人身份证。如果在90天以内没有申请外国人身份证，应支付罚金。申请外国人身份证时所需要的材料有：护照、在学证明书或入学认定书。
