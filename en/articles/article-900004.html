Project Introduction

The Australian exchange program is an activity tailored by the Australian Department of Education for international students, lasting from 2 to 10 weeks. Currently, Chinese students can choose a short-term exchange program lasting 2 weeks. Exchange students experience studying abroad, where students stay in host families and transfer classes to understand their adaptability and shortcomings. More importantly, they provide parents and students with a solid reference for future study abroad and study abroad planning. Currently, under the sponsorship and support of the South Australian Government's Department of Education, the program has achieved fruitful results and received high praise from Chinese parents and students.

Australia, the magical country of the Southern Hemisphere, is one of the safest and most livable countries in the world. Its environment is cleaner than New Zealand's, and it is undoubtedly one of the highest-income countries in the world, truly a developed nation. Immigration policies are relaxed, social welfare systems are sound, and its educational philosophy and training system are world-leading
