Considerations when purchasing a study abroad suitcase

Every international student inevitably needs to bring a lot of things on their first trip abroad, so a good bag is very important for carrying these items.

Bags can be divided into large bags and small bags based on their size and purpose. The former refers to various airline cases and trolley cases people carry when going out for study, travel, or business trips; The latter refers to various briefcases, fashion bags, and leisure bags that people often see on the street. Here, we mainly talk about large bags.

Large bags can be divided into two main categories based on their materials: soft cases and hard cases. Hard cases include ABS (styrene polymer) cases and PP boxes (injection molding). Soft cases generally refer to EVA (High-Density Foam) cases. ABS boxes are moisture-proof and pressure-resistant, so even in rainy or snowy weather, you don't have to worry about the contents getting damp. Also, due to the materials used, the boxes themselves have limited stretchability, making them suitable for storing valuable items like cameras and cameras. If the boxes are impacted by external factors, the contents inside are not easily damaged. Their locks are side lock combination locks. Another type of hard box, PP cases, is produced in one piece inside molds, so its durability is superior to ABS boxes. This is its biggest feature, and other features are generally similar to ABS cases. EVA soft boxes are different. Their main feature is their low weight—the body itself is lightweight, and the box has a certain degree of stretchability. It can hold more items than ABS or PP cases of the same specifications, so its large capacity and lightweight convenience are its biggest advantages. Its lock is a combination lock without a side lock, and some models don't even have a combination lock, requiring users to add an extra lock.

According to the characteristics of suitcases, they can be divided into airline suitcases, trolley cases, and briefcases. Generally, suitcases and briefcases are longer than wide and greater than those, but trolley cases are different—their height is greater than their length. The suitcase is characterized by a handle at one end that can be lifted or pulled, making it easy to operate and especially suitable for narrow aisles. As the name suggests, a trolley case has a built-in trolley rod that allows users to pull the handle out in several sections with the press of a button, pulling it out like a luggage cart. Its lightweight and labor-saving advantage is its advantage. Briefcases, on the other hand, are popular among consumers for their small size and easy portability.

There are many bag sizes and specifications, mainly 18 inches, 20 inches, 21 inches, 23 inches, 26 inches, 27 inches, 29 inches, 30 inches, and others. Specifically, the most commonly used 23-inch suitcases have length, width, and height of 610×420×200mm; 26-inch ones 660×490×230mm; and 27-inch bags 735×570×250mm. Besides the wide variety of sizes, this year's bags have abandoned the previous boxy and angular design in favor of the latest streamlined shapes. The color options have boldly expanded from wine red, dark gray, and dark green to trendy colors like bright red, lake blue, pale yellow, emerald green, powder blue, and apple green, winning the favor of young people. If you are going out but don't know what kind of luggage to buy, or if you plan to study abroad or work, then an extra-large flight suitcase sized 30–32 inches is an ideal choice.

When choosing a luggage, you should select according to your actual needs. First, determine the size and specifications of the bag based on your specific situation. For example, if you are traveling by plane, your carry-on must not exceed 21 inches; otherwise, you cannot carry it with you, and you must complete the checked baggage procedures.

Next, select based on the specified specifications. First, check whether the shell surface is smooth and free of scratches; Second, check whether the corners of the box are smooth and not rough. If there are aluminum alloy corners, also check if the wrapping is secure; Third, pull the box a few steps to check if the bottom wheel is flexible and how strong the steering ability is. The quality of the wheels is very important; multi-row wheels and rubber wheels should be chosen because rough handling on an airplane can easily damage the wheels; Fourth, press a button to pull out the pull rod and see if it can be opened and loosened smoothly and functionally intact; Fifth, if it is a hard box, lay the box flat and place a heavy object on the shell. If you can stand on the box yourself, it will be fine; if the box is of good quality, there will definitely be no problems; Sixth, and most importantly, check the lock's performance. For a combination lock, you can try switching any password, but when setting the password, be sure to choose a 3-digit number that is easy to remember and hard to forget, such as your date of birth or phone number. Otherwise, if you can't open the box, you'll just have to worry. After these checks, you can buy it with confidence. Maintaining the box is simple: just keep it away from fire sources and corrosive materials, and prevent damage caused by human factors.

每个留学生第一次出国都难免需要带很多东西，因此一个好的箱包对携带这些物品非常重要。

箱包按其体积与用途的不同，可分为大箱包和小箱包，前者是指人们外出求学、旅游、出差办公时所携带的各种航空箱、拉杆箱；后者则是平时走在街上经常能看到人们随身携带的各种公文包、时装包、休闲包等。这里主要讲大箱包。

大箱包按其材质不同可分为软箱与硬箱两大类。硬箱包括ABS(苯乙烯聚合物)箱与PP箱(注塑)，软箱一般就是指EVA(高密度发泡)箱。ABS箱具有防潮防压的特点，所以即使遇上雨雪天气，您也不必担心箱子里的物品会受潮变湿，而且由于其所用材质的关系，箱体本身没有很大的伸缩度，适宜存放如摄相机、照相机等贵重物品，万一箱子受到外来冲击，箱内的物品也不会轻易受到损坏，它的锁为带边锁的密码锁。而另一种硬箱PP箱则是在模具内一次成型生产出来的，故牢度较ABS箱要更胜一筹，这也是它最大的特点，其它特点则与ABS箱大致相同。EVA软箱则不同，它的特点在于其自重小，即箱体本身重量较轻，箱体又具有一定的伸缩度，它能比同规格的ABS、PP箱装下更多的物品，所以容量大、轻巧方便是它最大的优点，它的锁为不带边锁的密码锁，有的型号甚至没有密码锁，需使用者加配一把锁。

根据箱子的性能不同，可以把它们分为航空旅行箱、拉杆箱和公文箱。旅行箱与公文箱一般它们的长度大于宽度大于高度，而拉杆箱则不同，它的高度大于长度大于宽度。旅行箱的特点是它的一端装有把手，能提能拉，运行便捷，特别适合狭窄的过道。拉杆箱顾名思义，它有一内置式拉杆，使用者只需按动按钮，即可将拉杆分几截纵向拉出，象行李车一样拉着走，轻巧省力是它的优点。公文箱则以体积小、携带方便而深受消费者的喜爱。

箱包规格尺寸众多，主要有18英寸、20英寸、21英寸、23英寸、26英寸、27英寸、29英寸、30英寸等。具体地来说，最常用的23英寸箱子的长宽高为610×420×200mm，26英寸的为660×490×230mm，27英寸的是735×570×250mm。今年的箱包除了规格品种较多外，在设计上也摒弃了原来四四方方、棱角分明的特点，而推出了最新的流线造型，颜色方面也在原来只有酒红、深灰、深绿几款的基础上，大胆采用了大红、湖蓝、鹅黄、翠绿、粉蓝、苹果绿等流行靓色，赢得了年轻一族的喜爱。有的朋友如果要出门，却又不知该买什么样的箱包的话，如果您要出国深造或工作的话，那么规格为30--32英寸的特大航空箱是您理想的选择。

要选购一款箱包，应按实际需要来挑选。首先，要根据具体情况来确定所要选购的箱包的尺寸规格，如您外出是要乘坐飞机的话，那么随身携带的箱子必须不能大于21英寸，否则的话是不能随身带的，那就必须办理托运手续。

其次，根据确定的规格尺寸来挑选，一是检查箱壳表面是否光洁、无伤痕；二是检查箱子的棱角是否光滑不粗糙，如果有铝合金包角的话，还要检查一下包得是否牢固；三是拉着箱子走上几步，看一下底轮是否灵活，转向能力强不强,轮子的好坏非常重要，应该选择多排轮和橡胶轮，由于飞机上粗鲁搬运，很容易使轮子受损；四是按动按钮，拉出拉杆，看看是否收放自如、功能完好；五是如果是硬箱的话，把箱子放平，在箱壳上放一重物，好可以自己站到箱子上试一下，箱子质量合格的话，那是绝对没有问题的；六是最重要的就是检查一下锁的性能，密码锁的话，您可以随意调一个密码试一下，但要注意的是在正式调密码时，一定要选择便于自己记忆又不容易遗忘的3位数字，像出生日期、电话号码等都可以。否则到时打不开箱子，就只能干着急了。经过以上几项检查后，您就可以放心地买下它了。箱子的保养方法很简单，只要勿靠近火源和腐蚀性物品以及防止人为损坏。
