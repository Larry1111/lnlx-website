School Introduction

Brock University is located in St. Catherine's (St.Catharines) city center, located on the Niagara Peninsula in Ontario, Canada. The university is one of Canada's top ten most valuable comprehensive public universities and was named Best University of the Year in 1998. There are about 11,000 students enrolled, with international students from more than 60 countries. The university not only boasts top-notch faculty and advanced teaching facilities, but also offers a comprehensive and rich curriculum, making it one of the best universities in Canada. The university covers more than 400 acres, with a beautiful and elegant environment, and comprehensive sports and entertainment facilities, including three gymnasiums, a fitness center, an indoor rowing center, a large swimming pool, a fencing and dance activity center, as well as several tennis courts and sports fields, all open to students free of charge. There are various clubs on campus, such as culture, drama, speech work, and music. The library holds over 1.5 million volumes; It is the only university in Canada offering free online computer education. Students can send and receive emails for free at the school's eight computer centers, and also take over 200 computer courses for free. These initiatives have prepared Brooke students not only in their professional knowledge but also in practical experience for their future careers, resulting in over 98% of students finding jobs smoothly upon graduation over the years.

Language courses

Brock University offers high-quality language education for students from non-English-speaking countries. For students whose English proficiency is not sufficient to directly enter specialized courses, Brock University offers an English Intensive Program (IELP). After completing the English enrichment course, students can directly enroll in Brock University's specialized courses without taking the TOEFL or IELTS exams.

The English Intensive Program has three semesters per year, starting in January, May, and September. Each semester lasts 14 weeks, with a fee of 2,500 CAD per semester. Students must pay a 100 CAD application fee and a 500 CAD tuition deposit per semester when applying.

学校简介

布鲁克大学位于圣凯瑟琳(St.Catharines)市中心，在加拿大安大略省尼亚加拉半岛上。该校是加拿大前十名最有价值的综合性公立大学之一，曾被评为1998年度最佳大学。学校在校生约11000人，留学生来自60多个国家。大学不仅师资力量一流，教学设备先进，而且课程设置全面丰富，是加拿大全国最好的大学之一。学校占地面积400多英亩，环境优美清雅，拥有完善的体育娱乐设施，包括3座体育馆，健身中心、室内划艇中心、大型游泳馆、击剑和舞蹈活动中心以及多个网球场、运动场等，全部为学生免费开放。校园内有各种俱乐部，如文化、戏剧、演讲、音乐等。图书馆藏书达150多万册；该校是加拿大唯一所提供免费网上计算机教学的大学。学生不但可以在学校的8个计算机中心免费收发电子邮件，还能免费学习200余种计算机课程。这些举措使布鲁克的学生不仅在专业知识上，更在实践经验上为将来的事业做好了准备，因而历年有98%以上的学生一毕业就顺利地找到了工作。

语言课程

布鲁克大学可以为来自母语是非英语国家的学生提供高质量的语言教育。对于那些英语水平达不到直接入读专业课程的学生，布鲁克大学提供英语强化课程（IELP），学生完成英语强化课程后，可直接入读布鲁克大学的专业课，无需另考托福或雅思。

英语强化课程一年有三个学期，分别于1、5、9月开学。每学期为期14周，费用为2500加币/学期。学生在申请时需交纳申请费100加币及学费押金500加币/学期。
