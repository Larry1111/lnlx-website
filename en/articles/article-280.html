Futaba Foreign Language School (Chiba)

Founded in 1960, the school was founded by Dr. Ryūji Hayashi, who has over 40 years of experience in linguistic education and international exchange, as a member of the "East-West Education Series Organization," with a history spanning over 40 years. Located in Chiba City, a city rich in history and culture, it takes only about 35 minutes to reach Tokyo by express train. Prices are relatively low, and living expenses are only about 70% of those in Tokyo. The school's curriculum is designed to help students master Japanese proficiency while deepening their understanding of Japanese culture. In addition, there are a variety of courses tailored to students aiming to apply to universities, vocational schools, and similar institutions. All teachers are full of friendship and enthusiasm, respecting each student's individuality and requirements. The school also holds annual school festivals and activities to experience Japanese culture such as tea ceremony, flower arrangement, and Japanese cuisine. Other extracurricular activities include field trips, trips, sports meets, and drama viewings, as well as organizing student lectures on campus and within Chiba Prefecture. The school has maintained its reputation as an excellent school for many years.

Registration Requirements:

1. Within two years of graduating from high school, vocational, or technical school

2. Within two years of completing a college diploma

3. Bachelor's degree, under 27 years old
