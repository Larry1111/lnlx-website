Which students are suitable for Thailand's 2+2 international undergraduate program?

When it comes to studying abroad, people usually first think of countries like Europe and America, where the entry thresholds are very high and students must have excellent foreign language proficiency and be able to pass exams like TOEFL and IELTS. Additionally, the cost of studying abroad can reach hundreds of thousands or even millions, which deters many ordinary families, giving rise to the traditional belief that "studying abroad is a privilege for students from wealthy families."

As China's "Belt and Road" strategy deepens, the popularity of studying in Thailand has been rising year by year in recent years. As the country with the closest ties to China along the Southeast Asian Belt and Road Initiative, Thailand has introduced various preferential policies to attract Chinese students. Studying in Thailand has become the new favorite for studying abroad and the top choice for students from working families.

China attaches great importance to and supports educational cooperation and exchanges between China and Thailand. As early as 2007, the Ministry of Education, P.R. China and the Ministry of Education of the Kingdom of Thailand signed the "Agreement on Mutual Recognition of Higher Education Degrees and Degrees," which was renewed in 2010 to jointly promote mutual recognition of higher education degrees and credits between the two countries, facilitating further study and further education for students in each country.

I believe many parents and students have developed a strong interest in studying in Thailand. Here are the reasons for studying in Thailand.

1. Those who want to expand their career plans

After completing the 2+2 international undergraduate program in Thailand, students have more options for their future career plans—whether to pursue further studies or employment, whether to stay in Thailand or return to China, or to go to other countries.

2. Those who wish to pursue a master's or doctoral degree.

Talent is the primary resource for economic and social development. With economic globalization, the social status and income of highly educated individuals will continue to rise. Studying in Thailand for a 2+2 international undergraduate program will broaden your horizons and enhance your desire to improve your academic qualifications.

3. Those who want to connect with international talents

As a renowned university in Thailand, Udon Thani Raja University offers opportunities to meet international students from various countries during its studies, build good friendships, and while improving its social skills, it will undoubtedly become one of the most valuable social assets in life.

4. Those who want to experience international culture

Thailand is a country of immigrants, with a culture influenced by many nations. It values tolerance and tranquility, a love of peace, and multicultural harmony, embodying the magnanimous, unconventional, all-encompassing, and vibrant spirit of "the elephant is invisible"!

5. Those who want to learn English well

The 2+2 international undergraduate program in Thailand involves two years of intensive English training in China, taught in English in Thailand, immersing you in an English environment all day, which can quickly improve your English listening and speaking skills, and after graduation, you can proficiently apply English.

6. Those dissatisfied with the admitted institution

Students who are dissatisfied with their admitted institution or major and drift through university for a few years are better off going to Thailand for a 2+2 international undergraduate program, choosing a major they love, gaining a new starting point in life, reaching new heights, and achieving new leaps.

7. Poor college entrance exam results

Unsatisfactory college entrance exam results are not due to students' low IQ, but rather due to lax learning or difficulty adapting to certain subjects at certain stages. Thailand's lively and open international teaching model brings new motivation for students to learn.

8. Severe subject bias

Students with serious subject imbalances find it difficult to strengthen their weak subjects, resulting in a disadvantage in overall score competition and preventing them from getting into their ideal universities. Choosing to study in Thailand, then, is a way to bypass weak areas and achieve a turnaround success.

9. Students from working-class families who want to study abroad

For working-class students from working families who dream of studying abroad, studying in Thailand is a wise choice. The tuition cost is only one-fifth that of Western countries, and living expenses are comparable to second-tier cities in China, saving 500,000 to 800,000 yuan in study abroad costs.

谈到出国留学，人们一般会首先想到欧美等国家,这些国家留学入学门槛都很高，要求学生具备非常好的外语水平，能够通过托福、雅思等考试。另外，高达几十万、上百万的留学费用也使很多普通家庭望而却步，产生了“出国留学是富裕家庭学子的特权”的传统思想。

随着我国“一带一路”战略实施的逐渐深入，近几年赴泰国留学的热度逐年上升。泰国作为“一带一路”东南亚沿线与中国关系最为良好密切的国家，出台了各种优惠政策吸引中国留学生。泰国留学已成为当下出国留学的新宠，成为工薪家庭学子出国留学的首选。

我国对中泰两国的教育合作交流非常重视和支持，早在2007年就签订了《中华人民共和国教育部与泰王国教育部关于互相承认高等教育学历和学位的协定》，并于2010年续签，共同促进两国高等教育学历学位互认，推动两国高等教育的学分互认，便于两国学生在彼此国家进一步学习深造。

相信，很多家长和学生对赴泰国留学都产生了浓厚的兴趣，那么为您总结留学泰国理由如下。

1、想要拓展职业规划的

赴泰2+2国际本科完成学业后，学生对于未来的职业规划，是准备升学深造还是就业，是准备留泰、回国，还是去其他国家，选择余地更大，前途必将越走越宽广。

2、想攻读硕士博士的

人才是经济社会发展的第一资源，随着经济全球化发展，高学历者的社会地位和经济收入将变得越来越高。赴泰2+2国际本科将拓展你的视野，增强你提升学历的欲望。

3、想结交国际英才的

乌隆他尼皇家大学作为泰国著名皇家大学，学习过程中可以结交各国留学生，建立良好的友谊关系，在提升自我社交能力的同时，必将成为人生最宝贵的社会财富。

4、想接触国际文化的

泰国是一个移民国家，文化受到多国影响，崇尚忍让安宁、爱好和平和多元文化和谐共生，具有泱泱大度、不拘一格、包罗万象、生机无限的“大象无形”的精神包容！

5、想要学好英语的

赴泰2+2国际本科采取在国内两年英语强化训练，在泰国英语授课，整天沉浸在英语环境当中，可以迅速提高英语听力和口语能力，毕业后可以娴熟应用英语。

6、录取院校不满意的

对录取院校或专业不满意的学生，浑浑噩噩在大学混几年，不如赴泰2+2国际本科，选择自己喜欢的专业，获得人生新起点，开启人生新高度，实现人生新跨越。

7、高考成绩不理想的

高考成绩不理想，并非学生的智商低，而是由于某个阶段学习放松或不适应某科学习造成的。泰国活泼开放的国际化教学模式，会带给学生新的学习驱动力。

8、严重偏科的

严重偏科的学生很难将弱科补强，导致在总分的竞争上处于弱势，不能考上理想的大学。选择去泰国留学，则是一条可以绕过弱项、实现弯道超车的逆袭之路。

9、工薪家庭学子想留学的

对于有出国留学梦想的工薪家庭学子来讲，赴泰国留学是明智之举。学习费用仅为欧美国家的1/5，生活费用相当于国内二线城市，可节省50-80万的留学成本。
