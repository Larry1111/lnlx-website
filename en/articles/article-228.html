Harry Watt University is the eighth oldest university in the UK. Its predecessor was the School of Art Edinburgh, founded in 1821, named in honor of George Hull, treasurer to King James of England in the 16th century, and James Watt, the great inventor and engineer of the 18th century. Currently, the university has 17,000 students, including 10,000 from 150 countries around the world, making it one of the most international universities in the UK. The university's graduate employment rate also maintains a strong record.

The university is located in Edinburgh, situated on the shore of a lake surrounded by volcanoes. The city has 16,000 buildings renowned for architecture and history. Additionally, the international arts festival held every August nearly doubles the city's population. At one of the world's largest arts festivals, people can enjoy a wide variety of theater and dance programs, some inspired by student creations and others by cutting-edge professional companies.

Academic strengths

Heriot-Watt University places great emphasis on engineering and natural sciences. Among all higher education institutions in Scotland, it ranks first for its numerous top engineering disciplines. Its Mineral and Mining Engineering has achieved a 5* rating, while its Applied Mathematics and Built Environment have also achieved 5 levels. Additionally, it offers courses in petroleum industry, international banking, finance, practical mathematics, as well as brewing and distillation. Heriot-Watt University has top-tier research centers in many fields such as optoelectronics/lasers, civil engineering, marine/petroleum science, and information technology. The university's research is strongly supported by industry. Research revenue accounts for 45% of the university's total income, ranking among the top among more than 100 business schools in the UK. In 1996, its per capita research income ranked second in the UK, surpassing Oxford, Cambridge, and Imperial College London.

Notable majors: Science, Business, Engineering, Textile Design.

Admission requirements for undergraduate certificate programs: IELTS 5.0; TOEFL 61; Currently in senior year or graduated from high school.

Admission requirements for master's foundation courses: IELTS 5.0; Bachelor's degree certificate.

Language course start dates: Four times a year, in January, April, June, and September.

赫瑞·瓦特大学是英国第八所最古老的大学，它的前身是爱丁堡艺术学校，始创于1821年，因纪念十六世纪英国国王詹姆士的财务官乔治赫尔和十八世纪伟大的发明家和工程师詹姆士瓦特而得名。目前，该校共有17000名学生，包括来自世界150个国家的10000名学生，使该校成为英国最国际化的大学之一。大学的毕业生就业率也保持着良好的记录。

大学位于爱丁堡市，坐落在火山群环绕的大湖边。在这个城市中，有16,000座在建筑学和历史学上享有盛名的建筑物。此外，每年8月间的国际艺术节使城市人口剧增近乎一倍。在这个世界最大规模之一的艺术节上，人们可以尽情欣赏各式各样的戏剧和舞蹈节目，有的来自于学生的创作，有的出于行业前卫的专业公司的作品。

学术优势

赫瑞·瓦特大学十分注重工程学和自然科学学科。在所有苏格兰的高等学府中，这所大学因拥有为数众多的顶级工程学科而名列榜首。它的矿物和采矿工程学达到了5*级，与此同时，它的应用数学和建筑环境学也达到了5级。另外，它还开设了石油工业、国际银行、金融学、实用数学以及酿造、蒸馏等专业课程。赫瑞·瓦特大学在许多领域诸如光电子学/激光、土木工程、海洋/石油科学和信息技术领域拥有一流的研究中心。学校的科研受到了工业界的大力支持。其科研收入占学校总收入的45%，在全英一百多所商校中名列前茅。其人均科研收入曾在1996年在全英排名第二，超过了牛津、剑桥以及帝国理工学院。

知名专业：科学、商业、工程、纺织品设计。

本科证书课程入学要求：雅思5.0分；托福61分；高三在读或高中毕业。

硕士预科课程入学要求：雅思5.0；本科学位证。

语言课程开学日期：每年1、4、6、9月四次开学。

www.hw.ac.uk
