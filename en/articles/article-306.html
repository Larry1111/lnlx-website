Types of scholarships at American universities

Published on 2011-08-16·Source: Liaoning Overseas Study Service Co., Ltd.·Viewed 10,543 times

What we commonly refer to as scholarships, generally referred to as "Financial Aid" at American universities, can be divided into three categories.

Non-Service Scholarship

Scholarships, scholarships, tuition waivers, etc

Service Assistantship

Teaching Assistantship, Research Assistantship

School Loan

Generally aimed at American students

Main types of financial aid available to international students studying in the U.S

Scholarship Fellowship

Financial aid is the highest amount and most competitive non-service financial aid. Generally, receiving a financial aid is equivalent to a full scholarship, waiving tuition, accommodation, insurance, and living expenses, and also providing some personal expenses. To receive a full financial aid, students must be among the best in their country.

Scholarship

The scholarship can be awarded to students in a specified amount, or it can be a form of tuition and fee waiver. The chances of receiving a Scholarship are higher than a Fellowship, but the amount is smaller, and it is an honorary reward.

Tuition reduction Tuition—Waiver

U.S. university tuition is divided into in-state tuition, out-of-state tuition, and international student tuition. Generally, for outstanding international students, schools can charge in-state tuition fees, i.e., in-state tuition reductions or waivers.

Research Assistaship & Teaching Assistantship

Generally, it is a service salary; students receiving this funding must engage in certain assistant research and teaching positions to receive it.

Dormitory Waivers and Off-Campus Housing Savings on or Off-Campus Housing Credit or Saving

On-campus accommodation exemption means the school provides a certain amount of free accommodation; Off-campus accommodation savings refer to the cost saved by the school by arranging relatively cheaper off-campus accommodation.

On-Campus Job

A type of service salary. USCIS stipulates that international students can legally earn income by working on campus, with a maximum of 20 hours per week. For example, working in cafeterias, bookstores, libraries, or health clubs, or helping out in administrative offices.

我们通常所说的奖学金，一般美国大学称为“经济资助”(Financial Aid)，可分为三类。

非服务性奖学金(Non-Service Scholarship)

助学金(Fellowship)、奖学金(Scholarship)、学费减免(Tuition Waiver)等

服务性奖学金(Service Assistantship)

助教(Teaching Assistantship)、助研(Research Assistantship)

学校贷款(Loan)

一般针对美国学生

留美国际学生可获经济资助的主要种类

助学金Fellowship

助学金是一种金额最高，竞争最激烈的非服务性财政资助。一般情况下，如果获得助学金就相当于全奖，免除学杂费，住宿费，保险费和生活费外，还给学生一定的个人消费费用。要得到全额助学金，学生必须是所在国家最优秀的学生之一。

奖学金Scholarship

奖学金的形式可以是规定一定的金额数量颁发给学生，也可以是一种学杂费的减免。获得Scholarship的几率要比Fellowship大，但金额要少一些，是一种荣誉性的奖励。

学费减免Tuition—Waiver

美国大学的学费分为本州内学费，州外学费和国际学生学费。一般对优秀的国际学生，学校可以按照本州内学费收取，即减免成州内学费。

助研和助教Research Assistaship＆Teaching Assistantship

一般是服务性薪金，获得该资助的学生必须从事一定的助理研究和教学工作才能获得。

枝内住宿减免和校外住宿节省On or Off-Campus Housing Credit or Saving

校内住宿减免是学校提供一定的住宿免费；校外住宿节省是学校通过安排校外相对便宜的住宿而节约的费用。

校内工作On—Campus Job

服务性薪金的一种。美国移民局规定，国际学生可在校内合法工作获得收入，每周最多可工作20小时。如在食堂、书店、图书馆或健康俱乐部打工，或在行政办公室帮忙。
