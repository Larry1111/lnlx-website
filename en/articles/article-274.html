Nagano Foreign Language Institute is located in Ueda City, Nagano Prefecture. It is an excellent school with over 20 years of experience in foreign language teaching. Its Japanese language education for foreigners began 10 years ago and has been rated as an excellent school due to its rigorous teaching and excellent management. Although the school is located in Nagano Prefecture, transportation is very convenient, with the Shinkansen taking only one hour to Tokyo.

Nagano Prefecture has the best ecological environment quality and is the most livable prefecture in Japan, but its population has consistently declined, resulting in a shortage of labor and relatively more job and study opportunities.

Nagano Prefectural Foreign Language Institute is well-equipped, offers a good learning environment, and offers well-qualified dormitories. The school offers distinctive guidance for students on their further studies, and many students choose from here to pursue their ideal universities.

Registration Requirements:

1. High school, technical secondary, vocational school under 22 years old

2. College diploma, under 24 years old

3. Big Ben: Under 26 years old

长野外语学院位于长野县上田市，是一所具有20余年外语教学经验的优秀学校，其对外国人的日语教育始于10年前，由于其严谨的教学和出色的管理被评为优良校。该校虽然位于长野县，但交通十分方便，乘“新干线”到东京只要1个小时。

长野县是日本生态环境质量最好、最适于居住的县，但是人口却一直是负增长，因此劳动力不足，就职机会、半工半读机会相对较多。
