Saint Petersburg State University of Finance and Economics


[Introduction]: Saint Petersburg State University of Finance and Economics is recognized as the best center for finance teaching and research in Russia. The institution offers numerous suitable finance majors for preparatory students, undergraduates, master's, and doctoral students, providing a large faculty base.

School Introduction

Saint Petersburg State University of Economics (Санкт-Петербургский государственный экономический университет) includes 39 disciplines, 7 full-time teaching departments, 1 part-time teaching department, correspondence courses, preparatory departments, and a higher economics school (a place for training and improving economists and managers), as well as international education programs. The school has a broad foundational area necessary for successful training and research work. The university's library departments include: internet room, computer room, telecommunications center, language center, sports field, student club, printing house, cafeteria and cafeteria, and outpatient department. Undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students who need to live on campus provide student accommodation facilities.

Saint Petersburg State University of Finance and Economics was founded in 1930 and is located in Saint Petersburg, Russia. Saint Petersburg State University of Finance and Economics is one of Russia's earliest renowned universities specializing in finance and economics. Saint Petersburg State University of Finance and Economics has more than 13,000 students enrolled and more than 1,000 faculty members, including 23 doctoral students, 32 graduate students, 31 professors, 56 associate professors, and 12 teaching assistants. Under the direction of the Russian Ministry of Education, Saint Petersburg State University of Finance and Economics offers the following programs based on full-time education: Finance and Credit, Accounting, Analysis and Auditing, Statistics, World Economy, Labor Economics, Human Resource Management, Economics, National Economy, E-commerce (Commerce), Logistics and Supply Chain Management, Marketing, Social and Cultural Services and Tourism, Mathematical Methods in Economics, Economics with Applied Computer Science, Translation, and Law. Saint Petersburg State University of Finance and Economics also has a dedicated student office, medical center, football field, swimming pool, and student dormitories to assist international students. This allows students to rest and train well after their intense studies. The university ranks first nationwide in the Russian ranking of economic institutions.

Accommodation is $100/month

Medical insurance costs 3,800 rubles per year

Other courses at the college Foreign Language Integrated The teaching process mainly ensures high departmental standards and places greater emphasis on creating opportunities to discover students' individual talents. The curriculum includes compulsory courses (accounting for 70% of teaching time) and elective courses (requiring consideration of students and departments, accounting for 30% of teaching time).

In the first two years, students follow a unified curriculum, and after systematic general education, students choose departments and courses tailored for future careers, and are required to pass exams. The two vocational programs are eligible for bachelor's degrees in fields such as economics, statistics, business administration, and trade. In the fifth year, students undergo in-depth vocational training and complete a graduation essay on economic skills. Graduates who love scientific work can continue their education (according to their personal plans) and obtain a Master of Science certificate. Saint Petersburg University of Finance and Economics has extensive international exchanges, which run throughout teaching, research, skill improvement, consulting, sales, and other activities. Since 1948, our university has begun training international students. Over 45 years, about 3,000 students from 70 countries worldwide have received higher education, passed the Associate Doctoral and PhD defenses, and improved their professional skills. In recent years, the University of Finance and Economics has actively developed exchanges with universities around the world, especially with universities in France, Germany, Sweden, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom, the United States, North Korea, China, Vietnam, and other countries.

School Features

The university is also one of the largest economic research centers in the Russian Federation, specializing in the following specialties: macroeconomics, economic growth theory and welfare systems, finance, banking and insurance management, sales, auditing, as well as consulting, accounting and statistics, economic judgment, human resource management, mathematical economics models, economic informatics, and the computerization of economics.

Teaching achievements

Saint Petersburg State University of Economics is one of the most prestigious universities in Russia and is recognized as the best economics institution in the country. Its graduates hold very prominent positions in the scientific community, state agencies, as well as politics and business. Scholars at the university provide consultations to the government, parliament, and St. Petersburg administration, and work closely with entrepreneurial institutions. They also publish scholars' works, textbooks, and specialized academic works, and have a doctoral program for graduate students and 14 committees awarding associate and doctoral degrees. Additionally, for organizing and conducting research, the university has research centers, problem laboratories, and many small exploration and creative groups. Students have a rich extracurricular life. Sports activities such as swimming, light competition, shooting, boxing, university rowing, basketball, volleyball, and chess are very popular. The university student club plays an important role in leisure organizations, where students can showcase their talents in choirs, drama performance groups, dance troupes, and performing arts groups.

Additional note: Because this university is the best finance and economics university in the former Soviet Union, and economics is currently the most popular major in Russia, its fees are relatively high.

圣彼得堡国立财经大学


【导读】：圣彼得堡国立财经大学是在俄罗斯公认最好的财经教学与研究的中心。院校为预科、本科、硕士、博士学生设立了众多适合的财经类专业，提供庞大的师资力量。

学校简介

圣彼得堡国立经济大学(Санкт-Петербургский государственный экономический университет)包括 39 个学科， 7 个全日制教学的系， 1 个部分时间教学的系，函授课程，预科系和一个高等经济学校(这个是训练和提高经济师和经理的地方)，以及国际教育程序。 学校有一个广阔的为训练和研究工作的成功所必需的基础部分。 学校的图书馆部门包括：互联网室、计算机室、电讯中心、语言中心、运动场、学生俱乐部、印刷厂、食堂和自助餐厅、门诊部。 本科生、研究生 和 博士生需要在校住宿的，学校提供学校的学生住宿园地。

圣彼得堡国立财经大学成立于1930年，位于俄罗斯的圣彼得堡。圣彼得堡国立财经大学是俄罗斯早期著名的财经类大学。圣彼得堡国立财经大学拥有在校学生13000余人，在校教职员工个1000余人，其中博士生23位，研究生32位，教授31位，副教授56位以及12名助教。圣彼得堡国立财经大学在俄罗斯教育部的指示下以全日制教育为基础开设以下专业：金融与信贷，会计，分析和审计，统计，世界经济，劳动经济学，人力资源管理，经济学，国民经济，电子商务(商业)，物流与供应链管理，市场营销，社会文化服务和旅游，经济学中的数学方法，应用计算机科学的经济学，翻译，法学。圣彼得堡国立财经大学还设有专门为留学生提供帮助的留学生办公室，医疗中心，足球场，游泳馆，学生宿舍。使得学生们在紧张的学习之余，得到充分的休息和锻练。该校在俄罗斯经济类院校全国排行榜上名列第一。

3. 经济和管理系 生产管理 工业生产和改革的经济 经济控制论和数学模型 经济方案 企业和经济和计划

8. 学院的其它课程 外语 综合 教学过程中主要保证以高标准系教学，更注重创造条件来发掘学生的个人才能，教学计划包括必修课程(占教学时间的 70% )和选修课程(需照顾到学生和系里的情况，占教学时间的 30% )。

前两年按统一的教学计划教学，系统的普及教育结束后，学生们选系以及选取针对未来职业的课程，并要以考试为基础，按下来的两个职业教育，便有权获得诸如经济学、统计学、经营学、贸易学这些研究方向的学士学位。第五年进行深入的职业专门训练完成掌握经济技能的毕业作文。对于热爱科学工作的毕业生来讲可以继续接受教育(根据个人的计划)，并获得科学硕士证书。 圣彼得堡财经大学有着广泛的国际交流，这些交流贯穿整个教学、科研、提高技能、咨询、销售等活动的始终。从 1948 年起我校开始培养外国留学生， 45 年来共有来自世界 70 个国家的 3 千名左右的外国留学生接受了高等教育，通过了副博士、博士答辩，提高了专业技能。近年来财经大学积极发展同世界各国大学的交流，特别发展了同法国、德国、瑞典、荷兰、英国、美国、朝鲜、中国、越南等国大学的交流。

学校特色

学校也是俄联邦最大的经济科研中心之一，它有以下专业研究： 宏观经济学、经济增长理论及福利制度、金融、银行及保险业管理学、销售学、审计学以及其咨询、财会和会统、经济论断、人类资源管理、数学经济学模式、经济信息学及经济的电算化。

教学成就

圣彼得堡国立经济大学是俄罗斯最负盛名的大学之一，被公认为是俄罗斯最好的经济类院校。该校毕业生在科学界、国家机关以及政治和商业界都占据着非常显著的地位。学校的学者向政府、议会、圣彼得堡管理机关提供咨询，密切与企业家机构合作。还出版学者们的著作，出版课本及专题学术著作，并设有研究生 班的 博士生班以及 14 个授予副博士、博士学位的委员会，另外为组织和进行科研工作学校还设有科研中心、问题试验室及许多小型探索创作小组。 学生有丰富的课余生活，体育运动有游泳、轻竞技运动、射击、拳击、大学生赛艇、篮球、排球、象棋等颇受欢迎，在休闲组织中大学生俱乐部发挥着重要作用，学生们可在学生合唱团、戏剧表演团、舞蹈团、舞台艺术等团体中尽显才华。

补充说明：因为该校是前苏联最好的财经类大学，加上经济类学科是当前俄罗斯最热门的专业，所以其收费定的比较高。
