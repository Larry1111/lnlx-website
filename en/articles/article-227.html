The University of Leicester is an ancient university located in the eastern part of central England, with its main campus only one mile from the city center. Founded in 1921, it ranks among the top 20 in the UK in overall strength and is globally renowned in fields such as DNA, genetic technology, and spatial research. Its Mass Communication Research Centre is the UK's first academic institution dedicated to studying media and communication.

Leicester is located in central England, just a 75-minute train ride from London, and is a vibrant and friendly international metropolis. With a population of about 300,000, students make up about 12% of the city's population during their studies. Leicester is one of the greenest cities in the UK, with many green spaces and dedicated bike lanes. Recently, the city has also been rated as one of the best shopping cities in the UK. The nearest airports to the school are Heathrow in London, East Midlands in Nottingham, and Birmingham International Airport in Birmingham.

According to evaluations by the Higher Education Foundation of England, the University of Leicester has outstanding teaching and research achievements across all departments, receiving the highest ratings in research evaluations in biological sciences, cell biology, pharmacy, chemistry, engineering, geology, geography, astronomy, architecture, economics and society, education, English, history, art history, law, modern languages, politics, international relations, social work, and sociology. The university currently has over 16,000 students, 10% of whom come from more than 80 European countries, with 6% international students.
The university maintains solid exchanges with China in fields such as applied linguistics, management, mass communication, and museum studies, attracting many students to pursue master's programs.

Admission requirements for undergraduate foundation courses: Must be at least 17 years old, IELTS 5.0, currently in senior high school or having graduated from high school.

Language course start dates: Four times a year, in January, April, June, and September.

www.le.ac.uk

莱斯特大学是位于英格兰中部以东的一所古老大学，主校园离市中心只有一英里。成立于1921年，其综合实力居英国前20位，在DNA 基因遗传技术及空间研究等领域享誉全球。它的大众传播研究中心为英国第一个研究媒体与传播的学术机构。

莱斯特城市位于英格兰中部，离伦敦75分钟的火车车程，是一个亮丽友善的国际都市。人口约30万，学习期间学生人数占都市人口数约12%。莱斯特是英国绿化最好的城市之一，许多绿地空间及脚踏车专用道。最近该市也被评鉴为英国最棒的购物都市之一。离学校最近的机场有伦敦的Heathrow，诺丁汉的East Midlands 以及伯明翰的Birmingham国际机场。

在英格兰高等教育基金会所做的评价中，莱斯特大学的各科系都有颇高的教学与研究成绩，在生物科学、细胞生物、药学、化学、工程、地质、地理、天文学、建筑、经济学与社会、教育、英语、历史、艺术史、法律、现代语言、政治、国际关系、社工、社会学都获得了研究评价中的最高评价。该校目前有16,000多名学生，其中有10%来自于欧洲80多个国家，国际学生比例6%。
学校在应用语言学、管理、大众传播和博物馆研究等领域与中国有稳固的交流，有许多学生前来攻读硕士课程。

本科预科课程入学要求：年满17周岁，雅思5.0分，高三在读或高中毕业。

语言课程开学日期：每年1、4、6、9月四次开学。
