Saint Petersburg State University of Architecture and Engineering


[Introduction]: Saint Petersburg State University of Architecture and Engineering is the oldest institution in Russian architecture, with nearly two hundred years of history. Saint Petersburg State University of Architectural Engineering is a national higher education institution, federally owned and directly under the Russian Higher Education Commission.

Introduction to the Institution

Saint Petersburg State University of Architecture and Engineering is the oldest higher education institution in Russian architecture. Kenda was founded in 1832 according to the instructions of Emperor Nicholas I. Within Russia, it is also known as Saint Petersburg University of Architectural Design, a national higher education institution under federal ownership, directly under the Russian Higher Education Commission. The school and dormitories are located in the center of Saint Petersburg, not far south of the Winter Palace, Peter's Fortress, Kazan Cathedral, and others.

Over its 200-year history, Saint Petersburg State University of Architectural Engineering has changed its name several times: Civil Engineering School (1842), Civil Engineering Academy (1882), Leningrad Municipal Architecture Institute (1930), Leningrad Institute of Architectural Engineering (1941), and Saint Petersburg Institute of Architectural Engineering (1992). Since June 21, 1993, it has been using its current name. Kenda is a national higher education institution, federally owned and directly under the Russian Higher Education Committee.

Professional recommendations

School of Architecture: Bachelor's and Master's degree architects, expert architects, urban planning, and economics.

School of Civil Engineering: Bachelor's and master's degrees in architecture, civil buildings, building materials, components and structural products, restoration and reconstruction of architectural heritage, architectural planning.

School of Environmental Engineering and Conservation: Applied mathematics, heat and gas supply and ventilation, water supply and treatment.

Engineering and socio-ecological systems: Ventilation and environmental protection, rational use and conservation of water resources,

School of Automotive Transportation Engineering: Transportation Development Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Automotive and Automotive Transport Lifting, Construction and Highway Machinery and Equipment Automobiles, Highway Construction Machinery and Equipment Maintenance 240100 Transportation Management, Traffic Management Highway and Airport Engineering, Bridges and Traffic Tunnels, Construction Machinery and Automation.

Department of Economics and Management: Environmental economics, construction economics and management, transportation economics, industrial management, real estate and real estate, real estate records.

Undergraduate Study Period:4 years, and 5 years for architectural specialists.

Graduate students: Integrated bachelor's and master's programs in architecture require 7 years, while other programs require 6 years.

PhD:3 years [3].

Each year is divided into two semesters: classes start in September and February respectively.

Tuition fees

Foundation Course 1 year Tuition fees:$4,000/year (design), $3,500/year (other);

Undergraduate 4~5 years Tuition:3000~5000 USD/year;

Master's 2 years Tuition:4000~5000 USD/year;

Accommodation:$60-80/month;

Medical Insurance:$100/year;

Registration Fee:$50 per academic year.

圣彼得堡国立建筑工程大学


【导读】：圣彼得堡国立建筑工程大学是俄罗斯建筑方面最古老的院校，建校近两百年。圣彼得堡国立建筑工程大学为国立高等院校，联邦所有制，直属于俄罗斯高等教育委员会。

院校简介

圣彼得堡国立建筑工程大学是俄罗斯建筑方面最古老的高等学府。建大按皇帝尼古拉一世指示始建于1832年。在俄罗斯国内又称之为圣彼得堡建筑设计大学，为国立高等院校，联邦所有制，直属于俄罗斯高等教育委员会。学校和学校宿舍坐落在圣彼得堡的市中心，冬宫、彼得要塞、喀山大教堂等南面不远处。

在200年的历史中，圣彼得堡国立建筑工程大学数次易名：土建学校(1842年)，民用工程师学院(1882年〕，列宁格勒市政建筑学院(1930年)，列宁格勒建筑工程学院(1941年)，圣彼得堡建筑工程学院(1992年)从1993年6月21日起开始用今天这个名子。建大为国立高等院校，联邦所有制，直属于俄罗斯高等教育委员会。

专业推荐

建筑学院：学士级和硕士级建筑师、专家级建筑师、都市规划和经济学。

民用工程学院：建筑学士和硕士、民用建筑、建筑材料，构件和结构产品、建筑学遗产的恢复和重建、建筑规划。

环境工程学和保护学院：应用数学、热和气体供应和通风、水供应和处理。

工程和社会生态系统：通风和环境保护、合理使用和保护水资源、

汽车运输工程学学院：运输开发 学士和硕士、汽车和汽车运输提升，建筑和公路机械和设备汽车，公路建筑机械和设备维修240100 运输管理、交通管理公路和机场工程、桥梁和交通隧道、建筑机械和自动化。

经济学和管理学系：环境的经济学、建设经济学和管理、运输经济学、工业管理、房产和不动产、地产记录。

本科学习时间：4年，建筑专家类5年。

研究生：建筑类本硕连读需7年，其它本硕连读6年。

博士：3年[3] 。

每年分二个学期：分别从9月和2月开课。

本科 4~5年 学费：3000~5000美元/年；

硕士 2年 学费：4000~5000美元/年；
