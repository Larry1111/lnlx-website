After completing the French Study Abroad Evaluation interview, you will schedule a time to submit your study abroad visa application on the website of the French Education Service Center (Campusfrance). According to the scheduled time, bring the following French study abroad visa documents to Visa France to submit your visa application:

The documents for a French student visa include:

1. Proof of the interview for Campusfrance's study abroad evaluation in France

2. French student visa application form with a photo attached, along with a passport-sized white background photo;

3. Original and copy of passport, original and copy of old passport (if you have changed passports);

4. Original ID card, its photocopy, and translation;

5. French university admission letter and language admission letter;

6. Housing certificate (original and one copy):

If you have already rented the property: lease agreement

If it is free accommodation: proof of residence issued by the homeowner and French identification (ID card, residence permit, or passport)

If it is University Town housing: a housing certificate issued by C.R.O.U.S

Other conditions: clearly and intact explanation of information;

7. Proof of funds:

Bank deposit certificate (original and one photocopy)

Proof of parents' income: proof of employment (and its translation), and at least six months' worth of salary account payment records

8. Notarized birth certificate (original and one copy);

9. Students over 28 years old must provide proof of insurance coverage for their legal studies;

10. Original and copy of the French university registration payment certificate (students enrolled in private schools must provide this proof);

11. Copy of proof of extension of French university enrollment date (if the start date has passed, it must be provided);

法国留学评价面试完毕后，将在法国教育效劳中心（Campusfrance）的网站上预定递交留学签证申请的时间，并依据预定时间携带以下法国留学签证资料前往Visa France提交您的签证申请：

法国留学签证资料包括:

1. Campusfrance的法国留学评价面试的证明

2. 贴有照片的法国留学签证申请表，以及护照规格白底照片一张；

3. 护照原件及复印件，旧护照原件及复印件（假如换过护照的话）；

4. 身份证原件，及其复印件和翻译件；

5. 法国大学录取通知书及言语录取通知书；

6. 住房证明（原件及复印件一份）：

假如是已租住房：房屋租约

假如是免费住房：房主出具的住房证明，及其法国身份证明（身份证，居留证或者护照）

假如是大学城住房：C.R.O.U.S. 出具的住房证明

其他状况：完好分明的阐明资料；

7. 资金证明：

银行存款证明（原件及复印件一份）

父母的收入证明：工作证明（及其翻译件），以及至少六个月的工资账户的发薪记载

8. 出生公证书（原件及复印件一份）；

9. 28岁以上的学生需求提供在法求学期间的保险证明；

10. 法国大学注册交费证明原件及复印件（注册私立学校的学生必需提供这个证明）；

11.法国大学入学日期的延期证明复印件（如开学日期已过，需提供）；
