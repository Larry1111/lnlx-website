Saint Petersburg State Pedagogical University


[Introduction]: Saint Petersburg State Pedagogical University was founded in 1797, with a history of over 200 years, and is a world-renowned pedagogical university. It is the oldest pedagogical university in Russia and the only one bearing the Russian national name. It is recognized as a leading figure in education both in Russia and worldwide. Universities and scientific centers from China, the United States, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Finland, the Netherlands, Norway, and Hong Kong cooperate with the Pedagogical University.

School Introduction

Chinese Name: Saint Petersburg State Pedagogical University

Founded:1797

Location: St. Petersburg

Category: National universities

There are 500 international students studying at the school. Many Chinese students study in the Department of Language and Literature, some are studying Russian literature, others are studying languages, such as German, French, and English, or one of these languages, treating the other as a second foreign study. By adding Russian, they can master the foreign languages of three countries to adapt to the era of globalization. Additionally, the psychology major at the university is a world-class strength, but it is currently a lacking discipline in China. Among the 110,000 graduates, there are many Soviet and Russian heroes and labor heroes, and there are 2,000 honorary teachers.

Academic Programs

Currently, the university consists of 20 departments and 100 departments.

Department of Biology: Biology major.

Department of Chemistry: Chemistry major.

Department of Mathematics: majors such as Mathematics and Computer Science.

Foreign Language Department: English, German, French, Spanish, and other languages.

圣彼得堡国立师范大学


【导读】：圣彼得堡国立师范大学创立时间：1797年，已经有200多年的历史，是世界著名的师范大学。是俄罗斯历史最悠久的，也是唯一冠有俄罗斯国家名称的师范大学。它被公认在教育界是俄罗斯和世界的领头人角色。 有中国、美国、英国、法国、德国、芬兰、荷兰、挪威和香港等国家和地区和大学和科学中心与师范大学合作。

学校简介

中文名：圣彼得堡国立师范大学

创办时间：1797年

所属地区：圣彼得堡

类 别：国立大学

有500名留学生在学校学习。其中有不少中国学生在语言和文学系学习，有进修俄罗斯文学的，也有进修语言的，如进修德语、法语和英语，或进修这几种语言中的一种，把另一种做为二外来学，这样加上俄语，使自己掌握三国外语以适应国际化的时代。另外，该校的心理学专业是世界强项，也是我国目前缺乏的学科。在110000毕业生中有大量的苏联、俄罗斯英雄，劳动英雄，有2000名荣誉教师。
