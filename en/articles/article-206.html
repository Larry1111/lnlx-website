1 How to apply for expedited visas?

This consulate only provides expedited issuance to visa applicants attending international conferences. Applicants may fax specific matters to the consulate.

2. Which types of visas require advance appointment?

Only applicants for international marriage visas need to make an appointment in advance; other visa categories do not require a reservation.

3. What are the office hours of the Consular Department?

Summer (March–October): 9:00 AM–12:00 PM, 2:00–6:00 PM (Closing time 11:00 AM; Afternoon: 5:00 PM)

Winter (November–February): 9:00 AM–12:00 PM, 2:00–5:00 PM (Closing time 11:00 AM; Afternoon: 4:00 PM)

Visa and General Matters: Application — morning; Pickup — afternoon.

4. How many working days are required for the issuance of various types of visas?

Visa Category: Required working days (excluding weekends and public holidays)

General business: 5 working days
International conference: 5 working days
More than four times: 3 working days
Multiple reversals for 5 working days
Individual tourist visa 5 working days
Group tourist visa: 3 working days
Individual visa for tour groups: 3 working days
Family visit visa 6 working days
International marriage visa: 5 working days
Official visa: 4 working days
Reverse visa processing takes 3 working days

1、如何申请签证加急？

本领事馆只对参加国际性会议的签证申请人给予加急签发，申请人可把具体事项传真到本领事馆。

2、哪类签证需要提前预约？

只有国际结婚签证的申请人需要提前预约，其他类别的签证无需预定。

3、领事部的办公时间为？

夏季(3月-10月): 上午 9:00-12:00 下午 2:00-6:00 (闭馆时间上午 11:00; 下午:5:00)

冬季(11月-2月): 上午 9:00-12:00 下午 2:00-5:00 (闭馆时间上午 11:00; 下午:4:00)

签证及一般事务：申请——上午；领取——下午。
