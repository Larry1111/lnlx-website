Montpellier is the sixth largest city in France, located on the Mediterranean Sea, and is a famous historical and cultural city as well as a famous tourist destination. It has a mild climate, a population of over 200,000, and is renowned for its numerous historical sites, beautiful scenery, and simple local customs. It is known as the "City of Sunshine." It is also one of the cities in France with the highest concentration of tertiary institutions after Paris. The world's earliest medical university was founded in this city in 1289.

Montpellier is like a vast university town, occupied by dozens of tertiary institutions. Among public universities, Montpellier I, II, and III are the most famous. The I, I, and III are renowned for medicine, the second is renowned for its natural sciences, and the third is renowned for its deep foundation in the humanities.

Montpellier III University (Paul Valéry University) was founded in 1421 and is one of France's most renowned national universities. The university is located in the center of Montpellier, with extremely convenient transportation. There are more than 20,000 students enrolled. The university covers an area of 70,000 square meters and features several large teaching, office, and research buildings, as well as large libraries and museums, all of which are well-equipped. The university has 10 research institutes and admits nearly 20,000 students annually, including 2,200 international students from over 120 countries worldwide. The university also has branch campuses in Nîmes and Béze. It offers over 200 bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs covering natural sciences, literature, and arts, and enjoys a prestigious reputation among French universities. Montpellier III University is renowned for its School of Language and Culture, offering courses in English, French, German, Russian, Spanish, and Chinese, with over 3,000 international students from 50 different countries engaged in teaching and research in languages from around the world.

蒙彼利埃市是法国第六大城市，它濒临地中海，是法国历史文化名城和著名的旅游城市。它气候温和，人口约20多万，以古迹众多，风景秀丽，民风淳朴而著称于世。素有"阳光之城"之美名。它同时也是法国除巴黎之外大专院校最集中的城市之一。世界上最早的医药大学于公元1289年创立于该市。

蒙彼利埃整座城市宛如一所巨大的大学城，为数十所大专院校所占据。而公立大学则以蒙彼利埃一大、二大、三大最为著名。一大凭医学著称，二大籍自然科学见长，三大则以其深厚的人文科学基础而闻名于世。

蒙彼利埃第三大学（保尔-华莱利大学）成立于1421年，为法国最著名的国立大学之一。大学坐落于蒙彼利埃市中心，交通极为方便。有在校学生二万多名。学校占地面积70000平方米，拥有多栋规模巨大的教学、办公和科研大楼，以及大型图书馆和博物馆，设施十分完善。学校下设10所研究学院，学校每年接收学生近20000名，包括来自全球120多个国家2200名外国留学生。大学在尼姆、贝兹两市还设有分校。设置有200多个涵盖自然科学、文学、艺术等领域的学士、硕士及博士学位课程，在法国大学中享有盛名。 蒙彼利埃第三大学素以其语言文化学院而著称，设有英语、法语、德语、俄语、西班牙语和汉语等专业课程，3000多名外国学生，分别来自50个不同的国家，从事世界各地语种的教学与研究。
