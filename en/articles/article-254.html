Western Oregon University

1. Introduction

The University of Western Oregon, USA, was established in 1856 and is the first public university in Western Oregon, USA. After 150 years of development, Western Oregon University has grown into a comprehensive university combining arts and sciences. The university offers ten major departments: business, economics, computer information systems, creative arts, humanities, natural sciences and mathematics, psychology and sociology, teacher education, special education and health, and physical education, offering 47 degree programs. It currently has over 5,000 students, including more than 400 graduate students. The university has an elegant environment and complete facilities, featuring modern multimedia classrooms, extensive wireless communication networks, research labs closely connected to advanced American technology, and a modern library. Western Oregon University is committed to providing personalized learning opportunities, providing every student with an excellent learning environment where they can fully express their individuality, fully realize their potential, and fully stimulate their creativity. It has cultivated many successful individuals and is an ideal place to study.

Western Oregon University, located on the U.S. West Coast in Oregon, is only a 20-minute drive from the state capital Shalem and 65 minutes from Portland, the state's cultural center. It is a 2.5-hour drive from Seattle. Oregon is home to world-renowned companies such as Dell, Nike, and Intel, and is also one of the fastest-growing states in the U.S. and a famous tourist destination. Graduates have a wide range of employment opportunities.

2. Study and living expenses

University tuition is about $14,000 per year, which is relatively low among U.S. state university tuition standards and offers a strong competitive advantage. Students participating in exchange programs enter the degree program in their first year and receive a $2,700 scholarship from Western Oregon University, with outstanding students eligible for scholarships up to $9,000 per year.

Oregon City is a tax-free state in the United States, with low living costs. Students live locally on average $3,000-4,000 per year. Students can legally work 20 hours per week on campus, with a salary standard of $7.5-10 per hour. Full-time part-time work during holidays covers living and miscellaneous expenses.

3.2+2 Admission requirements: Passing two years of domestic university courses, with English proficiency at IELTS 5.0.

Montana State University (Billings), USA

1.Introduction

Montana State University (Billings) is a U.S. higher education institution verified and recognized by the U.S. Department of Education and the Chinese Ministry of Education. Founded in 1927, it is the third largest public university in Montana and has a long tradition of providing an excellent education system and community services. The school is located in the Yellowstone Valley, where you can overlook the entire campus from the exquisite sandstone cliffs. Billings is also a nationally recognized regional medical center, serving not only the entire state of Montana but also several neighboring states. Students have easy access to shopping, entertainment, transportation, and other services that are vital to themselves on campus. The Billings community and Montana have always advocated diversity, making it a home for international residents.

2. Features

· Only 150 miles from Yellowstone National Park

· Six colleges award degrees in more than 70 fields

· Student-to-faculty ratio 21:1

· The most cost-effective campus to live on in the state, with lower accommodation and meal prices than other schools

3. Tuition fees

Associate degree (technical college): $7,221/year; 2 semesters per person per year

Undergraduate (College of Health, College of Arts and Sciences, School of Business, School of Education): $14,236/year; Two semesters per person per year

Graduate Students: $15,034 per year; Two semesters per person per year

The annual living expenses for students at Montana State University (Billings) are approximately $5,000–7,500 (depending on individual spending needs)

4.2+2 Admission requirements: Passing two years of domestic university courses, with English proficiency at IELTS 5.0.

University of Maine

1. Introduction

The University of Maine is a U.S. higher education institution verified and recognized by the U.S. Department of Education and the Chinese Ministry of Education. It is the flagship university of Maine, located in the famous Orono University Town on the outskirts of Bangor. This university is also one of the most outstanding public universities in New England, offering the strongest programs and the most reasonable tuition standards.

2. Features

· One of the nationally authorized undergraduate microbiology majors (25 nationwide)

· One of the oldest and most prestigious honor colleges in the United States

· Maine Black Bears headquarters and NCAA Championship Division A sports program member units

· One of the top five forestry majors in the United States

· Ranked among the top 100 public universities in the United States

· The University of Maine was named one of the best universities in the United States by The Princeton Review in 2007; It was also selected by Kpringer magazine as one of the 100 most valuable universities in public education.

3. Tuition:

Undergraduate: $22,652 per year; Two semesters per person per year

Graduate students: $32,700; (Tuition for completing the entire master's program)

The annual living expenses for students at the University of Maine in the United States are about $5,000–7,500 (depending on individual spending needs)

4.2+2 Admission requirements: Passed two years of domestic university courses, with an English proficiency of IELTS 5.5.

1. Universities between China and the United States sign cooperation agreements, register with the U.S. consulate, and have guaranteed visas;

2. All credits earned in China can be transferred to a U.S. university. If students apply on their own, they can transfer credits for up to one year, saving $15,000 in tuition;

3. Students participating in the 2+2 student exchange program in the U.S. can receive 6 free credits of courses from U.S. universities in their senior year, equivalent to nearly 3,000 USD in scholarships;

4. If your English score is below IELTS 5, you may enter English courses early; meeting the requirements allows you to start degree programs;

5. Local living costs are low; saving $300 per month covers housing and meals, and the school offers opportunities to work within 20 hours per week on campus;

西俄勒冈大学

1.简介

美国西俄勒冈大学建立于1856年，是美国西俄勒冈州第一所公立大学。经过150年的发展，西俄勒冈大学已经发展成为一所文理兼备的综合性大学，大学设有商业，经济，计算机信息系统，创造艺术，人文学，自然科学和数学，心理学和社会学，教师教育，特殊教育及保健，体育教育等十个大系，共开设47个学位专业，现有在校生5000余人，其中研究生400余人。大学环境优雅，设施完善，拥有现代的多媒体教室，广泛的无线通讯网络，有与美国现代高新技术密切联系的科研室以及现代化的图书馆。西俄勒冈大学致力于提供个性化的学习机会，为每个学生提供良好的学习环境，让每个学生充分展示其个性，尽情发挥其潜力，完全激发其创造力，已培养出众多成功人士，是理想的求学之地。

西俄勒冈大学，位于美国的西海岸的俄勒冈州，距离州首府沙勒姆市仅有20分钟的车程，距离该州的文化中心城波特兰市有65分钟车程。距离西雅图市2.5小时车程。俄勒冈州是世界著名企业戴尔，耐克，英特尔所在地，也是美国经济增长最快的州之一，美国著名旅游风景地。学生毕业后具有较多的就业机会。

2.学习生活费用

大学学费每年约14000美元，在美国州立大学收费标准中处于较低水准，具有很高的竞争优势。参加交流项目的学生，第一年进入学位课程学习，由西俄勒冈大学给予2700美元奖学金，优秀学生还可以申请每年最高9000美金的奖学金。

俄勒冈州市美国免税州，生活费用低廉，学生在当地的生活费年平均在3000-4000美元，学生可在校内每周合法打工20小时，薪酬标准为7.5-10美元/小时，假期课全职打工，打工收入课满足生活费用及杂费的支出。

3.2+2入学条件：国内两年大学课程成绩合格，英语水平达到雅思5.0分。

美国蒙大拿州立大学（比灵斯）

1.简介

蒙大拿州立大学(比灵斯)是美国教育部及中国教育部核实、承认学历的美国高等院校。建于1927年，是蒙大拿州第三大公立大学，在提供优秀的教育体系和社区服务方面有着悠久的传统。学校坐落于黄石山谷之中，您可以在巧夺天工的砂岩峭壁之下俯瞰整个校园。比灵斯也是国家认可的区域医疗中心，不仅为整个蒙大拿州提供服务，同时还为周围的几个州提供服务。学生在校园内很容易获得购物、娱乐、交通和其他对学生自身十分重要的服务。比灵斯社区和蒙大拿州一直赞成多元化，因此已成为国际居民之家。

2.特色

·距黄石国家公园仅150英里

·六个学院70多个领域授予学位

·学生对教员比例21：1

·全州居住最合算的校园，食宿价格均低于其他学校

3.学费

专科（技术学院）：7，221美元/年；每人每年2学期

本科（健康学院，文理学院，商学院，教育学院）：14，236美元/年；每人每年2学期

研究生：15，034美元/年；每人每年2学期

美国蒙大拿州立大学(比灵斯)学生的生活费，每年的预算大约是5，000－7，500美元（根据个人的消费需求）

4.2+2入学条件：国内两年大学课程成绩合格，英语水平达到雅思5.0分。

缅因大学

1.简介

缅因大学（University of Maine）是美国教育部及中国教育部核实、承认学历的美国高等院校，是缅因州的旗舰大学，位于班哥尔城郊，著名的欧洛诺大学城。这所大学也是美国新英格兰地区最杰出优秀的公立大学之一，具有最强的专业和最合理的收费标准。

2.特色

·拥有获得国家授权的本科微生物学专业之一（全国共25所）

·全美国最古老、最有声望的荣誉学院之一

·缅因黑熊队总部和NCAA的锦标赛甲级体育项目成员单位

·全美国最强的五个林学专业之一

·美国排名前一百位的公立大学

·缅因大学在2007年被《普林斯顿评论》（The Princeton Review）评为美国最好的大学之一；也被克普林格杂志选为100个在公共教育方面最有价值的大学之一。

3.学费：

本科：22，652美元/年；每人每年2学期

研究生：32，700美元；（读完整个硕士研究生课程的学费）

美国缅因大学学生的生活费，每年的预算大约是5，000—7，500美元（根据个人的消费需求）

4.2+2入学条件：国内两年大学课程成绩合格，英语水平达到雅思5.5分。

1、中美双方大学签订合作协议，在美领馆备案，签证有保证；

2、在国内所修学分可全部转移到美方大学，如学生自己申请顶多能过转移一年的学分，可为学生节省1.5万美金的学费；

3、赴美参加2+2学生交流项目的学生，在大四学年可获得美方大学6个学分免费课程，相当于提供近3000美金的奖学金；

4、英语未达到雅思5分的，可提前进入英语课程学习，达到要求即可进入学位课程的学习；

5、当地生活消费低廉，节约点花每月300美金即可实现住房、吃饭的费用，同时学校提供在校每周20小时内打工的机会；
