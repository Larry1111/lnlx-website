Khabarovsk State Technical University


[Introduction]: Khabarovsk State Technical University in Russia was founded in 1958 and is one of the largest universities in Russia's Far East. Currently, there are 18,000 students and over 800 faculty members studying at our university, including 63 PhDs and professors, and 360 associate doctors.

Introduction

Khabarovsk is one of the largest cities in the Far East. The population reaches 650,000. The city lies on the banks of Russia's great Amur River. It is also a major national transportation hub for eastern Russia, as well as the Asia-Pacific and East Asian regions. Khabarovsk was founded in 1858, and with the construction of the Trans-Siberian Railway, it began to develop rapidly. Currently, Khabarovsk has navigational connections with China, Japan, North Korea, and South Korea. Khabarovsk is the main political, economic, and educational center of the Russian Far East. In Khabarovsk, there are the Consulate General of the People's Republic of China, the Consulate General of Japan, the Russian President's representative in the Far Eastern Federal Region, more than 10 universities and academies, and there are four theaters as well as geography, architecture, border regions, and art museums.

School achievements

Over the past 10 years, more than 500 international students have graduated from the school. Currently, there are 110 international students from Asia-Pacific countries studying at Khabarovsk State Technical University. At Khabarovsk State Technical University, students can study according to the following study plan and receive a degree diploma:

Bachelor's degree: Duration is 4 years.

Expert Degree: Duration of study is 5 years.

Master's degree: 2 years.

Candidate Degree: Duration of 3 years.

Doctoral degree: Duration is 3 years. The academic year lasts 10 months. Two semesters: September to February; March to June.

International students are required to study one or one and a half years in the university's preparatory department, depending on their major. The Russian preparatory department offers Russian language programs, including cultural programs. Small classes of 5-6 students, 20 class hours per week. Various Russian proficiency levels are available. The Russian language teaching and research office also has a Russian language examination center. Every foreign student who successfully passes the exam will receive a certificate of completion for Russian proficiency.

Hardware environment

The school provides dormitories for international students (one person or one family). The dormitory building is very close to the teaching school and is located at 140 Pacific Avenue in Khabarovsk.

Dormitory conditions and fees:

Dormitory 1: Two-room dormitory (including bathroom), with a kitchen for every 4-5 dormitory, and each room furnished with necessary furniture and a TV. Dormitory building can receive international long-distance calls, $60 per person per month, with 2 people per room. Dormitory 4: 2 people per room, with two shared kitchens and bathrooms on each floor, a shared bathtub on the first floor, and a gym available free of charge for students. $30 per person per month.

Diet:

There are three student cafeterias in the main building, two of which are Western-style. One is Chinese-style, specially designed for Chinese, Korean, and Japanese students, and is also popular among Russian students.

The school offers free provisions:

Use the school library; Use computers for internet access, gymnasium, and playground; The school's international service center provides airport pickup, assistance in designing study plans, and arranging visits to Khabarovsk, museums, theaters, and visits to Vladivostok, etc.

Medical Insurance:

Foreign students can bring medical insurance from home or apply at school. Medical insurance costs $50 per year.

Major:

Economic Direction: Industrial, Construction Economics, Finance and Credit; Commerce; Economic Operations Research and Mathematical Methods; Economic Management;

International Economics, Accounting and Auditing Technology Direction: Architectural Art; Architectural Art Design and Environment; Heating, Gas, and Ventilation Engineering; Water Supply and Drainage Engineering; Industrial and Building Architecture; Mechanical Manufacturing Engineering; Metal Cutting Lathe and Tool Engineering; Standardization and Commodity Inspection of Mechanical Manufacturing; Automotive, Highways, and Airports; Highway Bridges and Tunnel Bridges; Internal Combustion Engine Engineering; Automotive and Automotive Economics; Marine Power Mechanical Engineering; Forestry; Wood Chemical Processing Engineering; Forestry Machinery and Engineering;

Mining Engineering Informatics Track: Technical Systems Informatics and Management; Computer Systems and Networks; Information Systems and Technical Engineering; Economic Information Applications; Computer and Automatic Systems Software Engineering;

Liberal Arts Tracks: Tourism; Sociology;

Russian Literature Note: Except for the Social and Civilized Services, Tourism, and Russian language programs, which do not offer doctoral degree programs, other majors offer bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degree programs.

Fee Standards:

Foundation course: $1200/year;

Bachelor's and specialist degrees: 1500 USD/year, regardless of major;

Master's degree: $2,000;

Doctoral degree: $2,500;

哈巴罗夫斯克国立技术大学


【导读】：俄罗斯哈巴罗夫斯克国立技术大学创建于1958年，是俄罗斯远东最大学府之一。目前在我校学习有1.8万学生以及800多位教师，教师中有63位博士和教授、360位副博士。

简介

哈巴罗夫斯克市是远东地区最大城市之一。人口达65万人。城市靠俄罗斯伟大的阿穆尔河边。并且城市是俄罗斯东部和亚太及东亚地区的国家主要的运输中心。哈巴罗夫斯克市创建于1858年，由于建筑西伯利亚铁路，哈巴罗夫斯克市开始很快发展。目前哈巴罗夫斯克市和中国、日本、北朝鲜、韩国具有通航联系。哈巴罗夫斯克市是俄罗斯主要的政治、经济、教育远东地区中心。在哈巴罗夫斯克市有中华人民共和国总领事馆、日本总领事馆、俄罗斯总统在远东联邦地区代表、10多所大学、学院，并且在哈巴罗夫斯克市有4个剧场以及地理、建筑、边疆区、艺术博物馆。

学校成就

学校近10年内已有500多位外国留学生毕业。目前在哈巴罗夫斯克国立技术大学有来自亚太国家110位外国留学生学习。在哈巴罗夫斯克国立技术大学可按以下学习计划学习并获得学位毕业证书：

学士学位：学制4年。

专家学位：学制5年。

硕士学位：2年。

副博士学位：学制3年。

博士学位：学制3年。学年10个月。两个学期：9月-2月;3月-6月。

外国留学生按专业方向须在学校预科系读1年或1年半预科。俄语预科系学校提供俄语计划包括文化计划。小班授课5-6人，每星期20学时。各种俄语水平均可入读。俄语教研室还设有俄语考试中心。每位外国留学生顺利通过考试可获得俄语水平结业证书。

硬件环境

学校给外国留学生(1个人或1家人)提供宿舍。宿舍楼离教学校很近，位于哈巴罗夫斯克市太平洋大街140号。

宿舍条件及费用：

一号宿舍：两室宿舍(包括卫生间)，每4-5宿舍备有一间厨房，每间寝室备有必要的家具和电视。宿舍楼可以接国际长途电话, 每月60美元/人，每房间住2人。四号宿舍：2人一房间，每层有两个公用厨房，卫生间，一楼有公用浴池，还有健身房免费为学生提供服务。每月30美元/人。

饮食：

在学校主楼有3个学生食堂，其中两所西式。一所中式，专门为中国、韩国、日本学生提供的，俄罗斯学生也很喜好。

学校免费提供：

使用学校图书馆;使用电脑上网、体育馆及操场;根据学生需要学校国际服务中心提供接机、协助设计学习计划、安排参观哈巴罗夫斯克市、博物馆、剧场及参观海参崴市等。

医疗保险：

外国留学生可以从国内带来医疗保险或到校办理。医疗保险一年50美元。

专业：

经济方向：工业、建筑经济、财务与信贷;商业;经济运筹学及数学方法;经济管理;

国际经济、会计与审计技术方向：建筑艺术学;建筑艺术设计环境;供热煤气与通风工程学;供水及排水工程学;工业及房屋建筑学;机械制造工程学;金属切削车床与工具工程学;机械制造标准化及商检化;汽车公路及飞机场;公路桥与隧道桥;内燃机工程学;汽车与汽车经济学;船舶动力机械工程学;林业学;木材化学加工工程学;林业机械与工程学;

采矿工程学信息学方向：技术系统信息学与管理学;计算机系统与网络;信息系统与技术工程学;经济信息应用学;计算机与自动系统软件工程学;

文科方向：旅游;社会学;

俄罗斯文学注：除了社会与文明服务及旅游业及俄语不设博士学位课程，其它专业都设有学士、硕士和博士学位课程。

收费标准：

预科：1200美元/年;

学士及专家学位：不分任何专业一律1500美元/年;

硕士学位：2000美元;

博士学位：2500美元;
