Besançon University is a renowned public comprehensive university in France, founded in 1432. It offers a full range of programs and boasts a strong faculty. The university has about 1,200 staff members and approximately 21,000 students, including over 6,000 students in the language center. With its excellent faculty and excellent learning environment, the university has long served as a pilot unit for reforming France's higher education system and improving teaching methods. Its French language training center is a renowned international French teacher training center in France and even Europe, where French teachers from around the world receive training and exchange teaching experiences every year. The university has always been at the forefront of French education. In recent years, in accordance with the French government's educational policies, it has launched language and professional integration programs for international students, increasing language study time and improving teaching quality, while also adding professional preparatory courses to ensure more international students can successfully apply for professional studies.

Departmental Structure:

The college has five faculties under its jurisdiction and one language center: (1) School of Law and Economics (majors in international law, economic law, etc.); (2) School of Science (majors in life sciences, mathematics, physics, chemistry, mechanics, etc.); (3) College of Literature (linguistics, comparative literature, history, art, painting, etc.); (4) School of Medicine (majors in medicine, pharmacy, etc., with a notable focus on cardiac research); (5) School of Physical Education (majors in kinesiology, sports management, etc.); (6) Applied Language Training Center.

贝桑松大学是法国著名的公立综合性大学，成立于1432年，专业设置齐全，师资力量雄厚。学校有教职员工约1200人，学生约2.1万名，其中语言中心有学生6000多名。学校凭借优秀的师资队伍、良好的学习环境，一直作为法国高等教育体制改革、教学方法改进的试点单位，该校的法语培训中心是法国乃至欧洲享有盛名的国际法语教师培训中心，每年都有来自世界各国的法语教师在这里进行培训，交流教学经验。该校一直走在法国教育的前沿，近年来，根据法国政府的教育方针，针对外国留学生，开设了语言、专业连读项目，在增加语言学习时间、提高教学质量的同时，加入专业预科课程，从而保证了更多留学生能够成功的申请进入专业学习。

学院下属五个学院，一个语言中心： （1）法律经济学院（国际法、经济法等专业）；（2）理学院（生命科学、数学、物理、化学、机械学等专业）； （3）文学院（语言学、比较文学、历史、艺术、绘画等）；（4）医学院（医学、药学等专业、心脏研究较有名）； （5）体育学院（运动学、运动管理等专业）；（6）应用语言培训中心。
