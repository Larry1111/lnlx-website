Regulations and precautions related to studying and entering France

When international students come to France to study, they need to carefully understand the customs regulations regarding luggage, such as duty-free and prohibited items as stipulated by French customs, so they can avoid taxes or items being seized upon entry.

France's cash regulations allow travelers to bring any amount and currency of cash into France, but if the value of the currency exceeds 10,000 francs, it must be declared to French customs.

International students often prepare many daily necessities before going to France, such as toothbrushes, slippers, and even toilet paper. For such daily necessities, French customs regulations state that essential daily necessities for the student within one year are not subject to tax; exceeding this standard will be subject to import customs duties.

If you don't have a clear idea of what you need for daily necessities within a year, you can also prepare items worth no more than 1200 francs. As long as items fall below this standard, no customs duties will be charged.

French customs cracks down heavily on piracy, largely due to the abundance of fashion brands in France. French customs have even launched special anti-piracy campaigns. Therefore, international students entering France must be careful not to bring any counterfeit items, or they will face severe penalties.

France cracks down on pirated goods, including illegal drugs, pirated books, clothing, shoes, and more. In particular, some counterfeit brand brands commonly used by Chinese students are a key focus for French customs. If students carry too many counterfeit brands, they may be deemed criminal offenses.

Other items prohibited by French customs include dangerous items such as weapons and explosives, wild animals, and books banned by the French government. The likelihood of these items being carried by international students is very low, so just pay close attention.

留学生到法国留学时，需要仔细了解法国海关对于行李的规定，如法国海关规定的免税物品和违禁物品等，这样在入境的时候可以避免上税或被查扣物品。

法国对于现金的规定是，旅客可以携带任意数量、任意币种的现金进入法国国境，但如果携带的货币价值超过1万法郎，就需要向法国海关申报。

留学生经常会在去法国之前，准备许多日用品，例如牙刷、拖鞋甚至是手纸等，对于类似的日用品，法国海关的规定是，留学生一年内的必须生活用品不需要上税，超过这一标准就要征收入境关税。

如果对于一年内的必须生活品没有明确的概念，那么也可以按照随身物品价值不超过1200法郎的标准进行准备，只要在此标准以下的，都不会被征收关税。

法国海关打击盗版的力度非常大，这与法国本身时尚品牌众多有很大关系，法国海关甚至还开展过专项打击盗版的活动，因此留学生在进入法国时，要注意不能携带任何盗版物品，否则将受到严厉的惩罚。

法国审查盗版的内容有，非法药品、盗版图书、衣服、鞋子等，特别是中国留学生常用的一些伪名牌，更是法国海关的查收重点，如果留学生携带的伪名牌数量过多，还可能被认定为刑事犯罪。

法国海关禁止携带入境的物品还有武器和爆炸物等危险品、野生动物、法国政府禁止的书籍等，这些物品留学生携带的可能性极小，只要稍加留意即可。
