    Canada On the 2nd, the University of British Columbia (UBC) Board of Trustees approved a decision to raise international student tuition by 10% starting May 1 next year; and for the next four years, an annual increase of 3%. Student groups hope that after the tuition increase, the university will strengthen administrative transparency to ensure reasonable use of profits and benefit students.

    The UBC Board of Trustees held its meeting on the 2nd, chaired by Principal Arvind Gupta and Supervisor Lindsay Gordon; student representatives and UBC Royal Mounted Police were present as observers. Additionally, some students protested the university's tuition hike outside the venue.

    Anne Kessler, Vice President for Academic and University Affairs at UBC Alumni Society, said: "We understand that universities are facing financial difficulties due to provincial funding cuts. But we still oppose tuition increases because we believe everyone can access education, and we also consider the increase unreasonable. ”

    Student union representative Chris Roach pointed out that if tuition fees are to be increased, UBC needs to be more transparent and accountable in its administration, consult more with student groups, and ensure reasonable use of funds so that students can benefit.

    The UBC Board of Trustees has approved measures to raise international student tuition fees: for the 2015/2016 academic year, newly enrolled international students will pay a 10% higher tuition fee than the current one, effective May 1, 2015. For the next four years, international students' tuition will increase by 3% annually. As for current international students, an additional 2% must be paid annually, effective May 1 next year.

    Gu Aiwen emphasized that raising international student tuition fees is a difficult decision, but the university's move aims to ensure that UBC's teaching and research quality remains at a high level.

    加拿大卑诗大学(UBC)校董会2日通过决定，明年5月1日起调升国际生学费一成;未来4年内，每年上调3%。学生团体希望涨学费后校方加强行政管理透明度，确保合理运用收益，让学生受惠。

    UBC校董会2日开会，由校长顾爱文(Arvind Gupta)和校监戈登(Lindsay Gordon)主持;学生代表及UBC皇家骑警列席旁听。此外，也有部分学生在场外抗议校方涨学费。

    UBC校友会(Alma Mater Society)学术及大学事务副主席克斯勒(Anne Kessler)表示：“我们理解因省府削减拨款，大学面对财政困境。但我们仍然反对校方调升学费，因为我们相信每个人都能获得教育，同时我们认为加幅不合理。”

    学生会代表罗奇(Chris Roach)指出，若要加学费，日后UBC在行政管理方面需要更加透明和问责，与学生团体展开更多咨询，确保经费合理使用，让学生受惠。

    UBC校董会通过的调升国际生学费措施包括：报读2015/2016学年，新入学国际生缴交学费将比现行学费增加10%，生效期是2015年5月1日。而未来4年，国际生学费每年将上调3%。至于现已入读国际生学费，每年须多付2%，生效期同样是明年5月1日。

    顾爱文强调，调升国际生学费是困难决定，但校方此举是想确保UBC的教学及研究质素，能够维持在高水平。
