Address: Calgary, Alberta

Basic Introduction:

Located in downtown Calgary, it is Alberta's largest comprehensive institution, with nearly 50 years of history. The college features world-class practical laboratories and student-centered classrooms and facilities, with 93% of graduates finding jobs in related fields within six months of graduation. Beauvalry College offers many transfer and further study opportunities with Canadian universities.

Major or Department:

1. English language training and teacher training

Beauvalis Institute offers the largest English language learning program in Western Canada, offering direct transitions from academic English programs to Beauvaliers' vocational (certificate and diploma) courses. Courses are offered in spring, autumn, and winter.

2. Healthcare Courses: Including practical care, healthcare assistant, and disability studies certificate and diploma courses Process

Healthcare programs are in very high demand, with a 100% employment rate for graduates. All courses offer practical work experience for students, providing graduates with valuable hands-on work experience. Practical health laboratory facilities provide students with world-class learning opportunities for applied and hands-on experience. Canadian academic standards in the health sector are recognized as world-class. Transfer agreements give graduates the opportunity to continue their studies in health programs at other Canadian universities.

3 . Business school diploma courses

The nine business diploma programs offer each student the opportunity to study in a field of interest to them. All business diploma programs offer students an option for paid internships. Business courses are taught by experienced tutors with practical experience in the fields taught. The Business School is the department within the Beauvalier Institute that has the most transfer and admission agreements with other Canadian universities, allowing credits from the Beauvalier Institute to be transferred to other partner institutions. The Business School operates an incubator program to support emerging entrepreneurs in bringing their business ideas to market.

地址：阿尔伯塔省卡尔加里市

基本介绍：

位于卡尔加里市中心区域，是阿尔伯塔省最大的综合性学府，建校近50年。学院具有世界一流的实践实验室和以学生为本的教室和设施，毕业生93％在毕业6个月内找到相关领域的工作。博瓦立学院提供许多与加拿大大学的转学升学机会.

专业或院系:

1.英语语言培训和教师培训

博瓦立学院拥有加拿大西部最大的英语语言学习课程，从学术英语项目直接衔接到博瓦立学院的职业（证书及文凭）课程。课程春季、秋、冬季三季开班。

2.医疗保健课程：包括实用护理，卫生保健助理和残疾研究证书及文凭课程

医疗保健课程有非常高的需求，毕业生就业率100％。所有课程提供学生工作实践，能给毕业生提供宝贵的实际工作经验。实用的健康实验室设施为学生在世界一流条件提供应用和亲身实践的学习机会。加拿大在健康领域的课程学术标准被公认为是世界顶级的。转学协议让毕业生有机会进入其他加拿大大学的健康项目继续深造。

3．商校文凭课程

九类商业文凭课程为每个学生提供在有机会他们感兴趣的专业领域学习的机会。商业文凭课程都为学生提供一个选择带薪实习机会。商业课程是由经验丰富的并且在所授领域里有实践经验的导师授课。商学院是博瓦立学院中与加拿大其他大学拥有最多转学和升学协议的院系，可将博瓦立学院的学分转往其他协议院校。商学院营运孵化器项目，支持初露头角的企业家将其商业构想推向市场。
