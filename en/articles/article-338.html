Australia's education quality is world-class. Despite the recent global economic downturn, Australia's international education sector has maintained strong growth momentum. On March 18, 2009, Australia released an international student statistics report showing that in January 2009, the number of self-funded international student registrations in Australia increased by 21.4% compared to the same period the previous year, marking another breakthrough since the 2008 annual cumulative enrollment of international students for various courses at Australian institutions reached a record high of 20.7%. In the UK's Times 2008 World University Rankings, 12 Australian universities entered the global top 200, with 6 universities ranked in the top 50.

Australia is one of the countries with the best social welfare in the world, and new immigrants can enjoy 10 major basic social benefits. Australia provides the most comprehensive educational security for overseas students and is the only country in the world that provides education through legislation — The Overseas Student Education Services Act (ESOS) protects the rights of overseas students.
