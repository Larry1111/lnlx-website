University of California, Irvine

English name: University of California Irvine

School website: http://www.uci.edu/

Main Campus: California

International Undergraduate Preparation Program (IUPP): IBT score of 61 or IELTS 5.0 or above;

International Postgraduate Preparation Program (IGSPP): IBT score of 61 or IELTS 5.0 or above.

Tuition: $25,000—$29,500 per course

UCI is located in Irvine, about an hour's drive south of downtown Los Angeles. It is a well-known four-year public university in Southern California, founded in 1965. It ranks just behind Berkeley, Los Angeles, and Davis, and is among the top ten best public schools in the United States. The campus is very beautiful and charming, with innovative architecture, excellent faculty, and a cool climate—all favorites among students. Irvine also has a very pleasant climate, with dry, sunny summers and rainy skies, and winters not too cold. It's ideal for students who love summer weather, and with its proximity to the sea, it's especially popular among those who enjoy water sports. In the 2011 comprehensive ranking of U.S. universities, the university not only boasts brand-new and beautiful architecture, but its faculty and staff also have strong professional backgrounds, including two Nobel Prize winners.

Near the school is the famous Newport Beach, a world-renowned sailing hub that often hosts large-scale sailing and surfing competitions. Various gatherings are frequently held here, such as dance parties, tea parties, outdoor BBQs, sand volleyball games, and more. All these activities help you easily forget the pressure of studying.

加州大学欧文分校(简称UCI) 位于洛杉矶市中心以南约一个小时车程的欧文市（Irvine），是南加州地区相当知名的一所四年制公立大学，成立于1965年.排名紧追伯克莱、洛杉矶和戴维斯等大学之后，列全美最好的公立学校前十名之一。校园十分美丽迷人，新颖的校园建筑、优良的师资、凉爽的气候，均是学生们的最爱，欧文市在气候上也是非常的舒服、怡人，夏天干爽、晴空万里，不常下雨，冬天则也不会过于寒冷，适合热爱夏天气候的学生前往，加上又靠海，喜爱海上运动的人更是列为首选。2011全美大学综合排名第41位，学校除了拥有崭新优美的建筑外，教职人员皆具有深厚的专业背景，其中有两人为诺贝尔奖的获得者。

在学校附近有个著名的新港沙滩Newport Beach，这是个世界著名的帆船集中地，时常会举办大型的帆船运动比赛及冲浪比赛，这里也常会有人举办各类的聚会活动，如：舞会Party、茶会、野外BBQ、沙摊排球等等，这一切的活动，让你轻易的忘却念书的压力。
