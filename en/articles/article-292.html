Guide to applying for a Japanese student entry visa

Guideline 1:

Procedures for applying to Japanese embassies or consulates abroad.

Whether it is work or settlement, the applicant can apply directly for a visa at the local Japanese consulate. Fill out the visa application form and submit supporting documents corresponding to the reason for the application. Then, the Japanese embassy or consulate will submit the relevant documents to the Japanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which will forward them to the Ministry of Justice, which will review together with relevant departments. Applicants for the Advanced Labor Visa must submit it to the Ministry of International Trade and Industry for approval. If there are no objections, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs will issue the visa at the embassy or consulate. It generally takes about three months from application to visa acquisition.

Tip 2:

The procedure for applying to the Immigration Bureau within Japan.

According to Japan's Immigration Control Act, foreigners applying for student, skilled work, or ordinary work visas must apply to the Ministry of Justice for a "Certificate of Eligibility for Residence Status" to obtain an entry visa. If the applicant has relatives or friends in Japan, they can entrust them to apply at the local immigration bureau.

Guideline 3:

Then, the Immigration Bureau submits it to the Ministry of Justice for review and approval, including the guarantor's sponsorship qualifications, financial status, any illegal activities, and previous sponsorship reputation. After review and approval, the local Immigration Bureau issues a "Certificate of Eligibility" to the guarantor. The guarantor sends this certificate to the applicant, who then applies for a visa at the embassy or consulate.

Guideline 4:

Foreigners residing in Japan who wish to temporarily leave Japan for any reason must apply to the Ministry of Justice for a re-entry permit before leaving Japan. This permit is issued by the Minister of Justice. Within the validity period of this permit, you can enter and exit Japan at any time. If it expires for any reason, you can apply for an extension at the Japanese embassy or consulate abroad.

指南1：

向日本驻外使领馆申请的程序。

无论是做工，还是定居等，均可以由申请人直接向日本驻在当地的领事馆申请签证。填写签证申请表格，递交与申请事由相应的证明材料。然后，由日本大使馆或领事馆将有关档案材料报回日本外务省，外务省转送法务省，再由法务省会同有关部门共同审核。申请高级劳务签证的，则需送到通产省核准。如无不同意见，再由外务省批示驻大使馆或领事馆签发签证。从申请到取得签证，一般需要3个月左右时间。

指南2：

在日本国内向入境管理局申请的程序。

日本出入境管理法规定，申请留学、技术服务、普通工作签证的外国人，必须向法务省申请《在留资格认定的证明书》，方可取得入境签证。如果申请人在日本有亲友，可以委托他们向所在地的入境管理局申请。

指南3：

然后，由入境管理局呈报法务省，进行审查批准，包括审查担保人的担保资格，财产状况，有无违法行为以及以往担保信誉等。经过审查同意，便由所在地的入境管理局发给担保人一份《在留资格认定证明书》。担保人将此种证明寄给申请人，由申请人持此证明向大使馆或者领事馆申请签证。

指南4：

侨居在日本的外国人，因某种原因，要暂时离开日本，在离开日本前，应向日本法务省申请再次入境许可证。此证由法务大臣签证。在此证有效期内，可随时出入日本。若因某种原因过期，可到日本驻外使、领馆申请延期。
