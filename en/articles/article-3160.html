Address: Ottawa, Ontario

Basic Introduction:

Founded in 1967, it is one of the largest colleges in Canada. The college has an operating budget of $300 million, has about 600 full-time professors, and offers 176 full-time programs, serving 19,000 full-time students. Despite the current economic downturn, 90% of graduates typically find jobs within six months of graduation. The college has about 1,300 international students from over 100 countries. Argonquin College fully utilizes technology-assisted teaching and training, earning a strong reputation in the industry.

Priority Majors and Departments:

1. Hotel and tourism management

2. Media and design

3. Computer and information technology

地址：安大略省渥太华市

基本介绍：

成立于1967年，为加拿大最大的学院之一。学院有3亿美元的运营预算，拥有约600名全职教授和176种全日制课程专业，为19,000名全日制学生服务。尽管目前经济低迷，90％的毕业生还是通常能在毕业六个月内找到工作。学院拥有来自100多个国家约1,300名国际学生。亚岗昆学院充分利用科技辅助教学和培训，在业界具有良好的声誉。

优先专业和院系：

1. 酒店和旅游管理

2. 媒体和设计

3.计算机和信息技术
