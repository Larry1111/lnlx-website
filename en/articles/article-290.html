Inter-school programs in Japanese high schools

To promote broader exchanges between high schools in China and Japan, and to ensure that high school students aspiring to study in Japan have access to better Japanese educational resources, our unit conducted repeated evaluations for two years, and through on-site inspections by unit leaders and leaders of five key provincial high schools, we ultimately established cooperative relationships with Japan's Respected High Schools, Kashiwara High School, and Ryoyo High School.

This program is a new study abroad method in China, where students study full-time in their third year of high school in Japan for one year in the second semester of their third year of high school (every April), then proceed to university. Compared to traditional Japanese language schools, Japanese high schools offer academic education and have significant advantages in faculty, teaching, facilities, and Japanese language and cultural courses.

Introduction to Murakami Gakuen, a Japanese cooperative educational institution:

Murakami Gakuen is a Chuwa Sex Academy consisting of Higashi-Osaka University, Higashi-Osaka University Junior College, Higashi-Osaka University Keiai High School, Higashi-Osaka University Kashiwabara High School, and Higashi-Osaka University Affiliated Kindergarten. In recent years, to promote internationalization and achieve the school's internationalization, it has established sister school relationships with several key provincial high schools in various provinces of China, promoting teaching methods and student exchanges. At the same time, Murakami Gakuen's two high schools, Keiai and Kashiwara, actively recruit international students from overseas to attend high school in Japan.

Program Advantages and Comparison with Ordinary Self-Funded Study Abroad:

[Fees and Time]

● Ordinary individuals studying abroad at their own expense:

· When applying to a Japanese language school, you need to submit your high school diploma. In other words, you can only come to Japan about six months after graduation.

· Japanese language schools offer half-day classes, and it takes about two years of study to reach the Japanese proficiency required for entering a Japanese university.

· Tuition and living expenses for 2 years must be at least 300,000 RMB.

★China-Japan Intercollegiate Exchange Program:

· You can go abroad before graduating from high school. (That is, in April, the second half of the senior year semester).

· Transfer to the third year of a Japanese high school, where classes are full-day. After one year, you can be admitted to a Japanese university. You save over a year and enjoy teaching resources unmatched by Japanese language schools.

· The fee is only 1.55 million yen (about 120,000 RMB), which includes three meals a day and dormitory expenses.

· Monthly student aid provided by the Japanese government is available.

[Ensuring Visa Rates]

● Ordinary individuals studying abroad at their own expense:

· During the application period to language schools, you are required to take the Japanese language proficiency test, and you must reach at least Level 4 in Japanese.

· If you do not obtain the Level 4 Japanese certificate, you cannot guarantee a visa rate.

★China-Japan Intercollegiate Exchange Program:

· High schools in China and Japan have signed international exchange agreements. Studying in Japan is considered a student exchange between friendly schools, and Japanese language proficiency is not required.

· This program is an exchange between Chinese and Japanese schools, ensuring a high visa success rate. As of 2010, the visa rate was 100%.

[Dual Degrees]

● Ordinary individuals studying abroad at their own expense:

· Language schools are non-degree education and cannot award academic qualifications.

★China-Japan Intercollegiate Exchange Program:

· The Japanese high schools under the exchange agreement offer formal academic education in Japan, offering not only Japanese language instruction but also courses related to international students taking the senior high school entrance exam in Japan

· In addition to obtaining a Chinese high school diploma, students can also obtain a Japanese high school diploma.

[Accommodation: Safety]

● Ordinary individuals studying abroad at their own expense:

· Most language schools do not have their own dormitories and rent apartments near the school for students; A few schools provide dormitories, but meals are provided by students.

★China-Japan Intercollegiate Exchange Program:

· The school has dormitories and implements fully boarding management. Teachers are on duty 24 hours a day. Each room has 2-3 people, equipped with TV, refrigerator, air conditioning, etc. Dormitory rooms have bathrooms and water heaters.

· The school also has a cafeteria to provide nutritious meals for students.

[Easy Admission]

● Ordinary individuals studying abroad at their own expense:

· Language schools do not offer academic subjects, so when taking the Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students or Entrance Examinations, the academic subjects are entirely self-studyed.

★China-Japan Intercollegiate Exchange Program:

· To prepare for the Japanese University Admission for International Students or Entrance Examinations, the school offers supplementary courses for foundational subjects. There are also dedicated instructors for further university admission, and the school holds university recommendation qualifications.

Process:

Before entering the country

1. Attend the interview and register (September-November)

2. Students prepare visa documents and related fees; (November~January)

3. Submit visa application to the Japan Immigration Bureau; (January~February)

4. Obtain a visa and prepare to go abroad; (March)

After entering the country,

5. Start of semester, complete enrollment procedures; (April~May)

6. Take the Japanese Language Proficiency Test; (July)

7. Apply to various Japanese universities (October~February)

8. Take the Examination for Studying Abroad for Foreign Students; (November)

9. Second Japanese Language Proficiency Test; (December)

10. Winter break; (Late January ~ Mid-February)

11. University Visa Replacement (February~March)

12. University opening; (April)

为促进中日两国高中之间更广泛的交流，也为使有志愿赴日留学的高中生获得更好的日本教育资源，本单位经过两年的反复论证，也经过单位领导及5所省内重点高中的合作学校领导亲自实地考察，最终确定了日本敬爱高中，柏原高中及两洋高中的合作关系。

本项目是在国内高三下学期（每年4月）赴日本高中高三年级全日制就读一年，然后升入大学的全新留学方式，与传统的赴日本语学校就读相比，日本高中是学历教育，在师资，教学，设施上以及在日本语文化课教学上都具有极大的优势。

日本合作教育机构——村上学园简介：

村上学园是由东大阪大学、东大阪大学短期大学部、东大阪大学敬爱高等学校、东大阪大学柏原高等学校，东大阪大学附属幼儿园组成的中和性学园。近年，为了推进国际化进程，实现学校国际化为目的，目前已在中国各省份与一些省级重点高中结成姊妹校关系，进行两校间的教学教法以及学生间的交流。同时，村上学园的两所高中「敬爱」和「柏原」积极地招收海外的留学生到日本读高中。

项目优势及与普通个人自费留学对比：

【费用及时间】

●普通个人自费留学：

·申请日本的语言学校时，需要提交高中毕业证。也就是说在毕业半年左右以后才能来到日本。

·日本语学校为半天授课，要达到升入日本大学的日语水平需要2年左右的学习时间。

·2年的学费和生活费至少需要人民币30万以上。

★中日校际交流项目：

·可以在高中毕业前出国。（也就是高3下半学期的4月份）。

·编入到日本高中3年级进行学习，高中的学习是全天授课，学习 1年后可以考取日本大学。节省了一年多时间，并享用日本语学校无法比拟的教学软、硬件资源。

·费用仅为155万日币（约人民币12万元左右），其中包括1日3餐，宿舍的费用。

·每月可以获得日本政府提供的留学生援助金。

【确保签证率】

●普通个人自费留学：

·在申请语言学校期间要求参加日语等级考试，而且要求达到日语4级以上水平。

·没有拿到日语4级证书的话，无法保证签证率。

★中日校际交流项目：

·中日两国高中签订国际交流协议，赴日留学属于友好校间的学生交流，不要求日语能力。

·此项目为中日两国学校间的交流，可以保证签证成功率。截至2010年签证率为100%。

【双学历】

●普通个人自费留学：

·语言学校属于非学历教育，无法颁发学历。

★中日校际交流项目：

·交流协定的日本高中学校是日本正规学历教育，既有日语教育还为学生开设参加留学生在日高考的相关课程

·学生除了拿到中国高中毕业证书外还可以拿到日本高中毕业证书。

【食宿：安全性】

●普通个人自费留学：

·大多数语言学校没有自己的宿舍，是在学校附近帮学生租的房子；少数学校提供宿舍，但吃饭是自己做。

★中日校际交流项目：

·学校配有宿舍，实施全寄宿式管理。24小时都有教师值班。2-3人1个房间，房间内有电视、冰箱、空调等。宿舍内有浴室，热水器。

·并且学校配有食堂，为学生提供营养配餐。

【易升学】

●普通个人自费留学：

·语言学校因没有文化课的学习，所以参加日本留学考试或升学考试时，文化课程全依靠学生自学。

★中日校际交流项目：

·为对应日本留学考试或升学考试，学校特设相应基础科目的补习课程。且设有专门应对升学的指导老师，另外学校拥有大学推荐资格。
