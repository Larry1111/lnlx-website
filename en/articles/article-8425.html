Moscow State Linguistic University


[Introduction]: Moscow State Linguistic University was founded in 1804 and is one of Russia's oldest universities. It is a classic university in the field of humanism and serves as a scientific and cultural center for a continuous education system. Its main mission is to train qualified experts with advanced degrees in humanities and language education.

School Overview

Number of students: 10,000

School Introduction

Is it certified by the Chinese Ministry of Education: Certified

The school now has over 10,000 students (including various forms of education). The faculty is strong, including many renowned experts from Russia and even the world, over 70% of teachers hold academic qualifications, and the school also has more than 500 associate doctors, associate professors, and more than 200 PhDs and professors.

School major

The main majors include: Theoretical and Historical Linguistics, Language Learning Theory, Education, Psychology, Computer Science and Technology, Philosophy, History, Economics, Political Science, Sociology, Law, and Cultural Studies.

Related deeds

As Russia is about to officially launch the "Year of Chinese" campaign, Xinhua reporters conducted an exclusive interview with Haleeeva, known as the "Iron Lady" in Russia's education sector. Among the hundreds of university presidents in Moscow, women are very few. Haleeva is undoubtedly the most unique among them. "Brave," "determined," and "tough" are the media's adjectives for her.

Such a "strong-willed" principal spoke of China with tenderness. "I love China as much as I love my own country," Haleyeva said. "My father, a soldier, served in China for four years, I stayed in China for three years, and my brother was born in China, so China was firmly rooted in my heart from a very young age." After graduating from university, Haleeeva devoted herself to education, becoming the president of a language university at age 40, a position she held for more than 20 years. After four years in office, she offered Chinese courses and established an Oriental Language Teaching and Research Office. Today, students from three departments and five majors—Translation, Economics and Law, and International Relations and Social Politics—can study Chinese. Students have repeatedly won awards in All-Russian and Moscow Chinese competitions, and graduates are spread across Russian foreign departments, news media, and Chinese-funded enterprises. Today, under Haleeeva's leadership, Mo Yu University has not only made significant achievements in Chinese language teaching, but has also become an active promoter of international university exchanges, especially with Chinese universities. It is also an important member of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization's language cooperation base and the Shanghai Cooperation Organization Humanities University Alliance.

During China's "Year of Russian" in 2009, Haleeeva was invited twice to China. In May, she was awarded the title of honorary professor by Capital Normal University in China; In December, she was invited to attend the 60th anniversary celebration of Shanghai International Studies University[1], and both parties agreed to hold a celebration for the 20th anniversary of cooperation between the two universities this year. Speaking about the upcoming "Year of Chinese" event in Russia, Haleeeva was extremely excited. She said, "I am very pleased to hold the 'Year of Russian' in China and to start hosting the 'Year of Chinese' in Russia. I want to attend all the events, and the language university will also host more events because I believe our ties should be further strengthened." In Haleeeva's view, language cooperation is not only the foundation of cultural collaboration but also an important factor in economic and technological cooperation." Linguistics plays a key role in international communication; it can even be called the most important thing, because we live in a world of texts, from nanotechnology to information technology to bioengineering, everything is described through language and writing," she said. When envisioning the school's development plan for Russia's "Year of Chinese," Haleyeva said, "There are two directions for our efforts: one is to build a Confucius Institute, and the other is to sign a cooperation agreement with at least one Chinese university, such as Dalian University of Foreign Studies. I especially hope to solve the current shortage of faculty by opening a Confucius Institute. [1] The school has already begun negotiations with relevant Russian government departments and has also engaged with the Chinese side. ”

莫斯科国立语言大学


【导读】：莫斯科国立语言大学创立于1804年，是俄罗斯最古老的大学之一，大学是人文主义领域的经典大学，是一个不间断教育系统的科学文化中心，学校的主要任务是培养具有高等人文和语言教育学位的合格的专家。

学校概况

学生人数：10000人

学校简介

中国教育部是否认证：获得认证

学校现在有学生超过10000人(包括各种教育形式)学校师资力量雄厚，有许多俄罗斯乃至世界知名的专家，超过70%的老师有学术等级，学校还有500多名副博士、副教授和200多名博士、教授。

学校专业

学校的主要专业有：理论与历史语言学、语言学习理论、教育学、心理学、计算机科学和计算机技术、哲学、历史学、经济学、政治学、社会学、法律、文化研究等。

相关事迹

在俄罗斯即将正式启动“汉语年”活动之际，新华社记者对俄教育界素有“铁娘子”之称的哈列耶娃进行了专访。在莫斯科数百位大学校长中，女性寥寥无几。哈列耶娃无疑是最独特的一个。“勇敢”、“坚定”、“强硬”是媒体对她的形容词。

这样一位“个性”强悍的校长谈起中国[1] 时却充满柔情。“我爱中国就像爱自己的祖国，”哈列耶娃说，“身为军人的父亲在中国曾服役4年，我在中国呆过3年，弟弟也生在中国，因此在我很小的时候，中国就牢牢扎根在我心中”。 大学毕业后，哈列耶娃投身教育界，40岁时成为语言大学的校长，一干就是20多年。任职四年后，她开设汉语课并设立了东方语言教研室。如今，该校翻译系、经济和法律系、国际关系和社会政治学院3个院系5个专业的学生都可以研习汉语，该校学生屡次在全俄和莫斯科汉语比赛中获奖，毕业生更是遍布俄外交部门、新闻媒体、中资企业等。 如今，在哈列耶娃带领下的莫语大不仅在汉语教学上颇有建树，在国际大学交流，特别是与中国大学交流方面也成了积极的推动者，并且是上海合作组织的语言合作基地和上海合作组织人文大学联盟的重要成员。

2009年中国“俄语年”期间，哈列耶娃应邀两次来到中国。5月份，她被中国的首都师范大学授予荣誉教授的称号;12月份，她应邀参加上海外国语大学[1] 的60周年校庆活动，双方商定要在今年举办两校合作20周年的庆祝活动。 谈及即将在俄罗斯举办的“汉语年”活动，哈列耶娃显得极为兴奋。她说：“我非常高兴能在中国举办‘俄语年’和在俄罗斯开始举办‘汉语年’。我想参加所有的活动，语言大学也将举办更多的活动，因为我认为我们之间的联系应该进一步加强。” 在哈列耶娃看来，语言合作不仅仅是人文合作的基础，更是经济和技术合作中的重要因素。“语言学在国际交流中起着关键作用，它甚至可以说是最重要的东西，因为我们生活在一个文本的世界里，从纳米技术到信息技术再到生物工程，所有一切都是靠语言文字来进行描述，”她说。 畅想学校在俄罗斯“汉语年”的发展计划时，哈列耶娃说：“我们的努力方向有两个，一是建一所孔子学院，二是至少再与一所中国大学签署合作协议，比如大连外国语大学。我特别希望能通过开设孔子学院解决目前师资力量不足的问题。[1] 学校已经开始跟俄罗斯有关政府部门谈判，同时与中国方面也进行了接洽。”
