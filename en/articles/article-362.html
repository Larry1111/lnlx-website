Auckland University of Technology was established in 1895 and was originally named Auckland Institute of Technology. In January 2000, it was officially granted national university status by the New Zealand government, making it the eighth national university in New Zealand. It is regarded as the youngest and most dynamic university in New Zealand, and is also the largest science and engineering university in the country.
The school has two campuses: the Wellesley campus in downtown Auckland, and the Akoranga campus on the northern waterfront of Auckland. The Akoranga campus is only 10 kilometers from the city center, with shuttle buses between the two campuses that shuttle students every 20 minutes.
Auckland University of Technology offers departments including liberal arts, business, health research, science, and engineering, offering a wide range of certificate and diploma programs for employment, as well as bachelor's and master's degree programs. The university uses small class sizes, with an average of 16 students per class, ensuring every student receives excellent care.
[Location]
Auckland is New Zealand's largest city, with a population of over one million. It boasts direct international airports to Japan, the United States, Australia, and countries in the Pacific region, as well as the Port of Auckland, known as New Zealand's number one American port. It is truly the financial, trade, transportation, and cultural center of New Zealand. Auckland University of Technology is located in the heart of this multicultural city, known as the "Sailing Capital," adjacent to excellent harbors and bays. Auckland has a mild and pleasant climate, with an average summer temperature of 24 degrees and a winter average of 16 degrees. This well-developed, beautiful, and charming metropolis provides students with a vibrant environment for study and life.

Auckland University of Technology has two campuses, with its main campus located in the economic center of New Zealand--- in the bustling heart of Auckland, facing the famous University of Auckland across the street. The university is adjacent to a commercial center, making shopping and transportation extremely convenient. The other campus is located in the northern part of Auckland, with a school bus running between the two campuses every 20 minutes.

[History of Establishment]

Auckland University of Technology originated from Auckland Institute of Technology (AUT), founded in 1895, making it the earliest and largest higher technical college in New Zealand. In 2000, AUT was upgraded to a polytechnic university, earning it the reputation as New Zealand's youngest and most dynamic university. Currently, the university offers bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees, as well as certificates, diplomas, postgraduate programs, and awards internationally recognized diplomas. Currently, the total number of students enrolled exceeds 25,000, including about 700 international students from 43 countries studying and living at AU.

[College Features and Program Features]

The college offers a variety of certificate programs, diplomas, bachelor's degrees, and postgraduate master's degree programs. Subjects include language, arts, business, medicine, technology, culinary arts, and electrical engineering. All courses at the school are reviewed and approved by the New Zealand Education Review Authority, and it is a member of the New Zealand International Education Organization. The school uses small class sizes to facilitate mutual understanding between teachers and students. The school also provides one-on-one teaching services to enhance students' listening, speaking, and writing skills.

[Ancillary Facilities and Related Services]

The school is fully equipped, including a computer center, two libraries, a health center, a counseling center, a student work and study center (offering summer work-study opportunities), a career guidance center, a gym, a study assistance center, an international student club, and a cafeteria. The school also provides airport pick-up services for students, and there are academic counselors, psychological counselors, and learning skills consultants who provide continuous support and services.

[Tuition and Living Expenses]

Tuition fees range from NZ$13,000 to NZ$19,000 depending on the major. The basic living cost is NZ$6,500 per year.

School website: http://www.aut.ac.nz/

学校有两个校区：位于奥克兰市中心的威尔莱斯利Wellesley校区，位于奥克兰市北部海滨的阿克兰格Akoranga校区，阿克兰格校区距市中心仅10公里，两个校园之间有大巴车接送学生，每20分钟一班。
奥克兰理工大学下设院系包括文科、商业、健康研究、科学和工程等，可以为学生提供范围广泛的证书、文凭课程，方便学生就业，也可以提供学士学位及硕士学位课程。大学采取小班教学，平均每班16人，使每个学生都能得到很好的照顾。
[地理位置]
奥克兰是新西兰最大的城市，这座人口超过100万的城市拥有与日本、美国、澳大利亚和太平洋地区各国的直航国际机场，以及被称为新西兰第一美港的奥克兰港，是新西兰名副其实的金融、贸易、交通、文化的中心。而奥克兰理工大学就坐落在这座富有多元文化色彩且被成为“风帆之都”的城市中心，濒临优良的港口和海湾。奥克兰气候温和宜人，夏天平均气温24度，冬天平均气温16度。这座交通发达，美丽迷人的大都市为学生提供了一个充满生机的学习和生活环境。

奥克兰理工大学设有两个校区，其主校区位于新西兰经济中心---奥克兰的中心繁华地段，与著名的奥克兰大学隔街相望。学校毗邻商业中心，购物和交通极为方便。另一校区地处奥克兰北部，每20分钟有学校巴士在两个校区之间穿梭往来。

[创建历史]

奥克兰理工大学的前身是奥克兰理工学院（AUT），成立于1895年，是新西兰最早成立的、也是最大的高等理工学院。2000年，奥克兰理工学院升格为理工大学，因此也被誉为新西兰最年轻、最有活力的大学。目前，该校提供学士学位、硕士学位、博士学位以及证书、大专文凭、研究生课程等，并颁发国际承认的文凭。目前，在校学生总数已超过25,000人，其中大约有43个国家的700名留学生在AUT学习生活。

[学院特点及专业特色]

学院开设多种证书课程、文凭、学士学位及研究生硕士学位课程。包含语言、艺术、商业、医学、科技、厨艺、电机等科目。该校所有课程均经新西兰教育审议局审核通过，并为新西兰国际教育组织的成员之一。该校采用小班制授课形式以便于增进师生双方的了解。学校还提供一对一的教学服务以加强学生的听说和写作能力。

[附属设施及相关服务]

学校设施齐全，有计算机中心、两个图书馆、保健中心、咨询中心、学生工作研究中心（为学生提供暑期勤工俭学机会）、就业指导中心、健身房、助学中心、留学生俱乐部、餐厅。学校还为学生提供接机服务，并有学业的咨询顾问、心理咨询顾问和学习技能顾问为学生提供不间断的帮助和服务。

[学费及生活费用]

根据专业的划分，其学费为NZ$13000至NZ$19000不等。基本生活费用为NZ$6500/年。

学校网站：http://www.aut.ac.nz/
