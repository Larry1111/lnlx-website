The "two-year multiple-entry visa" for UK visitors has officially opened

The UK Home Office announced today that a brand-new two-year visitor visa for China will begin trial operation on Monday, January 11, 2016.

Under this pilot program, Chinese visitors applying for the standard 6-month visit visa in China mainland will be eligible to apply for a 2-year multiple-entry visitor visa, with the same application fee of £85.

The two-year visitor visa will apply to travel, business visits, attending meetings, or business inspections as types of travel purposes.

Applicants still need to undergo the same comprehensive verification before visa issuance, and Chinese visitors still need it Fill it outOnline application form and biometric information submission。

The Chinese government will provide the same visa to British citizens. The number of British citizens traveling to China continues to grow, and now UK citizens will benefit from the same visa arrangements.

The new visa initiative will be more convenient because we have plans in place Expanding mobile fingerprint services in the UK, This is the fingerprint service for visa applications, Expanded from 9 cities to 50 Chinese cities。

In the past year ending September 2015, the UK issued 484,065 visas to China, a year-on-year increase of 20%, including 404,084 visitor visas, an increase of 22%.

UK Immigration Minister James Brokenshire stated:

The launch of this new visa will offer Chinese visitors affordability and travel flexibility, thereby strengthening our premium visa services while ensuring protection at the UK border.

The number of UK-China visitors is increasing year by year, and this visa will enable Chinese visitors to better take advantage of the tourism and business opportunities offered by the UK.

1. Please do not rush to apply for a visa immediately after January 11 – You must be planning to travel to the UK within the next three months。

2. You must apply online on gov.uk website.

https://visas-immigration.service.gov.uk/applying-in-china

3. The trial will begin at 00:01 on Monday, January 11 (Beijing time). Applicants who apply and pay the application fee before this time are not eligible for this new visa arrangement.

4 . At this stage, applicants will still need to apply for and pay a £85 standard 6-month visitor visa. However, theirs Applications will be automatically included in the new two-year pilot program。 When applying, they should not click on the current two-year standard visitor visa, as they will be charged a higher fee of £324.

5. Applicants who successfully obtain a visa can still visit the UK if their passport expires before the two-year visa expires. They must travel with both their new passport and their old passport with a valid UK visa.

6. This pilot program is open to citizens of the People's Republic of China holding any type of Chinese passport who are applying for a standard visit visa in Chinese mainland, except for the following visit purposes:

  • Private medical care

  • Organ donation

  • Visiting scholar

  • ADS designated destination travel plans

The pilot program also does not apply to these types of visits: registered marriage/same-sex partnership visits, paid activity visits, transit visits.

英国内政部今天宣布：对华的全新两年访问签证将从2016年1月11日星期一试运行。

根据该试运行计划，在中国大陆申请标准的6个月访问签证的中国访客将有资格申请2年多次入境访问签证，并且申请费同样为85英镑。

两年访客签证将适用于旅行、商业拜访、参加会议、或商务考察这类型的出行目的。

申请者在签证签发之前仍然需要接受同样的全面核查，中国访客仍然需要填写在线申请表和提交生物信息。

中国政府将会为英国民众提供相同的签证。前往中国的英国公民数量不断增长，现在英国公民将受惠于相同的签证安排。

全新的签证举措将会更加便利，因为我们同时有计划扩张英国的移动指纹服务，即为签证申请采取指纹的服务，从9个城市扩张到50个中国城市。

截至2015年9月的过去一年间，英国在中国签发了484,065份签证，同比增长20%，其中404,084为访问签证，增幅为22%。

英国移民部长詹姆斯•布罗肯希尔（James Brokenshire）表示：

这个全新签证的推出将为中国访客提供经济上的实惠和出行灵活性从而强化我们优质的签证服务，并同时确保英国边境得到保护。

英中国访客数量逐年增长，该签证将使中国访客更好地利用英国提供的旅游和商业机遇。

1. 请不要在1月11日之后立即匆忙申请签证 – 您必须是计划是在未来3个月内前往英国。

2. 您必须在gov.uk网站在线申请。

https://visas-immigration.service.gov.uk/applying-in-china

3. 该试运行将从1月11日星期一(北京时间)00:01开始。在此时间之前申请并支付申请费的申请人不适用于这个新的签证安排。

4. 现阶段，申请者将仍然需要申请和支付85英镑的6个月标准访客签证。然而，他们的申请将会被自动归纳至新的两年试行计划中。他们在申请的时候，不应该点击现行的两年标准访客签证，因为他们将会被收取更高的价格为324英镑的费用。

5. 成功获得签证的申请者如果护照的有效期在两年签证有效期结束之前到期，他们仍然可以访问英国。他们必须同时持有新的护照和带有有效英国签证的旧护照出行。

6. 该试行计划开放给持有任何种类中国护照，并在中国大陆地区申请标准访问签证的中华人民共和国公民，但是以下访问目的的申请除外：

  • 私人医疗

  • 器官捐赠

  • 访问学者

  • ADS指定目的地旅游计划

该试行计划同样不适用于这些访问类型：注册结婚/同性伴侣关系访问,有偿活动访问,转机访问。
