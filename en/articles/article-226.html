The University of Surrey was founded in 1891 and was granted royal charter on September 9, 1966, becoming a comprehensive university. It is located in Guildfordshire, Surrey, in the southeast of England. Its research covers cutting-edge space technologies such as small satellites, and it has members of numerous academic societies.

Located 30 miles southwest of London, Surrey has a convenient and fast rail network, and the university is less than an hour from London and its two major international airports. A 10-minute bus ride from the city center brings you to a large sports center with a swimming pool and an ice rink. The University of Surrey campus offers beautiful scenery, open spaces, lovely lakesides, and lush woods, providing students with a safe and relaxed learning environment.

Currently, the school has over 14,000 students and more than 2,300 staff members, including more than 3,000 international students from 130 countries. Its graduate employment rate also ranks first in the UK.
The University of Surrey has nine highly rated fields, including Business and Management, Economics, Physics, Electronic Engineering, Psychology, Civil Engineering, and Materials Technology. The International Study Centre at the University of Surrey mainly offers international foundation courses. It provides the language and professional training necessary for pursuing undergraduate undergraduate programs. These include the following faculties: Faculty of Arts and Humanities, Faculty of Engineering and Physical Sciences, Faculty of Health and Medicine, Faculty of Management, and Faculty of Law.
The university's university curriculum is designed to cultivate talents with outstanding professional qualities and practical skills, meeting not only the needs of the UK workplace but also global trends. Therefore, graduates of the University of Surrey have more advantages in the workplace.

Undergraduate Foundation Course Admission Requirements: Graduates of 11th or 11th grade, IELTS 5.0 and TOEFL 500, and at least 17 years old.

Language course start dates: three times a year, in January, June, and September.

www.surrey.ac.uk

萨里大学成立于1891年，在1966年9月9日被皇家许可而成为综合性大学，位于英格兰东南的萨里市吉尔福德郡。其研究范围包括了小卫星等顶尖空间技术，并拥有众多学术团体的成员。

萨里市位于伦敦西南30英里处，拥有便捷快速的铁路网，大学距离伦敦和两大主要国际机场都不到1小时的路程。从市中心出发坐10分钟公交车，就有一个大型运动中心，内有游泳池和溜冰场。萨里大学校园风景优美，开阔的空间、优美的湖畔和郁郁葱葱的树林，学生可在此享受一个安全且轻松的学习环境。

目前学校共有学生14,000多名，教职员工2300多名，其中有超过3000多名来自130个国家的国际学生。其毕业生就业率也位于全英第一名。
萨里大学有9个获得优秀评级的领域包括商务和管理、经济学、物理学、电子工程、心理学、土木工程和材料技术。萨里大学国际学习中心主要提供国际预科课程。提供攻读大学本科课程专业所必需的语言和专业培训。包括一下这些学院：艺术及人文科学学院、工程及物理科学学院、健康和医学院、管理学院和法学院。
学校的大学课程设置旨在培养专业素质和实际技能都很出色的人才，不仅符合英国国内的职场需要，亦符合世界的趋势，因此萨里大学的毕业生在职场中拥有更多优势。

本科预科课程入学要求：高二或高三毕业生，雅思5.0分获托福500分，年满17周岁。

语言课程开课日期：每年1、6、9月三次开学。

www.surrey.ac.uk
