School Introduction

The full name of Bocconi University is Comercial University of Luigi Bocconi, commemorating the sacrifice of Ferdinand Bocconi's only son during the colonial war at the Battle of Adova (Ethiopia, May 1, 1896). Bocconi University is a private university, but through its policy of equal opportunity, its funding programs have enabled generations of outstanding students from economically disadvantaged families to pursue further studies at this renowned private university. Its first president and dean, Leipado Sabatini, a renowned civil rights lawyer, proposed Bocconi's mission as "promoting the integration of academia with people's lives."

Bocconi University is among the top in Europe in business and economics education. It is considered the best economics university in Italy. The university offers undergraduate, master's, and doctoral degrees, taught in English or Italian. Both undergraduate and graduate programs have student exchange programs with many universities worldwide. These include Harvard University's Department of Economics, University of Pennsylvania's Wharton School, Columbia University, NYU Stone School of Business, University of California, Boston University, University of Melbourne, and HEC Management School in France. In China, there are Tsinghua University's School of Economics and Management, Peking University, Fudan University, and Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Departmental Structure

Bocconi University mainly has departments of economics, finance, management, public administration, accounting, and mathematics. Many professors are graduates of top American universities and UK LSE master's or doctoral degrees. Bocconi University also has a leading law faculty in Italy, with its own law school offering degrees in law.

In the 2007 Wall Street Journal's global business school rankings, Bocconi University was ranked 20th, thanks especially to its School of Management (SDA Bocconi). In the same year's ranking of hiring preferences among major global companies, Bocconi's MBA graduates ranked 17th globally. In 2008, the Financial Times of London ranked Bocconi University's corporate decision-making education fifth in Europe.

Bocconi University and SDA Bocconi Business School offer vocational training programs for executive business management. The university is a member of the World Business Association (AMBA).

Since 2007, Bocconi University has been admitting high school graduates in China to study for the Bachelor of International Economics and Management degree.

学校简介

博科尼大学全称Comercial University of Luigi Bocconi，是为了纪念费迪南多•博科尼先生的独子在殖民战争的阿多瓦战役中牺牲（埃塞俄比亚，1896年5月1日）。博科尼大学是一家私立大学，但是凭借实行和发扬机会均等的教育政策，它的资助计划使得一届又一届的家庭经济劣势的优秀学生能够在这所著名的私立大学深造。它的第一任校长兼教导主任，莱昻帕多•撒巴蒂尼，一位著名的民权律师，提出了博科尼大学的使命是“促进学术与人民生活的融合”。

博科尼大学在欧洲的商科和经济学教育上处于前列。在意大利被认为是最好的经济类大学。大学提供本科、硕士和博士学位，使用英语或意大利语授课。在本科和研究生阶段都与世界许多大学有学生交流项目。其中包括哈佛大学经济系，宾夕法尼亚大学沃顿商学院，哥伦比亚大学，纽约大学史顿商学院，加利福尼亚大学，波士顿大学，墨尔本大学，法国ＨＥＣ管理学院。中国的有清华大学经济管理学院，北京大学，复旦大学，上海交通大学。

院系设置

博科尼大学主要有经济系，金融系，管理系，公共管理系，会计系和数学系。许多教授是美国顶尖大学和英国ＬＳＥ硕士或博士毕业生。博科尼大学还有意大利居于前列的法律系，拥有自己的法律学院并授予法律方面的学位。

博科尼大学在２００７年华尔街报全世界商学院排名中位列２０，尤其归功于其管理学院（SDA Bocconi)。在该报同年对于世界大公司招聘偏好的排名中，大学管理学院的ＭＢＡ毕业生位列全世界第１７位。伦敦金融时报在２００８年将博科尼大学的公司决策层人员教育排在欧洲第５。

博科尼大学和SDA Bocconi商学院提供针对企业管理执行层的职业培训课程。该大学为世界工商管理协会AMBA会员。

从２００７年开始，博科尼大学在中国招收高中毕业生入学就读 国际经济与管理 (Bachelor of International Economics and Management) 学士学位。
