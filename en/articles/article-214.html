Sejong University

1. Overview of the school

Sejong University was founded in 1940 and is located in Gunjam-dong, Gwangjin-gu, Seoul. It is a prestigious four-year comprehensive private university in South Korea, offering disciplines such as humanities, social sciences, business administration, hospitality and tourism, natural sciences, electronic information engineering, engineering, and arts and film. Among these, its majors in hospitality and tourism, arts, and animation are among the best in Korea. The university has a general graduate school, an MBA graduate school, and Sejong CYBER University, offering 49 master's degrees and 35 doctoral degree programs. Although the School of International Languages at Sejong University started relatively late, it leverages Sejong University's advanced teaching facilities and strength, fully draws on the best experiences of other universities, hires lecturers with rich teaching experience, and uses flexible and diverse teaching methods and tools to provide students with an excellent learning environment.

3. Admission Qualifications

1. Must have at least a high school diploma or have the same academic background. (Excluding vocational high schools, technical schools, and Korean ethnic high schools)

2. Completed at least 12 years of education abroad.

3. Highest academic qualifications: within 2 years of graduation.

世宗大学

一、学校概况

世宗大学（Sejong University）成立于1940年，位于首尔市广津区君子洞，是一所在韩国享有盛誉的4年制综合性私立大学，包括人文科学、社会科学、经营学、酒店旅游学、自然科学学、电子信息工程学、工程学、艺术影视学等学科，其中酒店旅游、艺术、动漫等专业在韩国处于领先水平。学校设有普通研究生学院、MBA研究生学院和世宗CYBER（网络）大学，拥有49个硕士学位和35个博士学位授予点。世宗大学国际语言学院虽然起步较晚，但依托世宗大学先进的教学设施和教学力量，充分借鉴他校的先进经验，聘请了有着丰富授课经验的讲师，运用灵活多样的教学方式和授课工具，将为学生提供优秀的学习环境。
