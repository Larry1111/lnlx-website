Our academy is located in Kawasaki City, Kanagawa Prefecture, between Tokyo and Yokohama, making transportation very convenient. It takes about 8 minutes on foot from the academy to JR Kawasaki Station. From JR Kawasaki Station, it takes only 20 minutes to Tokyo Station and 10 minutes to Yokohama Station, respectively. Additionally, prices in Kawasaki City are cheaper than in Tokyo, making it more convenient for international students to live and study.

Since its founding in 1987, the academy has been dedicated to Japanese language teaching. Most graduates of the academy go on to study at universities or vocational schools; after completing their studies, some work for Japanese companies, while others start their own businesses. The academy is committed to helping every student with lofty ideals build a solid foundation and take the first step toward their dreams.

School Features:

• Offers 1.5-year (October induction) and 2-year courses focused on listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills.

• Teachers with rich teaching experience engage in teaching work with enthusiasm.

• In addition to Japanese language learning, courses related to Japanese society, culture, and lifestyle habits are also offered.

• To improve college admission ability, provide tailored guidance to each student.

• The college has student dormitories that are convenient for commuting, fully equipped, and allow students to cook on their own.

• For students who achieve excellent grades and have good attendance, after review, partial tuition fee reductions will be awarded as a reward.

Admission Requirements:
1. Age between 18 and 28 years old
2. Have received 12 years of compulsory education in China (including students from three schools)
3. 150~200 hours of Japanese language study experience

本学院位于神奈川县川崎市，介于东京与横滨之间，交通非常方便。从学院到 JR 川崎站徒步大约只需 8 分钟。而从 JR 川崎站出发到东京站和横滨站分别只需 20 分钟和 10 分钟。另外川崎市的物价较东京的物价便宜，更便于留学生生活，学习。

学院自 1987 年建校以来专心致力于日语教学工作。从学院毕业的学生，大多数都继续进入大学或专门学校求学深造，学成之后，有的进入日本公司工作，有的还自己开公司。学院立志帮助每一位抱有远大理想的学生打下扎实的基础，迈好自己梦想的第一步。

学校特色：

• 设有 1 年半（ 10 月生）及 2 年课程，针对听、说、读、写能力进行指导。

• 具有丰富教学经验的教师以热忱的态度从事教学工作。

• 除了日语学习，还开设有关日本的社会、文化以及生活习惯等课程。

• 为了提高升学能力，对每一个学生进行具体的辅导。

• 本学院设有学生宿舍，上学方便，设备齐全，可自行烹饪。

• 对于成绩优秀、出勤率好的学生，经过审查，将给予部分学费减免的奖励。
