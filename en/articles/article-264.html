English name: University of Massachusetts

School website: http://www.massachusetts.edu

Main Campus: Massachusetts

English requirements: Undergraduate TOEFL550 (IBT80) or IELTS 6.5;

Dual Undergraduate Admission Requirements: IELTS 4.5, TOEFL 55 or passing our test, high school GPA 3.0;

Master's dual admission requirements: IELTS 5.5, TOEFL 69 or passing our test, with a score of 80 or above in eight university subjects.

Tuition: Undergraduate $19,500–23,200/year; Master's $23,500/year.

The University of Massachusetts, commonly known as the University of Massachusetts (UMASS), was founded in 1863 and is a public university in the Massachusetts region. With 63,000 enrolled students, it is the largest university cluster in New England, offering comprehensive, high-quality undergraduate to doctoral education. Undergraduate programs cover over 100 research fields, including: literature, history, mathematics, physics, communications, media and technology, accounting, nursing, tourism, engineering, finance, law, education, and more.

The Juris Doctor studied here for U.S. President Obama. The University of Massachusetts is a comprehensive university offering comprehensive, high-quality undergraduate to doctoral education. Undergraduate programs cover over 100 research fields and have five campuses: Boston, Amherst, Dartmouth, Lowell, and Medical School.

The University of Massachusetts (UMASS) boasts a long history of 140 years, offering high-quality teaching resources and a diverse student body. The faculty includes Nobel laureates, Pulitzer Prize winners, and members of the National Academy of Sciences and the American Institute of Medicine. Some faculty members have received prestigious national awards, including the National Book Award, as well as foundations established by prominent American figures, including the Fulbright Fellowship, Guggenheim Prize, MacArthur Award, and Mellon Award. The university has invested over $400 million in research. Even so, the university has established relatively reasonable tuition plans and provides generous scholarships and financial aid. Because of these advantages, the university has been rated by The Times of London as one of the best universities in the world.

马萨诸塞大学，俗称麻省大学（UMASS），成立于1863年，是马萨诸塞州地区一所公立大学。在校注册学生人数达到63，000人，是新英格兰地区最大的大学群体，提供全方位，高质量的大学本科至博士研究生教育。本科专业涉及100多个研究领域，包括：文学、历史、数学、物理、通讯、传媒与技术、会计、护理、旅游学、工程、金融、法律、教育等。

美国总统奥巴马在这里就读的法学博士。麻省大学是一所综合性大学，提供全方位，高质量的大学本科至博士研究生教育。本科专业涉及100多个研究领域，共有五所分校：波士顿校区（UMassBoston）、阿默斯特校区（UMassAmherst）、达特茅斯校区（UMassDartmouth) 、罗维尔校区（UMassLowell）、伍斯特校区（UMassMedicalSchool)。

麻省大学(UMASS)具有140年建校的悠久历史，学校拥有高质量的教学资源和多元化的学生群体。该学校的师资队伍不乏诺贝尔奖获得者，普利策新闻奖获得者，以及美国国家科学院和美国医学研究所会员。其中，一些教师曾获得过美国国家级著名奖项，包括美国国家图书奖，以及美国社会知名人士设立的基金会提供的奖项，包括富布莱特奖学金，古根海姆奖，麦克阿瑟奖和梅隆奖等。学校在研究领域投入的资金高达4亿美元以上。即便如此，学校对学生制订了相对合理的收费计划并给学生提供丰厚的奖学金和助学金。因为具有这种种优势，该大学被伦敦泰晤士报评为世界上最好的大学之一。
