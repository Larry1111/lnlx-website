Tokyo International Japanese Language Institute is located next to Shinjuku Station, the busiest area in Tokyo, adjacent to Shinjuku Gyoen Park, the most beautiful garden in Tokyo. Our school has students from more than ten countries around the world. To reduce student burdens, the school adopts an educational policy of low tuition, top-notch tuition fees, and well-equipped facilities, aiming to achieve the school's ultimate goal of cultivating outstanding Japanese language talent.
College Features:

Ø Not only does it offer pure Japanese language education, but there are also many extracurricular activities to help you understand and experience Japanese culture and customs.

Ø The college tailors teaching to students' aptitude, adopts a small class teaching system, and organizes classes according to ability.

Ø The goal is to take entrance exams for the Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students, Level 1 of the Japanese Language Proficiency Test, and advanced educational institutions such as Japanese universities and graduate schools.

Course Features:

1. Class classes are divided into general courses and advancement courses.

2. The college mainly focuses on further education courses, primarily aiming to train students for admission to graduate schools, universities, and vocational schools.

3. There are more than 20 classes from beginner to advanced, organized according to students' learning levels and pace.

1. The beginner class starts in April and October each year.

Registration Requirements:

1. High school under 20 years old

2. College diploma, under 25 years old

3. Bachelor's degree, under 28 years old

学院特色：

Ø 不仅进行纯粹的日本语教育，同时大量课外活动，帮助了解和体会日本的文化风俗。

Ø 学院因材施教，采取小班授课制，按能力编班。

Ø 目标定位于日本留学考试、日语能力考试1级、日本的大学、大学院等高级教育机构的入学考试。

课程特色：

1．班级上课，分一般课程和升学课程的两种。

2．学院以升学课程为主，主要以培养学生上大学院、大学和专门学校等高等教育机构为目标。

3．从初级到高级有20多个班级，根据学生学习水平和学习速度来编班。

1． 初级班每年4月份和10月份开学。

报名条件：

1. 高中 20岁以下

2. 大专 25岁以下

3. 本科 28岁以下
