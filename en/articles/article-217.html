Chosun University

1. Overview of the school

Chosun University was established in 1946 and is located in Gwangju, the art city of South Korea. The university ranks among the largest in South Korea. There are 30,000 students studying here. The university has 4 dormitory buildings with 3,200 beds, and the dormitories are wired. The school admits transfer students; those who have completed their first year or graduate from a junior college can apply for their second year; Those who have completed more than two years of junior college at a regular university can apply for junior year.

朝鲜大学

一、学校概况

朝鲜大学建于1946年，位于韩国艺术城光州市。大学规模在韩国名列前茅。有3万学生在此学习。学校有4栋宿舍楼，3200个床位，宿舍有有线。学校招收插班生，大一以上或大专毕业可以申请大二；正规大学完成二年以上专科毕业可以申请大三。
