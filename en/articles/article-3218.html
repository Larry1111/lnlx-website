University Introduction:
On August 1, 2007, Paisley University and Bell College merged to form the largest modern university in Scotland—the University of the West of Scotland, with campuses including Ayr, Hamilton, Dumfries, and the West of Scotland. Today, the University of the West of Scotland enjoys an enviable reputation for innovation and academic excellence, and through its four campuses, it serves as a base for higher education in western Scotland.

Today, the university has 2,000 faculty and staff spread across more than 100 disciplines, guiding over 18,000 students to obtain academic qualifications, technical skills, and enhance personal qualities—all of which are essential factors for enhancing their competitiveness in the job market and career choices.

The University of the West of Scotland consists of seven faculties: the Faculty of Business, the Faculty of Computer Science, the Faculty of Education, the Faculty of Engineering and Science, the Faculty of Health, Nursing and Midwifery, the Faculty of Media, Language and Music, and the Faculty of Social Sciences. Each faculty offers a range of specialized courses. Many courses offer students a year of paid work placements to strengthen their practical experience and career orientation.

The University of the West of Scotland is an international, innovative, and inclusive institution. Among more than 18,000 students, more than 1,000 are international students from more than 40 different countries.

Admission requirements:
IELTS score of 6, with no section below 5; those who do not meet the requirements will study at the school's language center

Application Materials:

1. Application form

2. High school graduation certificate or proof of enrollment (with English translation)

3. High school transcript (with English translation)

4. Two letters of recommendation

5. PS

6. IELTS or TOEFL score (if you don't have one, you can apply for a conditional offer first)

7. Research Objectives (Research Programmes only)

院校简介：
在2007年八月一日,佩斯利大学和贝尔学院合并创建了苏格兰最大的现代大学——西苏格兰大学，校区包括埃尔,，汉密尔顿，邓弗里斯及西苏格兰。今天，西苏格兰大学在创新和学术优秀上拥有令人羡慕的名声，同时，西苏格兰大学也通过它的四个校区为苏格兰西部地区提供高等教育的基地。

现今，大学有2000名教职员工，分布在100多个学科领域指导着18000多学生获得学历资格，技术技能和提高个人品质，这些都是增强其在就业市场的竞争力和选择职业所必须的重要因素。

西苏格兰大学由7个学院组成，商学院，计算机学院，教育学院，工程与科学学院，保健，护理和助产学院，传媒，语言和音乐学院及社会科学院。每个学院都提供一系列专业课程。许多课程都能为学生提供一年带薪的工作实习，以加强课程的实践和职业倾向。

西苏格兰大学是一个国际化的，创新的和兼容并包的机构。在18000多名学生当中，有1000多名国际学生来自40多个不同的国家。

入学要求：
雅思6分，单项不低于5分，未达到要求者进入学校语言中心学习

3. 高中成绩单(附英文翻译件)

4. 2封推荐信

5. PS

6. IELTS 或者TOFEL 成绩(如果没有，可以先申请有条件的offer)

7.研究目标(research programmes only)
