It is reported that in 2010, more than 10,000 Chinese students chose to study in France, and currently about 30,000 Chinese students are studying in France, with the number expected to reach 50,000 by 2015. The majors applied for Chinese students in France mainly include management, finance, economics, arts, culture, design, and language culture. Recently, French study abroad has made some policy adjustments, placing greater emphasis on the quality of student sources.

Aiming to increase the number of high-end international students

Currently, 278,000 foreign students are studying in France, accounting for 12% of all university students in France. Recently, France decided to encourage foreign students with bachelor's degrees or above to pursue further studies and research in France, with a focus on recruiting master's and doctoral students. According to France's European Times, Minister of Higher Education and Research Pécres recently set a goal to increase the number of foreign students enrolled for master's and doctoral degrees, while also preparing to strengthen inter-university cooperation. The number of international students participating in university cooperation programs should increase from the current 20% to half within three years.

According to AFP, during a discussion with foreign university students at the G8/G20 summit, Pécresse pointed out that France should clearly focus on graduate and doctoral students. She said, 'We hope that by 2015, we can achieve the new target of having more than half, reaching two-thirds, of the international students pursuing these two degrees.'

Pecres also believes that studying in France is not entirely satisfactory. Many foreign students come to France not for study or work; On the contrary, not many outstanding students choose to study in France. Therefore, "We hope to attract foreign students as much as possible through cooperation between France and foreign institutions, rather than through individual intermediaries." Pecres also believes that France should provide tutoring and assistance to students throughout their entire study abroad period from when they leave their home country until they complete their studies. She revealed that some Chinese students do not even speak French when enrolling at French universities.

Changes in the procedures for visas to France

Recently, students interested in studying in France should pay attention, as the procedures for obtaining a visa to France have also changed.

Change 1: You need to provide your passport, and the applicant must include a copy of the passport among the materials provided.

Change 2: To upload photos to the Pastel system personal homepage, applicants must upload a personal color photo of the same size as their own passport on the Pastel personal page. Applicants can upload photos in the motivations tab of the Campus France form.

Change 3: You must present your passport to take the French language test. On the day of the French exam, you must bring your ID card and passport; otherwise, you cannot enter the exam.

Change 4: Transcripts (undergraduate or associate degree) must be included in the materials provided.

Change 5: The "My Courses" section must be filled out and saved. Applicants must fill out and save their study plan for France on the Pastel website.

Studying in France requires careful school selection

Recently, Lyon Commercial School and Montesquieu School in France were suspected of illegal operations, and the cases are still under judicial review. Experts remind students to choose their schools carefully when studying in France and not register with private institutions without government contracts. After arriving in France, students should promptly register at the Education Office of the Chinese Embassy in France or on their website.

Study abroad experts suggest that when choosing a school, it's best to consider both the major and the city. It is recommended to apply to cities with mature study abroad programs, such as Nantes, Caen, Lille, Tours, Perpignan, Marseille, Burdeaux, and Nice, which are popular destinations for students. Those places have well-established overseas services and many Chinese students, who can help each other and provide valuable support for life safety and social integration. Girls are advised not to attend schools in Marseille, as Marseille is a port city and relatively chaotic. However, logistics and shipping programs in Marseille are still quite good. Students interested in business can choose schools in Reims, Savoie, and the surrounding areas of Paris. For hotel majors, it is recommended to choose Perpignan in the southern tourist area, and for tourism majors, Savoie or Tours are recommended.

France is not very supportive of cross-disciplinary master's studies. Students studying Chinese, History, English, or Business French in China have limited majors. It is recommended to choose majors with relatively relaxed entry requirements, such as administration or public administration, or hotels and tourism majors strongly supported by the French government.

据悉，2010年，共有超过1万名中国学生选择赴法深造，目前约有3万余名中国留学生在法攻读，预计2015年将达到5万人。中国赴法留学申请的专业主要涉及管理、金融、经济、艺术、文化、设计、语言文化等。近来，法国留学在政策面进行了一些调整，将更加重视生源质量。

欲增加高端留学生数量

目前有27.8万名外国学生在法国留学，占法国全体大学生的12%。近日，法国决定鼓励国外本科以上学历的学生去法国进一步深造和做研究，重点招收硕士、博士留学生。据法国《欧洲时报》报道，高等教育和研究部长佩克雷斯近日制定了增加注册攻读硕士和博士文凭的外国留学生数量的目标，同时还准备加强校际合作。院校合作项目留学生的数量应从目前的20%比例在三年内上升到一半。

据法新社报道，佩克雷斯在八大工业国/二十国集团峰会期间外国大学生座谈会上指出，法国应当明确地把重点放在研究生和博士生上面。她说，“我们希望能够到2015年之前，实现攻读这两个文凭的留学生比例超过一半、达到占三分之二的新目标。”

佩克雷斯同时认为，留学法国也存在不尽人意的地方。许多外国学生来法国留学并不是为了学习和工作；相反，优秀学生选择来法国留学的人数还不多。因此，“我们希望尽可能通过法国与国外院校展开合作的方式吸引外国学生，而不是通过个人中介”。佩克雷斯还认为，法国应当从留学生离开原籍国一直到他们结束学业的整个留学期间给他们提供辅导和协助。她披露，有些中国学生注册法国大学的时候甚至连法语都不会讲。

赴法签证程序内容有变

近来有意赴法留学的学生要注意了，赴法签证的程序也发生了一些变化。

变化一，要提供护照，申请者所提供的材料中要有护照复印件。

变化二，要在Pastel系统个人主页中上传照片，申请者要在Pastel个人页面中上传与自己护照同等规格的个人彩色照片。申请者可以在Campus France表格中的motivations 标签页来完成照片的上传。

变化三，参加法语考试要出示护照，参加法语考试当天要携带身份证以及护照，否则不能入场参加考试。

变化四，在提供的材料中要有成绩单(本科或者大专 )。

变化五，“我的课程”部分要填写并保存 ，申请者要在Pastel网站填写并保存自己的赴法学习计划。

在法留学需要慎重择校

近日，法国里昂高等商业学校及孟德斯鸠中学涉嫌非法运营，此案目前还在司法审理过程中。专家就此提醒：在法留学应慎重选择就读学校，不要在与政府没有合同的私立机构注册。留学人员抵达法国后应及时到中国驻法大使馆教育处或在该处网站上注册登记。

留学专家建议，选择学校最好从专业和城市两方面来考虑。建议申请留学项目成熟的城市，例如南特、卡昂、里尔、图尔、佩皮尼昂、马赛、布尔多和尼斯都是学生比较倾向前往的。那里的境外服务成熟，有较多中国留学生，可以互相帮助，在人生安全和融入社会上给予有利的帮助。女生最好不要就读马赛的学校，因为马赛是港口城市，较为杂乱，不过马赛的物流、航运专业仍然是不错的。有意就读商科的学生可以选择兰斯、萨瓦和巴黎周边的学校，酒店专业则建议选择南部旅游区的佩皮尼昂，旅游专业可以选择萨瓦、图尔。

法国不太赞成硕士跨专业学习，在国内学习汉语、历史、英语、商务法语专业的学生，这些学生专业选择面不广，建议赴法留学时选择入学门槛较宽松的专业，比如行政、公共管理专业就读或法国政府非常支持的酒店、旅游专业。
