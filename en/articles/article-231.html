Founded in 1848, City University of London is the third largest comprehensive national university in the UK and the largest in the London area. The university has been served by successive Mayors of the City of London. City University of London enjoys an excellent geographical location, allowing it to quickly grasp global trends. The university combines its long-standing traditional culture with modern teaching methods, with over 100 years of teaching and research experience, making it one of the most attractive universities in the UK today. Graduates of City University of London pursue many outstanding careers and serve as excellent role models.

City University of London is located on the north side of central London, in the UK's financial center and the famous European business district. Combined with its edge on the edge of the financial district, it is named after the City University. To the east of the university are the four major law schools, the headquarters of world-renowned trading companies, major law firms, and media companies. Nearby are many well-known medical university hospitals, the National Arts Centre, theaters, and more.

The University of London has over 21,000 students and more than 2,000 faculty and staff, with about 15% being overseas.

Undergraduate programs include: Business, Mathematics, Engineering, Computer Science, Journalism, Law, Business Management, Health Care, Health Sciences, Music, Social Sciences, and Language Courses.

Master's programs include: International Financial Accounting, Internal Audit Management, Insurance and Venture Capital, Banking, Financial Engineering, Accounting, Shipping Management, Arts Policy Management, Real Estate Investment, Asset Valuation, Investment Management, Continuing Education, Health Sciences, Journalism, Information Technology, Law, Social Humanities, Music, Mathematical Studies, and more.

Admission requirements for undergraduate foundation courses: at least 17 years old, IELTS 5.5 (minimum 5.0 for writing), currently in senior high school or having graduated from high school in China.

Graduate diploma program entry requirements: Bachelor's degree, IELTS 6.0 or above.

Language course start dates: Four times a year, in January, April, July, and September.

伦敦城市大学建于1848年，是全英第三大，伦敦地区最大的综合性国立大学。由历届伦敦市市长担任该校的校长。伦敦城市大学的地理位置绝佳，能快速掌握世界的动态。学校将历史悠久的传统文化与现代化的教学方法相结合，有着100多年的教学和研究经验，成为当今英国最具魅力的大学之一。从伦敦城市大学毕业的学生从事着很多优秀的职业，成为了很好的榜样。

伦敦城市大学位于伦敦市中心北侧的英国金融中心和著名的欧洲商业区，再加上位于金融区的边缘，因此以城市大学而命名。学校的东侧是4大律师学院、世界著名商贸公司总部、各大公司律师事务所和各媒体公司的所在地。在其附近还有很多知名的医科大学医院、国家艺术中心和剧院等等。

伦敦大学在校学生超过21000人，教职员工超过2000人，海外学生约占15%。

本科课程包括：商学、数学、工程、计算机、新闻、法律、商务管理、保健、健康科学、音乐、社会科学及语言课程等。

硕士课程包括：国际财政会计、内部审计管理、保险和风险投资、银行学、财政工程、会计、船运管理、艺术政策管理、不动产投资、资产评估、投资管理、继续教育、健康科学、新闻、信息技术、法律、社会人文科学、音乐、数学研究等。

本科预科课程入学要求：年满17周岁，雅思5.5分（写作部分至少5.0），中国高三在读或高中毕业。

研究生文凭课程入学要求：本科毕业，学士学位，雅思6.0以上。

语言课程开课日期：每年1、4、7、9月四次开课。
