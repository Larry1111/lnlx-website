Gwangju University, South Korea

1. Founded: 1980 (30 years of history)

2. Level format: 4-year comprehensive university (key local university)

3. Number of students: 10,000 enrolled students, 250 international students.

4. School Location: Gwangju Metropolitan City, Korea's fifth largest city, about 300 kilometers from Seoul, about 3.5 hours by car.

5. School Advantages

1) Employment and Entrepreneurship Center: A prestigious university that collaborates with over 8,000 Korean SMEs, ranking first in employment rate among universities in southwestern Korea.

2) Generous scholarships are provided
For the first semester of undergraduate studies, 40% of tuition is waived. In the second semester, based on previous semester grades, 30-100% of tuition is waived.

Most students receive scholarships with 40%-50% tuition waivers.

Note: For students with excellent college entrance exam results，Scholarships are offered in the first semester upon enrollment.

480-500Students who score 50% of tuition are waived，501-55060% tuition waiver，551-57970% tuition waiver，580All tuition fees are waived for those above the specified points。

Graduate school offers a 50% tuition reduction. Students can receive additional scholarships based on their individual learning abilities.

3) On-campus dormitory provided (well-equipped, low-cost): 6 months' worth of accommodation and meals is about RMB 6,000 (including three meals a day).

4) On-campus and off-campus part-time work provided: Our school aims to manage students well and ensure they can study with peace of mind. After 6 months of Korean language courses, attendance rate exceeds 80%. Students who need to work part-time are provided with on-campus or off-campus part-time jobs.

5) In the final semester of undergraduate studies, students can intern independently in Korea. This lays a solid foundation for employment.

6) No major restrictions: Korean language proficiency at TOPIK level 3 guarantees admission to our undergraduate or graduate school. Students can choose any major.

7) Students who have completed two years of college or university studies can transfer to other majors and graduate in two years.
8) During the Korean language course, Jeju Island cultural experiences are provided (60% funded by the school, 40% by students).
9) Main Majors

International operations, logistics distribution, tourism, hotel management, fashion design, interior design, visual image design (animation), industrial design, jewelry design, photography and imaging, performing arts and dance, early childhood education, nursing, sports and leisure, cyber security and police, news broadcasting, advertising planning, computer engineering, information and communication, electrical and electronic engineering, new renewable energy engineering, social welfare, psychotherapy, food nutrition, alternative medicine

10) Featured majors: Beauty and hairdressing, flight attendant services

11) Free airport pickup
12) Students applying for a one-year language course who reach TOPIK Level 3 within 6 months and apply for our undergraduate program can convert the remaining 6 months' tuition fees into the first semester of this course.

韩国光州大学

1. 建校时间：1980年（30年历史）

2. 层次形式：4年制综合大学（地方重点大学）

3. 学生人数：在校生10,000名,外国留学生250名。

4. 学校位置：韩国第五大城市“光州广域市”，离首尔约300公里，乘车需3个半小时。

5. 学校优势

1）就业创业中心名门大学，与8,000多家韩国中小企业合作，韩国西南部大学中就业率排名第一。

2）提供丰厚奖学金
本科的第一个学期免40%的学费。第二学期根据上学期的成绩，免学费的30-100%。

大部分学生都会获得免学费40%-50%的奖学金。

注：对于高考成绩优秀的学生，入学时第一学期提供更丰厚的奖学金。

480-500分的学生免学费50%，501-550分免学费60%，551-579分免学费70%，580分以上免全部学费。

研究生院减免学费50%。学生可根据个人的学习能力获得更多的奖学金。

3）提供校内宿舍（设备完善、费用低廉）：6个月的食宿费用人民币6,000元左右（含一日三餐）。

4）提供校内外打工：我校为了管理好学生使学生安心学习。韩国语课程6个月后，出席率达到80%以上有需要勤工兼学的学生，提供校内外打工。

5）本科最后一个学期可在韩国自主实习。为就业打下良好基础。

6）专业无限制：韩国语能力达到（TOPIK）3级，保证入我校本科或研究生院。学生可选择任何专业。

7）大专或大学读完2年的学生插班可跨专业，2年即可毕业。
8）韩国语课程期间提供济州岛文化体验(学校负担60%，学生负担40%)。
9）主要专业

国际经营、物流流通、观光、酒店经营、服装设计、装潢设计、视觉影像设计（动漫）、工业设计、珠宝设计、摄影映像、演艺舞蹈、幼儿教育、护士、运动休闲、网路保安警察、新闻广播、广告企划、计算机工程、信息通讯、电气电子、新再生能源工程、社会福利、心理治疗、食品营养、代替医学

10）特色专业：美容美发学科、空乘服务学科

11）免费接机
12）申请一年语言课程的学生，如果6个月内达到韩国语能力考试（TOPIK）3级水平，申请我校本科的情况下，剩余6个月的学费可转成本科第一学期的学费。
