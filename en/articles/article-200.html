In recent years, South Korea has gradually become a popular choice for Chinese students. With South Korea's emphasis on the overseas study market, a series of incentive policies have been introduced, making studying in South Korea the "new favorite" for Chinese students. Korean universities tend to have relatively relaxed policies, so TOPIK (Korean Language Proficiency Test) scores are key to successfully applying to study in Korea.

Applying to stay in Korea is not very difficult

Industry insiders say that because South Korea is highly open, as long as students want to study in Korea, the application process is not very difficult. Generally, as long as students have a high school or undergraduate diploma and pass the language test, they can apply for a school and a student visa. Study abroad consultants say that students who study Korean in China and have TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korea) can directly apply for undergraduate or graduate programs at Korean institutions; while students without language scores who want to study in Korea need to apply for the "language course + undergraduate (or graduate)" admission model. Language courses last 1 to 2 years, and after completing and passing the exam, students transfer to the university they applied for for degree programs.

Language requirements for applying to undergraduate programs have been lowered

Study abroad experts say that due to the financial crisis in the past two years, scholarship policies at Korean universities have been affected, resulting in less than ideal enrollment outcomes. This year, many Korean schools have lowered TOPIK score requirements from advanced to intermediate, which is a positive opportunity for students interested in studying in Korea. Experts say that the cost of studying abroad is low, language proficiency requirements are not high, and scholarships can be applied for 30% to 100%. Some institutions have even opened applications for domestic secondary vocational school students.

For undergraduate applicants, many good universities only require a TOPIK score of 4 and a high school diploma, and several top universities also measure students' college entrance exam scores. For graduate programs at Korean universities, Korean language proficiency is even more crucial. Because graduate students can apply for scholarships ranging from 30% to 100%, and students with level 4 can generally receive 50% scholarships in their first semester.

Industry insiders remind that the current employment situation in South Korea is not very good, and the probability of foreign students working in Korea after graduation is low. According to feedback from international students, most students who obtain a degree can find good jobs in major cities like Beijing, Shanghai, and Guangzhou that support their majors when returning to China.

近年来，韩国渐渐成为中国留学生的热选地。随着韩国对海外留学市场的重视，一系列鼓励政策相继出台，韩国留学已成为中国学生的“新宠”。韩国各高校的政策较为宽松，想成功申请韩国留学，TOPIK（韩国语能力考试）成绩是关键。

留韩申请办理难度不大

业内人士表示，由于韩国开放程度高，所以只要学生想去韩国留学，办理难度都不是很大。一般来说，学生只要拥有高中或本科文凭，语言成绩合格就能够申请到学校和留学签证。留学顾问表示，如果是本身在国内学习韩语，并且有TOPIK（韩国语能力考试）成绩的学生，可以直接申请韩国院校的本科或研究生课程；而如果是没有语言成绩，又想去韩国留学的学生，则需要申请“语言课程+本科（或者研究生）”的入学模式。语言课程会持续1到2年，完成并考试通过后，转入所申请的高校学习学位课程。

申请本科 语言要求有所降低

有关留学专家表示，由于前两年的金融危机影响了韩国院校的奖学金政策，导致招生情况不太理想，今年韩国不少学校都把对学生TOPIK的成绩要求从高级降低为中级，这对有意到韩国留学的学生是一个利好机会。专家表示，留学成本低、语言水平要求不高、能申请到30%到100%的奖学金，部分院校还开放了国内中专生的留学申请。

对于申请本科的学生，不少好的院校只要求TOPIK成绩4级、拥有高中毕业文凭即可，而几所顶尖院校也会衡量学生的高考成绩。而如果是申请韩国高校研究生课程，韩语水平则显得更加关键。因为研究生可以可以申请到30%到100%不等的奖学金，一般具备4级水平的学生，在入学第一个学期都能拿到50%的奖学金。

业内人士提醒，韩国现在的就业形势不是太好，国外学生毕业后留韩工作的概率不高。从留学生反馈的信息来看，如果回国的话，拿到学位的大部分学生都能在北京、上海、广州等大城市找到和专业对应的不错的工作。
