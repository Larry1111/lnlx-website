The University of Melbourne

School Overview
The University of Melbourne, abbreviated as the University of Melbourne, was founded in 1853 and is a world-renowned research university and a leading academic hub in the Southern Hemisphere. It consistently ranks in the top 50 in major authoritative world university rankings over the years. In the 2015 QS World University Rankings, it ranked 32nd globally. In the 2015 US News rankings, it ranked 40th globally. In the 2015 QS subject rankings, Education ranked 5th in the world and 1st in Australia; Law ranked 8th in the world and 1st in Australia; Medicine ranked 18th in the world and 2nd in Australia; Finance and Accounting ranked 14th in the world and 2nd in Australia; Architecture ranked 15th and 1st in Australia; Electrical and Electronic Engineering ranked 33rd and 1st in Australia; Computer Science ranked 13th and 1st in Australia.

Campus distribution

The University of Melbourne has three campuses and one arts department: Parkville, Hawthorn, Bumley, and Victoria College of Art.

Overview of Melbourne
Melbourne is Australia The capital of Victoria, Australia A cultural and industrial center Southern Hemisphere The most renowned cultural city. Melbourne is Australia Victoria The capital city Green space rate Up to 40%. The Greater Melbourne area covers 3,400 square miles (8,806 square kilometers), making it one of the largest metropolitan areas in the Southern Hemisphere. Melbourne's urban environment is very elegant and has won awards United Nations Habitat Award, and for many consecutive years United Nations Human Settlements Programme Rated as "the world's most livable city."

Curriculum

The University of Melbourne has 14 faculties and disciplines in different fields.
School of Architectural Design: Architecture, Building Management, Landscape Architecture, Urban Planning.
Faculty of Arts: Humanities, Social Sciences, Mass Media, Linguistics, Arts Management, Asian Studies, etc.
Business Schools: Accounting, Finance, Actuarial Science, Economics, Marketing, International Business, E-commerce.
Faculty of Education: Pedagogy, Educational Management, Child Education, Educational Psychology, TESOL, Special Education.
School of Engineering: Electronic Communication Engineering, Software Design Engineering, Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Water Resource Management, Global Positioning Information System Management, Computer Science
School of Land and Food Resources: Agronomy, Forestry, Food Science and Technology, Horticulture, Agricultural Economics, Forestry Management, Wildlife Conservation, Natural Resources Management.
Law School: Law, Banking and Financial Law, Construction Law, Corporate Law, Dispute Law, E-commerce Law, Energy Law, Administrative Law, Health and Medical Law, Intellectual Property Law, International Law, International Tax Law, Labor Relations Law, Information and Communication Law.
Faculty of Medicine: Clinical Medicine, Dentistry, Nursing, Psychology, Health Sciences.
Conservatories: Wind Music, Strings, Music Education.
School of Science: Life Sciences, Mathematics and Statistics, Physics, Chemistry, Geography, Environmental Science, Biomedical Sciences, Information Systems, Optometry.
School of Veterinary Medicine: Veterinary Medicine, Animal Research.
Melbourne Business School: MBA, DBA
Graduate School: Responsible for managing all doctoral research subjects.
Victoria College of the Arts: Fine Arts, Music, Dance, Drama, Arts Production Center, Film & Television.

Admission criteria

Bachelor's degree: IELTS: 6.5
Graduate: IELTS: 6.5

Tuition fees

Degree programs cost between AUD 14,000 and AUD 32,000 per year

墨尔本大学 The University of Melbourne

学校概况
墨尔本大学(The University of Melbourne)，简称墨大，始建于1853年，是一所世界著名的研究型大学，南半球首屈一指的学术重镇 ，在历年各大权威世界大学排名中，稳居前50。2015QS世界大学排名全球第32位 2015年US News全球高校排第40名 。在2015年QS学科排名中，教育学世界第5，全澳第1; 法律学世界第8，全澳第1;医学世界第18，全澳第2， ，金融与会计专业世界第14，全澳第2 ，建筑学世界第15，全澳第1, 电气电子工程世界第33，全澳第1, 计算机科学世界第13，全澳第1。

校区分布

墨尔本大学有3个校区和一所艺术院系，分别为Parkville 校区， Hawthorn 校区， Bumley 校区和维多利亚艺术学院.

墨尔本概况
墨尔本(Melbourne)是澳大利亚维多利亚州的首府，澳洲文化、工业中心，是南半球最负胜名的文化名城。墨尔本是澳大利亚维多利亚州的首府，城市绿地率高达40%。大墨尔本地区面积达到3,400平方英里(8806平方公里)，是南半球较广大的都会区之一。墨尔本城市环境非常优雅，曾荣获联合国人居奖，并连续多年被联合国人居署评为"全球最适合人类居住的城市"。

课程设置

墨尔本大学共有14个不同专业学科的院系.
建筑设计学院: 建筑学，建筑管理，景观建筑，城市规划.
文学院: 人文学科，社会科学，大众传媒，语言学，艺术管理，亚洲研究等.
商学院:会计，金融，精算，经济学，市场营销，国际商务，电子商务.
教育学院: 教育学，教育管理，儿童教育，教育心理学，TESOL，特殊教育.
工程学院: 电子通信工程，软件设计工程，工程学，环境工程，水资源管理，全球定位信息系统管理，计算机.
土地与食品资源学院: 农学，森林学，食品科技，园艺，农业经济，林业管理，野生动物保护，自然资源管理.
法学院: 法学，银行金融法，建筑法，公司法，争议法，电子商务法，能源法，行政法，健康医疗法，知识产权法，国际法，国际税法，劳工关系法，信息通信法.
医学院: 临床医学，牙科，护理，心理，健康科学.
音乐学院: 管乐，弦乐，音乐教育.
理学院: 生命科学，数学与统计学，物理，化学，地理，环境科学，生物医学科学，信息系统，验光学.
兽医学院: 兽医学，动物研究.
墨尔本工商管理学院: MBA，DBA
研究生院: 负责管理所有博士研究科目.
维多利亚艺术学院: 美术，音乐，舞蹈，戏剧，艺术制作中心，影视。

本 科：IELTS： 6.5
研究生：IELTS： 6.5
