Choosing a Korean school

Most prestigious universities are concentrated in Seoul, the capital of South Korea. Their educational standards, facilities, and social environment are all better than universities in other regions. While they have the advantage of being able to find part-time jobs more easily, tuition and dormitory fees are about 30% higher than those at universities in other cities.

Dormitories are also the same. Most universities in Seoul do not have dormitories, so foreign students enroll in specialized dormitories operated by others near the school to solve their dormitory issues. Therefore, students studying in Korea should first consider their parents' financial situation before deciding whether to enter a prestigious university in Seoul where tuition and dormitory fees are expensive, or a local city university with lower tuition and dormitory fees.

If you want to study in a local city, try to choose universities in cities like Busan, Incheon, Daegu, Gwangju, or Daejeon, which is very beneficial for the student council.

Important notes for applying to study in Korea

Unlike China, advanced countries like South Korea develop education through private and public universities compared to national universities. 98% of South Korean universities are private foundations and officially recognized higher education institutions by the Korean and Chinese governments. Many of these private universities have ranked among the world's top 100. Therefore, private universities are not informal educational institutions; in fact, there are many private universities in South Korea that have better faculty and facilities than national universities.

Visa rates vary among Korean universities; it's not that prestigious universities have higher rates, but rather that Chinese students have lower rates of illegal stay and higher visa rates.

Korean universities issue admission permits through internal review, but the visa is not guaranteed once the letter is issued; the visa must be approved only after review by the Korean consulate or immigration office.

When applying for Korean study or through local Chinese agencies, applicants must confirm whether there is a Korean university certificate certified by the Chinese Embassy in Korea.

韩国学校的选择

大部分的名牌大学都集中在韩国的首都汉城地区，教育水平以及学校设施，社会环境都比其他地区的大学优秀，虽然有比较容易找到打工的优点，但是也有学费以及宿舍费比其他城市所在大学贵30%左右的缺点。

宿舍也是，大部分汉城地区的大学校内没有宿舍，外国学生入学时，通过学校附近的他人经营的专门宿舍解决外国学生的宿舍问题。所以希望韩国留学的学生可以先考虑父母的经济能力，决定进入学费和宿舍费贵的汉城地区名牌大学，还是进入学费以及宿舍费低廉的地方城市大学。

如果想到地方城市留学，尽量选择直辖市 釜山，仁川，大邱 ，光州，大田等城市的大学，对学生会很有利。

申请韩国留学 注意事项

像韩国这样的先进国家与中国不同，相对于国立大学，以私立大学以及公立大学发展教育，韩国98%的大学是财团法人设立的私立大学，是韩国以及中国政府认证的正式高等教育机关，这些私立大学当中进入世界百强的大学也很多。所以私立大学并不是非正规的教育机关，反而私立大学比国立大学所拥有的教师队伍和设备都好的大学在韩国很多。

韩国各个大学的签证率都不同，并不是名牌大学签证率就高，而是中国学生的非法滞留率低的大学签证率高。

韩国大学通过内部的审查发行入学许可书，但是并不是入学许可书出来了签证就100%出来，而是最终通过韩国领事馆或出入境事务所的审查才能得到签证。

申请学生或者中国当地的中介公司办理韩国留学手续时必须确认是否有驻韩中国大使馆认证的韩国大学认证书。
