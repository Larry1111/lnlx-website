    Regarding this American universities What kind of impression do you have? Relaxed and stress-free......

    In fact, there are many Studying in the United States Students share a common impression about American universities that studying there is very free, but completing their studies is not easy. If you don't believe it, just look at the ranking of the most stressful schools in the U.S. Simply put, this ranking is about how many times you pull an all-nighter.

    First place: Caltech

    As the top scorer on the list, "there is no doubt he has the most rigorous science and engineering undergraduate education." Caltech is a small but close-knit group. "There are intensive science and engineering courses here, professors are always ready to assist students with various research topics, and students are required to have a comprehensive understanding of the subjects. All of this gives Caltech students the opportunity to access a wide range of yet demanding courses.

    Second place: Franklin Olin College

    That's right, Olin College is one of the best academically charged schools in the United States, but it is also the most competitive school among peers. The "hands-on, project-based" learning approach allows many students to maintain close connections with classmates and professors, but it also makes students want to invest more effort to showcase their "excellence."

    A student at Ohlin College said, "I need to be an effective communicator, an influential team member, and a capable thinker." This attitude of striving for excellence is common in schools, driving students to throw themselves into academics at all costs to achieve their goals.

    Third place: Marlboro College

    Marborough College allows students to study in different subjects until their junior year. As a result, students devote all their energy to the various courses they plan with their mentors. The heavy writing schedule can also make the first few years very tough because they always need to plan ahead about their next steps.

    Fourth place: Reed College

    Reed College requires the most required credits for first- and second-year students. As a result, students feel a lot of pressure. From the moment they enroll, they must learn how to learn quickly and earn enough credits as early as possible. This way, they can determine their major in their senior year and graduate smoothly within four years.

    Fifth Place: Harvey Mudd College

    Harvey Mother College is one of the five Clymont College Alliance schools in the Southern California Inland Empire. It is a small science and engineering school. For students who value both academics and want some enjoyable time, this is the perfect place. The strict academic requirements here may be very stressful for some students, but most quickly gain support from professors and the entire student body.

    Sixth Place Mingde College

    Although Mingde College allows students to explore people and disciplines of their own interest, the top professors here also set the highest standards for students. Therefore, students here know how to use critical thinking skills and social resources to best meet the academic demands from the school and beyond.

    7th place: Massachusetts Institute of Technology

    "MIT, a treasure trove of engineering, technology, and mathematics on the East Coast. Here, you'll find endless information and endless possibilities to broaden your horizons. ”

    Eighth place: Princeton University

    What makes them proud is that students here are fully committed to their studies from the moment they enroll, only having a chance to catch their breath after graduation.

    Ninth place: Swarthmore College

    Swarthmore College's quirky learning atmosphere offers a wide range of courses, allowing students to overload themselves across different academic fields. This has earned Swarthmore College a spot on the list of "Most Hardworking Schools."

    Tenth place, Weber College

    A student at Weber College said, "This is the first time in my life I've studied so hard, but being able to pass the exam here with a C is already pretty good." This statement shows how much effort students invest in academics.


    对于美国大学你有什么样的印象?轻松、无压力……

    事实上，很多在美国留学的同学对于美国大学的共同感受就是，在美国大学就读很自由，但完成学业并不轻松。不信，就看看全美压力最大学校排名，简单来说，这个排名比的就是通宵多少次。

    第一名 加州理工学院

    作为榜单上的第一名，“毫无疑问他拥有最严格的理工科本科教育。加州理工是一个小而紧密的群体。”这里有繁重的理工科课程，这里的教授随时都愿意为学生在各种研究上提供帮助，这里要求学生在学科上有全面地认知。所有这些，让加州理工的学生有机会接触一系列广泛的却又繁重的课程。

    第二名 富兰克林欧林学院

    没错，欧林学院是全美学术上最好的院校之一，但它同时也是同龄人之间竞争最激烈的学校。“亲身实践，以项目为基础”的学习方法让很多学生和同学以及教授都能保持紧密的联系，但也因为这种联系，让学生常常想要花更多精力以显示自己“出类拔萃”。

    一位欧林学院的学生说，“我需要成为一个有效的沟通着、有影响力的队员以及一个有能力的思考者”。这种精益求精的态度在学校当中普遍存在，让学生为了达到目标不顾一切地投入学术。

    第三名 马尔波罗学院

    马尔波罗学院允许学生参加不同学科的学习，直到大三才定下自己的专业。如此一来，学生们都把所有的精力倾注于他们和导师一起制定的学习计划中的各种课程上。繁重的写作计划同样会让学生的前几年十分艰苦，因为他们总是需要事先想好下一步该怎么走。

    第四名 里德学院

    里德学院对大一、大二的学生的要求的必修课学分是最多的。因此，学生们都备感压力。从入学开始，他们就要学会如何快速学习并尽早拿到足够的学分。这样他们才可以在高年级的时候确定自己的专业并在四年内顺利毕业。

    第五名 哈维玛德学院

    哈维母德学院是南加州内陆帝国的五所克里蒙特学院联盟院校之一。这是一所小型的理工科学校。对于那些既注重学术、又想要一些愉快的时光的学生，这里 再适合不过了。这里严格的学术要求对某些学生可能会造成极大的压力，但大部分的学生都能很快地受到来自教授以及全体学生的支持。

    第六名 明德学院

    尽管明德学院允许学生在自己感兴趣的人和学科上进行探索，但这里顶尖的教授对学生的要求也是最高的。因此，这里的学生都懂得运用批判性思考的技巧和社交资源来最好地完成来自学校乃至更高的学习要求。

    第七名 麻省理工学院

    “麻省理工学院，东海岸的工程、科技和数学的宝地。这里有无穷的信息、无限的可能来开拓你的视野。”

    第八名 普林斯顿大学

    让他们引以为豪的是，这里的学生从入学开始就全情投入于专业的学习，直到毕业，才有喘息的机会。

    第九名 斯沃斯莫尔学院

    斯沃斯莫尔学院怪异的学习氛围提供了大量的课程，让学生在不同的学科领域都投入超负荷的学习。这也让斯沃斯莫尔学院荣登“最用功的学校”榜单。

    第十名 韦伯学院

    韦伯学院的一名学生说：“这是我有生以来第一次学习如此刻苦，但是在这里能够拿着C通过考试算是不错了。”这句话可以看出来，这里的学生在学术上得投入多少精力。
