When studying in Canada, students and parents must promptly understand the costs of studying abroad in Canada and prepare financially. If students do not make adequate financial preparations, it can cause unnecessary trouble in their study abroad life. Here are some reminders from study abroad experts.

1. Financial planning is very important before studying in Canada

Before going to Canada, students should arrange their Canadian accounts and remittance plans in advance to avoid financial shortages affecting normal study and life upon arrival. Proper financial arrangements are the foundation for studying in Canada. The amount parents deposit into their Canadian accounts must cover the child's monthly expenses to prevent insecurity caused by financial shortages. Students abroad face unfamiliar faces and environments to some extent. If this cannot be guaranteed, children may consider returning home early, which goes against the original intention of studying abroad. Because of this, many parents do not want their children to go abroad without sufficient funds; without financial security, everything else is even less secure. Therefore, preparing financially before going abroad is a necessary and indispensable process. Besides effectively preparing the Canadian student visa security deposit, students and parents also need to assess risks.

2. Banks also have discount programs

Before studying in Canada, after opening an account in China, you can obtain remittance through a domestic bank. The cost of studying abroad in Canada is quite substantial. Therefore, if your remittance amount is as high as $20,000 for living expenses, you can enjoy the free banking program for students. This will also save you a lot on future banking costs, but the only requirement is that you must meet the bank's requirements and provide the following documents:

1. ID card

2. Valid student visa

3. Passport

4. A valid admission letter for studying in Canada

去加拿大留学，学生和家长对于加拿大出国留学费用一定要及时的了解和财务的准备。因为学生如果对于财务方面的准备没有到位的话，那么会给学生的留学生活带来不必要的麻烦。下面就是留学专家的提醒。

一、去加拿大留学前，资金规划很重要

留学生在赴加前应该提前办理好在加拿大的账户及汇款存款事宜，防止出现抵达加拿大的时候，由于资金短缺影响正常的学习生活，做好财物的安排才是保障在加拿大学习的前提。家长存入加拿大账户的金额要能保证孩子每个月的正常开销，防止孩子因为出现资金短缺产生不安全感，因为远在海外的学子，刚刚面对一切陌生的面孔和陌生的环境总会有或多或少的不安全感，如果不能保证这点，那么孩子会提早有回国的念头，这就违背了当初留学的初衷了。也正是因为这一点很多家长在没有准备足够的费用的情况下是不希望孩子出国的，经济没保障其他的就更没有保障了。因此出国之前做好财务方面的准备是一个必要的过程也是不可或缺的过程。除了加拿大留学签证保证金的有效准备外，学生和家长还要进行风险预估。

二、银行也有优惠计划

去加拿大留学之前，在国内办好帐户后，可以通过国内银行批汇，加拿大出国留学费用需要一笔不小的数目。所以如果你所汇金额高达2万美金的生活费的话，就可以享受学生的免费银行服务计划。这样也为你未来的银行业业务省去了不少的费用，但唯一的条件是必须符合该银行所要求的条件，并且需要提供以下证件:

1. 身份证

2. 有效学生签证

3. 护照

4. 有效的留学加拿大录取通知书
