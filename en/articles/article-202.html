Studying abroad often requires preparing a deposit in advance, but different countries have very different requirements for deposits. Some require a deposit period, while others do not require a fixed period but may require a freeze period. Therefore, for students planning to study abroad, it is best to understand the financial preparation issue one to two years in advance.

According to reports, the required guarantee deposit amount in South Korea is ten thousand US dollars. However, to increase the chances of a successful application, students applying to schools in the Seoul area are generally advised to save 100,000 RMB. South Korea does not have a fixed requirement for the deposit period, but there is a requirement for a freezing period. That is, after depositing the money in the bank, students applying to schools in the Seoul area need to ensure the freezing period is more than one year; schools in surrounding areas require a freezing period of more than six months (the amount cannot be less than 80,000 RMB). For students whose household registration is not with their parents, it is best for the deposit to be in the student's own name.

After graduating from high school, students who go to Korea to pursue a bachelor's degree must first take a one-year Korean language course in Korea before they are eligible to apply for undergraduate programs. Study abroad experts told reporters that Korean language education at various universities in Korea has a complete and comprehensive curriculum. Through studying at the Korean language stage, international students mainly adapt to future undergraduate courses in Korea, including language, study methods, and cultural life, and acquire some foundational professional knowledge and study skills they will soon study at university.

Studying a Korean language preparatory course in South Korea costs 30,000 to 50,000 RMB per year. After entering a major course, depending on the major, the cost ranges from 40,000 to 80,000 RMB. However, most Chinese students can receive about 50% scholarships, so tuition generally does not exceed 50,000 RMB. Living expenses vary slightly by city: Seoul is the most expensive in Korea, costing 3,000 to 4,000 RMB per month; Living expenses in surrounding areas are slightly lower per year, around 30,000 RMB.

Applying for scholarships in Korea is the best way to reduce costs, and there are two ways: 1. Obtain scholarships unconditionally offered by your school. Generally, the top ten universities in Korea generally offer scholarships for international students. If you are a graduate student at a prestigious Korean university, you can generally receive a scholarship that covers 100% of the tuition fees.

2. Another category targets students pursuing graduate degrees or above from well-known science and engineering universities, with higher application requirements: GRE score of 1300 or above 1400, old TOEFL score above 600, and the new TOEFL test introduced in September 2007 requires above 100; Undergraduate studies at internationally renowned or well-known Korean science and engineering universities; Students who have published important papers during undergraduate studies and received recommendations from professors at prestigious universities. Meeting these conditions generally grants scholarships.

Due to rising prices, inflation, and other factors, the cost of studying abroad in some countries has increased year by year over the past five years. Some have increased tuition fees, others have raised security deposits. This year, tuition in the US and Australia has risen by 10%-40%, the UK, Canada, and New Zealand by 5%-10%, and public schools in Singapore by 20%-30%. This year, tuition fees in major study abroad countries have generally increased by about 10%, while university tuition in South Korea is growing at around 5% per year.

海外留学往往要提前准备好担保金，但是，不同的国家对于担保金的要求会有很大的区别。有的要求有存期，有的不要求存期但可能会要求冻结期限，因此，对于想要出国留学的学生来说，最好提前一年到两年就了解好资金准备的问题。

据报道，韩国的担保金数额要求一万美元，但是为提高学生的申请成功几率，一般申请首尔地区院校的学生最好存够十万元人民币。韩国对担保金的存期没有确定的要求，但是有冻结期限的要求，也就是存进银行后，申请首尔地区院校的学生需要保证冻结期限在一年以上；周边地区的院校也需要冻结六个月以上（金额不能低于八万元人民币）。户口跟父母不在一起的学生，存款最好是学生本人的名字。

高中生毕业后，赴韩国攻读学士学位，必须先在韩国攻读一年韩国语课程，才有资格申请大学本科课程。留学专家告诉记者，韩国语教学在韩国各大学都有一整套完善的课程设置，通过韩国语阶段的学习，留学生主要是在语言、学习方法、文化生活方面适应未来在韩国大学本科课程学习，并学习一些即将在大学阶段就读的专业基础知识和学习技巧。

在韩国就读韩国语预科，一年的费用在人民币三万－五万元，升入韩国的大学专业课后，根据专业的不同，费用在人民币四万－八万不等，但是大部分中国学生都可以拿到50%左右的奖学金，因此学费一般不会超过五万元。而生活费在不同城市有略微不同：首尔是全韩国最贵的，一个月需要三千－四千元人民币；周边地区的生活费每年比首尔略少一些，三万元人民币左右。

留学韩国申请奖学金是减少费用的最佳途径，有两种方式：1.获得学校无条件提供的奖学金。一般韩国排名前十位的大学基本上都提供留学生奖学金。如果是韩国名牌大学的研究生，一般可以得到100%免除学费的奖学金。

2.还有一类针对攻读知名理工大学的研究生及以上学位的学生，申请的要求比较高，GRE成绩1300分或1400分以上，旧托福考试六百分以上，2007年9月实施的新托福考试需一百分以上；本科阶段就读国际知名或韩国知名的理工大学；学生在本科阶段发表过重要论文，得到知名大学教授的推荐。满足这些条件，一般也能获得奖学金。

因物价上涨、通货膨胀等因素，近五年来一些国家的留学费用逐年上升。有的增加学费，有的提高担保金，今年美国和澳大利亚学费上涨10%－ 40%，英国、加拿大和新西兰上涨5%－10%，新加坡公立学校上涨20%－30%，今年主要留学国家费用普涨10%左右，韩国大学学费则以每年5%左右的速度增长。
