How to apply for a visa for the study abroad examination in Japan

How to apply for a visa when going to Japan for entrance exams

If you have a sponsor in Japan, you can apply for a short-term visa to Japan. In short-term visas, going to Japan for "taking an exam" is generally called an "exam visa," and you need to submit your school's exam admission ticket when applying. Generally, as long as the application is correct, the visa will be issued based on the exam date.

Study abroad experience

How to overcome the adaptation phase after going abroad

Besides the difficulty of adapting to daily life, there are also significant differences in learning between Chinese students and local students.

First, there are differences in the education system.

Chinese students receive Chinese cultural education from a young age, focusing on learning textbook knowledge and rote memorization. They have learned knowledge points more deeply. They have strong exam-taking abilities. This is why most Chinese students have excellent scores in physics and chemistry. In contrast, Western education trains students who focus more on self-expression, communication skills, and innovative thinking. Their mastery of knowledge points and hands-on ability are higher than those of Chinese students. This is something students need to understand.

Second, differences in the education system cause students to struggle with learning:

Chinese students who are used to learning whatever the teacher teaches are not accustomed to expressing their own views and opinions. Therefore, "Chinese students not actively participating in classroom discussions" has become the most frequently mentioned aspect by Canadian schools when summarizing the characteristics of Chinese students.

For Chinese students, there are also several phenomena that may make adaptation difficult:

Phenomenon 1: Foreign teachers do not bring textbooks to class, but list more than ten reference books for students to borrow from the library after class; Phenomenon 2: At the start of the new semester, students have to write papers, but before the class begins, teachers assign homework. How to complete papers and how to learn from many in the library has become a difficult problem for Chinese students. Phenomenon 3: Teachers ask questions, students find answers.

There was an example: at UBC, Canada's most famous university, a professor posed a question on the blackboard in the first class and then asked students to seek answers. Some Chinese students could not accept this, thinking the professor's level was too low, but they didn't realize that the professor wanted students to learn to think independently and summarize, transforming knowledge into their own in search of answers.

In this regard, Chinese students should be mentally prepared before going abroad. They should not blindly assume their exam-taking skills are high or that their knowledge is deeper than that of local students. Nor should they stubbornly cling to their study habits in China. Otherwise, they will not be able to adapt to studying and living abroad.

Finally, I hope every student studying abroad can adapt well to local life, complete their studies soon, and realize their dreams.

去日本参加入学考试该如何办理签证手续

在日本有身份保证人的话就可以申请去日本的短期签证。在短期签证中，“为参加考试”去日本一般称为“考试签证”，申请时需要提交学校的考试准考证。一般情况下，只要申请内容没有问题，就会根据考试日期发给签证。

留学经验

如何克服出国后不适应阶段

除了在生活上的不适应。中国学生和当地学生在学习上也存在不小的差异。

首先，教育制度上有区别。

中国学生着从小接受的是中国的文化教育，学生注重课本知识学习，注重死记硬背。知识点学得比较深。应试能力较强。这就是为什么中国学生多数理化成绩非常好。而西方教育培养的学生更注重自我表达，注重交流技巧和创新思维。知识点掌握的广度和动手能力高于中国学生。这一点学生是需要了解的。

其次，教育制度的差异，造成学生在学习上的不适应：

习惯了老师教什么，就学什么的中国学生不习惯表达自己的看法和意见。所以，“中国学生不积极参与课堂发言”已经成为加拿大校方在总结中国学生特点时最常提到的方面。

而对于中国学生而言，也有以下几个让他们不适应的现象：

现象一，国外的老师上课不带教科书，列了十多本参考书让学生课后到图书馆借阅；现象二，新学期刚开课就要写论文，还没讲课老师就布置作业。如何完成论文，怎么在图书馆中博采众家之长，成了中国学生的难题。现象三，老师提问题，学生找答案。

曾有这样的例子：加拿大最著名的大学，UBC大学的教授在第一节课上就在黑板上提出一个问题，然后要求学生去寻求答案。于是有中国学生无法接受，认为这个教授水平太低，却不知道教授是要学生学会独立思考和总结，在寻求答案中将知识转化为自己的东西。

针对这一点，中国学生在出国之前要有心理准备。不能盲目的认为自己应试能力高，知识点掌握的深于当地学生。更不能死抱着国内的学习习惯不变。这样是无法适应国外的学习生活的。

最后，希望每一个赴国外留学的学生都能很好的适应那个当地的生活，早日完成学业，实现自己的梦想。
