The University of Exeter is located in Exeter, UK, with a population of about 100,000, making it an important commercial center in the southwest of the UK. The university has several campuses, with the largest, Streatham, covering 245 acres, featuring modern teaching buildings, student dormitories, and a comprehensive building including a graduate center built on the savannah. The university's outstanding research and teaching standards have made the University of Exeter one of the most popular universities in the UK. In 2010 British universities In the overall TIMES rankings, the university is ranked 9th, with Economics 6th and Accounting and Finance 2nd.

The University of Exeter has 10,500 full-time students and 2,500 part-time students. The total number of international students exceeds 1,200, with international students from 115 countries worldwide, accounting for about 10% of the total student body, of whom 200 come from various European countries.

It offers postgraduate foundation courses, undergraduate foundation courses, and intensive English courses. Computer science, dance, drama and film, French, geography, German and related languages, Italian, mathematics, statistics and computational studies, physics, and astronomy are all strong programs at the university. Finance and computer science are ranked among the top ten in the UK.

Renowned majors: Biology, Economics, Business Administration, Chemistry, Finance, Engineering and Computer Science, etc.

Admission requirements for undergraduate foundation courses: Must be at least 17 years old, IELTS 5.0, currently in senior high school or having graduated from high school.

Master's diploma program entry requirements: IELTS score of 6.5 or above to obtain a bachelor's degree.

Language course start dates: Four times a year, in January, April, July, and September.

www.exeter.ac.uk

埃克塞特大学位于英国埃克斯特市，这里人口大约10万人，是英国西南部重要的商业中心。该大学有数个校园，其中最大的Streatham校园面积245英亩，现代化的教学楼、学生宿舍和包括研究生中心在内的综合楼就建在稀树草原上。出色的大学研究水平和教学水平使得埃克塞特大学成为英国最受欢迎的大学之一。在2010年英国大学综合排名(TIMES)上，该校排名第9位，其中经济学排名第6位，会计金融专业第2位。

埃克塞特大学有全日制学生10，500人，Part-time students有2，500人。国际学生总人数超过1200人，其留学生来自世界115个国家,约占总学生数的10%，其中有两百学生事来自欧洲各国。

设有研究生预科课程、本科预科课程、英语强化课程。而计算机科学，舞蹈、戏剧和影视，法语，地理学，德语和相关语言，意大利语，数学、统计学和运算研究，物理学和天文学都为该大学强项专业。金融和计算机专业排名则位列全英前十。

知名专业：生物学、经济学、工商管理学、化学、金融学、工程与计算机科学等。

本科预科课程入学要求：年满17周岁，雅思5.0分，高三在读或高中毕业。

硕士文凭课程入学要求：雅思6.5分以上，获得学士学位。

语言课程开课日期：每年1、4、7、9月四次开学。

www.exeter.ac.uk
