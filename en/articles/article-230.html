The University of Glasgow Caledonia is one of the largest universities in Scotland, with a well-equipped campus located in the heart of the vibrant city of Glasgow. Since its founding in 1875, the university has been renowned for providing high-quality teaching and research. The University of Caledonia welcomes students from around the world and is known for its warm and friendly nature, allowing students to benefit greatly from a multicultural environment.

The university is located in the city center of Glasgow, a metropolis, with very convenient transportation and easy access to buses or trains. Glasgow also has its own international airport, and road and rail links are connected to Europe. The residents are warm and friendly, and the social security is good, making it a great place to live and work. The campus is very modern, and social and academic life is rich and colorful.

As one of Scotland's largest universities, the school has a total of 17,000 students, with about 3% international students from over 100 different countries.

The university has seven faculties: the Faculty of Architecture and Natural Environment, Caledonian Business School, Engineering and Computer Science, Health and Social Welfare, Life Sciences, Law and Social Sciences, and Nursing, Obstetrics and Community Health. Among them, Caledonia Business School has carried out many innovative projects in risk management, e-commerce, and international fashion markets. The school has art-grade laboratories and several outstanding research centers.
The university's strong relationships with various industries enable it to design courses focused on employability skills and provide the skills needed for the future. The university ranks second in Scotland, and its graduates are highly sought after in the job market.

Admission requirements for undergraduate foundation courses: Must be at least 16 years old, IELTS 5.0, currently in senior high school or having graduated from high school.

Admission requirements for master's foundation courses: Bachelor's degree, IELTS score of 5.0 or above (2 semesters require a GPA of 5.5 or above).

Language course start dates: Four times a year, in January, April, July, and September.

www.gcu.ac.uk

格拉斯哥喀里多尼亚大学是苏格兰规模最大的大学之一，校园设备齐全，位于活泼的格拉斯哥市的中心位置。自1875年成立以来，学校就一直以提供高质量的教学和研究而闻名。格拉斯哥喀里多尼亚大学欢迎来自全球各地的学生，以热情友好而著称，学生可以从多元文化环境中受益颇多。

大学位于格拉斯哥这个大都市的市中心，交通十分便利，可以方便搭乘公交车或火车。格拉斯哥市还有自己的国际机场，公路和铁路交通也与欧洲相连。市民热情友好，社会治安状况良好，是一个生活和工作的好地方。校园非常现代化，社交和学习生活丰富多彩。

作为苏格兰最大的大学之一，学校共有学生17,000名，国际学生约占3%，来自100多个不同国家。

学校共设7个学院，分别为建筑及自然环境学院、喀里多尼亚商学院、工程与计算机学院、健康与社会福利学院、生命科学学院、法律及社科学院、护理、产科及社区健康学院。其中喀里多尼亚商学院在风险管理、电子商务和国际时尚市场等领域开展了很多创新项目，学校有艺术级的实验室和好几个优秀的研究中心。
大学与各业界强有力的关系使得学校能设计以就业技能为重点且提供未来所需技巧的课程。学校大学排名是苏格兰第二，而且毕业生在就业市场中也是极受欢迎。

本科预科课程入学要求：年满16周岁，雅思5.0分，高三在读或高中毕业。

硕士预科课程入学要求：本科毕业，学士学位，雅思5.0以上（2学期课程要求5.5以上）。

语言课程开课日期：每年1、4、7、9月四次开课。

www.gcu.ac.uk
