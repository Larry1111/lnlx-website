We understand that the process of applying to study in the United States can often be frustrating, so we offer you personalized, one-on-one consultation services, carefully guiding you from the start of your study abroad preparation to successfully entering the U.S. campus.

Step one: Student consultation

■ Fill out the "Consultation and Evaluation Form" ■ On-site comprehensive evaluation of student qualifications and study abroad intentions, provide targeted study abroad recommendations, and preliminarily recommend suitable institutions.

Step two: Sign the contract

■ Develop a student study abroad schedule, introduce the admission requirements and characteristics of the desired universities and majors, and recommend U.S. institutions that meet their application criteria. Ultimately, both parties confirm the desired institution.

Step three: Apply to schools

■ Prepare personalized application materials to confirm the school ■ Send materials to the school ■ Receive school admission information ■ Receive admission results

Step four: visa counseling

■ Develop a visa training schedule ■ Prepare visa materials, schedule a visa interview, design your career, prepare visa Q&A, and conduct mock training.

Step Five, Consulate Interview

■ Bring all your visa documents to the U.S. embassy or consulate for a visa interview and receive the visa result.

Step Six, Pre-Trip Guidance

我们知道美国留学申请程序常常令人头疼，因此我们专门为您提供了个性化的、一对一的咨询服务，从您开始准备留学到顺利走进美国校园，全程为您细心护航。

第一步，学生咨询

■ 填写“咨询评估表” ■现场综合评估学生条件和留学意向，针对性提留学建议，可初步推荐合适院校。

第二步，签约

■ 制定学生留学时间表，向学生介绍意向院校和专业的入学要求及特点并推荐符合其申请特点的美国院校，最终双方确认要申请的院校。

第三步，申请学校

■准备确定申请院校的个性化申请材料 ■材料寄往学校 ■获得学校录取信息 ■取得录取结果

第四步，签证辅导

■制定签证培训时间安排表 ■准备签证材料，预约签证面谈时间，职业设计，签证问答准备及模拟培训。

第五步，领馆面试

■ 带齐签证材料赴美国使领馆签证面谈，获得签证结果。
