School Introduction

Politecnico di Milano is located in the city center of Milan, the capital of Italy's most prosperous Lombardy region. It is a university with a long history, a wide range of disciplines, and a strong faculty. Founded in 1863, Politecnico di Milano is the most prestigious industrial university in Italy for engineering, architecture, and industrial design, with over 1,000 professors, 18 departments, and more than 40,000 students. From bioengineering to information and communication technology, nanotechnology to aviation, the university participates in advanced research activities across many fields. Like many leading research institutions, the university conducts both basic research and technology transfer. Politecnico di Milano consists of seven campuses. Among them, six campuses are located in the Lombardy region: two campuses in Milan, Milano-Leonardo and Milano-Bovisa; The other four campuses are located in the following towns: Como, Lecco, Cremona, and Manto. There is also one campus in Pacenza.

Politecnico di Milano currently has 9 faculties, 26 undergraduate programs, and more than 40 master's programs, with a total enrollment of over 20,000 students. This modern Italian national university attracts international students and visiting scholars from more than 80 countries around the world. They mainly come from Spain, Portugal, Argentina, Brazil, Germany, France, Austria, the United States, China, the Philippines, Vietnam, Japan, South Korea, Arab countries, several African countries, and more.

Curriculum

Aerospace engineering, architectural projects, architecture and planning, bioengineering, architectural and environmental science and technology, chemical materials and engineering, architectural history, electrical engineering, electronics and information, thermodynamics, industrial design, art, communications and apparel, water conservancy, environment and surveying, management, economics and industrial engineering, mathematics, mechanical, nuclear power engineering, physics, structural engineering, and more.

学校介绍

米兰理工大学座落在意大利最富饶的伦巴第大区首府米兰市中心，是一所有着悠久历史，专业分布极为广泛，师资力量雄厚的大学。米兰理工大学成立于1863年，是意大利以工程学，建筑学和工业设计最负盛名的工业大学，拥有1000多名教授，18个院系，4万多名学生。从生物工程到信息与通讯技术、纳米技术到航空领域，大学参与很多领域的高级研究活动。跟很多典型的领先研究机构一样，大学从事基础研究，也从事技术转化等。米兰理工大学由7个校区组成。其中有6个校区位于伦巴第地区：2个校区位于米兰，Milano—Leonardo和Milano—Bovisa；其他4个校区位于下列城镇：Como，Lecco，Cremona，Manto。另外还有一个校区位于帕琴察。

米兰理工大学现有9个学院，26个本科专业和40多个硕士专业，注册学生总数超过20000人。这所现代的意大利大学国立大学吸引着来自世界上80多个国家的留学生和访问学者。他们主要来自西班牙，葡萄牙，阿根廷，巴西，德国，法国，奥地利，美国，中国，菲律宾，越南，日本，韩国，阿拉伯世界国家，非洲数国等等。

课程设置

航空工程、建筑项目、建筑和规划、生物工程、建筑和环境科学与技术、化学材料和工程、建筑史、电气工程、电子和信息、热力学、工业设计，艺术，通讯和服装、水利，环境和测量、管理，经济学和工业工程、数学、机械、核电工程、物理、结构工程等。
