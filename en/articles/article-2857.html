In recent years, studying for undergraduate or master's degrees abroad has become a popular choice for Chinese students. In daily application consultations, some junior college students often seem somewhat insecure: they apply to retake undergraduate programs and worry about spending time and money, since they already have associate degree experience; they apply for graduate studies but do not have a bachelor's degree. These are some of the dilemma many college students face when considering studying in the United States. So, how should junior college students apply to study in the United States? Puri Education points out that domestic college students are also recognized by American universities. The U.S. recognizes the credits taken by Chinese college students, and the credits are basically equal to those taken for undergraduates. Below, we will introduce several options for junior college students applying to study in the United States.

Currently, there are mainly two types of junior college graduates studying in the U.S.: "Bachelor's to Bachelor's Degree" and "Bachelor's to Master's Degree." The former is suitable for current college students and recent graduates, while the latter is suitable for college graduates with work experience.

1. Bachelor's degree transfer plan — transfer to the United States for undergraduate studies

In recent years, the number of applicants to transfer to the U.S. has been surging, and competition has become increasingly fierce. Here's a reminder: it's best to consult a professional study abroad consultant and explain your current academic status. Whether you're currently studying at a junior college, have already graduated, or how many credits you can transfer to your major, these factors all have a certain impact on your successful transfer to the U.S.

Regarding transferring to the U.S., unlike domestic universities, changing schools and majors is very common. Junior college graduates who choose to transfer to the U.S. for undergraduate studies generally have already earned a certain amount of credits at domestic universities, while the U.S. uses a credit system. If the transfer is successful, some of the credits you earned at the domestic university can be transferred to the U.S. institution. This greatly saves you time and money studying abroad, giving you a better advantage in applying for a master's degree after graduation.

There are also certain skills and knowledge to transfer credits. When applying for a transfer, not all credits can be transferred at once. Generally speaking, legitimate foreign schools have stricter credit transfer evaluations, so don't assume that all the credits you have taken in China will be transferred smoothly. Additionally, because the education systems in China and the United States differ, the curriculum and textbooks differ significantly, meaning junior college students need to put in more effort to overcome difficulties in course transition.

2. Graduate Transfer Plan — "Preparatory Course" or "Direct Master's Advancement"

1. Foundation course first

The first way to upgrade from junior college to master's is 'foundation first.' Some American institutions offer 'continuation' master's foundation courses (also called 'master's bridge courses') for junior college students. For students who graduate from two-year colleges or those with limited work experience, foreign universities generally require students to take a six-month to one-year master's foundation program to study the language and core undergraduate courses before proceeding to a master's program. The advantage is that master's foundation programs do not require high English proficiency, with an IELTS score typically only around 5.5. Moreover, American universities offer relatively flexible start dates for master's foundation programs, allowing students to choose according to their language proficiency and schedule.

Recommended top U.S. preparatory master's programs: University of Southern California, California State University, Pace University, University of South Florida, Oregon State University, George Mason University, and others.

Recommended top universities in the United States for upgrading from junior to master's degree: Northeastern University, Tiffin University, New York Institute of Technology, etc.

2. Direct master's advancement

The second way to upgrade from junior college to master's is "direct master's degree." Generally, students who graduate from a full-time three-year college diploma can choose the "associate degree-to-master's program." As long as the major matches, these students can directly enroll in master's programs. Generally, within no more than two years, they can obtain a master's diploma, and the master's degree obtained is recognized globally.

For most college students, obtaining a master's degree directly saves time and benefits the most. However, since China and the US have mutual recognition of academic qualifications, without a bachelor's degree, it is still difficult to directly apply for a master's degree from a U.S. university. If you are not familiar with transferring abroad, are not confident about the application, or do not want to rush to study abroad, you can choose to first "upgrade from college to bachelor's degree" in China to obtain a bachelor's diploma and degree, then choose to pursue a master's degree in the U.S.

近年来，出国读本科、读硕士一直是中国学子的热门选择。在日常申请咨询中，常有一些专科生显得有些底气不足：申请重读本科，担心耗费时间和金钱，毕竟已有了专科经历；申请读研，却没有本科文凭。这是众多大专生想留学美国的一些苦恼。那么，专科生究竟该如何申请美国留学呢？普瑞教育指出：国内大专生，也是受到美国大学认可的。美国认可中国大专生所修习的学分，且与本科修习的学分基本一视同仁。下面就为大家介绍一下专科生申请美国留学的几种方案。

目前专科生留学美国主要有两种：“专升本”和“专升硕”。前者适合大专在读生和应届毕业生，后者适合有工作经验的大专毕业生。

一、专升本方案——转学赴美读本科

由于近些年，转学美国申请人数在不断激增，竞争也越来越激烈。这里提醒大家，最好咨询一下专业的留学顾问，说明自己目前的学业状况，无论是专科在读，还是已经毕业，或者所学专业能转学分的多少，这些因素对成功转学美国都有一定的影响。

关于美国转学，与国内大学不同的是，在美国大学里，转学校、转专业是非常普遍的。专科生选择转学去美国读本科，一般都已经在国内高等院校修了一定的学分，而美国采用的是学分制，如果转学成功的话，你在国内高校所修的部分学分是可以转到美国院校的。这样就大大节省了你在国外读书的时间和费用，对将来毕业后申请硕士也更有优势。

转学分也有一定的学问和技巧。申请转学时，并不是所有的学分都能全部转过去。一般来说，国外正规的学校在学分转移的评定上都比较严格，所以不要以为你在国内选修的学分都可以顺利转过去。另外，由于国内和美国的教育体制不同，课程设置和教材也有很大区别，所以这就意味着专科生需要付出更多的努力，去克服课程衔接上的困难。

二、专升硕方案——“预科先行”或“直接升硕”

1.预科先行

专升硕的第一种方式为“预科先行”。美国的一些院校会为专科生提供类似“续本”的硕士预科课程（也可叫做“硕士桥梁课程”），对于二年制大专毕业的学生以及工作经验不足的三年制大专生，国外高校一般要求先读半年到一年的硕士预科课程，学习语言及本科期间的核心课程，才可以继续升读硕士学位课程。优势在于硕士预科课程对学生的英文能力要求不高，雅思一般只要求5.5分左右。而且，美国大学的硕士预科课程有比较灵活的开学时间，学生可根据自己的语言水平和时间自由选择。

硕士预科课程美国名校推荐：南加州大学、加州州立大学、佩斯大学、南佛罗里达大学、俄勒冈州立大学、乔治梅森大学等。

专升硕美国名校推荐：美国东北大学、蒂芬大学、纽约理工大学等。

2.直接升硕

专升硕的第二种方式为“直接升硕”。一般全日制三年大专毕业的学生都可以选择“专升硕”课程，只要专业对口，这类学生可直接报读硕士课程，一般最多不过二年的时间就可以拿到硕士文凭，取得的硕士学位能得到全球认可。

对于大多数大专生来说，如果能够直接取得硕士学位，无疑是最节省时间成本又同时能获得最大利益。不过，由于中国和美国之间是学历互认制的，因此如果没有本科学位，还是很难直接申请到美国大学的硕士学位的。如果你对转学到国外不够了解、对申请没太大的把握，或不想扎堆转学赴国外读本，还可以选择先在国内“专升本”，拿到本科文凭和学位，然后在选择赴美读硕。
