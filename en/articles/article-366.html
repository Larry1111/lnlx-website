College Introduction

The Milan Music School is one of the most important music institutions in Milan, Italy, founded in May 1891. At that time, Italian opera was in the era of Verdi, inspiring the entire European music scene. Young musicians and music educators initiated the founding of this music school. For over a century, it has trained countless outstanding orchestra conductors, performers, singers, and music teachers worldwide. Due to its prestigious status and the fact that its teachers mainly come from the world's top La Scala Opera House, the school has gained worldwide fame, with 800 students from around the globe studying and training here every year.

With the development of the times, the school has opened its doors to the world to keep pace with the rapid changes of the world. Its curriculum is exactly the same as that of the National Conservatory of Music, with main courses taught individually and group classes in groups. Exams are conducted by teachers from the National Conservatory of Music, who award students annual learning evaluations, year-end performance reports, and more. Besides classes, there are many rich and colorful practical activities, internship performances, concerts, and roles and performers for opera houses and orchestras

Harp, singing, guitar, clarinet, classical piano, composition, double bass, French horn, music education, symphony conducting, accordion, flute, recorder and transverse flute, poetic music, choral music and horn, electronic music, chamber music, oboe, bass, organ and organ composition, piano, saxophone, orchestral arrangement, percussion instruments, trumpet, cornet, trombone, guqin, double bass, violin, cello. Harmony, stage arts, composition, poetry, drama, literature, traditional total score composition and reading, chamber music, organ, piano, quartet, sight-singing and ear training, music aesthetics and history.

学院简介

米兰音乐学校是意大利米兰的最重要的音乐学府之一，始建于1891年5月由于当时意大利歌剧正处在威尔第的歌剧时代，带动了整个欧洲的音乐界。于是年轻的音乐家和音乐教育家们发起创立了这所音乐学校。一百多年来为世界各国培养出了无数优秀的乐队指挥，演奏家，歌唱家和音乐教师。由于它优越的地位和他们的教师主要来源于世界上最顶尖级的斯卡拉歌剧院，使这所学校蜚声于世界，每年拥有800名来自世界各地的学生在这里学习及进修。

随着时代的发展学校为了顺应世界的日新月异，他的大门也向全世界敞开。他的课程设置完全和国立音乐学院的课程相同，主课以单独授课的方式，共同课采取小组课制。考试由国立音乐学院的老师们主持考试，给学生颁发年度学习评语，年终的汇报演出等等。在学校内除上课之外还有很多丰富多彩的实践活动，实习演出，音乐会，为歌剧院和乐团提供角色和演奏员
