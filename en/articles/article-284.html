Reasons why a Japanese visa may be denied

A: When the Immigration Bureau of the Ministry of Justice reviews an "Student" or "Student" application, if it is determined that the reasons are not "Study" or "Studying," or that the applicant is deemed inqualified, or that the tuition bearer lacks sufficient financial resources, or the applicant has improperly written application documents, the visa will be refused.

Therefore, having sufficient information, handling application procedures carefully, or entrusting an experienced study abroad center to handle the process can reduce the rejection rate.

Why is the extension of the Japan Student (School) visa being denied?

A: Before the "Student" or "Student" visa expires, if the attendance rate in Japan is below 90%, or if there is no remittance record from the tuition payer in the bank account, the Immigration Bureau may refuse the extension.

People who are prone to visa rejection

Countries refuse to issue visas to certain individuals

Generally, countries do not issue visas to the following individuals: patients with infectious diseases, mental illnesses, drug users, drug traffickers, prostitutes, those who have been deported, those who endanger national security and social order, terrorists, those who obtain visas through forged documents, and those without guaranteed living expenses.

If the applicant's first visa application is due to a legally stipulated travel to a country (region) and is refused, there is no need to apply for a second time. If the reason for the first rejection is related to documents or materials, you should prepare all the documents before applying, or apply at the time agreed by the embassy (consulate) officials. It is especially important to strive for approval on the second attempt. Otherwise, the chances of obtaining a visa on the third application are even lower. Therefore, preparations after the first refusal are very important.

日本留学 (就学) 签证延期为何被拒绝呢？

Ａ： 「留学」或「就学」签证到期前，在日本办理签证延期时，提出在学出席率若低於９０％， 或银行存款簿没有学费负担人汇款记录，入国管理局可能拒绝签证延期。

容易拒签的人

各国拒绝给哪些人发签证

一般情况下，各国对下列人员都不会发给签证：传染病患者、精神病患者、吸毒者、贩毒者、娼妓、曾被驱逐出境者、危害国家安全和社会秩序者、恐怖分子、用伪证骗取签证者、生活费用无保障者等。

如果申请人第一次申办签证属于前往国家（地区）按法律规定而被拒绝的，申请人则没有必要再去第二次申办。如果第一次遭到拒绝的原因属于证件材料等方面的，则应该准备齐全证件材料后再去申办，或者按使（领）馆官员约定的时间再去申办。特别值得注意的是，一定要争取第二次获得批准。否则，第三次再去申办，获签的可能性就更小了。因此，第一次拒签后的准备工作十分重要。
