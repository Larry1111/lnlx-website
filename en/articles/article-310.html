Requirements and procedures for applying to study in Italy

Published on 2011-08-16·Source: Liaoning Overseas Study Service Co., Ltd.·Viewed 9,461 times

Requirements: Applying for undergraduate (three-year program): High school graduate, currently enrolled in university, college entrance exam score not less than 380. Master's (two-year program): Relevant major, currently in senior undergraduate year, graduated with undergraduate or graduate degree, and holds a bachelor's degree. Conditions:
Applying for undergraduate (three-year program): High school graduate, currently enrolled in university, with a college entrance exam score not less than 380. Applying for master's degree (two-year program): Relevant major, currently in senior undergraduate program, graduated with undergraduate or graduate studies, and holds a bachelor's degree. Process: Consultation → signing→ collection of original materials→ notarization and authentication→ pre-registration university→ visa application→ successful visa → overseas services

条件：
申请本科（学制三年）： 高中毕业、大学在读，高考成绩不低于380分 申请硕士（学制二年）： 相关专业、本科大四在读、本科毕业或研究生在读，有学士学位。 流程： 咨询→签约→收取原始材料→公证、认证→预注册大学→申请签证→签证成功→境外服务
