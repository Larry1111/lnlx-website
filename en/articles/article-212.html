Sun Moon University

1. Overview of the school

Sun Moon University is the international university in Korea with the largest number of international students. More than 800 international students from over 50 countries around the world study here. Sun Moon University is also home to the Asian branch of the World University Alliance (WUF). In 1998, President Lee Kyung-jun proposed the Asian University Alliance led by Sun Moon University, which carries out various activities to promote friendship and peace in Asia.

3. Admission Qualifications

1. Healthy high school graduates or students with equivalent qualifications need to complete 12 years of basic education equivalent to primary, middle, and high school in China.

2. Foreign students whose parents are both foreign nationals.

3. Applicants whose Korean language scores do not meet the school's requirements cannot advance to undergraduate programs.

4. Ethnic Korean students with good overall conditions can also consider it.

鲜文大学

一、学校概况

鲜文大学校是在韩国拥有外国留学生最多的国际化大学。全世界50多个国家来的800多名留学生在这里学习。鲜文大学校也是世界大学联合组织（WUF）亚洲地区支部所在地，1998年李京埈校长提议并由鲜文大学校主导的亚洲大学联盟，为增进亚洲的友谊与和平展开着各种形式的活动。

三、入学资格

1、身体健康的高中毕业生或具有同等学历的学生， 需要完成相当于我国小学，初中，高中，共12年基础教育。

2、 外国籍的学生，父母双方都是外国籍。

3、 韩语成绩未到达学校规定者，不能升入本科。

4、 综合条件好的朝鲜族学生也可以考虑。
