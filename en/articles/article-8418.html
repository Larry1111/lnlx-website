Siberian State Medical University


[Introduction]: Siberian State Medical University is one of the oldest medical universities in Russia. It was founded in 1978. At that time, it was the first higher medical department in Asia at Tomsk State University in Russia. Since the founding of the medical department, many outstanding scholars have contributed to the development of national medicine to some extent through their research achievements.

School Introduction

Siberian State Medical University (Сибирский государственный медицинский университет) was founded in 1888 and is located in the city of Siberia, Russia. Siberian State Medical University is one of the oldest medical universities in Russia and enjoys a prestigious reputation both in Russia and in Europe as a medical university. Siberian State Medical University has more than 700 faculty and staff members.

Specialized Settings

Siberian State Medical University, under the direction of the Russian Ministry of Education, offers the following majors based on full-time education: Law, Philosophy, Psychological Counseling Education, Organizational Psychology Education, Clinical Psychology, Special Psychology, Medicine, Pediatrics, Medical and Preventive Work, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Nursing, Accident Trauma Assessment, Forensic Medicine, Child Care, Rehabilitation Medicine, Sports Human Medicine, and Anatomy.

School facilities

Siberian State Medical University also has a dedicated international student office, medical center, football field, swimming pool, basketball court, tennis court, and student dormitory to assist international students.

西伯利亚国立医科大学


【导读】：西伯利亚国立医科综合大学是俄罗斯最古老的医学大学之一。成立于1978年。当时它作为俄罗斯托姆斯克皇家大学在亚洲地区的第一个高等医学系存在。从医学系成立的那天起，就有很多优秀的学者以自己的研究成果在一定程度上促进了国家医学的发展。

学校简介

西伯利亚国立医科大学(Сибирский государственный медицинский университет )成立于1888年，位于俄罗斯的西伯利亚市。西伯利亚国立医科大学是俄罗斯最古老的医科大学之一，在俄罗斯乃至欧洲医科类大学都享有盛名。西伯利亚国立医科大学拥有在校教职员工700余名。

专业设置

西伯利亚国立医科大学在俄罗斯教育部的指示下以全日制教育为基础开设以下专业：法学，哲学，心理咨询教育，组织心理学教育，临床心理学，特别心理学，医药学，儿科，医疗、预防工作，牙科，药剂，护理，事故创伤鉴定，法医学，儿童护理，康复医学，运动人体医学，解剖学。

学校设施

西伯利亚国立医科大学还设有专门为留学生提供帮助的留学生办公室医疗中心，足球场，游泳馆，篮球场，网球场，学生宿舍。
