School Introduction

Perugia University for Foreign Studies was founded in 1921 and is located in the city center of Perugia, a famous Italian city. It is the first stop for many foreign students studying in Italy. The founding mission of Perugia University for Foreign Students is to acquire knowledge of Italian language, culture, and historical civilization. Since its founding, Perugia University for Foreign Students has adhered to an open and broad educational philosophy, where people from various countries and cultures come together and exchange ideas. Today, it has developed into an international multicultural training center.

Today, our school's characteristics are especially evident in the teaching of Italian (non-native languages) and in teaching various subjects that use Italian as a medium, including arts, literature, economic systems, and production systems. Our mission is to promote the spread of Italian culture worldwide and to enhance mutual understanding among different groups.

In 1921, Perugia University for Foreign Nationals began offering various departments to foreigners, and to this day it has attracted tens of thousands of foreign students and teachers from around the world to study. In addition, the university has significant external promotion capabilities, influencing both abroad and various Italian language teaching centers, as well as in all regions that, due to historical reasons and current affection, are deeply interested in Italy and the Italian language, as well as in our country's science and culture

Perugia University for Foreign Countries has a long history and is the oldest institution in Italy for teaching Italian and promoting Italian culture and civilization. Its mission is to promote the spread of Italian language and culture worldwide and to enhance mutual understanding among different groups. Therefore, Perugia University for Foreign Students is known as the "Ambassador of Italy's International Exchange."

学校简介

佩鲁贾外国人大学创办于1921年，位于意大利著名城市佩鲁贾的市中心, 是许多国外学生留学意大利的第一站。佩鲁贾外国人大学的立校宗旨在于对意大利语言、文化及历史文明等知识，从创办以来，佩鲁贾外国人大学一直坚持开放和开阔的办学特点，各国人士，各种不同文化在此融会并交流，时至今日已发展成为一个国际化的多元文化培训中心。

本校的特点今日特别体现在意大利语(非母语)的教学中，以及其他以意大利语作为媒介的各个学科的教授，包括艺术，文学，经济体系，生产体系。本校的使命是为促进意大利语文化在世界各国的传播和为增进各个不同群体之间的相互了解而作出贡献。

1921年，佩鲁贾外国人大学开始为外国人设立各个科系，至今已吸引了世界各地的成千上万名外国学生和教师前来学习。除此之外，本校还拥有极大的对外宣传能力，影响遍及国外和各个意大利语教学中心，以及所有由于历史原因和现今因好感而对意大利和意大利语，对我国的科学和文化甚感兴趣的地区

佩鲁贾外国人大学历史悠久，是意大利资格最老的教授意大利语和传播意大利文化和文明的高等学校。起使命是促进意大利语言和文化在世界各国的传播，增进各个不同群体之间的相互了解，佩鲁贾外国人大学因此有“意大利对外交流的使者”之称。
