Urawa International Academy is located in Saitama Prefecture. The school is a 10-minute walk from JR Keihin-Tohoku Line Kita-Urawa Station, making transportation convenient: about 35 minutes from Tokyo Station and 30 minutes from Ikebukuro and Akihabara.

School Features

1. The school has a life counselor who emphasizes communication with students. To deepen communication and friendly interactions among students, our school offers extracurricular activities, social tours, school trips, and lectures on Japanese cultural experiences.

2. To ensure all students successfully pass the Japanese Language Proficiency Test and the Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students, small class sizes are conducted. Comprehensive and thorough guidance is provided to students who wish to enter university.

3. A scholarship system is in place, rewarding students with excellent grades and high attendance rates.

4. Student dormitories available, and apartments with good environments and reasonable prices can be introduced according to student requirements.

Registration Requirements:

1. High school, technical secondary, vocational school under 22 years old

2. College diploma, under 25 years old

3. Bachelor's degree, under 28 years old

2. 为了使全体学员顺利通过日本语能力考试、日本留学考试，实行小班授课。对于想考取大学的学生进行全面、彻底的指导。

3. 设有奖学金制度，对成绩优良、出勤率高的学生给予奖励。

4. 有学生寮，并可根据学生要求，介绍环境好、价格适宜的公寓。
