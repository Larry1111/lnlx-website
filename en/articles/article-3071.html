As the 2015 application season gradually enters its most intense phase, we have also summarized and analyzed the 2015 admission criteria for all U.S. universities.

Revealing six major changes and trends in U.S. college admission standards in 2015

Change 1: Application deadlines are moved up, and EA results come out earlier

Change 1: Application deadlines are moved up, and EA results come out earlier

Many public universities encourage or explicitly require applicants to submit materials early so that staff have enough time for review. Some schools even require students to apply as early as September of the previous year, and the announcement date for EA (Early Application) results is correspondingly moved up significantly.

Change 2: Further reducing reliance on high school teacher recommendations

Many university admissions staff say that recommendation letters written by high school teachers are increasingly lacking in reference value. NACAC surveys found that the proportion of colleges where recommendation letters have a "fairly important" impact has dropped from 21% to 17%.

Change 3: More emphasis than before on the difficulty of high school courses taught by applicants

Research shows that the more students who study overly difficult courses in high school, the more likely they are to succeed in college. As a result, university admissions departments are increasingly inclined to eliminate applicants who are easier to study through courses during high school.

Change 4: Greater emphasis on the quality of papers written by applicants

When it comes to essay applications to American universities, there is greater emphasis on the quality of the essays written by applicants, especially at top universities, which they value highly. They can even decide whether to admit an applicant based solely on this single item.

Change 5: More emphasis on applicants' performance in senior year

In the past, colleges focused heavily on the student's performance in the first two years of high school when admitting new students, but the situation is now changing. Barlinger from the University of Washington said, "We believe the performance in the senior year is what matters. We don't want to see them slack off at this time, but rather make their studies even harder." "

Change 6: The review of applicants' application materials has become stricter

Change 6: The review of applicants' application materials has become stricter

Some universities, including Harvard, are strengthening the review of applicants' submitted materials. More and more schools are using a specialized software called Turnitin to determine whether applicants' papers have plagiarized.

On the other hand, more universities have made WES degree authentication a mandatory option for applicants. In recent years, there has been a growing trend of degree fraud, and more university admissions offices have become more cautious.

New changes in undergraduate freshmen applying to study abroad: pay attention to the timing when applying, don't wait until a few days before the deadline to start submitting, and pay attention to your academic performance and essay writing during high school.

随着2015申请季渐渐进入最白热化的阶段，我们也对所有美国大学2015录取标准做了一次总结和分析。

揭秘2015美国大学录取标准六大变化及趋势

变化一：申请截止日期提前了 EA 出结果更早

变化一：申请截止日期提前了 EA 出结果更早

很多公立大学鼓励或明确要求申请者尽早提交材料，以便让校方的人员有足够的时间来进行审查。有些学校甚至要求学生在前一年9月就提出入学申请，而要申请EA (早申请)的结果公布日期也相应提前了不少。

变化二：进一步减少对于高中教师推荐的依赖

不少大学招生部门人员表示，现在高中教师写的推荐信越来越缺参考价值，NACAC的调查发现，那些高中教师所写推荐信具有"相当重要"作用的高校所占比例已经从21%下降到17%。

变化三：比以往更加注重申请者高中所学课程的难度

相关研究表明，在高中时越多学习过高难度课程的学生就越有可能在大学阶段取得成功，因此高校录取部门也日益倾向于淘汰掉那些在高中阶段专挑容易以课程学习的申请者。

变化四：更加重视申请者所写论文的质量

在美国大学申请文书方面，更加重视申请者所写论文的质量，特别是一流名校对此尤为看重，他们甚至凭此一项便能决定是否录取申请者。

变化五：更注重申请者高三学年的表现

以往高校在录取新生时总是高度聚焦于此人高中头两年的表现，但目前的情况正在发生变化，华盛顿大学的巴林格表示："我们认为高三这一学年的表现才是至关重要的，我们不希望看到他们此时松松垮垮地混日子，而是应该进一步加大学习难度。"

变化六：对申请者的申请材料的审查更为严格

变化六：对申请者的申请材料的审查更为严格

包括哈佛在内的一些大学正在加强对于申请者所提材料的审核力度。越来越多的学校开始采用一种名叫Turnitin的专业软件，来判别申请者所写论文是否存在抄袭行为。

另一方面更多的大学开始将申请者提供WES学位认证列为强制必须选项。近年来学历造假有增多的趋势，越来越多大学招生办变得更谨慎。

本科新生申请留学新变化，大家在申请时要注意时间，不要等到deadline前几天才开始递，并且要注重自己高中时期的学术成绩，文书的书写等方面。
