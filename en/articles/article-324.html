French visa application procedures

1. Register on the CELA assessment center website. You will receive a CELA registration number (which is personal for you only) and your personal page.
2. Go to your personal page and fill out the form. Please carefully fill in the relevant heading content in the form. You may complete your form in multiple times, but please make sure to save the information you provided after each completion. Once you have completed all your registration forms, please confirm the form.

3. Pay the exam fee at any Bank of China in your city via bank transfer or cash transfer as prompted. At the same time, return to your personal page and fill out the payment status form to ensure your payment is confirmed promptly.

4. Please check your personal page regularly every day. The CELA center will notify you through your personal page to select the date and time for your exam.

5. At any Bank of China in your city, pay the corresponding amount of the assessment interview fee as prompted by bank transfer or cash transfer. At the same time, return to your personal page to fill out the payment status form so your payment can be confirmed promptly.

6. Please send copies of all your assessment interview materials to the designated address via the post office. To ensure your documents are confirmed as soon as possible, you should prepare all your documents in advance.
7. After your documents are sent, please check your personal page regularly every day. If your documents are incomplete, the CELA Assessment Center will notify you through your personal page to provide the missing documents. If your documents are complete and have been reviewed and your assessment interview fee is confirmed, The CELA assessment center will notify you via your personal page to select the date and time for your CELA interview assessment.
8. On your personal page, select the date and time for your CELA assessment interview. Once selected, the date and time cannot be changed.
9. On the day of the assessment interview, please bring all your original documents.
10. After the interview, the CELA assessment center will transfer your materials to the visa office.
11. Please check your personal page regularly: The CELA assessment center will notify you that you can submit your visa application.

12. Go to the Visa France website to make a visa appointment. On the agreed date and time, please bring all documents to the visa office of the French Embassy in Beijing to submit your visa application.
13. Afterwards, you will receive a notification on your personal page to retrieve your passport.

1. 在CELA评估中心网站注册。您将获得一个CELA注册号（该号码仅为您个人所有）及您的个人页面。
2. 进入您的个人页面填写表格。请认真填写表格中的相关标题内容。您可以分多次填写完整您的表格，但是每次填写完毕，请注意保存您所填写的信息。当您全部填写完毕您的报名表后，请确认该报名表。

3. 在您所在城市的任意一家中国银行通过银行转账或现金转账等方式，按提示缴纳相应金额的考试费。同时回到个人页面填写付款情况表，以便您的付款及时得到确认。

4. 请每天定期查看您的个人页面，CELA中心将通过您的个人页面通知您选择您考试的日期和时间。

5. 在您所在城市的任意一家中国银行通过银行转账或现金转账等方式，按提示缴纳相应金额的评估面试费用。同时回到个人页面填写付款情况表，以便您的付款及时得到确认。

6. 请您通过邮局将您所有评估面试材料的复印件寄到指定地址。为了使您的文件尽早得到确认，您应提前将文件准备齐全。
7. 您的文件寄出后，请每天定期查看您的个人页面。如果您的文件不完整，CELA评估中心将通过您的个人页面通知您补充所缺文件。如果您的文件完整并审核完毕且您的评估面试费得到确认，CELA评估中心将通过您的个人页面通知您选择您CELA面试评估的日期和时间。
8. 在您的个人页面上选择您参CELA评估面试的日期和时间，日期和时间一旦选定，一律不得更改。
9. 评估面试当天请携带您所有文件的原件。
10. 面试结束后，CELA评估中心负责将您的材料转至签证处。
11. 请定期查看您的个人页面：CELA评估中心将会给您可以递交签证申请的通知。

12.进入 Visa France网站，进行签证预约，在约定的日期和时间，请您携带所有文件到北京法国驻中国大使馆签证处递交您的签证申请。
13. 随后，您将在您的个人页面收到取回护照的通知。
