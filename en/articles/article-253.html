1. Introduction to Yousong University International Business School:

Yousong University International Business School is an all-English-taught international business school, with professors from Europe and America who graduated from prestigious universities in Europe and America, including Harvard University, Yale University, University of Pennsylvania, University of Michigan, and Cornell University. Currently, students from more than 30 countries worldwide come from the school, and the school's goal is to cultivate Asia-level economic experts and business talents. Currently, the business school ranks first in Korea and is the only truly international business school.

The International Business School is currently accredited by the Association of Asia-Pacific Business Schools (AAPBS) and is a member of the AACSB (the most authoritative global accredited MBA program). Currently, only Guanghua Management School and China Europe International Business School are Chinese universities that have received these two accreditations

2. Advantages of the International Business School

1. Multilingual advantage

Provides all-English education, with students required to learn a second or third foreign language,

Including Korean and Japanese; students can also study Russian, Vietnamese, Malay, etc.

Students will be proficient in at least three languages upon graduation.

2 A top-tier team of renowned professors

60% of our business school professors come from the Ivy League alliance in the United States, including Harvard University, Yale University, Cornell University, Brown University, University of California, Berkeley, Pennsylvania State University, and other world-class institutions. The faculty is among the strongest in Korea and even Asia, ranking first among business schools in Korea.

3An international learning environment

4. International student team

Currently, students come from 30 countries worldwide, including the United States, Japan, Russia, and others. Student counselors and regular academic staff all come from English-speaking countries, creating a truly all-English environment.

1) A Little United Nations for students from 40 countries,

2) A multicultural learning environment

3) Vast language environment

4) Solid network of contacts

5) An equal and relaxed learning atmosphere

6) Study with students from well-known domestic universities: Beijing Foreign Studies University, Nanjing University, Sun Yat-sen University, Sichuan University, East China Normal University, Hunan University, Xi'an International Studies University, Hebei Normal University, Shandong University of Finance and Economics, Sichuan International Studies University......

5. A high-platform employment and internship network

With employment and internship networks from over 80 Korean companies and 14 multinational companies in the Fortune Global 500, graduates can choose to work for Korean companies or European or American companies after graduation.

6 , and opportunities to study at prestigious American universities

During the four years of university, you can implement the "2+2" program or pursue graduate studies at a prestigious U.S. university after graduation:

1. Georgia Tech, Georgia Tech (Ranked 17th in the nation)

2. State University of New York (Top 50 in the U.S.)

3. Kennesaw State University (Ranked among the top 70 in the nation)

7 . Low fees: currently, annual tuition plus living expenses is $14,000, about one-third of European and American institutions.

8 . A strong academic atmosphere, held every two weeks Distinguished Speakers bring together governments, professors, scholars, and entrepreneurs from various countries, providing knowledge across social, economic, and cultural fields—an academic atmosphere rarely matched by other schools.

9 . High-intensity study mode and multiple research project pressures (each semester is divided into two small terms, with course pressure and workload three times higher than those at typical Korean institutions).

3. Major Offerings

Undergraduate majors (BBA, four years) include: International Finance, International Marketing, International Business, Information Technology Management, and Entrepreneurship.

The MBA program offers the following directions: Financial Management, Strategic Management, Marketing Management, Information Technology Management, and Entrepreneurship.

4. Scholarship Amount and Quota

First Prize: Full tuition for the first year; 1-2 winners

Second Prize: 50%-80% of first-year tuition waived; 3-5 winners

Third Prize: 30%-50% of first-year tuition waived; 4-8 winners

Fourth Prize: 0%-30% tuition waiver for the first year; 5-10 winners

The specific number of spots depends on student performance

[The scholarship amount starting from the second year after entering solBridge International Business School depends on your academic performance. If your GPA (total GPA is 4.5) is above 3.7, you can continue to apply for a higher scholarship than at enrollment.]

5. Student Registration Qualifications:

Senior high school students, college graduates, and bachelor's graduates are all eligible;

Good English and academic performance are required

6. Determination of Scholarships:

The final scholarship amount is mainly determined based on IELTS mock test scores, academic performance at school, and recommendation letters from the school. Other English exams and awards can also be used as references.

Students from 30 countries worldwide, including the United States, Japan, Russia, and others, with student counselors and regular academic staff all from English-speaking countries, creating a truly all-English environment.

一、又松大学国际商学院简介：

又松大学国际商学院是一所全英文授课的国际商学院，授课教授都是来自于欧美国家，并且都毕业于欧美名牌大学，包括哈佛大学、耶鲁大学、宾夕法尼亚大学、密西根大学、康奈尔大学等。目前国际商学院学生来自于全世界30多个国家，商学院培养的目标着眼于培养亚洲地区级的经济专家和商务人才。目前该商学院在韩国排名第一，是唯一的一所真正国际商学院。

国际商学院目前已经获得亚太商学院联合会（AAPBS）的认证并已经成为美国国际管理教育联合会(AACSB，全球认证MBA项目最权威的机构) 的会员。目前能获得此两项认证的中国大学只有光华管理学院和中欧国际商学院

二、国际商学院的优势

1.多语言优势

实行全英文教育，同时要求学生学习第二或第三外语，

包括韩语、日语；学生也可以学习俄语、越南语，马来西亚语等。

学生毕业时将至少掌握三种语言。

2、顶尖的大牌教授团队

商学院的教授60%来自于美国常青藤大学联盟包括：哈佛大学、耶鲁大学、康奈尔大学、布朗大学，加州大学伯克利分校和宾西法尼亚州立大学等世界级名校，师资在韩国乃至亚洲最强，在韩国的商学院排名中名列榜首。

3、国际化的学习环境

4.国际学生团队

目前有来自于全世界30个国家的学生，包括美国、日本、俄罗斯等国家，学生辅导员和普通的教务人员等均来自于英语圈国家，真正的全英文环境。

1)40个国家学生的小联合国 ,

2)多元文化的学习环境

3) 广阔的语言环境

4)坚实的人脉网络

5) 平等宽松的学习氛围

6)与国内知名大学学生一起学习:北京外国语大学、南京大学、中山大学、四川大学、华东师范大学、湖南大学、西安外国语大学、河北师范大学、山东财政学院、四川外国语大学……

5.高平台的就业和实习网络

拥有韩国80多家企业和世界500强中14家跨国企业的就业和实习网络，学生毕业后可以选择在韩资公司和欧美公司就业。

6、美国名校学习机会

在大学四年中可以实行“2+2”或大学毕业后赴美国名校读研：

1. 佐治亚理工大学Georgia Tech（全美排名第17位）

2. 纽约州立大学New York State University（全美排名前50）

3. 肯尼索州立大学Kennesaw State University（全美排名前70）

7．费用低廉，目前一年的学费加生活费用为1.4万美元，为欧美院校的1/3左右。

8．浓厚的学术氛围，每两周一次的Distinguished Speakers汇集了来自各国政府、教授、学者和企业家，从这里可以了解到社会、经济和文化各个领域的知识，如此浓厚的学术氛围很少有学校可以达到。

9．高强度的学习模式、多重的科研项目压力（，每个学期分成两个小学期，课程压力和课程量是普通韩国院校的三倍。

三、专业设置

本科专业（BBA，学制四年）有：国际金融、 国际市场营销、 国际商务、 信息技术管理、创业学。

工商管理硕士（MBA，学制两年)专业设有以下几个方向：金融管理、战略管理、市场营销管理、信息技术管理、创业学。

四、奖学金额度及名额

一等奖：第一年学费全免；1-2名

二等奖：第一年学费50%-80%免除；3-5名

三等奖：第一年学费30%-50%免除；4-8名

四等奖：第一年学费0%-30%免除；5-10名

具体名额视学生成绩而定

【进入solBridge国际商学院后第二年开始的奖学金额度根据在校成绩而定，如GPA(总分为4.5)能在3.7以上,则奖学金额度可继续保持,如GPA4.0以上,可以申请比入学时更高的奖学金额度】。

五、学生报名资格：

在校高三学生、大专毕业生、本科毕业生均可；

要求英语良好、学习成绩良好

六、奖学金的确定：

最后奖学金额度的确定主要是根据雅思（IELTS）模拟考试的成绩、在校的学习成绩和学校的推荐信，其他的英语考试和获奖情况也可以作为参考。
