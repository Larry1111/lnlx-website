Q&A on Korean Student Visas

1. What visas are required to study in South Korea?

Answer: There are three visa categories. One is the C3 visa, a short-term visa for up to 3 months; The other is a visa for more than 6 months (D4 and D2)

2. I just want to study the language in Korea for 3 months. Is that okay?

Answer: No, you cannot. Because nowadays, whether you want to study a language or apply to university, you must apply for a return visa at the Korean Immigration Service. The study period for the return visa is more than six months.

3. What is the difference between the D2 visa and the D4 visa?

Answer: Both are return visas, but the visa terms differ. D2 is a student visa, valid for 1 year; D4 is a language visa, valid for 6 months.

4. Can you apply for a study visa (D2) without a language foundation?

Answer: Some universities allow you to apply directly for a student visa. After entering university, you can study both the language and your major courses, and the credits earned for language learning are directly counted toward your university credits. Among the schools our company represents, several allow you to apply for a student visa directly, which saves time and allows you to enter university directly.

5. Which visa is the main one for Korean universities?

Answer: Different student requirements require different visas. However, as long as the applicant meets the requirements, either D2 or D4 can apply.

6. Can a D4 visa be converted into a D2 visa?

Answer: Yes, you can. As long as you enter university, you can convert to a D2 student visa.

7. What visa do I need to work part-time?

Answer: According to Korean law, international students holding a D2 visa can legally work part-time.

8. After studying the language, can I convert to a work visa in Korea?

Answer: Yes, you can. As long as you are hired by a Korean company before completing your language studies, that company will help you convert to a work visa.

1、 韩国留学需要什么签证？

答：有三种签证类别。一种为C3签证，3个月以内的短期签证；另一种为6个月以上的签证（D4和D2）

2、 我就想在韩国学3个月语言，可以吗？

答：不可以。因为现在不管去学习语言，还是申请大学，都得在韩国入管局申请返签证。返签证的学习期限就是6个月以上。

3、 D2签证和D4签证有什么区别？

答：都是返签证，只不过签证期限不同。D2是留学签证，期限为1年；D4是语言签证，期限为6个月。

4、 没有语言基础，可以申请留学签证（D2）吗？

答：有些大学可以直接申请留学签证。进入大学之后，一边学习语言，一边学习专业课程，而且学习语言的学分直接算到大学学分上。我公司代理的学校中，有几所学校可以直接申请留学签证，既省时间，又能直接升入大学。

5、 韩国大学，主要拿哪一个签证？

答：学生条件的不同所申请的签证也不同。但是，只要申请的学生条件符合，D2或者D4均可申请。

6、 D4签证可以转换成D2签证吗？

答：可以。只要升入大学之后，可以转换成D2留学签证。

7、 我持什么签证才能打工？

答：韩国法律上规定，留学生持D2签证才能合法打工。

8、 能不能读完语言之后，在韩国转成工作签证？

答：可以。只要读完语言之前，被韩国的哪个公司录用，这个公司帮你转成工作签证。
