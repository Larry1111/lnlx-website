The University of Maine is a top university in Maine, located in the famous Orono University Town on the outskirts of Bangor. It is also one of the most outstanding public universities in New England, offering the strongest majors and the most reasonable tuition fees. The University of Maine has five colleges: the College of Arts and Sciences; the College of Education and Human Development; the College of Engineering; the College of Natural Sciences, the College of Forestry and Animal Husbandry, the School of Business, and the School of Public Policy and Rehabilitation, offering over 140 undergraduate and 80 graduate programs respectively.

English Name: The Universities of Maine

School website: http://www.umaine.edu/

Main Campus: Maine

English requirement: TOEFL530 or IELTS 5.0

Tuition: $22,652 per year

The University of Maine (The University of Maine at Orono) is the largest university in Maine, USA. It was founded in 1862. The University of Maine has more than 110,000 students in total. The University of Maine is commonly known as "UMaine" or "UMO." It is located in Orono, not far from Bangor. The Fogler Library at the University of Maine is the largest in Maine, with over one million books in total, and there are also some Chinese journals. The university has an anthropology museum. There is an ethnology and oral history museum, an art gallery, an observatory, a social science research center, a water and land research center, an experimental farm, and a marine research center. It is well-equipped with sports facilities and facilities.

The university once had 419 international students, accounting for 3.3% of the total student body at the time. The University of Maine has a relatively high acceptance rate for high school graduates, reaching 80%. Female students make up 53% of the total student body.

After the federal Congress passed the "Land Grant Act" in 1862, Maine was influenced by this and decided to establish the "Maine College of Agriculture and Technology" in 1865. It opened on September 21, 1868. At that time, there were only 2 teachers and 12 students; in 1871, the school offered three majors: agriculture, civil engineering, and mechanical engineering. In 1872, female students were admitted for the first time. In 1881, master's degrees were awarded. In 1897, it was renamed "University of Maine." In 1923, a graduate school was established; in 1930, the College of Education was founded; and in 1958, the Business School (the School of Business Management) was founded.

The quality of first-grade students in the 2002~2003 academic year is as follows: middle students scored 980~1170 on the SAT. 21% were in the top 10 in their high school class, 50% were a quarter of their class, and 86% were in their class

They are the top half of their high school class.

Among undergraduates, 6,875 are full-time students, and 1,636 are part-time. The latter make up 19.2% of the total. The ethnic distribution among undergraduates is as follows: 93% White, 2% Indian and 2% international students, and 1% each of Asian, Black, and Hispanic students. Maine is located in the northeast corner of the United States, closest to Europe, but farthest from Asia and Latin America, so the proportion of white students is especially high, while Asian, Hispanic, and African American students are particularly low.

Among undergraduates, 86% are native Americans, only 12% are non-state, and another 2% are foreigners. 45% board with university students, and most find their own accommodation off-campus. The university has no fraternities or sister organizations, and certainly no students participate.

The top five popular majors and their student proportions are: 16% of undergraduates study engineering, 15% study humanities education, 10% study business administration, 8% study health sciences, and 7% study social sciences and history. These together account for 56%. The Honors College currently has about 500 students. The students are highly qualified and have more opportunities to communicate with top-tier professors. The students are numerous and highly engaged, and discussions with them greatly help students understand what they are learning. Moreover, the courses at the college are entirely self-selected. For example, English majors can take courses in theory and physics, making them top-level professionals in various fields.

The university has 720 faculty members. The total annual cost per in-state student is $12,998, while out-of-state students are $20,738. Since 1998, the University of Maine has awarded $18 million in scholarships and $54 million in grants, with an average scholarship and financial aid of $7,880 per student per year. 46% of students receive loan grants.

缅因大学(The University of Maine，全称The University of Maine at Orono)是美国缅因州最大的大学。它1862年成立了。 缅因大学一共有11万多个学生。 缅因大学的短名是"UMaine"或者"UMO". 它在欧洛诺市(Orono), 离班哥尔市(Bangor)不太远. 缅因大学的佛格拉图书馆(Fogler Library)是缅因州最大的, 一共有一百万多本书, 也有一些中文杂志。该校有人类学博物馆。民族学及口述历史博馆，美术馆，天文台，社会科学研究中心，水陆研究中心，实验农场，海洋研究中心。体育场馆与设施齐全。

该校曾经有419位外国留学生，占当时学生总数的3.3%。缅因大学对高中毕业生的录取率比较高，达80%。女生占全校学生的53%。

在1862年联邦议会决定“赠地办学法案”后，缅因州受此影响，于1865年决定办“缅因农业与技工学院”。1868年9月21日开学。当时只有2位老师，12位学生，1871年学校开设了农业、土木工程机械工程3个专业。1872年首次招收女生。1881年开始授予硕士学位。1897年改名为“缅因大学”1923年成立研究生院，1930年成立教育学院，1958年成立商学院（即企业管理这院）。

是他们高中班上的前1/2的学生。

本科生中有6875人是全日制学习，有1636人是兼职学习。后者占总数的19.2%。本科生中的族裔分布如下：白人占93%印第安学生和留学生各占2%，亚裔、非裔、和西裔学生各占1%缅因州在美国的东北角上，离欧洲最近，而离亚洲及拉丁美洲最远，所以白人这生比例特高，而亚裔、西裔和非洲裔学生特少。

本科生中有86%是本洲人，只有12%是外州人，另有2%是外国人。有45%和大学生寄宿在学校，大部分人在校外自找食宿。这所大学没有兄弟会和姐妹组织，当然也没有学生参加。

五大热门专业及学生比例是：有16%的大学生学工程，有15%的人学教育，有10%的人学工商管理，有8%的人学保健专业有7%的人学社会科学和历史。以上共占56%。荣誉学院现有学生约500人。学生素质高，他们可以与第一流的教授有更多的交流机会。同学中高人如林，与他们的谈话讨论能极大地帮助自己理解所学的内容。而且该学院课程完全由自己选定。例如，英语专业的学生可认修理论,物理课程，对各行各业都是得心应手的顶尖人物。

该校有教师720名。本州生每人每学年总费用12998美元，外州生20738美元。自1998年以来缅因大学已经发放1800万美元的奖学金和5400万美元的助学金，学生平均得奖助学金是每人每年7880美元。46%的学生是贷款助学金。
