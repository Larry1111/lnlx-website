SAMU Education College is located in the bustling Shinjuku district of Tokyo. It is a language education institute offering Japanese language learning for international students. Founded in 1986, it is now celebrating its 25th anniversary. After graduation, the main schools for further study include 14 graduate schools, 31 undergraduate programs, 4 university junior colleges, and 17 vocational colleges, including the renowned University of Tokyo and Waseda University. Our campus is situated in the heart of Tokyo, making both living and transportation very convenient. The school's facilities and learning atmosphere create an excellent learning environment for students. Since April 2012, a new teaching building has been in use. We welcome all students to join our team, and their dreams start here.
Outstanding Faculty Lineup:

Our teachers have extensive teaching experience. We implement a regular teacher evaluation system to provide students with high-quality teaching services.

Scholarship System:

For students with excellent grades and a serious attitude toward learning, the school offers both on-campus and off-campus scholarships.

Guidance for further education and daily life:

Our school provides face-to-face individual guidance for further education, helping students smoothly enter their ideal universities. To help students quickly adapt to life in Japan, overcome difficulties, and solve their worries, all faculty and staff are always ready to lend a helping hand.

School Facilities:

The school building is a three-story building, fully air-conditioned, and includes a library, health room, free internet access computer classrooms, and dedicated bicycle parking.

Colorful extracurricular activities:

Every year, a variety of extracurricular activities are regularly held, such as bus trips, sports days, picnics, and extracurricular learning.

Exchanges with sister schools:

Regular exchange meetings are held with Japanese students from our sister school, Bridge Academy. We combine the Japanese learned in class with practical applications to deepen our relationships with Japanese people.

Convenient living environment:

Our school is located in the heart of Tokyo. It is about a 2-minute walk from Shin-Okubo Station on the Yamanote Line, making transportation very convenient. Nearby the school are a library, gymnasium, post and telecommunications office, bank, park, and other amenities, making it very convenient to live.

Alumni Reunion:

To provide graduates with a place to exchange information about work, study, and other areas, our university holds an alumni reunion once a year. Through alumni reunions, students are connected to enhance communication among graduates.

SAMU教育学院位于东京都最为繁华的新宿区，是一所提供留学生学习日语的语言教育学院，1986年建校，到至今迎来了第25周年校庆，毕业后主要升学学校有14所研究院，31所大学本科，4所大学专科，17所职业高专，其中包括著名的东京大学，早稻田大学等，本校位于东京的中心地区，无论是生活还是交通都是非常方便，学校的硬件设施和学习氛围都能为学子们创造一个优良的学习环境，2012年4月开始使用新教学大楼，欢迎广大的莘莘学子加入我们的队伍，梦想从这里起航。
优秀教师阵容：

本校教师具有丰富的教学经验。实行定期教师评价制度，为学生提供高质量的教学服务。

奖学金制度：

对于成绩优秀，学习态度认真的同学，学校设有校内及校外奖学金。

升学及生活指导：

本校为学生提供面对面的个人升学指导，帮助学生顺利升入理想的大学。为了学生尽快适应在日本的生活，帮助学生克服生活上的困难及解决烦恼，全校的教职人员都会随时伸出援助之手。

学校设施：

学校校舍为三层建筑，全部装有空调设备，并设有图书馆，保健室，免费使用因特网的计算机教室和专用的自行车停车场。

多彩的课外活动：

每年定期举行各式各样的课外活动，如巴士旅行，运动会，野餐，课外学习等。

与姐妹校之间的交流：

定期举行与姐妹校Bridge Academy的日本学生之间的交流会。将在课堂上学到的日语与实际应用相结合，加深与日本人之间的交留。

便利的生活环境：

本校位于东京的中心地区。从山手线的新大久保车站徒步2分钟左右即可到达，交通非常方便。学校附近有图书馆，体育馆，邮电局，银行，公园等非常便于生活。

同学会：

为了给毕业生提供互相交流工作,学习等方面信息的场所，本校1年举行一次同学会。通过同学会将学生联结在一起，以增进毕业生之间的交流。
