1. Overview of the school

Lincoln University is located on the outskirts of Christchurch, about 20 kilometers from the city center. Founded in 1878, it was once a branch of the University of Canterbury, mainly teaching practical agricultural sciences. It was established as an independent comprehensive university in 1930. As a renowned university in New Zealand, Lincoln University is dedicated to teaching and research in fields such as business and management, natural resources, engineering, and social sciences, achieving remarkable results.

Currently, the student body has just surpassed 14,000 and continues to grow every year, with half of them coming from overseas. International students from more than fifty countries study here, making it known as New Zealand's most international university, boasting the highest proportion of international students and offering a variety of internationally recognized degrees and diplomas.

As the only higher education institution in New Zealand offering degrees in landscape design, parks, and recreational management, Lincoln University has nearly 50 hectares of campus and 730 hectares of surrounding farms and gardens. Additionally, the university has about 1,000 hectares of farmland in Canterbrae Province, making it a rich land resource. The university's main focus is research, with many ongoing research programs and providing new agricultural technologies and variety improvements nationwide in New Zealand.

一、学校概况

林肯大学位于基督城郊外，距离基督城市中心约20公里，于1878年成立，一度曾是坎特伯里大学的分院，主要教授实用农业科学，于1930年成立为独立的综合性大学。林肯大学作为新西兰的知名大学，致力于商业与管理、自然资源、工程学、社会科学等众多领域的教学和科研，并取得显著成果。

目前学生人数刚超过14,000且每年不断成长，其中一半来自海外各地，来自五十多个国家的国际留学生在此学习，被誉为新西兰最国际化的大学，具有最高留学生比例，能够提供国际认可的各种学位和毕业文凭。

作为新西兰唯一的一所提供园林设计、公园和娱乐管理学位课程的高等学府，林肯大学拥有近五十公顷的校区以及周围的730公顷的农场及花园，另外学校还有其他约1000公顷的农地在坎特布雷省，土地资源相当丰富。学校主要重心是研究为主，并且有许多研究的计划正在进行，并提供新西兰全国农业的新技术与品种改良。
