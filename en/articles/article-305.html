The main entrance exam for American universities

When evaluating students' admission qualifications, U.S. universities must provide additional test scores in addition to their academic performance and English language proficiency, using globally standardized standards. The main entrance exams include:

IELTS: IELTS exam

IELTS: An international English proficiency test jointly organized by the British Council, Cambridge University Examinations Board, and IDP Education Group. Over 2,500 U.S. institutions, including all eight Ivy League schools, recognize IELTS scores. Scores are valid for two years.

Exam registration requires logging in at http://ielts.etest.edu.cn.

TOFEL: TOEFL Exam

TOEFL is an English proficiency test organized by the Educational Testing Service (ETS) in the United States. It assesses students' ability to understand and use English in listening, speaking, reading, and writing in a university environment. The maximum score for the internet-based test is 120, and it is valid for two years. To register for the test, you need to log in to http://toefl.etest.net.cn

SAT: Academic Ability Assessment Test

Administered by the U.S. Educational Testing Service (ETS), this score is an important reference for applying to top American undergraduate programs. It assesses students' abilities in logic, analysis, reasoning, and other aspects. Registration for the exam must be made www.collegeboard.com the website. Currently, there are no SAT test centers in China; candidates must travel to Hong Kong, Singapore, and other places to take the exam.

GRE: The U.S. Graduate Entrance Examination

Organized by the U.S. Education Testing Service (ETS), it is required by various graduate schools (excluding management and law schools) in the United States and Canada to provide exam scores from applicants. Reference website: www.est.org/gre

GMAT: Graduate entrance exam for Business Administration

The MBA entrance exam is computerized, and you must answer each question before proceeding to the next one. Exam registration: http://gmat.etest.edu.cn results are valid for 5 years. Reference website: www.mba.com

美国大学在评审学生的入学资格时，除了学生提供的在校学习成绩，还需提供一些额外的考试成绩，以全球统一的标准，考核学生的学术水平和英语语言能力。主要入学考试包括：

IELTS：雅思考试

IELTS：是由英国文化协会、剑桥大学考试委员会和IDP教育集团共同举办的国际英语水平测试。包括全部8所常春藤盟校在内的2500多所美国院校认可雅思考试成绩。成绩有效期二年。

考试报名需登录http://ielts.etest.edu.cn.

TOFEL：托福考试

TOFEL是由美国教育考试服务处ETS举办的英语水平考试。考核学生在大学环境下对英语的听、说、读、写的理解和使用能力，网考成绩满分为120分，有效期二年。考试报名需登录http://toefl.etest.net.cn

SAT：学术能力评估考试

由美国教育考试服务处ETS举办，该成绩是申请美国名校本科学习的重要参考。考查学生的逻辑、分析、推理等方面的能力。考试需通过网站www.collegeboard.com报名。目前SAT考试在国内没有考点，考生须前往香港、新加坡等地参加考试。

GRE：美国研究生入学考试

由美国教育考试服务处ETS主办，是美国、加拿大的大学各类研究生院(除管理类学院，法学院)要求申请者必须提供的考试成绩。参考网站：www.est.org/gre

GMAT：经企管理研究生入学考试

工商管理硕士的入学考试，采用计算机化考试，必须答完每一道考题方可进入下一道考题。考试报名：http://gmat.etest.edu.cn成绩有效期为5年。参考网站：www.mba.com
