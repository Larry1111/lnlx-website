When you finally receive an admission letter from your dream institution, feeling excited and thrilled, it's time to move on to the next round: applying for your visa. At this point, the school will send you an I-20 form issued by USCIS, and you can take it to the U.S. Embassy to apply for an F1 student visa. Our senior consultants will provide you with one-on-one guidance, from preparing visa documents to answering various questions, helping you clarify the process, simplify complexity, and successfully land across the ocean.

■ Login www.fmjfee.com, Based on the information on Form I-20, fill out Form I-901 and pay the $200 "Student and Exchange Scholar Information Management Fee" (SEVIS) online, print the receipt, or pay an additional $35 to obtain the original receipt;

■ Purchase visa fees at CITIC Industrial Bank: equivalent to 131 USD RMB, and keep the two copies of the CITIC Bank receipt;

■ Call the U.S. Embassy or Consulate in China 2-4 weeks in advance to schedule a visa interview;

■ Log in to the website of the U.S. Embassy in China and fill out the DS160 visa application form online

■ Visit the U.S. embassy or consulate to meet the in-person visa.

当你终于收到心仪院校的录取通知书，兴奋和激动后，你就该进入下一轮闯关了：办理签证。这时，学校会为你发送一份美国移民局签发的I-20表格，持此表前往美国使馆办理F1学生签证。我们的资深顾问会对你进行一对一的指导，从签证材料的准备到各种问题的回答，帮助你理清脉络、化繁为简，成功登陆大洋彼岸。

■ 登录www.fmjfee.com, 根据I-20表信息，填写I-901表，并在线支付200美金的“学生和交流学者信息管理费”（SEVIS费），打印收据，或者多支付35美金获取收据原件；

■ 前往中信实业银行购买签证费：相当于131美元的人民币，妥善保留中信银行的两联收据；

■ 提前2-4周致电美国驻华使领馆，预约签证面谈；

■ 登录美国驻华使馆网站，并在线填写DS160签证申请表

■ 前往美国使领馆参见面签。
