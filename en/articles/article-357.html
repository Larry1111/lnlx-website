Important notes for studying in New Zealand

[Visa Processing Steps]
Generally, there are four steps to processing a student visa application:
Step 1: Application materials will be reviewed by the staff receiving the application. If important documents such as identification, admission letters, or proof of financial status are missing, your application will be returned.
Step 2: After receiving your application, the visa officer will review the materials to determine whether you are a genuine student and whether you have sufficient funds to study abroad. At this stage, USCIS will verify your documents and may require you to submit additional documents.
Step 3: After the review is completed, the visa officer will make a decision on the application and notify the applicant in writing (AIP letter).
Step 4: If your application is approved in principle (AIP), once you meet the conditions for in-principle approval, the visa officer will issue a student visa.
[Guidelines for Approval in Principle]
After the student's application is approved in principle, applicants must provide:
● A recent acceptance letter issued by a New Zealand educational institution, specifying the start and end dates of the courses the applicant has paid;
● Proof of the student's deposit during their stay in New Zealand.

[Requirements for Conduct]
Applicants aged 17 or above who plan to stay in New Zealand for more than 24 months should submit a notarized certificate of no criminal discharge, issued less than 6 months from the date of application. Applicants must also provide a copy of a letter of non-criminal action issued by the local police station or criminal investigation team, dated less than 6 months from the application submission date. New Zealand Immigration Department should not accept letters of non-criminal action issued by the applicant's employer, security department, street, or talent center.
[Health Requirements]
Applicants planning to stay in New Zealand for more than 12 months must provide a complete physical examination and chest X-ray.
* All medical examination forms must be completed and signed by a hospital designated by Immigration New Zealand, and the completion date of the examination should be less than 3 months from the date of application submission. Pregnant women and children under 11 years old do not need to undergo a chest X-ray.
[Trial Time]
The processing time is about 30 days. Applications with incomplete documents require longer processing, so it is recommended not to purchase a ticket before obtaining your visa

[Work Permit]
For those who have studied at New Zealand tertiary institutions for more than two years or are graduate students or above, and are about to obtain a degree; Or those who have taken language courses for more than half a year and have an IELTS score of 5 may work part-time during their studies, but the weekly part-time time must not exceed 20 hours.

[Visa Validity Period]
Generally, student visas are issued until the date tuition and living expenses are paid. Specifically, if the curriculum is based on the New Zealand academic year, then for higher education courses, visas will be issued until March 31, and for primary and secondary school courses, until February 28. If the course duration is less than one year or the program can start at any time of the year, the visa will be issued within one month after the tuition payment date.

[Visa Application Fees]
The latest student visa application fee announced on November 29, 2010, is 1,220 RMB (the embassy may change the fee periodically).

【签证审理步骤】
一般来说，审理学生签证申请有四步：
Step1 申请材料将由接收申请的工作人员检查，如果缺少重要材料，如身份证明，录取通知书，经济状况证明等，您的申请将会被退回。
Step2 签证官收到申请之后，将会对申请材料进行审核，来决定是否是真实的学生，是否有充足的留学资金等。在这一步，移民局将核实材料，并可能需要您提交补充材料。
Step3 在审理完成之后，签证官将对申请作出决定并以书面形式通知申请人（AIP信）。
Step4 如果您的申请被原则批准（AIP），在您满足了原则批准条件之后，签证官会发下发学生签证。
【原则批准须知】
申请人在学生申请被原则批准之后, 需提供：
● 由新西兰教育机构出具的最新接收信，写明申请人已交费的课程的起止时期；
● 学生在新西兰停留期间保证金的证明。

【品行情况的要求】
17岁或以上的申请人如果打算在新西兰停留24个月以上，应该提交未受刑事处分公证书，其出具的日期距提交申请之日应该少于6个月。同时申请人还必须提供由当地派出所或刑侦大队出具的未受刑事处分证明信的复印件，其出具的日期距提交申请之日应该少于6个月。请新西兰移民局不接受由申请人所在单位，单位保卫部，街道或人才中心出具的未受刑事处分证明信。
【健康情况的要求】
计划在新西兰停留12个月以上的申请人必须提供一份完整的体格检查与胸部X光表。
* 所有体检表必须由新西兰移民局的指定医院填写并签署，体检完成的日期距提交申请之日应该少于3个月。孕妇以及11岁以下的儿童不用进行胸部X光检查。
【审理时间】
审理时间大约为30天。文件不全的申请审理所需时间更长，建议在获得签证以前请不要购买机票

【工作许可】
如在新西兰大专院校就读2年以上课程或研究所以上学生，并将取得学位者；或就读半年以上语言课程且IELTS达5分者，可于就学期间兼职，惟每周兼职时间不得超过20小时。

【签证有效期】
一般来说，学生签证会签发至学费和生活费所付至的日期。具体来说，如果课程是按照新西兰学年制定的话，那么对于高等教育的课程，签证将发至3月31日，中小学课程签发至2月28日。如果课程时间少于一年或课程可以在一年中的任何时间开始，签证将签发至学费付至日期后的一个月之内。

【签证申请费用】
2010年11月29日最新颁布的学生签证申请费是1220人民币(使馆会不定期更改申请费)
