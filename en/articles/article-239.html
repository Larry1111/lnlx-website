Understanding British Foundation Courses

What is the foundation course:

UK foundation courses are located directly on university campuses and are part of the university, ensuring quality education and students' prospects. Tuition fees are only 30,000 to 50,000 RMB higher than domestic foundation courses. Foundation courses usually start in September each year and end in June of the following year; Some schools also offer courses starting in January and ending in August each year. Whether starting in September or January, the purpose of foundation courses is to help students smoothly enter university in September of that year to study undergraduate courses

Absolute advantages of UK foundation courses:

UK Foundation Programme provides a good language environment, helping students better adapt to studying at top UK universities. This is something domestic Foundation Courses cannot match. UK Foundation Programme strictly enforces a dual admission policy to ensure admission. As long as you pass the foundation course, you can unconditionally progress to a degree program without needing to apply or retake IELTS

Who is the UK foundation course suitable for:

1) Graduated from high school or completed the second year of high school, and want to study for a university undergraduate degree in the UK;

2) Graduated from a college or bachelor's degree within three years without a degree and want to pursue a master's degree in the UK;

3) Graduated with a bachelor's degree but not very good grades and want to pursue a master's degree at a prestigious UK university;

4) Graduated with a bachelor's degree but poor language performance and unable to directly pursue a master's degree;

5) Graduated and wanted to change majors to pursue a master's degree;

Generally, during university preparatory courses, students choose to study both compulsory and elective courses. Compulsory courses include English and mathematics; Elective courses are chosen based on the major students wish to pursue in undergraduate studies. The scope of electives is very broad, including law, natural sciences (physics, chemistry, biology), business management, economics, advanced mathematics, art and design, and almost all foundational courses for undergraduate majors. Typically, students will choose to take two to three elective courses. After completing a one-year foundation program, students can meet the admission requirements for undergraduate studies and enter UK universities to continue their undergraduate studies.

什么是预科：

英国预科是直接坐落在大学校园内，是大学的一部分，教育质量有保证，学生的前途有保证。而学费仅比国内大学预科贵3 - 5万人民币。预科课程通常在每年九月入学，到第二年的六月结束；部分学校也有开设每年一月开学到八月结束的课程。不管是九月开学，还是一月开学，预科课程的目的都是帮助学生能够顺利地在当年九月进入大学学习本科课程

英国预科的绝对优势：

英国预科提供良好的语言环境，使学生能更好适应英国顶尖大学的学习。这是国内预科无法相比的。英国预科严格执行双录取政策，保证录取，只要预科成绩合格，无条件升读学位课程，无需再申请或重考雅思

英国预科适合人群：

1） 高中毕业或者完成高二学习，想去英国读大学本科；

2）三年大专毕业或本科毕业无学位，想去英国读硕士；

3）本科毕业但成绩不是很好，想去英国名牌大学读硕士；

4）本科毕业但语言成绩不好，无法达到直接读硕士的水平；

5）毕业想换专业读硕士；

一般在大学预科课程期间，学生将同时选择学习必修课程和选修课程。必修课程包括英语和数学；选修课程则根据学生将来进入本科想要攻读的专业来选择修读，选修课程的范围非常广泛，包括了：法律、自然科学（物理、化学、生物）、商业管理、经济学、高等数学、艺术设计等等，几乎包括了所有大学本科专业的基础课程。一般学生将会选择两到三门选修课程学习。这样在经过为期一年的预科课程学习后，学生可以达到大学本科的入学要求，从而进入英国大学继续攻读大学本科课程。
