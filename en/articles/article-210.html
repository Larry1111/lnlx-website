Yonsei University

1. Overview of the school

Yonsei University is located in Seoul, the capital of South Korea, and has a peaceful and beautiful campus, just 20 minutes away from Seoul's commercial center. Established in 1885, as the oldest university in Korea, Yonsei University celebrated its 100th anniversary in 1985. It is not only a top prestigious university in Korea but also ranks among the top hundred prestigious universities in the world. Over the years, Yonsei University has grown into a comprehensive university with 17 colleges, 83 departments, and 11 professional diploma institutions. All 83 departments offer bachelor's degree programs, 53 departments offer master's programs, and 49 departments can grant doctoral degrees. Yonsei University currently has more than 40,000 students enrolled.

3. Admission Qualifications

Hold a formal high school diploma or above, and have received at least 12 years of education overseas.

1) Korean language application: Foreign students who meet undergraduate admission requirements and need to first strengthen their Korean language.

2) Applying for undergraduate education: graduated from a formal high school or technical secondary school, with excellent academic performance.

3) Applicants for English-taught master's programs: Graduate with a formal bachelor's degree, proficiency in EnglishTOEFL 600 Or IELTS6.5

延世大学

一、学校概况

延世大学(Yonsei University)位于韩国首都首尔，拥有一个宁静优美的校园，距离首尔的商业中心仅20分钟路程。创建于1885年，作为韩国最古老的大学，1985年延世大学举行了100周年校庆。不仅在韩国属于一流名牌大学，也位居世界百所名牌大学之列。延世大学成长至今，已成为拥有17个学院、83个系科、11个专业文凭教育机构的综合性大学。学校83个系科全部提供学士学位课程，53个系科开设硕士课程，49个系科可授博士学位。延世大学现有在校生40000多人。

三、入学资格

具备正规高中毕业以上学历， 在海外接受满12年以上的教育。

1）申请韩国语：符合本科入学要求，需要先加强韩国语的外国学生。

2）申请本科：正规高中或中专毕业，成绩优秀。

3）申请英文授课硕士课程：正规本科毕业，有学士学位，英语达到TOEFL 600 或 IELTS6.5
