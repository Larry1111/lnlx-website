Japan promotes a fast and convenient visa policy

"Employment Ice Age" A large number of Chinese fresh undergraduate graduates have begun choosing to study abroad. In 2008, Japan launched the highly attractive "300,000 International Students Admission Plan," leading to an unprecedented wave of undergraduate students studying in Japan. A large number of Chinese fresh undergraduate graduates choose to study in Japan. So, is Japan ready to accept them?

Implement fast and convenient visa policies

For a long time, the Immigration Bureau of Japan's Ministry of Justice has faced criticism from various sources regarding visa approvals for Chinese students. Issues such as lengthy processing times and complicated procedures have been issued. As the overall quality of students studying in Japan improves, Japan will need to make substantial improvements in this area. In fact, the Immigration Control Office made relevant improvements last year: applicants with a bachelor's degree will no longer be required to submit proof of Japanese language study hours, nor will they need to take third-party Japanese language proficiency tests. This will help attract a large number of outstanding Chinese undergraduate graduates to study in Japan.

Implement unified management of international students

Providing international students with a stable learning and living environment, including necessary scholarships and a certain proportion of tuition reductions. Additionally, comprehensive dormitories are essential. Only in this way can international students study with peace of mind, and Japan can truly acquire "talent."

Currently, Japan still maintains a dual management system for international students: "enrollment" and "studying abroad." Students attending language schools are called "shusei"; students attending high schools, vocational schools, universities, etc., are called "international students." Currently, over 80% of "in-school students" choose to enter Japanese universities after completing the language transition, so such a "binary" distinction is clearly no longer necessary. We hope that Japan's "unified" management of international students can be implemented as soon as possible.

Assistance is provided for international students to graduate and find employment

When a large number of international students complete their studies in Japan and face job hunting, active assistance from Japan is essential. First, it is necessary to facilitate visas. Is it possible to extend the current "180-day" employment period after graduation to "1 year"? Second, Japanese schools and companies must genuinely provide employment assistance to international students. Universities should provide information to build employment platforms with companies; Japanese companies should abandon "differential treatment," hiring international students equally regardless of nationality and ensuring they have the same promotion opportunities as Japanese employees.

“就业冰河期”大批的中国应届本科毕业生开始纷纷选择出国留学，日本08年出台了极具吸引力的“30万留学生接收计划”，所以，日本留学迎来了前所未有的本科生“出国潮”。大量的中国应届本科毕业生选择日本留学，那么，日本方面是否已做好接收的准备了呢。

推行快捷便利的签证政策

一直以来，日本法务省入国管理局(入管)对中国学生的签证审批遭到来自方方面面的批评。诸如审批时间过长、手续繁琐等。随着赴日留学学生素质的整体提高，日本在这方面将必须有实质性的改进。其实，入管已在去年做出了相关改进，对最终学历为大学本科的申请者，将不再要求提交日语学时证明，更无需参加由第三方组织的日语能力相关考试。这将有利于吸引大量优秀的中国本科毕业生选择日本留学。

实施留学生“一元化”管理

为留学生提供安定的学习和生活环境，包括提供必要的奖学金和一定比例的学费减免。另外，完备的宿舍提供亦必不可少。只有这样留学生才能在无后顾之忧的基础上安心学业，日本也才能得到真正的“人才”。

目前，日本对留学生仍沿袭“就学”和“留学”二元化管理。就读语言学校的学生称之为“就学生”；就读高等学校、专门学校、大学等的学生称之为“留学生”。而现在“就学生”中80%以上的学生都会在完成语言过渡后选择升入日本大学，如此“二元化”区分显然没有再存在的必要。我们希望日本对留学生的“一元化”管理能够尽快付诸实施。

为留学生毕业就职提供援助

大批的留学生在日本完成学业后，当面临择业时，日本方面给与积极的援助必不可少。首先是在签证方面提供便捷，如今后是否有可能将目前的毕业后签证就职期间的“180天”时间延长至“1年”。其次，就是日本学校和企业要真正为留学生提供就业援助。大学方面要提供信息搭建与企业的就业平台；日本企业更是要摒弃“差别对待”，不分国籍一视同仁雇用留学生，并使之有与日本雇员同样的升迁机会。
