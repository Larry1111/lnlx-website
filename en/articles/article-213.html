1. Overview of the school

Founded in 1937, Sangmyeong University is a renowned four-year comprehensive university. It has two campuses: Seoul and Cheonan, with 11 colleges and 5 graduate schools. It has 20,000 students. In 2006, it ranked 23rd among comprehensive universities in Korea and first in animation and comics. The school's strong disciplines include computer software, art design, multimedia, information and communication, business management, civil engineering, and securities. The school has produced film and television stars such as Kim Jae-won and Park Seul-mi. The university's Korean Language and Culture Center is highly renowned in Korea and is the top choice for students learning Korean.

一、学校概况

详明大学建于1937年，是一所4年制著名综合大学。有首尔和天安两个校区，设有11个学院、5个研究生院。有学生2万名。2006年全韩国综合大学排名第23位，动画漫画专业韩国排名第一。学校的优势学科有计算机软件、艺术设计、多媒体、信息通讯、经营、土木、证券等。学校培养的影视明星有金栽元、朴率美。大学韩国语言文化中心在韩国极负盛名，是学生学习韩国语的首选大学。
