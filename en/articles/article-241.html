Advantages of studying in the UK

Published on 2011-08-09·Source: Liaoning Overseas Study Service Co., Ltd.·Viewed 9,352 times

● English is the native language, and English training is authentic and advanced.

● The social environment is beautiful and safe, economically developed, and the standard of living is high. Many activity venues are open for free.

● As the founder and leader of British education, the British Diploma is widely recognized worldwide by ensuring top-notch education quality through an advanced and comprehensive education supervision system and education system. The UK also has the lowest dropout rate in the world, with over 95% of students able to complete their studies.

● The UK has over 3,000 institutions offering a wide range of courses; Course content is compact, with more economical time and costs, with undergraduate programs lasting 3 years and graduate programs lasting 1 year. With the lower exchange rate of the British pound, tuition fees are about 30% cheaper than before, significantly reducing education costs.

● UK institutions offer thousands of scholarships for international students, with over 21,000 international students receiving funding from the UK government each year.

● Free Healthcare (NHS): International students can enjoy free medical services provided by the National Health Care Service System.

● Working legally, accompanied by relatives: Students above the foundation level can work 20 hours per week, those below the foundation level can work 10 hours per week, and can work full-time on holidays. Additionally, for courses of more than six months, relatives can accompany the students.

● International Graduate Program: Direct two-year work visa: Undergraduate graduates, regardless of major, can legally work in the UK for two years, with very simple visa procedures.

● Flexible curriculum offerings, with preparatory courses offered by universities lowering admission standards for prestigious universities.

● 英语是母语，英语培训水平地道而先进。

●社会环境优美、安全，经济发达，生活水准高，很多活动场所免费开放。

● 作为英系教育的创立者和领导者，凭借先进完善的教育监督机制及教育体系，保证其一流的教育质量，英国文凭受到各国普遍认可。英国的辍学率也是世界最低的，95%以上的学生都能完成学业。

● 英国有3000多所院校，开设范围及其广泛的课程；课程内容安排紧凑，在时间和费用上更经济，本科学制3年，研究生学制1年。英镑汇率降低，学费较以往便宜30%左右，教育成本降低明显。

●英国院校为国际学生设立了成千上万的奖学金名额，每年大约21,000多名国际学生得到英国政府的资助。

●免费医疗（NHS）:国际学生可享受国民医疗保健服务系统提供的免费医疗服务。

● 打工合法，亲属陪读：预科以上学生每周可以打工20小时，预科以下学生每周可以打工10小时，假期可全天打工。另外学习六个月以上的课程，亲属可陪读。

● 国际毕业生计划，可直接获两年工作签证：本科毕业的学生，无论何种专业，可合法在英国打工两年，并且签证手续十分简便。

● 课程设置灵活，各大学开设的预科课程降低名校入学标准。
