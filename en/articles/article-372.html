School Introduction:

Chinese: University of Bologna; Italian: Università di Bologna; Honorific: Mother of the University.

The University of Bologna is recognized as the oldest university in the world, and is ranked alongside the University of Oxford in the UK, the University of Paris in France, and the University of Padua in Italy, as one of Europe's four major cultural centers.

The University of Bologna is located in Bologna, the capital of Italy's Emilia-Romagna region. It was founded by Innerio in 1088 and is the world's first university. Originally known for its law and medicine programs, it has a history of over 900 years. In 1988, the University of Bologna was officially recognized as the alma mater of all European universities in the "European University Charter" jointly signed by the presidents of 430 European universities. To promote teaching, enhance teaching functions, and improve teaching quality, the university adopted a multi-campus system divided into five campuses: the main campus is located at 33 Zamboni Street in Bologna, while the other campuses are located in Cesena, Forlì, Ravenna, and Rimini. In 1998, the Buenos Aires branch was also established. Currently, the University of Bologna has 23 faculties, 68 departments, and 93 research centers.

This university boasts a very strong faculty and advanced teaching facilities. Each faculty has its own laboratories and multimedia classrooms, the medical faculty has clinical clinics for various departments, and the astronomy department also has its own observatory. In addition, the University of Bologna has a well-developed auxiliary education system, including libraries, museums, and archives. Among them, the libraries alone are divided into university-level, faculty-level, faculty-level, institute-level, and research center levels. The university-level library of the University of Bologna is under the Italian Ministry of Culture, holding 920,000 volumes, including 14th-15th century editions and nearly 10,000 ancient books, with a focus on literature and history; The University of Bologna also has a world-renowned historical archive housing important documents from the university's founding to understand the development of Western education, disciplines, and the history of Italian universities and academia; To facilitate teaching for teachers and student practice, almost every discipline is equipped with a dedicated museum. Among the most famous are the Astronomy Museum, Anatomy Museum, Pathology Museum, Anthropology Museum, Physics Museum, Geology Museum, Mineralogy Museum, Zoology Museum, and a botanical garden covering about 2 hectares.

Departments and Majors:

1. Mathematics, Physics, and Natural Sciences faculties, including eight majors: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Astronomy, Geology, Biology, Informatics, and Natural Sciences. Mainly studied basic theory. There is also a Chemistry Research Institute.

2. Law school (including the Graduate School of Law, Court Application Research Institute, and the Center for Medieval Civil Law) and an Administrative Law Advanced Study Institute.

3. The Normal College offers three majors: Italian Language and Literature, Education, and Foreign Languages and Literature. The main subjects are Italian language, Latin, pedagogy, and history.

4. The Faculty of Economics and Business trains undergraduate students in economics and trade, offering courses in economics, commerce, political economy, and finance.

5. Population and Statistics The School of Insurance offers three majors: Statistics, Statistics and Demography, and Statistics and Economics. Mainly studies courses in statistics, mathematics, methodology, demography, and sociology. It has an Institute of Anthropology.

6. The School of Engineering Chemistry mainly trains applied chemical talents, offering courses mainly related to chemical engineering practice, experiments, and analysis.

8. The School of Political Science is divided into four majors: Social Politics, Administration, Economic Policy, and Political History. The main subjects are political science, constitutional law, sociology, and philosophy.

9. The College of Agriculture trains undergraduates in agriculture and animal husbandry. The courses offered cover basic knowledge of agriculture and animal husbandry, with a greater emphasis on cultivating applied talents and developing diverse talents.

10. The Faculty of Medical Surgery is the most prestigious faculty at the University of Bologna, offering majors in surgery, internal medicine, otolaryngology, and dentistry. Courses include: Medical Research Applied Methods, Human Morphology, Biological Cell Structure, Biological Functions, and Human Organs (first three years), followed by specialized studies.

11. Faculty of Languages and Philosophy. Majors: Literature, Philosophy, Modern Foreign Languages and Literature, Arts, Music, Drama, History.

12. College of Veterinary Medicine.

13. School of Medicine. Majors: Pharmaceutical Sciences, Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Technology and Technology. Courses include: Chemistry, Microbiology, Toxicology, Herbal Medicine, Pharmaceutical Inspection, etc. Admission requires a letter of recommendation, academic achievements in the major, and recognition from supervisors.

The mother of European universities

On September 18, 1988, marking the 900th anniversary of the University of Bologna, 430 university presidents from Europe jointly signed the European University Charter in Bologna's Grand Square, officially declaring the University of Bologna as the "Mother of Universities" (Latin: Alma Mater Studiorum), meaning the alma mater of all European universities.

学校简介：

中文：博洛尼亚大学； 意大利语：Università di Bologna；尊称：大学之母。

博洛尼亚大学是公认的世界上历史最悠久的大学，与英国的牛津大学、法国的巴黎大学、意大利的帕多瓦大学并称为欧洲四大文化中心。

博洛尼亚大学坐落于意大利艾米利亚-罗马涅大区的首府博洛尼亚，由依内里奥于1088年创立，是全世界第一所大学。创建之初以法律和医学两个专业著称，至今已有900多年的历史。1988年在430所欧洲大学校长共同签署的“欧洲大学宪章”中，博洛尼亚大学被正式宣称为欧洲所有大学的母校。为了促进教学推广、发挥教学功能、改善教学质量，学校采用了多校区体制，共分5个校区：主校区位于博洛尼亚市的赞鲍尼大街33号，其他校区分别在Cesena，Forlì，Ravenna和Rimini。1998年还成立了布宜诺斯艾利斯分校。博洛尼亚大学目前共有23个学院，68个系和93个研究中心。

这所大学拥有极其雄厚的师资力量，教学设施也十分先进。各学院都有自己的实验室和电化教室，医学院设有各科的临床诊所，天文学部还拥有自己的天文台。此外，博洛尼亚大学的辅教系统相当完备，如图书馆、博物馆以及档案馆等。其中，仅图书馆就分为校级，学院级、学部级、研究所级和研究中心级。博洛尼亚大学校级图书馆隶属于意大利文化部，馆内共有藏书92万册，包括14-15世纪的图书版本及将近万册的古籍，藏书偏重于文史类；博洛尼亚大学还拥有举世闻名的历史档案馆，其间收藏了大学建立以来的重要文献，以了解西方的教育发展、学科发展以及意大利大学、学术的发展历史；为方便老师教学与学生实践，大学几乎每个学科都配有相应的博物馆，其中比较著名的有天文博物馆、解剖学博物馆、病理博物馆、人类学博物馆、物理学博物馆、地质学博物馆、矿物学博物馆、动物学博物馆以及一个占地约2公顷的植物园。

院系专业：

1、数学、物理、自然科学院、包括数学、物理、化学、天文、地质、生物、信息和自然科学共8个专业。主要学习基础理论。还有一个化学研究所。

2、法学院(包括法学研究所，法庭应用研究所、中世纪民法研究中心)以及一个行政法进修学院。

3、师范学院有意大利语言文学、教育、外国语言文学三个专业。主要学习意大利语言、拉丁语、教育学和历史。

4、经济贸易学院主要是培养经济贸易本科学生，开设经济贸易课、商业、政治经济学和经济与金融课。

5、人口与统计保险学院开设统计、统计与人口学、统计与经济学三个专业。主要学习统计学、数学、方法论、人口学、社会学等课程。下设一个人类学研究所。

6、工程化学学院主要是培养应用化工人才，开设的课程主要有关化工实践、实验和分析的应用课。

8、政治学院分为4个专业：社会政治、行政、经济政策和政治史。主要学习政治学、宪法、社会学、哲学。

9、农学院培养农业和畜牧业本科生。开设的课程有农业和畜牧业基础知识，更注重于应用人才的培养和多样型人才的发展。

10、医学外科学院是博洛尼亚大学实力最雄厚的一个学院，有外科、内科、耳鼻喉科、牙科专业。课程有：医学研究应用方法、人类形态学、生物细胞结构、生物功能和人体器官(前三年)，以后为专业学习。

11、语言和哲学院。专业：文学、哲学、近代外国语言和文学，艺术音乐戏剧、历史。

12、兽医学院。

13、医药学院。专业：医药学、制药化学工艺和技术。课程有：化学、微生物学、毒理学、草药学、制药检验等。入学需推荐信、专业学术成就、导师认可。

欧洲大学之母

1988年9月18日，博洛尼亚大学建校九百年之际，欧洲430个大学校长在博洛尼亚的大广场共同签署了欧洲大学宪章，正式宣布博洛尼亚大学为欧洲“大学之母”（拉丁文：Alma Mater Studiorum），即欧洲所有大学的母校。
