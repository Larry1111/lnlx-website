Montana State University (Billings) was founded in 1927 as a four-year public university and is the third largest public university in the Montana State University System. It was rated a Tier 4 national university by U.S. News & World Report, ranked 151st in the nation, and was included in the Yale Daily University Guide "Top 200 in the U.S." The university is located in the Yellowstone Valley, where you can overlook the entire campus beneath its exquisite sandstone cliffs.

Overview of Montana State University Billings

Montana State University Billings is a four-year public university established in 1927. Many young people drawn to the natural beauty of the Rocky Mountains travel here to study forestry, wildlife management, ecology, and geology. Montana State University Billings also excels in journalism, business management, engineering, pre-law, agriculture, and international communication.
It was rated a fourth-tier national university by U.S. News & World Report, ranked 151st nationally, and was selected as one of the "Top 200 in the U.S." (Yale University Daily University Guide).
Montana State University Billings offers majors in finance, law, textiles and apparel, engineering technology, management, environment, architecture, education, science, tourism, agriculture and forestry, humanities and arts, social sciences, biology, physical education, journalism and communication, information science, medicine, language, and natural sciences
