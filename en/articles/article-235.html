The University of Manchester (English: University of Manchester; abbreviated as UMIS) was founded in 1851 and is the largest single-campus university in the UK, located in Manchester, a major city in northern England. Currently, the university has over 40,000 students enrolled in over 500 departments or programs, with over 10,000 faculty and staff, and an annual budget of £600 million. The university receives 60,000 applications annually from around the world, the highest in the UK. According to The Times: "The University of Manchester enjoys a respected reputation in almost all disciplines, with the most notable in life sciences, engineering, humanities, economics, sociology, and social sciences." Additionally, Manchester Business School ranks 25th globally. The University of Manchester Library John Lelands Library ranks third in the UK in terms of book collection, behind only Oxford and Cambridge. In the "Shanghai Jiao Tong University World University Rankings," it ranks fifth in the UK, sixth in Europe, and 40th in the world, with the largest number of international students in the UK.

Admission requirements for undergraduate foundation courses: At least 17 years old, IELTS score of 5.5, currently in high school or having graduated from high school.

Language course start dates: Four times a year, in January, April, July, and September.

www.manchester.ac.uk

大学本科预科课程入学要求：年满17周岁，雅思5.5分，高中在读或高中毕业。

语言课程开课日期：每年1、4、7、9月四次开学。
