Advantages of studying in New Zealand

Australia used to be one of the most popular long-established study abroad destinations among Chinese students, but since the end of last year, as the entry requirements for these established countries have been adjusted, studying abroad and immigrating have become increasingly difficult. Many international students planning to study in Australia or currently studying there have started to consider giving up.

However, the New Zealand government has introduced many favorable policies, taking advantage of this opportunity to vigorously promote studying in New Zealand, making immigration and studying in New Zealand increasingly popular. According to feedback, starting from mid-August 2010, the number of people consulting about studying in New Zealand has increased significantly, with a large portion being current students and others who failed last year's college entrance exam.

According to industry experts, there are three major advantages to studying in New Zealand now.

Advantage 1: High visa rate and no language requirements

Compared to established study abroad countries, New Zealand can be considered a relatively good way to study abroad right now. This is because Australia requires IELTS scores to apply for a visa, Canada requires a deposit period of over 12 months, and the success rates of UK and US visas are unstable.

For Chinese students, the New Zealand student visa does not require English proficiency tests such as TOEFL or IELTS. Not only is the visa process short, but the success rate is also very high. Usually, students prepare the relevant documents for the school and visa according to their own circumstances and can obtain the visa in about two months.
Advantage 2: The advantage of study abroad expenses is prominent

Studying in the UK or US requires about 250,000 RMB per year, while countries like Canada and Australia require around 200,000~250,000 RMB per year. Currently, the New Zealand dollar exchange rate is about 1:5.3 to RMB. Statistics show that the annual undergraduate tuition in New Zealand is about 18,000 New Zealand dollars, which is about 100,000 RMB. Including living expenses, the total annual cost is about 130,000~140,000 RMB.

Advantage 3: New Zealand schools protect the rights of international students

In New Zealand, all institutions wishing to enroll international students must sign the "Industry Rules for Care and Guidance of International Students." The New Zealand Academic Assessment Board and the New Zealand Polytechnic Quality Association conduct monitoring activities, both including assessing the institution's compliance with industry regulations.

澳大利亚曾是中国学生最热衷的老牌留学国家之一，但从去年末至今，随着各个老牌留学国家的留学门槛调整，留学、移民开始变得困难。不少计划去澳大利亚留学甚至正在澳大利亚学习的留学生产生了放弃的念头。

然而新西兰政府出台诸多利好政策，借此契机大力推进新西兰留学，移民、留学新西兰变得热门起来。据信息反馈2010年8月中旬开始，咨询新西兰留学的人数明显提升，其中一大部分是学校在读生，还有一部分为去年高考失利的学生。

据业内专家分析，现在赴新西兰留学有三大优势。

优势一：高签证率、无语言要求

对比老牌留学国家，新西兰可以说是目前一个较好的留学渠道。因为，澳大利亚一定需要雅思成绩才能办理签证申请，加拿大有超过12个月的保证金存期要求，英国和美国的签证成功率不稳定。

针对中国学生的新西兰留学签证，无须提供如托福或雅思的英语成绩即可获得签证，不仅签证时间短，而且成功率非常高。通常学生根据自身情况准备相关申请学校和签证的文件，在两个月左右的时间即可获得签证。
优势二：留学费用优势凸显

到英、美国家留学，每年大概需要25万元人民币以上，而加拿大、澳大利亚等国家每年需要20万~25万元人民币左右。目前，新西兰元对人民币的汇率在1：5.3左右。据统计，新西兰本科阶段一年的学费在1.8万新西兰元左右，折合人民币约10万元，加上生活费，一年总费用在13万~14万元左右。

优势三：新西兰学校保障留学生权利

在新西兰，所有希望招收国际学生的机构必须在“对国际学生的照顾与指导之行业规则”上签字。新西兰学历评估委员会和新西兰理工学院质量协会的监察活动，均包括评估该机构遵从行业规则的情况。
