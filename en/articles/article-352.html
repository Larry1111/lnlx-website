1. Overview of the school

Founded 50 years ago, the University of New South Wales has been ranked among the top ten universities in the Asia-Pacific region by Asia Weekly for its world-class teaching and research standards. NSW graduate students have achieved remarkable success in their respective fields or careers. As a member of the 21 prestigious university associations, the University of New South Wales had three scholars who received the "Rhodes" Medal in 1998. The University of New South Wales currently has 33,000 students and 5,000 faculty and staff. With 5,000 international students, the University of New South Wales is the most vibrant and multicultural university community. Over the past 50 years, UNSW has trained thousands of students spread across the globe, dominating government, business, and industry in Asia and Australia.

Basic information

School type: Public

Established: 1949

Location: Sydney, New South Wales. The 38-hectare main campus is located in Kensington, about 20 minutes from Sydney's business district. The art school is located in Paddington, a suburb of Sydney, surrounded by many art museums.

Transportation: KENSINGTON is located in the eastern suburbs of Sydney, about 10 minutes from Sydney's business district and Sydney Harbour, only 5~10 minutes from popular East Coast beaches such as COOGEE Beach and BONDI Beach, and UNSW is just a 15-minute taxi ride from the international airport

School ranking: 44th in the world overall ranking

City Overview:

Sydney is the capital of New South Wales, Australia. It is not only Australia's largest, oldest, most prosperous, and most colorful city, but also a major hub for manufacturing, commerce, finance, transportation, tourism, and trade. It is also the world's largest wool sales center and one of the most famous cities internationally.

Sydney is located in the southeast of mainland Australia and has a Mediterranean climate, with mild weather and abundant sunshine throughout the year. Located in the Southern Hemisphere, the season is exactly opposite to China's: December to February is summer, with sea breezes and an average temperature of 22°C, making it feel cool and comfortable; June to August is winter, with an average temperature of 13°C. The annual average temperature difference is only 7.8°C. The average annual precipitation is 1205 millimeters. There are 342 days a year when the sun is bright.

Honors and Features:

One of Australia's eight prestigious universities, with a century of brilliant academic achievements.

The university's nine major departments (Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences; Faculty of the Built Environment; College of Fine Arts; Faculty of Commerce and Economics; Faculty of Engineering; Faculty of Law; Faculty of Medicine; Faculty of Science; AGSM offers over 400 undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral programs.

Among them, the "Australian Graduate School of Management" was rated the number one MBA school in the Asia-Pacific region by Asia Inc. in 2002, and the UK's "Age of Finance" named it the best MBA school in Australia in 2001 and 2002

It is a highly prestigious member of UNIVERSITAS 21, with members from world-class universities across Asia, Europe, and North America.

Ranked among the top ten universities in the Asia-Pacific region by Asia Weekly

• International focus

UNSW attracts international students from over 120 countries (more than any other university in the world) and maintains strong cooperative relationships with many renowned universities. Through international exchange and mutual visits, many new ideas are introduced to the school. At the same time, UNSW encourages students to participate as international exchange students, leveraging its strong education system and research alliances to engage with more than 200 prestigious universities worldwide. This interdisciplinary approach to cultural exchange is also one of the most valuable teaching methods offered by the school.

• Curriculum diversity

UNSW University not only offers a wide range of courses with varying breadth and variety, but also creates more opportunities for students to choose courses. Students enjoy a balance between marginal online teaching and indispensable face-to-face instruction, while fully leveraging the advantages of interdisciplinary sciences for learning and research. Moreover, UNSW is a market leader in master's taught programs, designing numerous educational programs to meet the needs of employers and professionals.
2. Major Offerings

Aerospace science and technology, environmental science, statistics, bioengineering, engineering science, petroleum engineering, business, accounting, education, arts, law, design, digital media

3. Admission Requirements

Undergraduate English score requirement: IELTS 6.5 (no section below 6.0)

Master's English proficiency requirement: IELTS 6.5 (no section below 6.0)

School website: http://www.unsw.edu.au/

一、学校概况

创办于50年前的新南威尔士大学以其一流的教学和研究水平，被亚洲周刊评为亚太地区前十位的大学。新南威尔士的研究生在各自领域或事业上成就显著。作为21所名校大学联合会的成员之一，1998年新南威尔士大学有3位获得“罗德斯”奖的学者。新南威尔士大学现有33,000名学生，5,000名教职工。拥有5,000名国际学生的新南威尔士大学是最富有生机和多元文化的大学社区。在过去的50年里新南威尔士大学培养了成千上万的学生，他们遍布世界各地，在亚洲和澳洲的政府、商业、工业领域占主导地位。

基本信息

学校类型：公立

建校时间：1949年

地理位置：新南威尔士州悉尼市。38公顷的主校园坐落于肯辛顿，距悉尼的商业区约20分钟路程。艺术学院位于悉尼近郊的帕丁顿，周围有许多艺术博物馆。

交通状况：KENSINGTON位于悉尼东郊，距悉尼商业区和悉尼海港约10分钟路程，离人们喜爱的东海岸海滩如COOGEE海滩和BONDI海滩只有5～10分钟，UNSW距国际机场乘出租车只需15分钟

学校排名：世界综合排名44位

城市概况：

悉尼市是澳大利亚新南威尔士州的首府，它不仅是澳大利亚最大、最古老、最繁荣、最多姿多彩的城市，是澳大利亚的主要制造业、商业、金融业、交通运输业、旅游业中心和贸易港，也是世界上最大的羊毛销售中心，国际上最著名的都市之一。

悉尼位于澳大利亚大陆东南部，悉尼属地中海型气候，一年四季温和，阳光充足。因地处南半球，季节刚好与中国相反：12月至2月为夏季，夏季吹海风，平均气温22℃，使人感觉凉爽舒适；6月至8月为冬季，平均气温为13℃。年均温差仅为7.8℃。年平均降水量为1205毫米。一年中有342日享受着明媚的阳光。

荣誉和特色：

澳洲八大名校之一，拥有百年辉煌学术成就史.

学校的九大院系（Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences; Faculty of the Built Environment; College of Fine Arts; Faculty of Commerce and Economics; Faculty of Engineering; Faculty of Law; Faculty of Medicine; Faculty of Science; AGSM）提供超过400 种的本科,研究生,博士生课程.

其中的“Australian Graduate School of Management” 更是被Asia Inc. 评为2002年亚太地区第一的MBA 学校,英国的“财经时代”将其评为2001,2002 澳大利亚最好的MBA院校

属于声望极高的UNIVERSITAS 21成员，该组织成员均来自亚洲，欧洲和北美等国的世界一流高校.

被“亚洲周刊”评为亚太地区的十佳大学

•国际焦点

UNSW大学吸引着来自超过120个国家的国际留学生（比世界上任何一所大学都多），学校还与许多著名大学保持着良好的合作关系。通过国际交流与相互访问，许多新的观念被带进学校，同时UNSW鼓励学生作为国际交换留学生，利用其强大的教育体系和研究联盟，与全世界超过200所享有声望的大学进行交流。这种交叉式的文化交流方式，也是学校提供给学生最有价值的授课方式之一。

•课程多样性

UNSW大学不仅提供大量的具有一定宽度、广度、多样性的课程，也为学生创造更多选择课程的机会。学生享有在边缘式网络教学和不可或缺的面授式教学之间的平衡，同时可以充分利用交叉科学的优势进行学习和研究。不仅如此，UNSW还作为硕士授课类课程教育的市场领导者，设计出众多满足雇主和专业人士需要的教育课程。
二、专业设置

航空科学技术、环境科学、统计学、生物工程、工程科学、石油工程、商业、会计、教育、艺术、法律、设计、数字媒体

三、入学要求

本科英语成绩要求：雅思 6.5分（单项不低于6.0分）

硕士英语成绩要求：雅思 6.5分（单项不低于6.0分）

学校网站：http://www.unsw.edu.au/
