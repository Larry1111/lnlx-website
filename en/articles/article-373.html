School Introduction:

The University of Rome, abbreviated as Rome First University, is also known as Rome I. The University of Rome is the largest national university in Italy, with 870 professors and 185,000 students. It was established in 1303. The university has 21 faculties (19 faculties, including the Faculty of Aeronautical Engineering and the Faculty of Archives and Library), such as the Faculty of Architecture and the Faculty of Letters, with over 130 departments. As of 2002, the university offered 160 first-level degree programs, 167 professional degree programs, 102 departments, 162 doctoral programs, 142 master's programs, and 1,635 scholarship application slots, including scholarships for international students. English-taught courses include: a two-year master's program in Government Administration and Humanities.

Location:

The University of Rome is located in the Italian Republic on the Arnin Peninsula in southern Europe, like a giant boot plunging deep into the deep blue Mediterranean. Together with Sicily on the tip of the boot, it almost splits the vast Mediterranean in half. Its entire territory is mainly mountainous and hilly, with the Alps as a barrier to the north, bordering France, Switzerland, Austria, and Slovenia. It is bordered by the sea on the east, south, and west, and includes the two major islands of Sicily and Sardinia. Within Italy, there are two independent countries: one is the Republic of San Marino in the northeast; The other is the Vatican, located in Rome, the capital of Italy. This world's smallest independent country is the center of the Roman Catholic Church.

Curriculum:

Law, politics, business and economics, statistics, literature and philosophy, engineering, architecture, spatial engineering, natural sciences and science, teacher education, and medical schools.

学校简介：

罗马大学即罗马第一大学的简称，又称罗马一大。罗马大学是意大利最大的国立大学，教授870人，学生185000，1303年建立。大学共设有21个学院(19个学院和航空工程学院和档案和图书馆学院)，如：建筑学院、文学院等，其中有超过130个以上的系。至2002年，大学有160个专业的一级学位课程，167个专业的专业学位课程，102个系，162个专业的博士学位课程，142个专业的硕士学位课程，同时还有1635个奖学金申请名额，其中包括针对外国留学生发放的奖学金。英语授课课程有：2年的政府管理和人文事务硕士专业。

地理位置：

罗马大学位于欧洲南部亚宁半岛的意大利共和国象一只巨大的长筒靴深入湛蓝的地中海, 连同靴尖上的西西里岛一起, 几乎把浩瀚的地中海分成两半.它全境以山地、丘陵 为主 ,北以阿尔卑斯山为屏障,与法国、瑞士、奥地利、斯洛文尼亚接壤,东、南、西三面临海，有西西里和撒丁两个大岛屿。在意大利境内还有两个独立的国家：一个是地处东北部的圣马力诺共和国；另一个是梵蒂冈，位于意大利首都罗马，这个世界上最小的独立国家是罗马天主教会的中心。

课程设置：

法律、政治、商业和经济、统计、文学和哲学、工程、建筑、空间工程、自然科学和理学、师范以及医学院。
