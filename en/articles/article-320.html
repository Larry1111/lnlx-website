Toulouse 1 University, also known as Toulouse University of Social Sciences, is one of the oldest universities in Europe and one of the most renowned public universities in France. Founded in 1229, it gained great fame in the Middle Ages due to the many famous scholars who came to lecture here. In 1970, the ancient University of Toulouse was divided by academic fields into today's Toulouse 1, 2, and 3 universities, covering various disciplines. The 1st University has maintained a high academic standard in economics and law, ranking first among all public universities in France in law, economics, and management. At the same time, the Toulouse School of Economics (TSE), under Toulouse 1 University, ranks first in European economics research. Unlike the general economics programs at Toulouse 1, this school uses English instruction from the master's level, admits a small number of Chinese students each year, and has a 50% dropout rate each year. According to the statistical report dated March 13, 2003: "There are 404 research centers in 18 European countries. In economic research, Toulouse I is ranked first by the European Economic Association, surpassing the London School of Economics." ”

The main campus of Toulouse 1 University is located in the city center and has several branch campuses; One campus was renovated from the site of a former cigarette factory and is located on the banks of the Brienne Canal, just a few hundred meters from the main campus.

Toulouse University I mainly focuses on liberal arts, offering majors such as law, politics, economic management, and science and technology. The university has 18 laboratories, one industrial economics school, and a federation, with a strong faculty of 450 researchers and nearly 800 doctoral supervisors, and has established close cooperative relationships with more than 500 enterprises and institutions. Each year, more than 2,500 students are recommended to intern at major enterprises, and seminars between companies and students are held irregularly. Among many well-known local enterprises,

For example, MOTOROLA, Alcatel, and others have gathered a large number of DESS diploma holders. The school's strong network of cooperative relationships with enterprises provides students with more opportunities to understand corporate culture and creates broader employment opportunities for them.

The university offers more than a dozen teaching programs in law, economics, management, history, information, languages, mathematics, politics, sociology, and physical education. Toulouse I University is at the forefront of research theory in many fields, especially in industrial economics, and enjoys a strong reputation in GREMAQ, LEREPS, and L'IDEI. Its teaching closely monitors the socio-economic realities. To meet the region's demand for aviation talent, Toulouse I continuously strengthens talent training in space technology, telecommunications, and new technologies beyond its areas of expertise in economics and law. The university library provides students with over 470,000 academic works and more than 3,000 continuously updated journals, two large bookstores, five libraries, and 25 research centers. The information exchange center is equipped with over 400 computers and equipped with 20 multimedia classrooms for student use.

Toulouse I University has always been an ideal choice for international students: international students account for 12% of the total, and currently, 80 foreign institutions have established inter-university exchange relationships with the school. European exchange programs make up the majority, with 200 students from different countries joining the school family each year, and an equal number of students studying abroad. For them, this is a rare opportunity to improve their foreign language skills, learn about foreign cultures, and gain work experience.

图卢兹第一大学又称图卢兹社会科学大学，它是欧洲最古老的大学之一，也是法国最著名的公立大学之一。它建于1229年，在中世纪由于许多著名学者来此讲学，使之名声显赫。1970年，古老的图卢兹大学按照学术领域分为了今天的图卢兹一大、二大和三大，涵盖各个学科，其中一大在经济学和法学专业上一直保持着较高的学术水平，其在法律、经济学、管理学领域在全法国公立大学中排名第一。同时图卢兹第一大学名下的图卢兹经济学院（Toulouse School of Economics, TSE)在欧洲经济学研究中排名第一。不同于图一大的普通经济专业，该经济学院从硕士阶段起采用英文教学，每年招收少量的中国学生，并且每年50%的淘汰率。根据2003年3月13日统计报告：“欧洲18国有404所研究中心，在经济学研究方面，图卢兹一大被欧洲经济协会排在了首位，超过了伦敦经济学院。”

图卢兹第一大学本部位于图卢兹市中心，并设有多处分校园；其中一个校园是利用一家原卷烟厂旧址整修而成，坐落在离本部仅几百米之遥的布里埃纳(Brienne)运河岸边。

图卢兹一大以文科为主，开设的专业有法律、政治、经济管理、科学技术等。学校拥有18所实验所、一所工业经济学院、一个联合会，师资力量雄厚，共有450名科研工作者和近800名博士生导师，与500多家企业、机构建立了密切的合作关系。每年推荐2500多名学生到各大型企业实习，不定期举办企业与学生之间的研讨会。在当地的很多知名企业里，

比如，MOTOROLA、Alcatel等，云集了一大批DESS文凭获得者。学校与企业良好的合作关系网给学生提供了更多的机会了解企业文化，同时为学生创造了更广阔的就业机会。

学校包括法律、经济、管理、历史、信息、语言、数学、政治、社会学与体育学十多个教学项目。图卢兹一大在很多领域都处于研究的理论前沿，特别是工业经济学方面，在GREMAQ、LEREPS和L’IDEI都享有盛誉，教学工作密切关注社会经济现状。图卢兹一大为适应本地区对航空领域人才的需求，在自己擅长的经济、法律领域之外，不断加强空间技术、电信通讯、新技术等方面的人才培养，学校图书馆为学生提供470000多件学术著作和3000多册不断更新的期刊，两个大型书店，5个图书馆和25个研究中心，信息交流中心备有400多台计算机，设有20个多媒体教室可供学生使用。

图卢兹一大一直以来都是外国学生的理想选择院校：留学生占总人数的12%，现目前与学校建立校际交流关系的有80所外国机构，欧洲交流项目占主要部分，每年，200名来自不同国家的学生加入到学校的大家庭中，同时也有相同数量的学生到外国学习。对于他们来说，这都是提高外语水平、了解外国文化和积累工作经验的难得机会。
