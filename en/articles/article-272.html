The school is located in Kokura Ward, Kitakyushu City, and is one of the schools within the YMCA School Group. YMCA is known as an international sex education organization, with schools spread across Osaka, Kobe, Hiroshima, Fukuyama, Tokyo, Fukuoka, and other regions in Japan.

The Kitakyushu YMCA Japanese Language School was established in 1990. Most of the students come from Asia. After graduation, a large portion of the students go on to universities and graduate schools. Kitakyushu City is adjacent to Fukuoka City, together forming a commercial and industrial center in southern Japan. The city is beautiful, bustling yet elegant, making it an ideal place to live and study.

Kitakyushu YMCA Japanese Language School has been rated as an excellent school for its high-quality teaching, strict management, and outstanding student achievements, which has led to a consistently high visa approval rate.
