Why choose to study in Italy?

Tradition: The first university in human history was officially established in Italy, and the roots of the modern university tradition lie in Italy. As early as ancient Roman times, Italy already had universities.

Culture: Italy's scientific and humanistic culture has ancient and noble origins. It inherits ancient Greek and Roman culture, and begins with the awakening of the Renaissance, making it an unavoidable theme in tracing Western cultural civilization and exploring East-West cultural exchange.

Innovation: Italian universities are undergoing a period of reform in technology, management, and teaching, which will make them leaders in educational innovation in Europe. Italy is one of the first countries to advocate for the establishment of a European common space for education and research.

Opportunities: Italian companies have always been eager to promote multicultural exchange, providing reliable opportunities for training and internships. Italian universities have a long tradition of dialogue and personnel exchange with well-known companies, with thousands of students undergoing phased training at companies each year.

Acceptance: Italy is a country of etiquette, with warm and hospitable people and a pleasant climate. It has long attracted large numbers of immigrants.

传统：人类历史上的第一所大学正式诞生在意大利，现代大学传统的根在意大利。早在古罗马时代意大利已经有了大学的存在。

文化：意大利的科学和人文文化有着古老和高贵的起源。它上承古希腊、罗马文化、下启文艺复兴的人的觉醒，是追溯西方文化文明和探索东西文化交流绕不过的主题。

创新：意大利大学正在经历一个在技术、管理和教学方面的改革时期，这一改革将使之成为欧洲在教育创新领域的领军国家。意大利是最先倡导建立欧洲教育和科研共同空间之一机构的国家之一。

机会：意大利企业一向乐于促进多元文化的交流，它们将为培训和实习提供可靠的机会。意大利大学与知名企业之间有着悠久的对话和人员交流的传统，每年有数千名学生在企业进行阶段性培训。

吸纳：意大利是一个礼仪之邦，它的人民热情好客，气候宜人，长期以来它一直在吸纳大批的移民。
