1. School Overview:

The University of Otago is New Zealand's first university, founded in 1869. The university enjoys an international reputation for its high-quality teaching and leading practical research.

The University of Otago currently has over 22,000 undergraduate students and more than 3,800 students pursuing graduate diplomas, master's, and doctoral programs.

The University of Otago adopts an international education system, with 1,100 international students from over 50 countries and connections with many institutions worldwide. It also has a strong alumni network, through which 60,000 graduates from 110 countries have been contacted.

The quality of life for students is a distinctive feature of the University of Otago. Dunedin, where the university is located, is truly a university town where studying has become a way of life. The campus is a beautiful and charming place, featuring both classical and modern architecture, and it is only a few minutes' walk from the city center.

2. School Advantages and Features:

  1. New Zealand's oldest national university
  2. Outstanding academic reputation and a strong research culture
  3. Graduates maintain high recognition rates in employer surveys
  4. Students who obtain a foundation certificate are guaranteed admission to 25 other universities in Otago and Australia, as well as some universities in the UK, Canada, and the US.
  5. Dunedin has produced New Zealand's greatest writers, poets, artists, and musicians. The campus lifestyle is highly popular among New Zealand students.

3. Departments and Majors:

Business School------- Economics, Accounting, Finance, Management, Information Management, Information Systems, Tourism, Marketing

School of Medicine------- Anatomy, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Pharmacology, Physiology, Pathology, Nutrition, Epidemiology, Genetics, Geriatrics, Immunology, Pharmacology, Nursing, Product Science, Physical Therapy, Virology, Medicine (including internal medicine, surgery, ophthalmology, pediatrics, dentistry, gynecology, obstetrics, radiology, psychiatric treatment, etc.)

The Faculty of Humanities ----- Education, Anthropology, Art History and Theory, Theology, Learning of Multiple Foreign Languages and Cultures, Classical Literature (Greek, Latin), History, Film and Media Studies, Geography (Geography, Urban Geography, Biogeography, Hydrology, Topography, Climatology), Law, Modern Literature and Film Music, Philosophy, Politics and Environment, Visual Culture, etc.

The Academy ----- Biology, Biotechnology, Botany, Chemistry, Textiles, Nutrition, Food Science, Computer Science, Design, Terrestrial Oceanography, Ecology, Physics, Electricity, Engineering, Environmental Science, Geology, Oceanography, Mathematics, Microbiology, Physics, Physiology, Psychology, Statistics, Surveying, Communications, Wildlife Management, Zoology, and more.

  1. Admission requirements:

Bachelor's degree: Domestic students complete two years of a three-year associate degree, and one year of bachelor's degree in four years
IELTS score of 6.0 (no section below 6.0)
Graduate diploma: Bachelor's degree
IELTS score of 6.5 (no individual section below 6.0), and some specific majors require 7.0
Graduate: Bachelor's degree
IELTS score of 6.5 (no individual section below 6.0), and some specific majors require 7.0
Preparatory Course: Senior year students have an average score above 70, and second-year students score 80. If the score is 10 points below this level, admission is possible
5-week foundation bridge course.
Additionally, transfer credits are provided for domestic junior college and undergraduate students, helping many students reduce about one year's worth of credits.

  1. Tuition/Living Expenses (Unit: New Zealand dollars, for reference only; actual fees are subject to the school's announcement)

Undergraduate Liberal Arts: 18,084-22,034/year; Science: 24,049/year
Living expenses: 10,000-12,000 per year

  1. Accommodation arrangements:

On-campus accommodation: campus residential area and college international student apartments
University residential areas with self-provided meals, with various sizes of shared apartments provided by the university.
Shared rental: can be an apartment or a suite, with bedrooms available as decorated or unfurnished. There are the following types: single rooms / small apartments (with their own kitchen or shared bathroom) / unit residences / apartments
Homestay: Students may also request to be hosted by a local host, with the host providing all meals, electricity, and water costs included in the meal fee.

  1. Start date:

Undergraduate and graduate programs start in February and June

一、学校概况：

奥塔哥大学是新西兰的第一所大学，始建于1869年。该大学以其优质的教学与领先的实用研究而享有国际声誉。

奥塔哥大学现拥有22，000多名大学生和3800多名正在攻读研究生文凭、硕士、博士课程的学生。

奥塔哥大学采用国际教育体制，拥有1100名来自50多个国家的留学生，并同全世界的许多机构建立了联系。这里还设立了一个强大的校友网络，我校110个国家的60，000名毕业生已通过该网络取得了联系。

学生的生活品质是奥塔哥大学的一个显著特点。该校所在的但尼丁城是一个名副其实的大学城，在那里，学习已经成为了一种生活方式。校园是一个美丽迷人的地方，既有古典建筑又有现代建筑，而从市中心走到这里也不过只需要几分钟的路程而已。

二、学校优势及特色：

  1. 新西兰历史最悠久的国立大学
  2. 出众的学术声誉和浓郁的研究文化
  3. 毕业生在雇主调查中保持很高的认可率
  4. 取得预科证书的学生可以保证进入奥塔哥和澳洲其他25所大学和英国，加拿大，美国的部分大学。
  5. 达尼丁曾诞生出新西兰最伟大的作家、诗人、艺术家和音乐家。校园的生活方式在新西兰学生中极有名气。

三，院系与专业设置：

商学院------- 经济、会计、金融、管理、资讯管理、信息系统，旅游、市场营销

医学院-------解剖学、生物化学、微生物学，药理学，生理学，病理学，营养学、流行病学、遗传学、老年医学、免疫学、药物学、护理学、物产科学、物理治疗、病毒学，医学（包括内科，外科，眼科，儿科、牙科，妇科，产科，放射科，精神病治疗等）

人文科学院-----教育学、人类学、艺术史及理论、神学、多种外国语言与文化学习、古典文学（希腊文，拉丁文）、历史学、电影及媒体研究、地理（地理学，城市地理，生物地理，水文，地形学、气候学）、法律、现代文学与电影音乐、哲学、政治与环境、视觉文化等。

科学院-----生物学、生物技术、植物学、化学、纺织学、营养学、食品学、计算机科学、设计学、陆地海洋学、生态学、物理，电学、工程学、环境科学、地质学、海洋学龄、数学、微生物学、物理学、生理学、心理学、统计学、测量学，通讯学、野生动物管理、动物学等。

  1. 入学要求：

本科： 国内3年大专完成两年，四年本科完成一年
雅思成绩6.0分（各单项不低于6.0分）
研究生文凭：本科毕业，学士学位
雅思成绩6.5分（各单项不低于6.0分），某些特定专业要求7.0分
研究生： 本科毕业，学士学位
雅思成绩6.5分（各单项不低于6.0分），某些特定专业要求7.0分
预科：高三学生成绩平均分70分以上，高二学生80分。如低于此分数10分，可以入读
5周预科桥梁课程。
另外针对国内专科和本科在读的学生提供转学分， 为很多学生减免了大约1年时间的学分。

  1. 学费/生活费（单位：新新西兰元，仅供参考，具体学费以校方公布为准）

本科 文科：18,084-22,034/年 理科：24,049/年
生活费 10,000－12,000/年

  1. 住宿安排：

校园内住宿：校园居住区和学院留学生公寓
自备伙食的大学居住区,由大学提供不同规格的合住单元公寓。
共同租房：可以是公寓或套房，卧室分装修和不装修两种。有下列几种：单人房间/小公寓（有自己的厨房或共用的浴室）/单元住宅/公寓/
寄宿家庭：学生也可要求安排当地人的寄宿家庭, 由房东提供所有的伙食，电费，水费包括在伙食费内。

  1. 开学日期：

本科及研究生开学：2月、6月
