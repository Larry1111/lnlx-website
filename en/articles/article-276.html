Nagano International Culture College is located in Kami-Suwa City, Nagano Prefecture. This city is a medium-sized city with a population of about 100,000, making it a famous tourist destination in Japan. The city is adjacent to the beautiful Japanese Alps and faces the picturesque Lake Suwa, making its tourism economy very developed. Additionally, Nagano International Culture Academy has only one Japanese language school in the city, offering students a good opportunity to work and study, and the school allows students to pay half a year's tuition upfront.

Among more than 260 Japanese language schools, Nagano International Culture Academy is a small to medium-sized school, with 120 students. The teachers are friendly and serious, and the teaching is serious and responsible. As a result, the pass rate for the Level 1 Japanese is among the highest among many schools, and 90% of graduates are admitted to various types of Japanese universities, making it a very popular school.

Registration Requirements:

1. Within two years of graduating from high school, vocational, or technical school

2. Within two years of completing a college diploma

3. Bachelor's degree from a university and under 27 years old
