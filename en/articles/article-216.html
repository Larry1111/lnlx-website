Kyonggi University

1. Overview of the school

Kyonggi University was established in 1947 with the mission of cultivating talent to realize the ideal of national development and shared prosperity for humanity. The founding mission of Kyonggi University is to pursue truth, pursue sincerity, and pursue a life of self-love, nation, and all humanity—truth, goodness, and beauty. Currently, Kyonggi University in Suwon has three colleges—College of Humanities and Arts, College of Social Sciences, and College of Science and Engineering—with eighteen departments. The Seoul branch has ten departments, making it one of the leading comprehensive universities both domestically and internationally. Kyonggi University has achieved tremendous development. Since its founding, to cultivate high-level scientific research talent, Kyonggi University has established general research institutes. Thirteen research institutes including the Architecture Research Institute, Business Research Institute, and Tourism Research Institute. There are also fifteen affiliated institutions, including the Central Library and the Agricultural Economics Museum in Korea. In addition, Kyonggi University has five affiliated research institutes in basic sciences and medical physics. Mainly dedicated to academic research and regional social development, to cultivate competitive talent and promote international education and research, Kyonggi University has established sister school relationships with prestigious foreign institutions in the United States, Russia, China, Japan, and other countries, actively facilitating research and exchange among faculty and students.

京畿大学

一、学校概况

京畿大学于1947年在为实现国家发展和人类共同繁荣的理想而培养人才的使命下设立的。京畿大学的建校宗旨是，探究真理，精诚所致，追求热爱自我，民族，以及全人类的真善美生活。现今，位于水原京畿大学设有人文艺术学院，社会科学学院，理工学院等三个学院十八个系，位于首尔的分校设有十个系，作为国内外有数的综合性大学。京畿大学已取得了巨大的发展。建校以来，为了培养高级科研人才，京畿大学成立了一般研究院。专门建筑研究院，专门经营研究院，专门旅游研究院等十三个研究院。中央图书馆和国内唯一的农经博物馆等十五个学校附属机关。此外，京畿大学还设有基础科学，医学物理等五个附属研究所。主要致力于学术研究与区域社会发展的研究，为了培养具有竞争力的人才，促进国际教育与研究的发展，京畿大学与美国，俄罗斯中国，日本等国外名门院校结为姊妹学校，积极促进教师，学生间的研究与交流。
