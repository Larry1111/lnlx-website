The National University of Angers is a public university with a 700-year history, located in Loire Valley, one of France's famous cultural and wine-producing destinations. Angers is a modern city that combines cultural and entertainment facilities. Surrounded by beautiful suburbs and rural atmosphere, the city is a pleasant modern city that offers students a pleasant French-style lifestyle compared to a metropolis, while also offering a desirable high-quality lifestyle with a rich historical and artistic heritage. Angers has convenient transportation, with Paris by train taking only an hour and a half. The University of Angers has about 17,000 enrolled students studying various majors from more than 10 countries worldwide. The university values scientific cooperation and teaching flexibility, with the International Relations Service Department cooperating and academic exchanges with more than 75 universities in more than 30 countries worldwide.

The University of Angers is a school that combines traditional teaching methods with a pioneering and innovative spirit. The curriculum arrangements across various departments are designed to ensure that students' courses and diplomas are effectively recognized in the future. The university can award 230 different diplomas, with 846 professors offering courses and lectures for over 1,200 majors, and the teaching buildings and offices cover an area of 135,200 square meters.
Angers is developing rapidly, but its city is not very large, and housing costs are much cheaper compared to Paris.

The university has modern teaching buildings and facilities fully suited for modern teaching and research, and effective cooperation among students, teachers, administrators, and technical staff ensures this. The university has now made all necessary preparations for its development.
The University of Angers in France is a democratic university with strong human rights. The university pays great attention to its students and strives to meet their needs, striving for a common goal: the university hopes every student can succeed.

University courses
With about 230 diplomas and degrees, different faculties offer different majors:
School of Law, Economics and Social Sciences; Economic Law, Public Law, Economics and Management, Business Management, Horticultural Management
The Liberal Arts College; Contemporary and Classical Literature, History, English, German, and College of Sciences; Human and Sociology — Psychology, History, Philosophy, Geography, Bioengineering, Communication, and Science – Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science.

法国国立昂热大学是一所具有700年历史的法国公立大学，位于法国著名的文化和产酒胜地之一的Loire Valley。昂热是一个集文化和娱乐设施为一体的现代化城市。城市被美丽的郊区和乡村气息所环绕，是一座宜人的现代化城市，比起大都市而言更可以使学生们体验到宜人的法国式生活，同时也为同学们提供令人向往的高质量生活并具有丰富的历史、艺术遗产。昂热市交通便利，坐火车到巴黎只要1个半小时的车程。昂热大学拥有大约17，000名注册学生就读不同的专业，来自世界l0多个国家。学校重视科学合作和教学的灵活性，国际联系服务部己和全世界30个多国家超过75所大学学府合作并进行学术上的交流。

昂热大学是一所既有传统教学方式，又具有开拓创新精神的学校。学校在各个部门的课程安排，均为了让学生所学的课程和文凭在未来能够得到有效的承认。该校可授予230种不同的文凭，846名教授为1200多种专业提供课程及讲座，学校的教学楼及办公室的面积为135200平方米。
昂热市发展迅速，但其城市的规模并不很大，与巴黎相比其住房的费用要便宜的多。

大学拥有现代化的教学大楼和设施，完全适合于现代化的教学与研究，并且学校的学生、教师、管理人员、技术人员之间的有效合作为此提供了保证。学校现己为其发展做好一切必要的准备。
