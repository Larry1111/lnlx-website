Lancaster University was founded in 1964 and is located in Lancashire, England. Lancaster University enjoys an excellent academic reputation and consistently ranks among the top universities in the UK. In the 2008 UK Guardian Good University Guide, Lancaster University ranked tenth out of 113 universities in the UK. The university boasts the UK's top Department of Environmental Science and the world-renowned LUMS School of Management.

Lancashire is located in the northwest of England, bordered by the Irish Sea to the west. Close to the beautiful Lake District and other scenic tourist attractions, the rail and road networks around Lancaster University are extremely convenient, taking only 3 hours by train to London and 1 hour to Manchester International Airport. The campus environment is safe, friendly, and attractive, and it takes only 10 minutes by bus from here to reach Lancaster city center.

The university has approximately 17,000 students and highly qualified faculty members, with international students accounting for about 15%. Lancaster University ranks among the top ten universities in the UK, achieving outstanding results in both research and teaching. It has received good ratings in teaching evaluations, with 12 departments rated as 'excellent.' Lancaster University has five colleges: the Colleges of Applied Science, Environmental and Natural Sciences, Anthropology, Social Sciences, and Management. Departments rated 5* include Business and Management, Physics, Sociology, and Statistics. Departments rated 5 include Applied Sociology, Computing, Education Studies, English, Environmental Science, European Languages and Cultures, Law, Linguistics, Psychology, and Religious Studies. Lancaster University is a world-leading research institution, and its wide range of undergraduate, master's, and PhD programs are popular among international students.

University Foundation Course: Start dates in September and January, with a course duration of 3 semesters.

Admission requirements: IELTS 5.0, currently in senior year or having graduated from high school.

Language course start dates: three times a year, in January, June, and September.

www.lancs.ac.uk

兰卡斯特大学始建于1964年，位于英格兰的兰开夏郡。兰卡斯特大学享有非常好的学术声誉，一直在英国大学排名中名列前茅。2008年英国卫报优秀大学指南中，兰卡斯特大学在英国113所大学中名列第十。该校拥有英国最顶尖的环境科学系和世界闻名的LUMS管理学院。

兰开夏郡位于英格兰西北部，西邻爱尔兰海。接近美丽的湖区和其他景色优美的观光胜地，兰卡斯特大学周边的铁路公路交通网极为便利，坐火车至伦敦只需3小时，到曼彻斯特国际机场只要1小时。校园环境很安全、友好并富有吸引力，从这里乘公交车去兰卡斯特市中心只需10分钟。

全校约有17,000名学生及水平优秀的教职员工，国际学生约占15%。兰卡斯特大学名列英国大学前十名，在研究和教学方面都取得了杰出的成绩，在教学评介上评价良好，有12个科系名列“优等”。兰卡斯特大学有五大学院，分别为：应用科学、环境与自然科学、人类学、社会科学与管理学院。评鉴为5*的科系有：商业与管理、物理、社会学、统计学。评鉴为5的有：应用社会学、电脑、教育研究、英语、环境科学、欧洲语言与文化、法律、语言学、心理学、宗教研究。兰卡斯特大学是世界领先的研究领导机构，学校广泛的学士学位、硕士学位和PhD课程都受到了国际学生的欢迎。

大学预科课程：开学日期为9月、1月，课程长度为3个学期。

入学要求：雅思5.0.，高三在读或高中毕业。

语言课程开课日期：每年1、6、9月三次开学。
