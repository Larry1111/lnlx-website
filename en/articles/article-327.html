In recent years, Studying in France Gradually heating up. The reason lies in France's well-established education system, low cost of living, and comprehensive social welfare advantages.
1. Elegant, convenient, and developed France
France is recognized for having the most well-planned railway system in Europe, and the successful development of the TGV is unrivaled across Europe. It has shortened the distance between major cities to just a stone's throw away.
France is also a world-class country in science and technology innovation, the world's fourth largest industrial power, and the top tourist destination. French is the official language of the United Nations, many international organizations, and more than 20 countries, and is also one of the most widely used languages of international organizations worldwide. Mastering this language means having a special "PASS" to access 47 different countries across five continents, including Switzerland, Belgium, and Canada.
2. A well-developed education system with many choices of institutions
French Higher Education One of the features of the system is its completeness: both long-term and short-term courses; It offers both general and vocational courses, with a distinction between applied and research-based training programs; Students can attend comprehensive universities as well as apply to specialized colleges; Each type of program has its own unique admission requirements, duration, teaching methods, and other characteristics; These features meet different objectives while ensuring that everyone can obtain a recognized diploma.
3. Comprehensive benefits and affordable consumption
Welfare benefits in France are generally better in Europe. French citizens (French nationals) and residents residing in France receive equal benefits.
Regarding the journey Studying in France Chinese students
——National universities Tuition is completely free, and elite colleges enjoy tuition fees equal to those of domestic students.
— The most affordable is the housing subsidy. This generous benefit allows international students to save quite a bit on rent, which is where France excels over other English-speaking (forum) countries. The maximum subsidy can reach 50% or 60%. For cohabitants, the subsidy can even reach 90%.

近年来，法国留学渐渐升温。其原因在于法国完善的教育体制，低廉的消费及完善的社会福利等有利条件。
一、优雅、便利、发达的法国
法国被公认为拥有欧陆规划最完善的铁路系统，TGV的研发成功更是傲视全欧。它使各大城市间的距离缩短为咫尺天涯。
法国还是世界一流的科学与技术创新国，世界第四大工业强国和第一大旅游目的地国，法语是联合国和许多国际组织及20多个国家的正式官方语言，也是世界上应用最广泛的国际组织语言之一。掌握这一门语言，也即意味着拥有了一张通向包括瑞士、比利时、加拿大等在内的五大洲47个不同国家的特殊“PASS”。
二、教育体制完善，院校选择多
法国高等教育体制的特点之一就是完善：既有长期课程, 又有短期课程 ；既开设普通与职业化课程,又区分应用性和研究性培养训练；既能就读综合大学, 又能报考专业学院；每一类课程都有其特殊的录取条件, 学制时间, 教学方法等方面的特点；这些特点既能满足不同的目的, 又能使所有人都有可能获得一项受认可的文凭。
三、福利完善，消费低廉
法国福利待遇在整个欧洲较好。法国的公民（法国籍人士）和在法国居留的居民在福利待遇上是同等的。
对于赴法国留学的中国学生来说
——国立大学全免学费，精英学院享受和本国学生同等的低廉学费。
——最实惠的就是住房补贴。这项优厚的福利待遇使得留学生在房租上能省下不少钱，这一点是法国优于其他英语(论坛)国家的地方。补助金额最高的能达到50% 或60%。同居者的补贴甚至为90%。
