Russian Peoples' Friendship University


[Introduction]: People's Friendship University of Russia is a renowned public university located in Moscow, Russia. It was founded in 1961 by Soviet leader Khrushchev, originally named Lumumba People's Friendship University, dedicated to training young Communist Party members in Third World countries. Today, People's Friendship University of Russia has become one of the most renowned comprehensive universities in Russia.

After the collapse of the Soviet Union, this university added many business courses and can be considered Russia's "Ivy League" university. It ranks only behind Moscow State University and Saint Petersburg University, ranking third overall in Russia and second in faculty level. Additionally, Russia's People's Friendship University, Moscow State University, and Saint Petersburg State University are the only three universities in Russia that can independently establish and control their teaching plans. Every year, government-sponsored students, self-funded students, and inter-university exchange students from over 160 countries and regions enroll.

The university enjoys a worldwide reputation. The university is an important research center, with over 500 people owning their own inventions and innovations.

Peoples' Friendship University of Russia, abbreviated as PFUR.

Russian Peoples' Friendship University (Российский университет дружбы народов), originally named Lumumba People's Friendship University, was then named Patrice Lumumba People's Friendship University. It is a symbol of the struggle for independence and liberation of oppressed peoples in Third World countries. In total, more than 50,000 international students from more than 160 countries have received and completed higher education at this university, which fully combines traditional higher education with patriotic education for youth.

All foreign cosmonauts at the Gagarin Cosmonaut Training Center receive Russian language training in the preparatory department at Friendship University. In addition to obtaining a professional diploma, graduates from Friendly University also receive an interpreter certificate.

The Peoples' Friendship University of Russia was founded in 1960 by Soviet leader Khrushchev, originally named Lumumba People's Friendship University, dedicated to training young Communist Party members in Third World countries. At that time, it was named Patrice Lumumba People's Friendship University and symbolized the struggle for independence and liberation of oppressed peoples in Third World countries. In total, more than 50,000 foreign students from more than 160 countries received and completed higher education at this university, which fully combines traditional higher education with patriotic education for youth. It is an emerging and renowned institution focused on international relations and world culture, known as the "cradle of world-class politicians." UTU is a federal national university that operates on the basis of global and Russian culture, science, education, humanities, and democracy.

University enrollment

UTU is an international university, attracting students from more than 100 countries and regions around the world every year to study here. Currently, UTU has about 13,000 students enrolled, including over 11,000 undergraduates and around 2,000 postgraduates. UTU has 14 world-renowned scholars, over 1,000 professors, and nearly 1,800 associate professors. The university holds various international academic conferences and various forms of world cultural exchange activities every year. In addition to receiving professional diplomas and degrees, UTU graduates can also obtain translator certificates.

On January 20, 2000, UN Secretary-General Annan delivered a message to the university's 40th anniversary, saying: "The purpose of founding the school is very good, and the quality of teaching is very high. The university has made significant contributions to world peace and development over the past forty years. We wish for even greater achievements in the future." The head of UNESCO, the head of the World University Student Organization institutions, and then Russian President Vladimir Vladimirovich Putin all attended the university's 40th anniversary celebration.

The Russian Peoples' Friendship University has 135 teaching and research offices, 4 faculties (School of World Economics and Business; School of Tourism and Hotel Management; School of Foreign Languages; Physics, Chemistry, Automation, and Computer Research Institute) 5 campuses and 10 departments (Preparatory Departments, Engineering, Physics, Mathematics and Natural Sciences, Agriculture and Forestry, Medicine, Ecology, Environment and Environmental Protection, Social Humanities, Economics, Law). Currently, there are 13,218 students enrolled, including 11,496 undergraduate students, 1,180 postgraduates, and 2,640 international students from 102 countries and regions.

The Russian Peoples' Friendship University has 14 world-renowned scholars, 1,030 professors, and 1,741 associate professors. The university holds various international academic conferences annually and organizes various forms of world cultural exchange activities.

UTU's curriculum not only takes into account the characteristics of secondary education in the countries and regions where international students are located, but also takes into account the characteristics of the professional activities students will pursue after graduation, adopting corresponding syllabi. UTU is one of the best schools for international students to master Russian. After one year of preparatory course, international students can master Russian as well as general basic professional knowledge, laying the groundwork for further major choices and continuously studying Russian during the first four years after enrollment.

UTU's library holds over 2 million volumes and includes a gymnasium, hospital, international cultural center, and a partial computer network connected to the international network.

The university is located next to the Forest Park in southwest Moscow and is one of the most beautiful places in Russia. The university has a university town, 13 student dormitory and apartment buildings, and is equipped with comprehensive sports and cultural facilities. The university is right next to the metro and is just 30 minutes from the city center, making transportation very convenient.

The school has a comprehensive television center where students can watch TV programs from around the world. The school has a well-developed medical system, with routine preventive and health check-ups for students twice a year. The school has a dedicated international student association, which regularly organizes visits, tours, and various cultural activities for international students.

The school has four cafeterias and eight dining halls, with a monthly living allowance of about $200. Student dormitories are quite good, with rooms of 18 square meters per three students, equipped with single beds, wardrobes, desks, and bedding provided by the school. Sheets are changed weekly, and there are public showers and kitchens. International students enjoy excellent and convenient study and living at this school. The school is a top school recommended by both the Russian Ministry of Education and the Chinese Ministry of Education.

Russian Peoples' Friendship University was founded on February 5, 1960. At that time, the Soviet government decided to establish Russian People's Friendship University to support the construction of other socialist countries and to cultivate socialist talent for other countries. Over its 49 years of development, the university has enjoyed a worldwide reputation. Today, Russian Peoples' Friendship University is a leader in university education worldwide, producing over 470,000 graduates, including 4,300 associate and doctoral degree holders who work in 165 countries worldwide. The university has 57 fields and majors, training about 23,000 bachelor's, expert, and graduate students from 131 countries (over 450 ethnic groups), including over 1,000 ethnic Russians and about 3,500 foreign students. The university currently has 2,112 faculty members, including 332 professors and PhDs, 807 associate professors and associate doctors, 61 academicians of the Russian Academy of Sciences, 50 teachers awarded the title of Honored Scientist of the Russian Federation, and 26 faculty members from foreign academies of sciences. The university is an important research center, with over 500 people possessing their own inventions and innovations.

Departments and majors

Since 1988, after aligning with the international degree system, the People's Friendship University of Russia began teaching on a bachelor's-master's model and trained highly educated professionals. This model is implemented in most specialized programs. Some majors do not offer bachelor's degrees; after completing their studies, graduates obtain professional certificates or other corresponding certificates. The bachelor's degree program lasts four years (excluding preparatory courses). Upon successful completion, graduates are awarded a diploma and bachelor's degree certificate issued by the state.

Undergraduate graduates are entitled to a two-year master's program at the graduate school. After completing two years of study and defending their master's thesis, they can receive a master's diploma and a master's degree certificate issued by the state.

Renowned graduate

Since its founding in 1960, Peoples' Friendship University of Russia has trained a large number of professionals worldwide, especially in Asia, Africa, and Latin America. Its graduates include more than a dozen heads of state and dozens of ministers and ambassadors.

大学人数

友大的图书馆藏书量超过200万册，拥有体育馆、医院、国际文化中心、与国际网连接的计算机局部网络。

该校坐落在莫斯科西南森林公园旁，是俄罗斯最美丽的地点之一。学校有大学生城，13幢学生宿舍楼和公寓楼，有完善的体育文化设施，学生城紧临地铁，30分钟可到达市中心，交通十分便利。

学校有四个食堂，八个餐厅，每月生活费200美元左右，学生宿舍较好，每3个学生一个房间，18平方米，有单人床、大衣柜、写字台、床上用品，均由校方提供，每星期换一次被单，有公共沐浴间和厨房，留学生在该校学习和生活非常优越和方便。该校是俄罗斯教育部和中国教育部首推学校。

俄罗斯人民友谊大学创立于1960年2月5日，当时，苏联政府为了援助世界其它社会主义国家的建设，为其它国家培养建设社会主义的人才，所以决定创建俄罗斯人民友谊大学，主要为其它国家培养人才，在其发展的49年间，大学在世界范围内享有盛誉。现今，俄罗斯人民友谊大学在世界范围内都是大学教育的佼佼者，学校培养了超过47万的毕业生，其中，4300名副博士和博士，他们工作在世界上的165个国家。学校有57个培养领域和专业，培养了来自131个国家(450多个民族)的大约2.3万学士、专家、研究生，其中包括超过1000人的俄罗斯族和大约3500外国学生。大学现有教师2112名，其中332名教授、博士，807名副教授、副博士，61名俄罗斯两院院士和， 50名荣获俄罗斯联邦功勋科学家称号的教师， 26名国外院校的科学院的教师。大学是一个重要的科研中心，超过500人拥有自己的发明和创新。

系别专业

自1988年起，与国际学位制接轨后，俄罗斯人民友谊大学开始按学士-硕士模式教学，并培养受过高等教育的专业人士。这个模式在大多数专业教学中得以实行。某些专业不进行学士的培养，在学业结束后，毕业生取得专业人士证书或其它相应等级证书。学士学位制四年(不包括预科)，毕业生顺利完成学业后，被授予国家颁发的毕业证书和学士学位证书。

本科毕业生有权进入研究生院学制两年的硕士班学习。结束两年学业后，通过硕士论文答辩后，可获得国家颁发的硕士毕业证书和硕士学位证书。
