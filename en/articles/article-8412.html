Saint Petersburg State University of Mechanics, Information Technology and Optics


[Introduction]: Over the past century, Saint Petersburg State University of Mechanics, Information Technology and Optics has accumulated scientific and technological momentum, offering a unique educational environment and ground-level infrastructure for scientific and innovative prosperity. Its main research areas include optical technology, management systems, and precision mechanical engineering.

Introduction to the Institution

Saint Petersburg State University of Information Technology, Mechanics and Optics is one of the oldest universities in Russia. Founded in 1900, it is a five-year educational institution designed to train qualified personnel in precision mechanics and optics. The university has a strong faculty, with 562 experts and lecturers. This includes 120 Doctors of Science and 310 Doctoral Doctors.

One of Russia's renowned engineering schools and a source of pride for the country. It ranks among the world's top in computer science and technology, especially in software design, maintaining the top three globally in recent years. It is the only higher education institution in the former Soviet Union that focuses on optics, optoelectronic technology, precision mechanics, and computer technology. It is the location of the Russian Ministry of Education's Academic Systems Association for Optics and Instrument Manufacturing.

The university's main research areas include optical technology, computer information engineering, management systems, precision mechanical engineering, electronic engineering and electronics, and applied research in basic mathematical technology, among others. The main basic research areas include: quantum electronics and nonlinear optics, biological tissue optics, physical optics and spectroscopy, laser and optical technology, energy monitoring, centerless optics, computer management application technology, principles of nonlinear systems, computer networks, and supercomputers.

The college offers 150 full-time courses and seven departments composed of 52 teaching and research offices. These departments are: Optical Information Systems and Technology (19% of students), Physical Engineering (15%), Computer Technology and Control Systems (34%), Precision Mechanics and Technology (15%), Computer Science and Software Engineering (2%), Natural Sciences (7%), and Humanities (8%). The college produces bachelor's, master's, and doctoral students. The graduate school has 24 PhD and Associate Doctoral degree programs, with 8 specialized committees responsible for doctoral and associate doctoral defenses. Specific program names include: Optics and Electro-Optics, Information Technology and Computer Engineering, Computer Technology and Electronic Communication Systems, Laser Technology and Biomedical Optics, Economic Management Information Systems, Instrument Manufacturing, Engineering and Computer Imaging, and others. The university's curriculum and comprehensive training system will help graduates find paid jobs and seek employment opportunities.

Advantages of the institution

1. Accumulation of scientific and technological potential. Starlight Machine — is a mainstream academic center in the field of optics and ICT;

2. A unique educational environment;

3. Ground-level infrastructure for science and innovation;

圣彼得堡国立机械、信息技术与光学大学


【导读】：圣彼得堡国立机械、信息技术与光学大学建校百年来积累了科学技术势能，拥有独一无二的教育环境，也具有用于科学和革新繁荣地面基础设施。主要研究领域为光学技术、管理系统和精密机械工程等。

院校介绍

圣彼得堡国立信息技术机械与光学大学，俄罗斯最为古老的高校之一。该校建立于1900年，是为培训精密机械和光学方面的合格人才而建立的5年制教育学校。大学师资力量雄厚，拥有562位专家和讲师。其中包括120位科学博士，310位博士。

俄罗斯著名的工科学校之一，是俄罗斯的骄傲。在计算机科学技术方面是世界前几名，特别是软件设计上近几年始终保持世界前三名。是原苏联唯一一所以光学、光电技术、精密机械和计算机技术为教学核心的高等院校。是俄罗斯教育部光学与仪器制造学术系统协会的所在地。

大学的主要科研领域为光学技术、计算机信息工程、管理系统、精密机械工程、电子工程和电子学、基础数学技术学应用研究等等。 主要基础研究领域主要包括：量子电子学和非线性光学、生物组织光学、物理光学和光谱学、激光和光学技术、能量监测、无心光学、计算机管理应用技术、非线性系统原理、计算机网络和超级计算机。

学院全日制教学提供150个课程，52个教研室组成的七个系，这些系是：光学信息系统和技术(学生数占学生总数的19%)、物理工程(占学生总数的15%)、计算机技术和控制系统(占学生总数34%)、精密机械和技术(占学生总数的15%)、计算机科学和软件工程(占学生总数的2%)、自然科学(占学生总数的7%)、人文科学(占学生总数的8%)。学院培养学士、硕士和博士。大学研究生院设置了24个理博士和副博士学位专业，8个负责博士、副博士答辩的专门委员会。 具体专业名称包括：光学和电光学、信息技术和计算机工程、计算机技术和电子通讯系统、激光技术和生物医药光学、经济管理信息系统、仪器制造、工程和计算机图像等。大学的教学计划和全部培训体系将有利于毕业生找到有偿工作和寻求就业的机会。
