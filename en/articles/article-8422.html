Moscow Aerospace University


[Introduction]: Moscow University of Aerospace and Cosmos is located in Moscow, the capital of Russia, and is one of the world's most renowned key universities. Founded in 1929, many aerospace scientists from around the world have lived and studied here. Since the 1930s, China has continuously sent large numbers of students and experts to study and work here. All the aerospace experts of China's first spacecraft, the "Shenzhou" spacecraft, launched in November 1999, studied at this university.

School Introduction

Chinese Name: Moscow Aviation Institute

Abbreviation:МАИ

Founded:1930

Location: Moscow

Category: Public universities

Type of school: Science and engineering

The Moscow State Aviation Institute was established on August 29, 1930, and is a specialized aviation engineering university.

Moscow State Aviation Institute is renowned worldwide for its strong research faculty, advanced equipment, and high-quality teaching. Many Russian experts in aerospace and high-tech fields graduated from this university. Designers from Russia's Tupolev, Mikoyan, and Jakovlev Design Bureaus were the first generation of professors at the Aviation Institute. Many Russian national academicians, scientists, and some famous cosmonauts graduated from the Aviation Academy.

Departmental Structure

Currently, the university has nearly 20,000 students enrolled, boasts a strong faculty, and has 6 research institutes, 9 departments, and more than 60 majors.

Undergraduate, master's, and doctoral programs at the university are admitted without entrance exams; majors related to military directions are not selected.

1. Aircraft Mechanical Department

2. Aircraft Engine Department

3. Department of Electronic Power and Information Operation

4. Radio Electronic Aircraft Department

5. Department of Economics and Management

6. Aerospace Department

7. Department of Applied Mathematics and Physics

8. Department of Applied Mechanical Engineering

9. Humanities Department

莫斯科航空航天大学


【导读】：莫斯科航空航天大学位于俄罗斯首都莫斯科市，是闻名世界的重点大学之一。该校始建于1929年，世界众多的航天科学家都在此生活和学习过。中国从30年代不断选派大批学生和专家在此留学和工作。1999年11月中国首次发射的“神洲”号宇宙飞船的航空航天专家均在该校留学过。

学校简介

中文名：莫斯科航空学院

简 称：МАИ

创办时间：1930年

所属地区：莫斯科

类 别：公立大学

学校类型：理工

莫斯科国立航空学院建于1930年8月29日，是专业航空工程大学。

莫斯科国立航空学院以其雄厚的科研师资力量，完善的设备和高质量的教学享誉世界。俄国众多的航空航天及高技术领域的专家都毕业于这所大学。俄罗斯Tupolev(图波列夫)设计局、Mikoyan(米格)设计局、Jakovlev(雅可夫列夫)设计局的设计师曾经是航空学院的第一代教授。许多俄罗斯的某种国家院士、科学家及一些著名的宇航员都出自航空学院。

院系设置

目前该校有在校生近20000人，师资力量雄厚，有6个研究院9个系，60多个专业。

学校本科、硕士、博士均免试入学，涉及军事方向的专业不可选。

1.飞机机械系

2.飞行器发动机系

3.电子动力与信息操作系

4.无线电电子飞行器系

5.经济与管理系

6.航空宇宙系

7.应用数学与物理系

8.应用机械系

9.人文系
