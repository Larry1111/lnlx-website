Founded in 1964, the University of Waikato is a relatively new university renowned for its business management, education, and computer science. In recent years, due to the development of e-commerce, the university has also offered related subjects.
The University of Waikato is located in Hamilton, North Island, with a population of about 120,000, and is a 90-minute drive from downtown Auckland. The University of Waikato focuses on interdisciplinary programs and professional research. The academic qualifications offered by the university are built on two principles: flexibility; and a strong emphasis on scientific research. This requirement allows students to flexibly combine their majors with minors according to their interests and abilities, while ensuring continuity and laying a solid foundation for employment or further study.
The University of Waikato currently has about 12,000 students, with the proportion of Indigenous Māori being the highest among the seven universities in New Zealand. In addition to working in major New Zealand companies and government services, a significant proportion of graduates also go on to develop their careers overseas. Due to its relatively young age and modern facilities, the university is actively developing, flexibly applying to subjects and allowing students to choose courses across departments to unlock everyone's potential.
In addition to innovative teaching styles, the university has consistently maintained international-level academic research standards, with research funding received as a proportion of funding to enrollment among New Zealand universities.
Features
It is a government-funded, internationally recognized national university with a spirit of progress and innovation. It is the only university in New Zealand offering a bachelor's degree program in computer science and mathematical sciences. Graduates have strong employment competitiveness after earning a bachelor's degree, earning the title "New Zealand's hope for the 21st century."

怀卡托大学成立于1964年，是一所较新的大学，以企业管理、教育和计算机著名，近年来由于电子商务的发达，该校也开设这一类有关的科目。
怀卡托大学位于北岛的汉密尔顿市，人口约为12万，离奥克兰市中心驱车90分钟。怀卡托大学的重点放在跨学科项目和专业研究方面。该校提供的学历是根据以下两个原则建构起来的，一是灵活性；二是重点强调科技研究。这样的要求使学生可以根据个人兴趣和能力灵活地把专业与辅修学科结合起来，同时又能确保专业的连贯性，为学生就业或继续学习打下一个坚实的基础。
怀卡托大学目前学生约有一万两千多名学生，其中土著毛利人所占的比例是全新西兰七所大学之中最高。毕业生除在新西兰大企业及政府服务外，也有极大比例前往海外发展。由于校龄较轻，设备新颖，目前学校正积极发展，在学习的科目上尽可能发挥弹性，并容许学生跨系选课，以激发每个人的潜力。
在教学作风创新之余，该校亦一直维持国际级的学朮研究水平，所获得的研究资金按照资金数额与在校学生数的比例在新西兰大学中是最高的。
特色
这是政府资助、高水准国际认可，具有进步创新精神的国立大学。它是新西兰所有大学中唯一一所提钩计算机及数理科学学士学位课程的大学， 学生获学士学位后具有较强的就业竞争优势， 被称为“21世纪新西兰的希望”。
