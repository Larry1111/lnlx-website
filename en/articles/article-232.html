The University of East Anglia (UEA) was established in 1963 and is situated in 320 hectares of woodland, with all campus buildings no more than a 5-minute walk from each other. The university consists of 18 schools and several research institutes. The university has remarkable achievements in many areas, including teaching and research.

Norwich, where the university is located, is a friendly and safe city with all the characteristics of a "university town." The picturesque city of Norwich is located in the most beautiful rural center of East England. Norwich city center is only 4 kilometers from the university campus, with several buses running between the campus and the city center. Norwich is a historic city with many medieval architectural monuments, the most spectacular being the 11th-century cathedral and Norman Castle overlooking the entire city. The whole city is full of youthful energy, and the cost of living is relatively low. The city has many street cafes and international restaurants offering a wide range of cuisines, from Indian, Chinese, Greek, and Thai cuisines to Mexican, Spanish, Italian, and British dishes. Additionally, Norwich is only thirty kilometers from the sea, and the Norfolk coastline is dotted with wildlife reserves, small markets, and traditional villages.

The university's eleven research units have been rated as internationally rated as excellent and are also global centers for climate and environmental research. Currently, there are 14,740 members, including 11,120 undergraduates, 3,620 graduate students, and 2,211 international students, of whom about 130 are Chinese students.

Notable majors: Environmental Science, Biological Sciences, Education, History, Social Work, Pure Mathematics, Communication and Media Studies.

Admission requirements for undergraduate foundation courses: at least 17 years old, IELTS score above 5.0, currently in senior high school in China or having graduated from high school.

Graduate diploma program admission requirements: Bachelor's degree, IELTS 5.5 or above.

Language course start dates: Four times a year, in January, April, July, and September.

www.uea.ac.uk

东英吉利大学（UEA）成立于1963年，位于方圆320公顷的林地中，所有校区建筑彼此之间均不超过5分钟步行距离。该校由18个学院和一些研究机构组成。学校在教学和科研等诸多领域都有着辉煌的成就。

该校所在的诺里奇市是一座友好而安全的城市，有“大学城”的所有特征。风景如画的诺里奇市处于东英吉利地区最美丽的乡村中心。诺里奇市中心距离大学校园仅4公里，有多班公共汽车往返于大学校园和市中心。诺里奇是一座历史古城，城中有许多中世纪的建筑古迹，其中最为壮观的就是十一世纪的天主大教堂和俯瞰全城的诺曼城堡。整个城市富有年轻人的朝气，生活费用相对低廉。市内有很多街边咖啡馆和各国风味餐馆，提供各类美食，从印度、中国、希腊和泰国菜系到墨西哥、西班牙、意大利和英国菜肴，应有尽有。此外，诺里奇距离海边仅三十公里，诺福克郡的海岸线上遍布着野生动物保护区、小集市和传统的村落。

大学的十一个研究单位被评为国际优秀水准，也是全球的气候环境研究中心，目前共有14,740名，其中本科生11,120名，研究生3,620名，国际学生2,211名，其中约有130名是中国学生。

知名专业：环境科学、生物科学、教育学、历史学、社会工作学、纯粹数学、传播与媒体研究。

本科预科课程入学要求：年满17周岁，雅思5.0分以上，中国高三在读或高中毕业。

研究生文凭课程入学要求：本科毕业，学士学位，雅思5.5以上。

语言课程开课日期：每年1、4、7、9月四次开课。

www.uea.ac.uk
