Russia's four most popular majors

1. Computer and Engineering Majors

Computer science is a strong major at Russian universities, with the most renowned being the computer science program at Saint Petersburg State Technical University. Ranked among the top five internationally, it is a corresponding institution to Tsinghua University in China. Every year, Tsinghua University has exchange students who come to Tsinghua University for further study. Every year, students from developed countries including the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom come to the university to pursue master's or doctoral programs in computer science.

Because the courses are advanced and difficult, those who earn master's and doctoral degrees are among the best among students, making them especially favored by major international computer industry companies, offering excellent career prospects.

Russia's heavy industry and aerospace industry are internationally significant and hold very important positions. Russian universities also offer highly advanced professional education in this area. Mechanical engineering and control technology at Saint Petersburg State Technical University are excellent majors and are in urgent national demand.

2. Medical Fields

Russian medicine is also world-class internationally. It is well known that many countries' medical programs are not open to foreign students, but Russia is an exception and has relatively low requirements for students: only graduates of formal domestic high schools can apply. Famous institutions such as Moscow Sedonov Medical College and St. Petersburg Pavlov Medical College have long histories and outstanding achievements. These universities offer comprehensive majors, advanced facilities, rigorous teaching, and have trained many renowned medical experts. It is believed that outstanding students from these universities will surely make a mark in the domestic medical field.

3. Arts

Russian universities' art majors are also internationally renowned. Music programs include Tchaikovsky Musicology; Fine arts include the Repin Academy of Fine Arts, Surikov State Art Institute, and St. Petersburg Art and Design Institute; Some national universities of culture and arts and comprehensive universities offer world-class teaching quality. Russia itself is also a country rich in artistic atmosphere. Especially St. Petersburg, a very Europeanized city where you can see Western Renaissance sculptures, paintings, and exquisite buildings everywhere.

Music and dance are a source of pride for Russians, and being able to study in a country that has produced world-class masters like Tchaikovsky and Ulanova is undoubtedly the dream of art enthusiasts.

4. Economics

Economics-related majors only began to gain popularity in Russia in the 1990s. With the dissolution of the former Soviet Union, the national economic system underwent tremendous changes, shifting from a planned economy to a market economy. People's economic concepts fundamentally changed, studying economic development laws and learning to master and control markets became hot topics, and economics majors became popular choices among university students. Russia's reforms provide us with many experiences and lessons to draw from, and studying economics courses at Russian universities also provides us with more resources.

Due to historical and geographical reasons, the economies of China and Russia have always maintained a subtle complementary relationship. Trade between the two countries is very frequent. This also provides business opportunities for students majoring in economics to showcase their skills. Russia's current foreign trade business has expanded to Southeast Asia, Africa, and South America. If students studying international trade understand both Russian and English, they will become sought-after talents in those countries.

俄罗斯四大热门专业

一、计算机和工程类

计算机科学是俄罗斯大学的优势专业，名气最大的要数圣彼得堡国立技术大学的计算机专业。国际排名前五位，是我国清华大学的对口院校，每年清华大 学都有交换生到圣技术学习深造。每年都有来自一些发达国家包括美国、加拿大、英国在内的学生前往该校就读计算机硕士课程或博士课程。

由于课程的设置比较先进，难度也较大，能够拿到硕士学位和博士学位的人都是学生中的佼佼者，因而也特别受到国际各大计算机行业的公司青睐，就业出路都非常好。

俄罗斯的重工业和航天工业在国际上举足轻重，占有非常重要的地位。俄罗斯大学在这方面的专业教育也十分先进。圣彼得堡国立技术大学的机械工程、控制技术等都是很好的专业，是国家紧缺的专业。

二、医学类

俄罗斯的医学在国际上也是首屈一指，众所周知世界上很多国家的医学专业是对外国留学生不开放的，但俄罗斯却例外，而且对于学生的要求也不高：只 要国内正规高中毕业即可申请。像著名的莫斯科谢东诺夫医学院、圣彼得堡巴甫洛夫医学院等都有着悠久的历史和辉煌的业绩。这些大学专业齐全，设施先进，教学 严谨，培养出了许多著名的医学专家。相信在这些大学里出来的优秀学生一定会在国内的医学界有所作为。

三、艺术类

俄罗斯大学的艺术类专业在国际上也很有名气。音乐类有柴可夫斯基音乐学；美术类有列宾美院、国立苏里科夫艺术学院、圣彼得堡艺术设计学院；还有 一些国立的文化艺术大学以及综合类大学的美术系都能提供世界一流的教学质量。俄罗斯本身也是一个充满艺术氛围的国家。尤其是圣彼得堡，一个非常欧化的城 市，到处可以看见西方文艺复兴时期的雕塑、绘画作品和精美的建筑物。

音乐和舞蹈是俄罗斯人的骄傲，能够在培养出象柴可夫斯基和乌兰诺娃这样的世界级大师的国家深造，无疑是爱好艺术的学生梦寐以求的愿望。

四、经济类

经济类专业是90年代才开始在俄罗斯热起来的专业。随着前苏联的解体，国家经济体制发生了巨大的变化，从计划经济转变为市场经济，人们的经济观 念也起了根本的变化，研究经济发展规律，学会掌握和控制市场成了热门话题，经济类的专业也随之成为大学生选择的热门专业。俄罗斯的改革给我们提供了许多可 以借鉴经验和教训，在俄罗斯的大学里学习经济类的课程也同样能给我们提供更多的素材。

由于历史和地理的原因，我国和俄罗斯的经济始终存在着一种微妙的互补关系。两国之间的贸易往来十分频繁。这也为学习经济类专业的学生提供了大展 身手的业务空间。俄罗斯目前的外贸业务已经拓展到东南亚、非洲和南美。如果学国际贸易的学生即懂俄语，又会英语，将会成为那些国家的抢手人才。
