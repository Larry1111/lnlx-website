Address: Sudbury, a mining city in northern Ontario

Basic Introduction:

Cambrian College of Applied Arts and Sciences was founded in 1966 and has a total enrollment of about 14,000 students. More than 4,500 full-time students study in more than 80 majors, and more than 9,500 students take 900 half-day courses. It is a Canadian public college recognized by the Chinese Ministry of Education， Student Cooperation Program (SPP) programs One of the first batch of institutions, an officially accredited DLI college in Canada that accepts international students.

Priority Majors and Departments:

1. Mining industry clusters (mining technology, chemical technology, civil engineering technology, environmental monitoring and impact assessment, technical majors). -Industrial machinery equipment technician , Heavy Industry Technician, HVAC Technician )

[if !support Lists] [endif] Chemical Engineering Technology - Laboratory Process Control (3-year program) Chemical Engineering Technology - Lab & Process Control （CHLP）

The only college in Ontario offering this major. Curriculum: Wet chemistry, sample preparation and analysis, operation of analytical instruments. Wide employment scope: petrochemical products, food testing, pharmaceuticals, fabrics and dyes, mineralogy, environmental research, water purification, wastewater management, metallurgy, nanoelectronics. Graduates are 100% employed and work in positions related to their major.

[if !supportLists] [endif] Mining Engineering Technology* (MNTY)

The only college in Ontario offering this major. Mainly focuses on mining machinery, ventilation, geology, and surveying; graduates can intern at local mining companies. Annual salary can reach 80,000 CAD.

[if !supportLists] [endif] Heavy Equipment Technician (2-year program) (HDTN)

Brian College This major is a leader in this discipline, equipped with CAD 4.2 million in teaching facilities. Graduates of the program have won gold or silver awards in Ontario university student skills competitions. Graduates can work in forestry, Employment in mining and construction industries, as well as in heavy equipment manufacturing plants and truck shops.

2. Community service clusters (early childhood education, development services, youth work, social work). )

[if !supportLists] [endif] Early Childhood Education (2 years) * (ECEP)

Ke Bryan College is the president school of the Ontario Early Childhood Educators Association, and Canada urgently needs talent in this field.

3. Computer engineering technology, network technician, information technology business analysis

[if !supportLists] [endif] Computer System Technology (3-year program) Computer System Technology (CETY)

Ontario's only certified training academy authorized by both Microsoft Microsoft and Cisco, with CCNA, CCNP, CCNAS, CCNP, and MCSA training content embedded in professional courses. Upon graduation, you can take certification exams and obtain the corresponding level of qualification without additional training.

地址：安大略省北部矿业城市萨德伯里市（Sudbury）

基本介绍：

凯布莱恩应用文理学院创建于1966年，在校学生总数约14,000余人。4,500余名全日制学生在80多个专业学习，另外还有9,500余名学生分别选修900个半日制课程。是中国教育部承认的加拿大公立学院，学生合作计划（SPP) 项目的首批院校之一，加拿大官方认证的可接收国际学生的DLI学院。

优先专业和院系：

1.矿业产业集群（矿业技术、化工技术、土木工程技术、环境监控和影响测评，技工专业-工业机械设备技师, 重工技师，暖通空调技师)

[if !supportLists] [endif]化学工程技术-实验室过程控制（3年制） Chemical Engineering Technology - Lab & Process Control （CHLP）

安省唯一开设此专业的学院。学习内容：湿化学、样品制备和分析、分析仪器的操作。就业面广：石油化工产品、食品检测、药品、面料和染料、矿物学、环境研究、水的净化、废水管理、冶金、纳米电子。毕业生100% 就业并在所学专业的相关岗位工作。

[if !supportLists] [endif]采矿工程（3年制）Mining Engineering Technology* （MNTY）

安大略省唯一开设此专业的学院。主要学习矿业机械、通风、地理地质、勘测，毕业生可以在本地矿业公司实习。年薪可达80,000加币。

[if !supportLists] [endif]重型机械设备（2年制）Heavy Equipment Technician （HDTN）

凯布莱恩学院该专业是此学科的佼佼者，拥有420万加元的教学设备，该专业历届学生都在安省高校学生技能竞赛中获得金奖或银奖。毕业生可在林业、采矿和建筑行业以及在重型设备制造厂和卡车店就业。

2.社区服务集群（幼儿教育，发展服务工作，青少年工作，社工)
