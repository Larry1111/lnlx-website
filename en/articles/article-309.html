The Marco Polo Program and Turandot Program for Studying in Italy are organized and managed by the Italy-China Foundation, jointly invested by the Italian Cariplo Foundation, and collaborate with the following institutions: the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Education for Universities and Research, the Italian Embassy in China, the Chinese Embassy in Italy, and the Italian University Presidents Association. The aim is to strengthen academic exchanges between Italy and China, jointly supported by the Chinese and Italian governments, and with government subsidies from Italy, free university admission.

Marco Polo Program: The Marco Polo Program is a Sino-Italian government study abroad program initiated in 2005 by the Italian University Presidents' Union (CRUI).
An educational program aimed at promoting Chinese students to study and further their studies in Italy. Mainly aimed at students studying undergraduate or master's degrees in Italy. Turandot Program: The Turandot Program is designed by Italy for Chinese art students (fine arts, music, etc.) who study in Italy.

意大利留学马可波罗计划和图兰朵计划是由意中基金会负责组织管理，意大利Cariplo基金会共同投资，并有来自以下机构合作：意大利外交部、意大利教育部高校及研究, 意大利驻中国使馆、中国驻意大利使馆、意大利大学校长联合会。旨在密切意大利与中国之间的学术交流，由中意两国政府共同支持，并享受意大利政府补助，免费就读大学。

马可波罗计划：马可·波罗计划是由意大利大学校长联合会（CRUI）于2005年发起的中意政府留学项目，
旨在促进中国学生到意大利学习和进修的教育计划。主要针对去意大利学习本科或硕士学位的学生。 图兰朵计划：图兰朵计划是意大利针对中国艺术生（美术类、音乐类等）去意大利留学的学生。
