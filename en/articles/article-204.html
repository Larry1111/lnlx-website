Materials required for the Korean student visa

Documents required for a South Korea student visa

1. Application for admission.
2. Financial guarantor's employment and income certificate (having a business license with an official seal is preferable, and a monthly income not less than 5,000 RMB).
3. Copy of bank deposit certificate and fixed deposit slips of at least 3 months from the four major banks: Bank of China, Bank of Communications, China Construction Bank, and China Merchants Bank.
4. The statement of purpose for studying (very important, detailed records of the reason for studying in Korea) must be signed and stamped by the applicant at the bottom right.
5. The applicant's resume (including Chinese and English name, contact number, and detailed address) must be signed and stamped by the applicant at the lower right corner.
6. Original and copy of final academic qualification, transcript certificate, and copy.
7. Double-sided copy of the applicant's ID card or passport.
8. Two color photos (4cm ×5cm).
9. Copies of the applicant's and financial guarantor's household registration booklets. If the applicant's household registration is different from the parents', a notarization of kinship relationships is required.
10. Double-sided copies of parents' ID cards (double-sided copies of the financial guarantor's ID cards).
11. Applicant's name seal.

Applicants to study in South Korea need to pay attention to the following aspects

1. Applicants must bring their household registration book, resident card, and the original documents of their respective ID cards, invitation letters, and visa issuance permits; If the applicant has a workplace, they must also provide an employment certificate with the name of the unit, address, legal representative's name, phone number, and date on the proof letter, stamped with the company seal.
2. Invitation letter, visa issuance permission, and other visa application documents are valid for 3 months; if the applicant has obtained the original of these documents, it must be submitted; if only the visa issuance number is available, it can be used for the application.
3. If some documents are not yet ready when applying for a visa, applicants may explain that they will be supplemented later; Applicants can also prepare materials as indicated at the reception window and resubmit them; No need to reschedule;
4. Supplementary materials must be submitted directly at the window. It is recommended not to send them by fax to avoid missing or losing them;
5. When submitting supplementary materials, the consulate's notification form and supplementary materials must be attached;
6. Submitted materials will not be returned.

韩国留学签证所需材料

1、入学志愿书。
2、财政担保人在职及收入证明书(有营业执照并加盖公章较好，而且月收入不得少于5000元人民币)。
3、银行存款证明书和中国银行、交通银行、建设银行、招商银行等4大银行3个月以上定期存款单复印件。
4、就学理由书(非常重要，详细记录韩国留学理由)右下方需要申请人签字并盖章。
5、申请人履历书(包括中、英文名字，联系电话，详细地址)的右下方需要申请人签字并盖章。
6、最终学历原件，成绩单证明原件及复印件。
7、申请人身份证双面复印件或护照复印件。
8、彩色照片2张(4cm×5cm)。
9、申请人及经济担保人户口本复印件，假如申请人的户口与父母的户不在一起时，需要做亲属关系公证。
10、父母的身份证双面复印件(经济担保人的身份证双面复印件)。
11、申请人的名章。

韩国留学申请人需要注意以下几个方面

1、申请人须携带户口簿、居民证及各自身份证、邀请函、签证颁发的许可书等签证申请文件的原件进行申请;如申请人有工作单位，还需提供有单位名称、地址、法人代表姓名、电话号码以及证明信日期等内容的在职证明书，并加盖公司公章。
2、邀请函、签证颁发的许可书等签证申请文件的有效期为3个月;如果申请人已拿到这类申请文件的原件就必须递交原件，如果只拿到了签证发给的编号也可以用编号进行申请;
3、申请人在申请签证时如有部分资料未来得及准备的前提下，递交时可以说明会在事后补充;申请人也可以按照接受窗口所标明的内容，准备好材料后重新递交材料;无需重新预约;
4、补充材料需要直接在窗口递交，建议不要通过传真发送，以防发生收不到或丢失的情况;
5、在递交补充材料时，需将领事馆的补充材料告知单及补充材料一并附上;
6、所递交的材料是不返还的。
