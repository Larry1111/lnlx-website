Liaoning Overseas Study Service Co., Ltd. is a limited liability company approved by the Liaoning Provincial Department of Education and initiated by the Liaoning (Department of Education) Education International Exchange Service Center. It was founded in 1996, formerly known as the Liaoning Provincial Study Abroad Service Center. The company is the first institution in Liaoning Province to obtain the Ministry of Education's self-funded overseas study agency qualification. The approval document number is (Jiaowai Zongzi Renzi [2000] No. 88), and it has passed the international ISO9001 quality management system certification.

The company adheres to the business philosophy of "integrity-based, high-quality service." We aim to build a conscientious study abroad project and strive to be an institution trusted by the people, achieving both economic and social benefits. With years of experience, numerous successful cases, and comprehensive services, we have won widespread praise from society.

Currently, the company has established long-term and stable cooperative relationships with universities and educational institutions in more than twenty countries, including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, Thailand, Japan, New Zealand, Ireland, Germany, France, Italy, Belgium, Denmark, Russia, South Korea, and Singapore. It has successfully processed admission applications and study visas for thousands of students studying abroad. The company is also responsible for cooperative education between Liaoning Province's high and secondary schools and foreign universities, successfully running cooperative education projects between Liaoning universities and universities in the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, and Thailand; and cooperative education projects between secondary vocational schools and higher vocational schools in Australia, Japan, South Korea, and other countries. It has formed a multi-level, multi-channel, and comprehensive self-funded study abroad service system.
