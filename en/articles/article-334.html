SPP Program: A New Shortcut for High School Students Studying in Canada

The SPP program has been in place for over nine months since mid-July 2010. Due to its simple procedures and lower entry barriers, it has attracted a large number of students to enroll. According to relevant data, the SPP visa pass rate reached 96% in 2010. For students with an IELTS score of no less than 5.5, the success rate of participating in this program is even higher.

Even those with low IELTS can enroll in September

"After the college entrance exam, I plan to study in Canada, but my child is busy reviewing and doesn't have time to prepare for language exams. Is it still possible to make it in time for this autumn enrollment?" When consulting with study abroad agencies, Aunt Chen's biggest worry was that if her child couldn't enroll on time, it might delay the school for a whole year. During this period in China, the focus was mostly on learning English, which wouldn't help much for studying. "If you want to go to Canada after the college entrance exam and currently don't have IELTS scores, you only need to provide twelve months of bank history, and by September this year, you can start language courses in Canada." Study abroad experts specifically pointed out that students can access this pathway to further their studies, thanks to the SPP (China-Canada Student Cooperation Program) launched by Canada in mid-July 2010 last year.

It is reported that under this program, if a student does not have an IELTS score, they must provide a twelve-month bank history certificate, commonly known as a security deposit for living expenses and tuition fees, costing 200,000 to 300,000 RMB. If a student already has an IELTS score with a score of no less than 5.5, they only need to provide one semester's tuition or a deposit of 5,000 Canadian dollars.

Even more encouraging news for students is that SPP institutions not only recruit high school graduates but also include secondary vocational students, current college/undergraduate students, vocational high school students, and college/undergraduate graduates.

SPP visa approval rate reaches 96%

In 2010, the number of Chinese students studying in Canada reached a record high. This is closely related to the simplified procedures and lowered entry thresholds for students studying in Canada after the SPP program. Although the school application threshold has decreased, another hurdle for studying abroad—the visa approval rate—has become a concern for students and parents. Official data shows that last year, the SPP student visa approval rate reached as high as 96%. "This figure is already very high in terms of visa approval rate. Of course, compared to those without an IELTS score, students who can provide an IELTS score of 5.5 have a higher visa approval rate. "Study abroad experts say that if students are about to take the college entrance exam this year but do not yet have an IELTS score, it is recommended to take the exam as soon as possible. An IELTS score of 5.5 is not difficult for ordinary high school students, but it can greatly increase the visa approval rate. Generally, student visas are processed starting in July, so it is recommended that schools usually apply one to two months earlier.

Experience shows that the visa processing time for this program has been significantly shortened. If students have completed the medical examination before submitting their application, they can receive their visa in as little as 5 days.

Students cannot apply individually

Currently, 44 member schools under ACCC (Canadian Association of Community Colleges) participate in the SPP program. Colleges participating in the SPP program generally have short durations, relatively low tuition fees, a wide range of majors, and employment-oriented courses that are practical and beneficial for employment, further studies, and immigration. After completing the college, students can either choose to attend a university to earn a bachelor's degree from a prestigious university or work first and apply for immigration after two years.

However, one condition for restricting student applications is that students cannot apply for admission under their own name; they must apply through study abroad agencies for unified applications. "This also reflects the government's strong oversight of the program. Students applying in separate groups places high standards on review, but with unified submission through agencies, there is already a screening process at the intermediary stage." Study abroad experts explained that visas in Shanghai cannot be processed separately; they are collected by agencies and sent to the visa office in Beijing for processing. It is reported that to ensure the quality of student teaching, the Canadian Embassy will track students participating in the SPP program in real time in all aspects of their studies, attendance, and living in Canada, ensuring students succeed in Canada and reassuring parents.

SPP stands for Student Cooperation Program. This is a special application channel set up at the Beijing Visa Office for students attending ACCC member institutions, aiming to ensure program integrity and manage risks while improving the approval rate. Currently, the SPP program only recognizes IELTS scores and must be in Class A (Academic IELTS for studying abroad, Gen-Eral Training for immigration or labor export. The two exams differ only in reading and writing, and the listening and speaking sections are the same, but the G category is much easier and the scoring standards differ). Language exams in other categories are not used as reference standards, such as TOEFL.

ACCC is a Canadian public consortium of institutions offering post-secondary education and vocational skills retraining, with over 100 member schools. Most offer language, transferable university credit courses, and practical technical courses, with some offering paid internship programs.

SPP计划从2010年7月中旬到现在已经实施9个月有余。由于手续简便、门槛降低，吸引了大量学生前往就读。有关数据显示，2010年SPP签证通过率达96%。对于雅思不低于5.5分的学生来说，参与这个计划的成功率更高。

低雅思也能9月入学

“高考之后有计划要去加拿大留学，可是孩子一直在忙于复习也没有时间准备语言考试，是不是还可以赶上今年秋季入学？”陈阿姨在留学中介咨询时，最担心的就是孩子如果不能如期入学，也许就会耽误一年的时间。而这段时间在国内也无非就是学学英语，对于学习来讲并不会有太多的帮助。“如果是高考后想去加拿大目前没有雅思成绩的，只需要提供十二个月银行历史证明，今年9月就可以在加拿大修读语言课程了。”留学专家特别指出，学生可以有这样的升学渠道，得益于去年加拿大从2010年7月中旬推出的SPP（中加学生合作计划）。

据悉，在该计划中，学生如果没有雅思成绩，则需要提供十二个月银行历史证明，也就是通常所说的担保金，对生活费和学费的担保，费用在20-30万人民币。如果学生已经有雅思成绩并且不低于5.5分，只需要提供一学期学费或者5000加元存款就行。

对学生来说更利好的消息是，SPP院校不只针对高中毕业生招生，还涉及中专生、大专/本科在读生、职高生、大专/本科毕业生等学生群体。

SPP签证通过率达96%

2010年，中国赴加拿大留学生达历史新高。这与实施SPP计划后，学生留学加拿大手续简化、门槛降低有很大的关系。学校申请门槛虽然降低，但是申请留学另一道关卡—签证通过率，成为了学生和家长关心的问题。官方数据显示，去年SPP学生签证通过率高达96%。“这个数字在签证通过率来讲已经是很高的了。当然相比没有雅思成绩，能够提供5.5分雅思成绩的学生签证通过率会更高。”留学专家介绍说，如果是今年即将要参加高考而目前还没有雅思成绩的学生，建议可以尽快参加一次考试，雅思5.5分的成绩对于普通的高中生来说并不是难事，但是却可以大大提高签证通过率。一般学生的签证在7月份开始办理，建议学校申请的时间一般往前推1-2个月就可以。

从经验来看，该计划签证周期明显缩短。学生如果递交申请之前已完成体检，最快5天就可以拿到签证。

学生不能个人申请

目前共有44所ACCC（加拿大社区学院协会）旗下的会员学校参加SPP计划。参与SPP计划的学院一般学制短，学费相对便宜，而且专业众多，并以就业为导向课程比较实用，有利于就业、升学及移民。学生在读完学院后，既可以选择入读大学拿名校本科学士学位，也可以选择先工作，两年后就可申请移民。

不过对于学生的申请进行限制的一个条件是，学生不能以个人名义来申请进入该计划，必须通过留学中介统一申请。“这也体现了政府对于该计划的一个监控力度。学生分散地申请，对于审核提出了很高的要求，而通过中介统一递送，那么在中介这个环节就已经有一个筛选了。”留学专家介绍说，而且签证在上海是不能单独做的，会由中介收集之后全部送到北京的签证办公室来做。据悉，为了保证学生的教学质量，加拿大使馆将对参与SPP计划的学生在加的学习、出勤、生活等各方面进行实时跟踪，保障学生在加学有所成，同时让家长放心。

SPP 即为学生合作计划。这是为前往加拿大社区学院协会（ACCC）成员院校的学生，在北京签证办公室设立的特别申请渠道，旨在保证计划完整性管控风险的同时提高申请通过率。目前SPP计划只认可雅思成绩，且必须为A类（A类Academic雅思用于留学，G类Gen-eral Training雅思用于移民或劳动力输出，两种考试除了阅读和写作不一样，听力口语题一样，但是G类要比A类简单许多，评分标准也不一样）。其他类别的语言考试不作为参考标准，如托福。

ACCC 加拿大公立、提供中学后教育及职业技能再培训的院校联盟，有100多所会员学校，大多提供语言、可转大学学分课程以及实用性较强的技术课程，部分提供带薪实习项目。
