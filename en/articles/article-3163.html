Address: Beibu Gulf, Ontario

Basic Introduction:

It shares a 700-acre campus with Nipisin University. The college offers 55 diploma and certificate programs and has linkage agreements with Nipisin and several other universities to enable students to further their studies and further their studies. Canado College has 3,500 students and 200 international students. Canado College offers globally renowned aviation programs: helicopter flight training, avionics, aircraft structural repair, and aircraft maintenance. Our Digital Film program teaches students how to use the digital equipment and special effects software employed by today's mainstream film studios.

Major or Department:

1. Digital Film Major (Beibu Gulf Campus)

We believe the Chinese film industry is experiencing explosive growth, and Canado Academy is seeking a partner institution to launch joint education programs (1+2) or (2+1) to provide learning opportunities for students aspiring to work in this exciting field.

2. Early Childhood Education (Beibu Gulf and Toronto Campuses)

Canada has a strong demand for early childhood teachers, and there is an increasing need for teachers who are bilingual in both English and Mandarin. The rigorous training offered by Chinese educational institutions is second to none, and Canado Academy hopes to establish collaborative programs where international students can complete their studies at the Beibu Gulf or Toronto campuses.

3. Business and Commercial Accounting (Beibu Gulf and Toronto Campuses)

The Business and Business Accounting diploma program is an excellent foundational course for students who wish to find employment immediately after graduation or to pursue further studies at university. Students will be able to improve their business and communication skills, obtain diplomas issued by Canado College, and lay a solid foundation for further studies at universities that partner with our school. Canado College hopes to find high-quality Chinese institutions to collaborate on joint education programs, providing more attractive pathway course options for students wishing to study abroad.

地址：安大略省北部湾

基本介绍：

与尼皮辛大学共享700英亩的校园。学院提供55项文凭和证书课程，并与尼皮辛和其他几所大学拥有衔接协议，以便学生进一步学习和深造专业。卡纳多学院有3500名学生和200名国际学生。卡纳多学院拥有享誉全球的航空项目：直升机飞行培训、航空电子设备、飞机结构修理和飞机维修。我们的数字电影专业教导学生如何使用当今主流电影制片厂使用的数字化设备和特效软件。

专业或院系：

1. 数字电影专业（北部湾校区）

我们认为中国电影行业有井喷式增长，卡纳多学院寻求一家合作院校开展联合办学项目（1+2）或（2+1）为有志于在这个激动人心的领域工作的学生提供学习机会。

2. 幼儿教育（北部湾和多伦多校区）

加拿大对幼教教师有强劲的需求，并且越来越多需要掌握英语和普通话双语的幼教教师。中国教育机构提供的严格训练是首屈一指的，卡纳多学院希望能够建立合作项目，国际学生能在北部湾或多伦多校区完成学业。

3. 商业及商业会计（北部湾和多伦多校区）

商业和商业会计文凭课程对希望毕业后马上就业的学生或继续大学深造的学生都是非常优秀的基础课程。学生将得以提高他们的商务和沟通能力，获取卡纳多学院颁发的文凭，并为前往与我校有合作关系的大学进一步深造学位打下了坚实基础。卡纳多学院希望寻找优质的中国院校合作开设联合办学项目，为希望出国留学的学生提供更具吸引力的衔接课程选择。
