The University of Tours is a public comprehensive university founded in 1841. Initially, it was just a medical school, but later established the Faculty of Law, Faculty of Letters, and Polytechnic Institute, becoming a comprehensive university. The University of Tours has more than 50 departments, a full range of majors, and offers both traditional academic education and vocational skills training. The University of Tours currently has about 24,000 students and nearly 1,200 faculty and staff. Tours is located on the banks of the Loire River, 220 kilometers from Paris, and only a 70-minute high-speed train ride. Tours has a population of only 130,000, but it is a major economic center in the Loire Valley. Tours boasts picturesque scenery and distinctive architecture. With the expansion of Paris's industry, Tours now owns various industries such as machinery, chemicals, and pharmaceuticals, and places great emphasis on developing the tertiary sector, especially tourism and conferences. Tours has nurtured many internationally renowned scholars and literati, such as Balzac, Rabelais, and the mathematician and philosopher Descartes. The master artist Leonardo da Vinci also spent his later years here.

Legal Management: Law, Private Law, Public Law, Commercial Law, Commercial Law and International Law, European Law, Public Administration, Social and Economic Administration, Economic Science, Economics and Management, Management Science

Science and Technology: Earth and Space Sciences, Mathematics, Mathematical Engineering, Physics, Chemistry

Biological Sciences: Life Sciences, Cell Biology and Physiology, Tissue Biology, Population Biology and Ecological Environment, Environmental Science, Health and Social Sciences

Stage Three Majors:

DEA: Biological and medical imaging and signaling, computers, mathematical analysis and applications, music and musicology, urban and land, cell and molecular infectious diseases and vaccines
