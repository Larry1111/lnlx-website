Pavia University is located in Pavia, the wealthiest Lombardy region in Italy. Pavia is situated by the Ticino River, offering beautiful natural scenery and a high quality of life. It is only 30 kilometers from the regional capital, Milan, and just a half-hour drive.

Pavia is a historic city. During the Roman era, it served as the capital of the Lombard "Kingdom of Italy." The city's architectural styles are rich and diverse, ranging from Roman to Renaissance styles, encapsulating the unique features of ancient European architecture.

Pavia also boasts the charm of a modern city. Located in Italy's most developed industrial delta, it boasts a thriving economy, well-developed infrastructure, convenient transportation, and is very easy for students to travel and shop, making it a great place to live.

Pavia is a famous university town. Besides Pavia University, renowned institutions such as the Pavia Conservatory are also located here. The presence of top-tier institutions brings a rich cultural atmosphere to the city. University students, especially international students, have become an important part of city life.

School Introduction:

The University of Pavia is one of the oldest universities in the world, originally founded by Roman Emperor Lotario the Great in 825 AD as the Pavia College. In 1361, Roman Emperor Charles IV decreed that the academy be converted into a university. As the alma mater of famous physicist Volt, inventor of the battery, and Nobel Prize in Medicine winner Camiro Gorky, the University of Pavia has nurtured generations of renowned scholars since its founding. In the 2009–2010 comprehensive university rankings published by Italy's La Repubblica, Pavia University ranked first and is known as Italy's "Oxford University."

Today, the university has 3 campuses, 9 colleges, 104 majors, with a total enrollment of over 25,000 students and a faculty-to-student ratio of 1:19. Pavia University is a research university that has established 337 exchange programs with globally renowned universities such as Cambridge, Oxford, University of Barcelona, and University of Edinburgh in terms of international academic exchange, and has signed talent placement agreements with many multinational companies, providing 3,300 valuable internship opportunities annually at internationally renowned firms.

The university is well-equipped and has an independent sports center where students can freely study and train, especially in water sports such as kayaking and rowing. Many world champions choose to train at Pavia University's sports academy. Leveraging the advantage of Pavia's Ticino River, Pavia University hosts annual canoeing competitions at the University of Pavia Pisa and Pavia Oxford University. In addition, the sports center houses modern sports clubs such as fencing, rowing, rugby, and gliding, providing students with broad opportunities for activities and the development of individual talents.

帕维亚大学座落在意大利最富饶的伦巴第大区帕维亚市。帕维亚依傍提契诺河，自然风景优美，生活品质高。其距离大区首府米兰市仅30公里，驱车前往只需半小时。

帕维亚是座历史名城，在古罗马时期，作为伦巴第人“意大利王国”的首都，城内建筑形式丰富多样，从罗马风格到文艺复兴风格一应俱全，浓缩了整个欧洲古建筑的特色。

帕维亚同样拥有着现代城市的魅力，由于位于意大利最发达的工业三角洲地带，其经济发达，基础设施完善，交通便利，学生的出行，购物都非常方便，适宜居住。

帕维亚是有名的大学城，除了帕维亚大学外，帕维亚音乐学院等著名学府也坐落在这座城市。一流学府的存在给这座城市带来了浓郁的文化气息。大学生特别是国际学生已经成为城市生活的重要成员。

学校简介：

帕维亚大学是全球最古老的大学之一，前身为公元825年罗马皇帝Lotario大帝建立的帕维亚学院。1361年罗马皇帝卡洛四世下旨将学院改成大学。作为著名的物理学家电池的发明者伏特，和诺贝尔医学奖的获得者卡米罗高尔基等的母校，帕维亚大学建校至今，培养了一批又一批的著名学者。2009至2010年度，在意大利共和报公布综合类大学排名中，帕维亚大学名列第一，被誉为意大利的“牛津大学”。

如今，大学共有3个校区，9个学院，104个专业，注册学生总数超过25,000人，教师与学生比例为1:19。帕维亚大学是一所研究性大学，在国际学术交流方面，与全球知名大学，如剑桥大学，牛津大学，巴塞罗那大学，爱丁堡大学等建立了337个交流项目，并与许多跨国企业签订人才输送协议，每年提供3300个进入国际知名企业实习的宝贵机会。

大学内设施齐全，下设独立的体育中心，学生们可以自由的学习和训练，特别是皮划艇和赛艇等水上项目，许多世界冠军都选择到帕维亚大学的体育学院进行训练。借助帕维亚Ticino河的优势，帕维亚大学每年都会举办帕维亚-比萨大学和帕维亚-牛津大学皮划艇比赛。除此之外，体育中心内设击剑，划船，英式橄榄球，及滑翔艇等各项现代体育运动俱乐部，为学生提供了广阔的活动天地，发展个性特长。
