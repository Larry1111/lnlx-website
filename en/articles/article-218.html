Korea University

1. Overview of the school

Korea University was founded in 1905 and has embraced the lofty ideal of "saving the nation through education" from the very beginning. Over the past century, it has nurtured countless outstanding talents in Korean history. Korea University is not only a prestigious institution in academic research and education but also a tireless effort for the nation and people, striving to be the leader of Korean universities. After more than a century of history, Korea University has become a world-recognized prestigious institution.

3. Admission Qualifications

1. High school diploma or above.

2. Age 18-25 (for bachelor's degree holders, the age limit can be extended to 25).

3. Mainly Han Chinese students.

4. Have a guarantee of payment for study expenses.

5. Both parents and the individual hold Chinese nationality, and have no record of being refused a Korean visa or engaging in illegal activities.

高丽大学

一、学校概况

高丽大学始建于1905年，从当初就怀抱“教育救国”的崇高理想。它在走过的一世纪里，培育了韩国历史上无数的优秀人才，高丽大学不仅仅是学术研究和教育的名门，他还为国家和民族而坚持不懈地奋斗，争做韩国大学的领头羊。经过百余年历史的洗礼，高丽大学已经成为世界承认的名门学府。

三、入学资格

1、高中以上学历。

2、年龄18-25周岁（本科以上学历可放宽至25周岁）。

3、汉族学生为主。

4、具有支付学习费用的保证。

5、父母及本人都持有中国国籍、并无韩国被拒签或非法行为纪录。
