In 1845, Queen Victoria of the United Kingdom founded Queen's University. Queen's University Belfast is one of the largest universities in Northern Ireland, with a high reputation in many disciplines, including many traditional vocational programs such as medicine, pharmacy, and architecture. Queen's University Belfast has affiliated teaching colleges, Stranmillis and St. Mary's. Because the college is dedicated to research, it is an outstanding and research-intensive university.
Queen's University Belfast is located in the southern part of Belfast, the capital of Northern Ireland, UK. Air transport is especially convenient, with 35 daily flights to and from London, taking only one hour to fly. The city's architecture is antique and the scenery is pleasant. The local atmosphere is influenced by local schools and students. The school is close to the Ulster Museum and the City Art Gallery, enriching students' extracurricular life.

The college has approximately 24,560 students and 1,600 staff members, with international students accounting for 6%. Queen's University has 20 faculties: Faculty of Biological Sciences, Faculty of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Faculty of Education, Faculty of Electronics, Electronic Engineering and Computer Sciences, Faculty of English, Faculty of Geography, Archaeology and Paleoecology, Faculty of History and Anthropology, Faculty of Languages, Literature and Performing Arts, Faculty of Law, Faculty of Management at Queen's University, Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Faculty of Medicine, Dentistry and Biomedical Sciences, School of Music and Sonic Art, School of Nursing and Midwifery, Pharmacy, School of Planning, Architecture and Civil Engineering, School of Politics, International Issues Research and Philosophy, School of Psychology, and School of Social Policy and Society.
Queen's University Belfast offers two main types of courses: lectured courses and research-based courses.

Notable majors: dentistry, pharmacy, civil engineering, chemistry, environmental science, electronic engineering, food science, business research, music, and more

Admission requirements for undergraduate foundation courses: Must be at least 17 years old, IELTS 5.0, currently in senior high school or having graduated from high school.

Language course start dates: Four times a year, in January, April, July, and September.

1845年，英国维多利亚女王创办了女王大学。贝尔法斯特女王大学是北爱尔兰最大的大学之一，在许多学科上有着很高的声誉，包括许多传统的职业课程，包括：医学、药学和建筑学。贝尔法斯特女王大学有着附属的教学院校，Stranmillis和St.Mary’s。因为学院致力于研究，是一所卓越的、研究密集型大学。
贝尔法斯特女王大学坐落于英国北爱尔兰首府贝尔法斯特市的南部，空中交通特别方便，每天有35班航班来往于伦敦空中，飞行只需1小时时间。城市的建筑古色古香，风景环境宜人。当地的气氛环境受当地的学校和学生影响着。学校靠近Ulster博物馆和城市艺术馆，可以丰富学生的课余生活。

学院拥有大约24560名学生和1600名教职员工，国际学生比例占6%。女王大学有20个学院，分别为：生物科学院，化学和化学工程院，教育院，电子、电子工程和计算机科学院，英语学院，地理、考古和古生态学院，历史和人类学院，语言、文学和表演艺术学院，法学院，女王大学管理学院，数学和物理学院，机械航空工程学院，医学、牙医和生物医学科学院，音乐和索尼克艺术学院，护士和助产士学院，药剂学，规划、建筑和土木工程学院，政治、国际问题研究和哲学院，心理学院，社会政策和社会学院。
贝尔法斯特女王大学的课程主要有两个形式：讲授式课程和研究型课程。

知名专业：牙科、药剂学、土木工程、化学、环境科学、电子工程、食品科学、商业研究、音乐等

本科预科课程入学要求：年满17周岁，雅思5.0分，高三在读或高中毕业。

语言课程开课日期：每年1、4、7、9月四次开学。
