Luton University in the UK and the Bedford campus of De Montfort University merged in 2006 to form a new university. As one of the new universities in the UK, the University of Bedford has established a strong reputation and attracts an increasing number of international students to study here. The university supports the integration of personal, professional, and career development, continuously providing students with higher levels of service to ensure excellent teaching standards.

Luton is located about 45 kilometers north of London, with convenient public transport and multiple trains and buses running through it every hour.
The main campus of the University of Bedford is located in the center of Luton, surrounded by many large shopping centers, restaurants, train stations, and bus stations. Combined with an extensive transportation network, student life is very convenient.
The University of Bedford has spent £45 million to expand its two campuses, aiming to provide students with the best environment and learning facilities.

The University of Bedford has nearly 16,000 students, including almost 3,000 international students from around the world.
The University of Bedfordshire is currently the largest institution offering undergraduate and postgraduate education in Bedfordshire, and also provides more students with opportunities for higher education. The school's curriculum is broad and professional, closely integrated with employers and UK companies, providing students with extensive industry training and certification courses.
The main faculties and majors of the university are:
Business School: Mall Advertising and Public Relations, Business and Finance, Leisure and Tourism, Sports and Management, Language Communication, Law.
School of Creative Arts and Technology: Computer and Information Systems, Arts and Design, Biology and Medical Sciences, Media and Performing Arts.
School of Health and Social Sciences: Health Care, Psychology, Social Sciences, and Social Services.
Faculty of Education and Sports Science: Sports and Sports Sciences, Education.

Undergraduate admission requirements: high school diploma or above, IELTS score of 6.0 or above.

Graduate admission requirements: IELTS score of 6.0 or above, bachelor's degree.

Language course start dates: Classes start in February, April, June, and September each year.

英国鲁顿大学与德蒙福特大学贝德福德校区，在2006年合并成为一所新的大学。作为英国新大学之一，英国贝德福德大学已经树立了良好的信誉，吸引着越来越多的国际学生来此求学。学校支持个人、专业和职业生涯相结合的发展，为学生不断提供着更高水平的服务，以此来确保优秀的教学标准。

Luton位于伦敦北部约45公里处，公共交通方便，每小时有多班火车和巴士穿梭其中。
英国贝德福德大学主校区位于Luton市中心，周围有很多大型购物中心、餐馆、火车站及汽车站，加上四通八达的交通网络，使学生生活非常方便。
贝德福德大学花费了4500万英镑对两个校园进行扩建，志在为学生提供最好的环境和学习设施。

贝德福德大学拥有近16,000名学生，其中有近3,000名国际学生，来自世界各地。
英国贝德福德大学是当前贝德福德郡最大的提供本科及研究生教育的院校，同时也为更多的学生提供了接受高等教育的机会。学校的课程设置广泛和专业，与企业雇主和英国公司紧密结合，为学生提供广泛的行业培训和认证课程。
学校的主要学院和专业有：
商学院：商场广告和公共关系、商务和金融、休闲与旅游、运动与管理、语言交流、法律。
创造艺术与科技学院：计算机与信息系统、艺术与设计、生物学与医药科学、传媒与表演艺术。
健康与社会科学学院：健康护理、心理学、社会科学和社会服务。
教育与体育科学学院：运动和体育科学、教育。

本科入学要求：高中毕业及以上，雅思6.0分及以上。

研究生入学要求：雅思6.0分及以上，大学本科毕业，学士学位。

语言课程开学日期：每年2、4、6、9月开课。

www.beds.ac.uk
