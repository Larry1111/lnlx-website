Singapore offers a diverse array of world-class education, and the cost of studying abroad in Singapore is even more reasonable. Although some third-country branch campuses in Singapore have tuition fees comparable to those of the main campus, studying abroad in Singapore is relatively affordable.

Singapore's primary education standards are renowned worldwide, with students often leading in international science competitions. Singapore's primary schools reject exam-oriented teaching and focus on tapping students' potential. The six-year primary education system requires students to take the primary school departure exam, and students are assigned to different secondary school courses based on their school progress and abilities.

At the secondary school level, emphasis should be placed on developing analytical and judgment skills. Singapore's education system is an excellent choice. Secondary education is a four-year program, and after completing the secondary course, students take the Cambridge General Standards Examination in Singapore and pursue further studies at Singapore junior colleges and polytechnics.

Secondary school tuition is 225 SGD/month

Primary school tuition is 181 SGD/month

International High School IB Curriculum:

The IB diploma is recognized by nearly 100 countries and over a thousand universities, including Oxford, Stanford, MIT, and others, and scholarships are also offered

Tuition: About 29,000 SGD per year, including accommodation and tuition.

Tuition for studying in Singapore (self-paid): 12,000~13,000 SGD/year

Allowance: 80% scholarship

Actual tuition fees for studying in Singapore: about 2260~2310 SGD per year

Students receiving the allowance must sign a contract and agree to work for a Singaporean company for three years after graduation.

Singapore's public universities enjoy a prestigious international reputation, ranking among the top in Asia and joining the ranks of globally renowned universities. The national university and Nanyang Technological University are ranked 30th and 77th globally, respectively, with programs spanning multiple majors and fields. The tuition subsidy programs established by public universities provide students with 70% to 80% of the tuition subsidies.

新加坡提供多样化的世界级教育，新加坡国外留学费用更是合理。尽管一些第三国设立在新加坡的分校学费和主校不相上下，新加坡国外留学费用相对较为低廉。

新加坡小学教育水准在全球享有盛誉，学生在国际科学竞赛中经常独占鳌头。新加坡小学拒绝应试教学，注重挖掘学生潜力，小学6年制教育，6年级必须参加小学离校考试，根据他们的学校进度和能力，分配到不同的中学课程就读。

在中学阶段，需重点培养思维分析和判断能力。新加坡教育体系正是上佳选择。中学施行4年制教育，中学课程结束后参加新加坡剑桥普通水准会考，进入新加坡初级学院、理工学院等深造。

中学学费225新币/月

小学学费181新币/月

国际高中IB课程：

IB文凭被近百国家一千多所大学承认，包括：牛津、斯坦福、麻省理工等，而且提供奖学金

学费：约29000新币/年，其中包括住宿费、学费。

津贴：80%助学金

新加坡留学实际学费：约2260~2310新币/年

接受津贴的学生则须签署契约，并同意在毕业之后就职于新加坡公司企业三年。

新加坡公立大学在国际上享负盛名，不仅在亚洲排名前列，更跻身进入国际知名大学行列，国立大学和南洋理工大学全球排名分别为第30位和第77位，课程横跨多个专业和领域，公立大学设立的学费津贴计划，为学生提供70%至80%的学费津贴。
