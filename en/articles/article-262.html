English name: University of San Francisco

School website: http://www.usfca.edu/

Main Campus: California

Entry requirements: Undergraduate IBT 79 or IELTS 6.0; Master's IBT 79-100 or IELTS 6.0-7.0, GRE or GMAT;

Dual Undergraduate Admission Requirements: IELTS 5.0 or IBT 48;

Tuition: ESL: $2,475 (summer session 6 weeks), $4,950 (spring/fall semester 15 weeks);

Undergraduate $34,430/academic year; Master's $965–$1,075/credit.

The University of San Francisco (USF) was founded in 1855 and is an outstanding comprehensive university with a century-old reputation. It was the first formal higher education institution in the western United States, and five years later, the University of California, Berkeley was founded. When it was thirty, the elderly man named Stanford began to establish the renowned Stanford University. In 1930, it was officially renamed the current "University of San Francisco." Currently, more than 8,000 students from 57 countries study here. In the 2007 ranking of universities by the authoritative American magazine U.S. News & Report, the University of San Francisco was ranked 112th among more than 3,600 universities nationwide. Its business school was ranked one of the world's top 100 business schools by The Wall Street Journal in 2005. The University of San Francisco is a representative career development university in the United States. Its undergraduate education is renowned for its entrepreneurial spirit and is known as the cradle of Chinese chairmen.

California, home to the University of San Francisco, has a developed economy. San Francisco is located on the west coast of the United States, making it the financial center of the western United States. It has 40 banks and 147 branches, is home to the Pacific Coast Stock Exchange and the headquarters of the largest bank in the United States, Bank of Americas. At the same time, the city is also known worldwide as Silicon Valley, the IT industry hub. It is also known as a "vacation paradise" and is one of the most beautiful cities in the United States. The city also has an education center. Stanford University and UC Berkeley are both in the Bay Area (the Bay Area formed by the various cities surrounding San Francisco Bay and San Francisco, known as the Foggy City). The climate here is quite pleasant. It has a typical maritime climate, with an average temperature of 10.5°C in January and 15°C in July, meaning winters are mild and summers are mild.

The University of San Francisco has an outstanding teaching team with genuine and practical knowledge, 93% of whom hold doctoral degrees. They teach attentively and enthusiastically. Small class sizes: The teacher-to-student ratio is 1:16, with most classes having no more than 20 students. Small classes allow professors to provide one-on-one guidance with students, and most of the professors' time and energy are devoted to teaching students rather than writing academic papers individually, resulting in higher teaching quality. The University of San Francisco also emphasizes the integration of theory and practice: it regularly invites local business administrators and entrepreneurs to give lectures on campus; and annually, it organizes many international conferences and seminars, inviting leaders from around the world to interact and exchange ideas with students, and has invited several Nobel laureates to give talks at the university. Companies near the University of San Francisco, such as the San Francisco Stock Exchange, The Chronicle, Oracle Inc., biotech companies, or the California Environmental Protection Agency, all offer opportunities to take your first step in your career; The Bay Area is home to the headquarters of six of the Fortune Global 500, with highly developed information and biochemical technology, renowned museums, top architecture, design, journalism, computer science, communications, finance, fine arts, film, and more. You will find opportunities for internships, service internships, or volunteering. Since its founding, the school has aimed to cultivate students' dedication to their studies and a spirit of service to society. The school was established Course and academic research programs provide various opportunities for teachers, students, and researchers to enhance their academic literacy and professional level through their studies and research at the university, becoming successful talents needed in the 21st century.

旧金山大学 ( 简称 USF) 成立于 1855 年 , 是一所具有百年声誉的优秀综合性大学，是美国西部第一个正式的高等教育机构，5年后，加州伯克利大学才出生。等它三十而立的时候，那个叫斯坦福的老人才开始建立了著名的斯坦福大学。1930年，它正式更名为今天的“旧金山大学”(University of San Francisco)。目前共有来自57个国家的8000多名学生在此学习。在2007年美国权威杂志《美国新闻与报道》评选出的大学排行榜中，旧金山大学在全美3600多所大学中位列第112位。它的商学院在2005年被华尔街日报评为全球最好的100家商学院之一。旧金山大学是美国职业发展型大学的代表，它的本科教育以创业精神著称防治于世，被誉为华人董事长的摇篮。

旧金山大学所在的加州经济发达，旧金山大学所在的美国西海岸旧金山市，是美国的西部金融中心，有40家银行及其147家分行，是太平洋岸证券交易所和美国最大的银行美洲银行总部所在地，同时这个城市也是全球著名的IT行业中心硅谷。也是堪称“度假天堂”的城市，是美国最美的城市之一。该城市还有一个教育中心，斯坦福大、伯克利(UC Berkeley)都在湾区(围绕着旧金山湾的大大小小城市与有雾都之称的旧金山合成所谓的湾区)。这里气候不错。是典型的海洋气候，一月平均气温：10.5度，七月平均温度15度，也就是说冬天不冷，夏天不热。

旧金山大学拥有杰出的真材实学的教学团队，他们中93%具有博士学位，他们悉心教导，热心敬业。旧金山大学小班授课：学校教师与学生的比例为1：16，大多数班不超过20人，小班上课使教授与学生进行1对1的指导，而且教授的大部分时间和精力均用于学生教学，而非个人撰写学术文章，更具高教学质量。旧金山大学还注意理论与实际结合：旧金山大学将定期邀请市内商业行政人员、企业家到校讲学；而在每年将组织很多国际会议及研讨班，邀请世界各地领导人与学生接触和交流，曾邀请过多名诺贝尔奖获得者到校演讲。旧金山大学附近的公司，例如旧金山证券交易所、纪事报、甲骨文公司、生物科技公司或者加州环保局等，你都有机会在此迈出职业生涯的第一步；湾区是财富世界500强中6强的总部所在地，高度发展的信息和生化科技，著名的博物馆，顶尖的建筑、设计，新闻业，计算机科学，通讯，金融业，美术，电影等等，你都有找到实习或服务实习或当志愿者的机会。学校自建校以来，以培养学生刻苦修习学业，服务社会的精神为目的。学校设立的课程和学术研究项目给教师、学生及研究人员各种机会，使他们通过在校的学习、研究工作提高自身的学术素养及专业水平，成为 21 世纪所需要的成功人才。
