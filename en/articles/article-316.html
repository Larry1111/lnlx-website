School Overview:

Savoie University is located in the city of Chambéré, in southeastern France. Chambéré is the capital city of the Savoie region, bordering Italy and Switzerland to the east, and is one of France's most famous tourist cities. It is a famous ski resort—a must-pass in the Alps. It takes only 3 hours by high-speed train from Paris.

The capital, Chambé-Rés, has a permanent population of 56,000, and the renowned University of Savoie is located here. It is a city that combines modernity with a long history, and also serves as a transit hub for Lyon to Geneva and Italy. The school is just a 15-minute walk from the city center, and it has three campuses: Chambéré, Anas, and Lake Béqui.

The University of Savoie is a national comprehensive university founded in 1945. After more than 50 years of development, it has become a comprehensive university with seven faculties, 37 national-level research institutes, and more than 40 master's and doctoral degree programs. As of May 2001, there were a total of 12,133 students enrolled, including 818 foreign students from 87 different countries. 58% of enrolled students study in the first stage of university (associate degree), 34% in the second stage (undergraduate and master's), and 6% in the third stage (doctoral). The university has 554 professors, including 385 researcher-level teachers. The campus covers an area of 85,703 square meters.

The International Language Center of the University of Savoie is affiliated with the University of Savoie and is the only recognized school in the Savoie region specializing in teaching French to foreign students. It is a higher education institution that provides French language training for foreign students about to enter French universities for their majors and future professors to teach French. It is also a member of the French Language Center and the European French Language Association. The International Language Center has its own library, multimedia classrooms, stadium, cafeteria, and advanced language equipment. There, you can use these devices free of charge just like French students and freely browse the INTERNET. There is also a Language Exchange Association for International Students, which organizes various cultural exchange activities to help students improve their French skills quickly.

Departmental Structure:

● College of Science and Technology: Department of Materials Science, Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, Department of Computer Science and Social Science Applications, Department of Engineering Technology, Department of Earth and Astronomical Sciences, Department of Life Sciences

● School of Foreign Languages and Literature: Department of English, Department of German, Department of Spanish, Department of Italian, Department of Applied Foreign Languages, Department of Modern Literature

● Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences: Departments of History, Geography, Philosophy, Psychology, and Sociology

● School of Economics, Law and Management: Department of Economics and Social Administration, Department of Law, Department of Economics and Management

● Vocational and Technical College: Department of Industrial Systems Engineering, Department of Alpine Equipment Management, Equipment and Protection

● European University College for the Deaf: Special Education

● French Training Institute: This is France's national French DELF and DALF exam center, and can award the University of Savoie French Diploma (DU) and the Paris Chamber of Commerce French Diploma (CCIP). Chinese students applying to this school will first study French at the center for one year. After passing the exam, they can enter a major or enroll in other institutions according to their personal preferences. On the first day of school, foreign students take oral and written exams, then are assigned to classes based on their proficiency, with a maximum of 15 students per class.

学校概况：

萨瓦大学座落在香柏雷市，位于法国的东南部。香柏雷是萨瓦大区的省会城市，东面紧邻意大利和瑞士，是法国最有名的旅游城市之一。著名的滑雪胜地——阿尔卑斯山脉的必经之地。从巴黎乘坐高速火车只需３小时。

首府香柏雷有５６０００常住人口，著名的萨瓦大学就坐落于此。它是一座结合现代化和悠久历史的城市，同时，也是里昂到日内瓦到意大利的中转站。从市中心只需步行１５分钟即到学校，它分有三个校区：香柏雷、阿纳斯和伯基湖。

萨瓦大学是一所国立综合性大学，成立于1945年，经过50多年的发展，已经形成一所拥有七所学院、37个国家级研究所、40多个硕士学位授予点和博士授予点的综合性大学。截止到2001年5月，在校生共计12133名，其中有来自87个不同国家的818名外国学生。58%在校生在大学第一阶段（大专）学习，34%在校生在大学第二阶段（本科、硕士）学习，6%在校生在大学第三阶段（博士）学习。学校有554名教授，其中385名研究员级教师。校园面积85703平米。

萨瓦大学国际语言中心隶属于萨瓦大学，是萨瓦地区唯一一所被认可的专门教授外国学生法语的学校。她为即将进入法国大学攻读专业的外国学生和将来从事法语教学的外国教授而进行法语培训的高等教育机构，同时它也是法国大学法语中心和欧洲法语协会的会员。国际语言中心拥有自己的图书馆、多媒体教室、体育场、食堂和先进的语音设备，在那，你可以和法国学生一样，无偿地使用这些设备，并可以自由浏览INTERNET网。同时还有外国学生语言交流协会，组织学生进行各种文化交流活动，以帮助学生尽快地提高法语水平。
