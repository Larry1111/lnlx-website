Three major considerations for studying in France

France attracts us with perfume, fine wine, and fashion. Its excellent education system and leading technology and humanities make us yearn for it. Coupled with its low study abroad costs, free degree programs, high visa rates, and a wide variety of majors, more and more students are falling in love with France, and France is becoming a new choice for Chinese students studying abroad. However, for Chinese students, studying in France still requires the following three major considerations:
Language proficiency issues
When it comes to studying in France, language is a very important factor. Every year, 80% of Chinese students who study in France are held back due to language issues, and 60% can't even get into university. Therefore, Chinese students should build a solid foundation in French before studying in France, as this can greatly shorten the adaptation period. Domestically, there are now many ways to learn French, and the tuition is relatively low. Of course, students who can afford can also choose French training schools with good teaching quality to learn French.
Major selection issues
Currently, Chinese students studying in France are too concentrated in their majors. Statistics show that in 2003, 49% of Chinese students studying in France majored in business; In 2004, 43% majored in economics. This situation is unfavorable for Chinese students returning to China for future development. It is recommended to choose a major based on your own situation and career goals, rather than blindly following trends.
Issues with the education system
The French education system is very different from China's, adopting a management model of 'lenient entry, strict exit.' It's easy to get started but more difficult to learn, with many universities having a first-year dropout rate as high as 50%. Therefore, studying in France is not easy. Chinese students studying in France need to improve their own conditions, which requires sufficient understanding and thorough preparation.

法国吸引我们的有香水、美酒、时装，法国良好的教育制度和领先的科技、人文让我们向往，加上留学费用低廉、免费学位教育、留学签证率高、专业种类齐全的优势，使得越来越多的学生爱上了法国，法国也正成为了中国学生留学的新选择。然而对中国学生来说，去法国留学仍需要以下三大注意：
语言水平问题
就法国留学条件来说，语言是很重要的一关。每年到法国留学的中国学生中，因为语言问题导致留级的占了80%， 60%的学生甚至连大学校门都进不去。因此，中国学生应先打好法语基础后再去法国留学，这样能大大缩短留学适应期。就国内而言，现在就有不少学习法语的途径，学习费用也比较低。当然，有条件的学生也可在法国选择教学质量比较好的法语培训学校来学习法语。
专业选择问题
目前，去留学法国的中国学生攻读专业过于集中。统计数据显示，2003年，赴留学法国的中国学生中，有49%的人攻读商科专业；2004年，43%的中国学生攻读经济专业。这种现状对中国学生今后回国发展不利，建议根据自己的实际情况和职业发展目标选择专业，而不是盲目跟风。
教育体制问题
法国的教育体制和中国的有很大不同，采用宽进严出的管理模式，入门容易，但学习难度较高，很多大学第一年的学生淘汰率高达50%。因此，法国的书并不好念，在留学法国中国学生需要提升自己的自身条件，对此必须要有足够的认识和充分的准备。
