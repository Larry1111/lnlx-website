How long does it take to apply for a student visa (general visa)?

Currently, it takes about 12 weeks from receiving the application at the Adelaide Overseas Student Assessment Centre to making a decision on the application result. Within 8 weeks of receiving the application, we conduct a preliminary review, though this period is not fixed. After passing the preliminary review, we will notify the student to submit all required materials in the notice, including the school admission confirmation for each course and the passport. Once all these materials are submitted, we may make a decision on your application promptly. Generally, visas are not issued before 16 weeks before the start of school. Currently, the review period is 12 weeks. Applicants should consider the time required for visa review and allow sufficient time after visa approval to handle personal affairs in the country and enrollment procedures in Australia. Applicants also take this time into account when considering the time required to process the visa application.

目前，从阿德莱得海外学生评审中心收到申请到对申请结果作出决定证大约需要12周的时间。在收到申请8周之内我们进行预评审, 这个周期不定。通过申请预评审后，我们会通知学生提交通知中要求的一切材料,包括每门功课的学校录取确认书及护照。所有这些材料递交后我们可能会很快对你的申请作出决定。一般而言签证不会早于开学前16周签发。 目前的评审时间是12周，申请人应考虑到签证的评审时间，在签证批准后留有充足的时间办理个人在国内的事务及在澳大利亚的入学手续。申请人在考虑办理签证申请所需时间的同时也将此时间考虑在内.
