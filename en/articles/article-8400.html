Major universities in Russia have started their semester classes. For students going to Russia for the first time, it can be overwhelming to prepare what to pack for studying abroad. To ensure everyone can travel abroad smoothly, here is a luggage list for studying in Russia. Students in need can refer to it.

File class

(Carry it with you, do not put it in checked luggage)

1. Passport including visa and flight tickets

2. School admission notice. Copy of the Ministry of Internal Affairs endorsement letter, original school contract (if any);

3. "AIDS Test Report," "International Travel Health Examination Certificate," and "International Immunization Certificate";

4. Original diploma and transcript, notarized documents, and corresponding dual authentication;

5. More than 20 3*4 color photos with white background, and at least 10 color one-inch photos with white background;

6. One black or blue gel pen or fountain pen for filling out the arrival form on the plane.

7. Important documents and their copies

*Documents required for *public derivatives will not be discussed in detail here

8. Address, phone, and contact book for emergency contact (most people keep them on their phones or devices; it's recommended to back up a paper copy, which might be useful in critical situations).

Daily necessities

1. Students with myopia need to bring both contact lenses and glasses

2. Bedding (single bed size duvet covers, pillowcases, and sheets);

3. Umbrella;

4. A set of nail clippers and a sewing kit;

5. Slippers, towels, toothbrush, comb, hangers, and personal toiletries (mainly for the initial transition process, not listed individually according to personal needs);

*When visiting Russia in summer, remember to bring sunscreen, and it should be one with a high sun protection index. Lip balm, moisturizer, body lotion Russia's climate is dry, so moisturizing products are always useful. But in Russia, these items are not only cheap but also of excellent quality, and can be purchased locally later.

6. Rice cooker (size varies by individual)

7. Lunchbox (better if sealed well), chopsticks, spoon, cup

*Other kitchen items such as cutting boards, pots, pans, etc. can be purchased in Russia at moderate prices and no need to bring your own

Electronic products

1. Power plug adapter and electrical outlet (power strip)

*Russia uses both Russian and European standards. Both plug tips have cylindrical wires, and European is slightly thicker than Russian ones. You can also buy them locally. Prices range from 5 to 20 rubles (2 to 8 yuan each), or Taobao sells plug combo products, suitable for different countries.

2. Mobile phone and charger. Data cable. Headphones

3.ipad

4. Power bank (according to the Civil Aviation Administration, as long as the power bank has a capacity not exceeding 20,000mAh, it can be brought on the plane)

5. Computer

Apparel

Russia has a large temperature difference between day and night, so pay special attention. Rainproof outerwear and snowproof down jackets are good choices to keep warm in Russia's winter. Scarves or shawls can usually be used for warmth. Russia often has heavy snow in winter, so when walking outdoors, waterproof shoes and pants are very necessary. Windproof raincoats It's best to have pockets for windbreakers. Hoods. Loose ones can be layered inside when it's cold.

1. Down jacket (can be worn on the plane in winter to reduce luggage weight);

2. Winter hat, gloves, and scarf (essential);

3. Pants (jeans, casual pants, dress pants, etc.);

4. Men may consider bringing a suit (for formal occasions), and women can bring several formal dresses;

5. Jackets, shirts with short and long sleeves, socks, underwear, skirts, boots, and ties, etc., can be carried in appropriate quantities according to personal needs.

6. Hat (shades in summer, windproof and warm in winter)

7. Sunglasses (for sun protection in summer, for snow blindness prevention in winter)

8. Shoes (cotton boots, sports shoes, formal leather shoes, casual leather shoes, slippers)

*In winter, Russian indoor heating is sufficient, so you only need to wear short-sleeved clothes indoors, without heavy jackets; Winter clothing for cold protection is generally only worn when going out, so students are advised not to bring too many heavy clothes.

Stationery category

1. Gel pen and refill;

2. Pencil;

3. Notebook;

4. Staplers, sticky notes, transparent tape, etc

(1-4: Since these items are low-value, buying locally won't cost much, so you can skip them if possible.) )

5. One Russian-Chinese and one Chinese-Russian dictionary (it is recommended that students buy an electronic dictionary domestically, as paper dictionaries are heavy and inconvenient to carry); If students have computers, consider not bringing a dictionary, as they can install Russian translation software themselves.

6. It's recommended to bring a voice recorder to record the teacher's lectures. Instant note-taking is a challenge for Russian students, so recording class content and repeatedly dictating it is a good learning method. So choose a voice recorder with good performance.

Pharmaceuticals

It mainly depends on personal circumstances; it is recommended not to bring Chinese herbal medicine but to bring over-the-counter medicines.

Bring some of each (not too many), such as cold medicine, laxative, anti-inflammatory, band-aids, safflower oil or Yunnan Baiyao, throat care medicine, fever reducer, painkiller, toothache medicine, anti-allergy medicine, and stomach medicine. It's best to keep the medicine and instructions separate. It is recommended to prepare mosquito repellent, eye drops, band-aids, rhubarb soda tablets (for constipation), and Huoxiang Zhengqi water (for heatstroke prevention).

*Moscow is often filled with willow catkins in summer, so those with rhinitis or sensitive skin are especially cautious.

俄罗斯各大高校都开始开学了，对于初次去俄罗斯留学的同学来说，准备留学俄罗斯的行李物品还是不知所措，为了保证大家都能够顺利出国，下面为大家整理了俄罗斯留学行李清单，各位有需要的同学可以参考一下。

文件类

(随身带，不要放到托运行李中)

1.含签证的护照及机票;

2.学校录取通知书.内务部签证信复印件，学校合同原件(如有);

3.《艾滋病检验报告单》、《国际旅行健康检查证明书》和《国际预防接种证书》;

4.学历证书及成绩单原件和公证件，以及相应的双认证;

5.20张以上3*4白底彩色麻面照片，10张以上白底彩色一寸照片;

6.黑或蓝色中性笔或钢笔一支，用于在飞机上填写入境单。

7.重要文件及其复印件

*公派生所需携带的文件材料这里就不特别提了

8.地址电话通讯录，方便紧急联络使用(一般大家都存在手机或设备里，这里建议备份一个纸质的，说不定关键时有用)

日常用品类

1.如有近视的同学需带隐形眼镜和框架眼镜;

2. 床上用品(单人床尺寸的被套、枕套和床单);

3. 雨伞;

4. 指甲剪一套、针线盒;

5. 拖鞋、毛巾、牙刷、梳子、衣架及个人洗漱用品(主要为初到的衔接过程准备，根据个人需要，不一一列举);

*夏季去俄罗斯，防晒霜记得带，还需防晒指数较高的。润唇膏、润肤霜、身体乳俄罗斯气候干燥，滋润产品一直都用得上，但是这些东西在俄罗斯，不止价格便宜质量还非常好，后期可在当地购买。

6. 电饭锅(大小随个人而定)

7. 饭盒(密封性好些).筷子.勺子.水杯

*其他的厨房用品如砧板，锅碗瓢盆等都可以在俄购买，价格中等，无需带

电子用品

1. 转换插头.电源插座(排插)

*俄罗斯用的是俄标和欧标，插头尖端两个插线都是圆柱形的，欧标比俄标略粗些。在当地也可以买得到。5-20个卢布不等(2-8元钱一个)，或者淘宝有卖插头综合宝的，适合各个国家。

2.手机及充电器.数据线.耳机

3.ipad

4.充电宝(民航局规定，只要容量不超过20000毫安的充电宝，是可以带上飞机的)

5.电脑

服装类

俄罗斯昼夜温差大，要特别留意。防雨外衣和防雪羽绒服是抵御俄罗斯冬天寒冷的好选择。围巾/披巾平时可用做御寒。俄罗斯冬季常大雪，在户外走动，防水一点的鞋子和裤子是很有必要的。防风雨衣防风雨衣最好要有口袋.有帽兜。宽松点的，冷的时候里面可以加几件衣服。

1.羽绒服(如冬天登机，可穿在身上，减轻行李重量);

2. 冬帽，手套和围巾(必备);

3. 裤子(牛仔裤，休闲裤和西裤等);

4. 男生可考虑带一套西装(用于出席正式场合)，女生也可带几套正式场合穿的礼服;

5. 外套，长短袖衬衫，袜子，内衣，裙子，靴子和领带等，可根据个人需要适量携带。

6.帽子(夏天遮阳，冬天挡风保暖)

7.墨镜(夏天遮阳，冬天防雪盲)

8.鞋子(棉靴，运动鞋.正式皮鞋.休闲皮鞋.拖鞋)

*冬天俄罗斯室内有暖气，在室内只须穿中短袖的衣服即可，无须穿厚实的外套;防寒冬衣一般只是外出的时候穿，所以建议学生不需要带太多的厚重衣服。

文具类

1. 中性笔及笔芯;

2. 铅笔;

3. 笔记本;

4.订书机、便签条、透明胶等

(1-4，由于这些物品均为低值品，在当地购买的话也花费不多，可省略就省略。)

5. 俄汉、汉俄字典各一本(建议学生在国内买电子词典，因为纸质的词典很重，非常不方便携带)；如学生有电脑，可考虑不带字典，因为学生可自行安装俄语翻译软件。

6.录音笔推荐带一支，用于记录老师的讲课内容，即时手记笔记对俄罗斯学生来说都是个难题，录制课堂内容回去反复听写是个不错的学习方法。所以录音笔效果要选择好些的。

药品类

主要根据个人情况决定，建议不要带中草药，要带成药。

感冒药、腹泻药、消炎药、创口贴、红花油或者云南白药、护嗓药、退烧药、止痛药、治牙痛的药、抗过敏药和胃药等各带一些(数量不要太多)，药品和说明书最好分开放。建议准备防蚊水、眼药水、创口贴、大黄苏打片(防便秘)、藿香正气水(防中暑)等药。

*莫斯科夏天会有不少杨花飞舞，建议鼻炎、皮肤易敏感者特别留意。
