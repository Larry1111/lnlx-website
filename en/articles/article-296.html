Guaranteed study abroad in Japan

Since the Ministry of Education established a nationwide electronic registration, review, and verification system for higher education diplomas in 2001, online queries and certification of Chinese higher education students' academic qualifications have become systematic, greatly reducing the difficulty of degree review. Naturally, the focus of review for studying in Japan has naturally shifted to the rationality of financial guarantees. Study abroad education consultants remind parents to be aware of several misconceptions about financial guarantees:

Misconception 1: Relatives in Japan with sufficient financial strength can guarantee the position

Some applicants or their parents say they have a relative in Japan who has been there for many years, currently has their own company or works at a large company, and is very wealthy. When further inquiring about the relationship between the relative and the applicant, it is often the parents who circle around for a long time before focusing on the applicant's relationship with the applicant themselves. Undoubtedly, this relationship does not have the so-called close blood relation and cannot serve as a guarantor for the applicant.

Misconception 2: Having Japanese nationality, or working and living in Japan with sufficient financial strength is sufficient to guarantee the guarantee

Although the Japanese Immigration Control Act does not explicitly specify the relationship between the applicant and the payment provider, when applying for a Japanese sponsorship for Japanese students, the applicant is usually approved to have a certain blood relationship with the payment provider. Applicants or friends of the applicant's parents are not permitted.

Misconception 3: Residing in Japan and having a relative within three degrees of kinship with the applicant can guarantee a deposit of 3 million yen

For studying in Japan, if the payer is a relative of the applicant, the applicant usually needs to be within three relatives, such as: the applicant's parents, stepparents, grandparents, maternal grandparents, siblings and their spouses, and the applicant's siblings and their spouses, etc. If the above relatives are in Japan, they usually need to hold Japanese nationality or have resided permanently in Japan for three years or more.

自2001年教育部将全国高等教育学历证书电子注册审核备案并建立查询系统以来，网上查询、认证中国高等教育学生学历已经系统化，这大大降低了学历审查难度。理所当然的，日本留学的审查重点自然转到了经济担保的合理性上。留学教育顾问，提醒家长们注意关于经济担保的几点误区：

误区一：在日本亲属并具备足够的经济实力就可以担保

有的申请者或者他们的父母会讲，在日本有个亲属，已经到日本多年，目前在日本有自己的公司或是在大公司工作，非常有钱。当进一步询问这位亲属与申请人的关系时，往往是父母绕了半天才绕到与申请者本人的关系上。毫无疑问，这种关系不具备那种所说的亲近的血缘关系，是不能作为申请者担保人的。

误区二：具备日本国籍、或在日本工作及居住并具备足够的经济实力就可以担保

虽然在日本入管法中没有明确规定申请者与经费支付者必须是什么样的关系，但日本留学在做日担保的情况通常被批准的是申请者与经费支付者具有一定的血缘关系。申请者或申请者父母的朋友是不可以的。

误区三：在日居住、与申请者是三亲之内的亲属具有300万日元存款就可以担保

日本留学，如果经费支付者是申请者的亲属通常要求是三亲之内，如：申请者的父母、继父母、祖父母、外祖父母、父母的兄弟姐妹及其他们的配偶，申请者的兄弟姐妹及其他们的配偶等等。如果上述亲属在日本，通常要求具备日本国籍或者在日本永居三年或三年以上。
