Admission requirements

n Study requirements: Students must take the high school entrance exam in China and receive a high school admission letter.

n Age requirements: Applicants for grade 12 must not be over 20 years old; Grade 11 must not be over 19 years old; Grade 10 must not be over 18 years old.

n Time requirements: Students must have studied in Australia for at least 16 months and cannot regress for more than one and a half years.

Further education assessment

Australian high school admission uses an assessment-based system, combining regular grades and state exams to balance score differences among schools to form Gaokao scores and determine admission status.

Recommended admission plans

A． Elite type—elite private secondary schools

n Suitable for: junior high school graduates, current students in their first or second year of high school;

n Language requirements: IELTS 4 / AEAS above 36; applying with us grants IELTS exemption;

n Duration: 10-30 weeks of language + 1.5-3 years of high school;

n Guarantee fee: 600,000–900,000 RMB;

n Academic requirements: Average high school grade above 80;

n Age requirements: 10th grade no older than 18 years old, 11th grade no older than 19 years old.

Solution Analysis:

Australian elite private high schools generally have a long history, and tuition and application fees are quite expensive. When admitting overseas students, these schools have annual quota limits, so applications must be submitted about a year in advance. Generally, students who transfer midway are not admitted. Some schools even require an interview to be admitted.

Elite private schools place great emphasis on the all-round development of students, providing various cultural and sports activities as well as opportunities to participate in society.

B ． Economical — public middle schools in various states

n Suitable for: junior high school graduates, current students in their first or second year of high school;

n Language requirements: IELTS score above 4, eligible for I ELTS exemption through our application;

n Duration: 10-30 weeks of language + 1.5-3 years of high school;

n Guarantee fee: 500,000–700,000 RMB;

n Academic requirements: IELTS scores with a high school English score above 75% may be waived;

n Age requirements: Grade 10 must not exceed 18 years old, Grade 11 must not exceed 19 years old;

Solution Breakdown:

Each Australian state has dozens to hundreds of government-funded public schools, distributed across various regions, mainly targeting local Australian students. Each state's education department uniformly recruits overseas students, selecting suitable public secondary schools for overseas students based on campus facilities, faculty, and other factors, and determines the annual proportion of overseas students admitted by each school. Each state has different final exam systems; some require overseas students to have studied for at least two years before they can take the final exam.

C ． Mass-Oriented — An International Private College

n Suitable for: junior high school graduates, first- or second-year high school students, and students attending three schools;

n Language requirements: IELTS score above 4; applying with us may exempt IELTS;

n Duration: 10-30 weeks of language + 1.5-3 years of high school;

n Guarantee fee: 500,000–700,000 RMB;

n Academic requirements: Admission to high school with an English score above 75% is eligible for IELTS exemption;

n Age requirements: 10th grade no older than 18 years old, 11th grade no older than 19 years old.

Solution Analysis:

International private colleges cater to the diverse backgrounds of international students, offering small classes with low entry barriers suitable for students from secondary or three-year schools of varying learning levels. Tuition fees are relatively low.

推荐入学方案

A． 精英型——精英私立中学

n 适应人群：初中毕业、高一／二在读；

n 语言要求：IELTS 4分/AEAS 36分以上，通过我们申请可豁免IELTS；

n 学 制：10-30周语言+1.5-3年高中；

n 担保费用：60-90万人民币；

n 学术要求：中学平均成绩在80分以上；

n 年龄要求：10年级不超过18周岁、11年级不超过19周岁。

方案解析：

澳大利亚精英私立高中一般都具有悠久的历史，学费、申请费也相当的昂贵。这类学校招收海外学生时，每年都有名额限制，因此，需要提前一年左右的时间递交申请，一般不接受中途插班的学生入读。部分中学甚至要求通过面试才能录取。

精英私立学校非常注重学生的全面发展，学校为学生提供各类文体活动与参与社会的机会。

B． 经济型——各州公立中学

n 适应人群：初中毕业、高一／二在读；

n 语言要求：IELTS 4分以上，通过我们申请可豁免I ELTS；

n 学 制：10-30周语言+1.5-3年高中；

n 担保费用：50-70万人民币；

n 学术要求：中学英语成绩在75％以上可豁免IELTS成绩；

n 年龄要求：10年级不超过18周岁、11年级不超过19周岁；

方案解折：

澳大利亚各州都有几十到上百所由政府出资的公立学校，分布在每州的各个地区，主要的招生对象为澳大利亚的当地学生。各州的教育部统一对海外学生进行招生，根据校园设施、师资力量等条件选择适合招收海外学生的公立中学，并根据情况决定各个学校每年招收海外学生的比例。各州的终考制度不同，部分州规定海外学生必须接受2年以上的学习时间，才能参加终考。

C． 大众型——国际型私立学院

n 适应人群：初中毕业、高一／二在读及在读三校生；

n 语言要求：IELTS 4分以上，通过我们申请可豁免IELTS；

n 学 制：10-30周语言+1.5-3年高中；

n 担保费用：50-70万人民币；

n 学术要求：被高中录取且英语成绩在75％以上可豁免IELTS成绩；

n 年龄要求：10年级不超过18周岁、11年级不超过19周岁。

方案解析：

国际型私立学院针对国际学生的不同背景，设置小班授课，入学门槛较低，适合不同学习层次的中学或三校生。学费相对较便宜。
