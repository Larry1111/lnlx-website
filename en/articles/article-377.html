Foreign students wishing to study in Singapore must apply for a student pass. Once applicants receive an admission notice from their school, they should promptly submit their student pass application to the relevant departments of the Singapore Immigration and Checkpoints Department. Specific regulations for Chinese students attending the following types of schools for student passes.

Foreign students wishing to study in Singapore must apply for a student pass. Once applicants receive an admission notice from their school, they should promptly submit their student pass application to the relevant departments of the Singapore Immigration and Checkpoints Department. Specific regulations for Chinese students attending the following types of schools for student passes.

Approved private schools

Materials required to apply for the student pass

* Two copies each of Form 16, Form V39S, and Form V36. All forms are correctly completed and signed by the applicant and guarantor in the respective sections. At least one of the two forms must be the original.

* Two recent color (passport-style) photos. One of the photos is posted in the upper right corner of Form 16.

* Notarized birth of the applicant.

* Applicant's highest diploma and transcript (notarized).

* Applicant's ID card and parents' ID cards (photocopies)

* Applicant's passport (photocopy).

* Copy of the local guarantor's ID card.

* A properly completed and school-signed Form V36A.

* Bank deposit certificate (150,000 RMB).

Government/government-aided/autonomous schools

Materials required to apply for the student pass

* Before the school start date, first-time applicants must submit the following documents at least two months in advance.

* One copy each of Forms 16, V39S, V39Q, and V103. All forms must be correctly filled out and signed by the applicant and local guarantor in the respective sections.

* A recent color or black-and-white passport-type photo, attached to the upper right corner of Form 16;

* Applicant's passport (photocopy);

* Applicant's birth certificate (original and copy);

* Copy of the local guarantor's ID card;

* The original admission notice from the school to the Immigration and Registration Bureau. The period from the notice to the date of submission of application materials should not exceed one week.

Polytechnic colleges and universities

Materials required to apply for the student pass

* Before the school start date, first-time applicants must submit the following documents at least two months in advance.

Submit electronic application materials through the Student Pass online application system, including the following documents:

* Applicant's passport (photocopy)

* Admission letter issued by a Singaporean higher education institution

* Online application notice for student passes issued by Singapore's higher education institution

外籍学生若希望在新加坡读书，必须申请学生准证，申请者一旦收到学校的录取通知，应尽快向新加坡移民与关卡局的有关部门递交学生准证申请。中国学生就读下列各类学校办理学生准证的具体规定。

获得批准的私立学校

申请学生准证所需材料

* 表格16、表格V39S和表格V36各两份。所有表格正确填写，并由申请人和担保人在相应栏目签字。两份表格中必须至少有一份是原件。

* 两张近期彩色（护照型）照片。其中一张照片贴在表格16的右上角。

* 申请人的出生公证。

* 申请人的最高学历证书和成绩单（公证）。

* 申请人身份证及父母身份证(复印件)

* 申请人护照(复印件)。

* 本地担保人的身份证(复印件)。

* 一份正确填写并由学校签字的表格V36A。

* 银行存款证明（15万人民币）。

政府/政府辅助/自主学校

申请学生准证所需材料

* 距离学校开学日期，首次申请者必须至少提前两个月递交以下资料。

* 表16 、表V39S、表V39Q 和表V103 各一份。所有表格正确填写，并由申请人、本地担保人在相应栏目签字。

* 一张近期彩色或黑白护照型照片，贴在表16的右上角;

* 申请人护照(复印件);

* 申请人的出生证(原件及复印件);

* 本地担保人的身份证(复印件);

* 学校致移民与登记局的录取通知原件。从通知发出日至申请材料递交日，应不超过一周。

理工学院和大学

申请学生准证所需材料

* 距离学校开学日期，首次申请者必须至少提前两个月递交以下资料。

通过学生准证网上申请系统递交电子申请材料，包括以下文件:

* 申请人护照(复印件)

* 新加坡高等教育机构发出的录取通知书

* 新加坡高等教育机构发出的网上申请学生准证通知
