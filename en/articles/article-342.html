Australian undergraduate education

Australia has 39 universities (37 public and 2 private), and its higher education quality and academic standards are internationally renowned. Although the number of universities is far less than that of the UK and the US, among these 39 universities, 17 have ranked among the world's top 200 universities. In other words, nearly 50% of Australian universities have world-class educational quality.

Professionalism

Universities typically offer departments such as business, architecture, medical science, health sciences, nursing, law, engineering, information technology, humanities, and literature, with a few offering programs such as veterinary medicine and arts.

Educational system

Most Australian undergraduate programs enroll in spring and autumn each year, with a three-year duration, while double degrees and certain disciplines (such as law and medicine) last 4-5 years.

Tuition fees

Tuition fees typically range from 16,000 to 22,000 Australian dollars per year, varying by institution and major.

Recommended admission plans

A. Elite — Foundation + Bachelor

n Suitable for: Graduates of 11th or 11th grade

n Language requirement: IELTS 5.5

n Duration: Foundation (1 year), Undergraduate (3-5 years)

n Guarantee fee: 600,000-800,000

n School major: No restrictions

n Admission requirements: Average high school score above 70

Solution Analysis:

Undergraduate programs at Australian universities generally require overseas high school graduates to complete about one year of foundation coursework to adapt to the learning style of Australian universities (especially the well-known Big Eight universities).

n Australian universities generally have their own foundation courses or designated colleges to offer foundation courses.

n After completing the one-year foundation program, international students can decide whether they can enter their original chosen institution based on their grades, and may also transfer to other better universities based on their results.

B. Premium Type—Direct Bachelor from High School

n Target audience: High school graduates

n Language requirement: IELTS 6.0/6.5

n Duration: 3-4 years

n Guarantee fee: 600,000 to 700,000

n School major: Not accepted by all institutions

n Admission requirements: Gaokao scores must be at least above the second-tier university cutoff, and regular high school scores above 80

Solution Analysis:

n Some Australian universities admit undergraduates based on Chinese college entrance exam results, without needing to complete the preparatory course, allowing them to enter the first year of university directly.

n The language requirements are relatively higher than those of the foundation course.

In terms of majors, some universities limit their majors to business and computer science, while others allow admission based on all majors based on college entrance exam scores.

C. Shortcut—IBT course = Diploma + Bachelor

n Target Audience: Chinese senior high school graduates / students from three universities (secondary vocational, junior college, vocational schools)

n Language requirement: IELTS 6.0

n Duration: Diploma program (8-16 months) + Undergraduate (2-4 years)

n Guarantee fee: 600,000-700,000

n School majors: Limited, confined to business, information systems, mass media, tourism and hotel management.

Solution Analysis:

n Students can obtain their degree in as little as 2 years and 8 months, which is more time-saving and cost-effective than the foundation model, but subject to certain major and institution restrictions.

n Graduates of high schools, vocational schools, or vocational schools recognized by the Chinese Ministry of Education can study an 8 to 16 month fast-track diploma program. After graduation, they do not need to start from the first year of undergraduate studies, but can directly advance to the second year of a relevant undergraduate program at an Australian institution.

澳大利亚共有39所大学（37所公立大学和2所私立大学），其高等教育质量与学术水平在国际上享有盛誉。虽然从大学数量上，远不如英国和美国。但就这在39所大学中，有17所大学跃居全球名校200强。换言之，澳大利亚近50％的大学都拥有一世界级的教育质量。

专业

大学内通常设置商科、建筑、医疗科学、健康科学、护理学、法律、工程、信息技术、人文学、文学等学科，少数学校有兽医学、艺术等专业。

学制

多数澳大利亚本科课程每年有春、秋两季入学，学制3年，双学位及个别学科（如法律、医科等）学制为4-5年。

学费

学费每年通常在16000一22000澳元之间，根据院校、专业不同而存在差异。

推荐升学方案

A. 精英型——预科（Foundation）+ 本科（Bachelor)

n 适合人群：高二或高三毕业生

n 语言要求：IELTS 5.5

n 学 制：预科（1年）十本科（3-5年）

n 担保费用：60-80万

n 学校专业：不受限制

n 入学要求：高中平均成绩70分以上

方案解析：

n 澳大利亚大学本科课程，一般都要求海外高中毕业生修读1年左右的预科，以适应澳大利亚大学的学习方式（特别是大家熟悉的八大名校）。

n 澳大利亚大学一般都有自己预科或指定学院教授预科课程。

n 留学生完成1年预科后，根据成绩决定能否升人原先选择的院校，也可根据成绩转入其它更好的大学。

B. 精品型——高中直接升本科（Bachelor)

n 适应人群：高中毕业生

n 语言要求：IELTS 6.0/6.5

n 学 制：3-4年

n 担保费用：60万一70万

n 学校专业：非所有院校接受

n 入学要求：高考成绩至少在二本分数线以上，高中平时成绩80分以上

方案解析：

n 部分澳大利亚大学根据中国高考成绩录取本科生，无需读预科，直接进入大学一年级。

n 相对语言上的要求比预科高。

n 在专业上，部分院校仅局限于商科、计算机等专业，也有部分学校的所有专业都能根据高考成绩录取。

C. 快捷型——IBT课程=文凭（Diploma) + 本科(Bachelor)

n 适应人群：中国高三毕业生／三校生（中专、大专、职校）

n 语言要求：IELTS 6.0

n 学 制：文凭课程（8-16个月）＋本科（2-4年）

n 担保费用：60-70万

n 学校专业：受到限制，局限于商科、信息系统、大众传媒、旅游酒店管理。

方案解析：

n 学生最短可以在2年8个月获得学位，较预科模式省时、省钱，但有一定专业及院校限制。

n 中国教育部认可的高中、中专或职校毕业生可通过攻读8至16个月的快速文凭课程，毕业后无需从本科一年级读起，而是直接升人澳大利亚院校相关专业的本科二年级就读。
